## 1. Summary

**CHANGE NEEDED (18), most serious first:**
1. **C6.** Twilio and carriers forbid "third-party lead generation" and models that "connect" customers to other companies. Fix the campaign description, privacy wording and data flow before registering the campaign (Twilio forbidden categories; CTIA 5.1.2.2).
2. **B1.** Route pest and pre-emergent or advertised weed jobs only to businesses with an AZ Dept. of Agriculture license. Ask Hava's own "advertising for… pest management" may trigger licensing (A.R.S. §3-3601(4), §3-3615(A), §3-3613(B)).
3. **A3.** Ask Hava's own ads for plumbing, roofing and similar trades must not say or imply that Ask Hava does the work (A.R.S. §32-1165; §32-1101(A)(1)).
4. **B6.** Alarm jobs go only to Board-certified alarm businesses, and camera or low-voltage jobs only to ROC R-67 (or equivalent) licensees. Inspections go only to certified home inspectors (A.R.S. §32-122.05(A), §32-122.02; R4-9-103 R-67).
5. **A4.** Handyman routing:
   - Enforce the $1,000 cap (labor plus materials).
   - Block split jobs.
   - Block permit, smoke-alarm, sprinkler and gas work.
   - Keep the "not a licensed contractor" wording (A.R.S. §32-1121(A)(14), (C), (D)).
6. **C1.** Get E-SIGN prior express written consent from businesses for job-card texts. Keep every homeowner text purely informational (47 CFR 64.1200(a)(2), (f)(1), (f)(13)).
7. **C5.** The automated voice call must say "Ask Hava LLC" at the start and give a callback number (47 CFR 64.1200(b)(1)–(2)).
8. **D1.** "Every quote shown" is false, because some quotes are refused. "Insurance on file" needs a "self-reported" qualifier. The "$25 lead" claim needs substantiation (FTC Act §5; A.R.S. §44-1522; 16 CFR 465.7).
9. **G2.** Show "Homeowner prefers Spanish" only to businesses that ticked "We speak Spanish". Never allow skipping a job on language (A.R.S. §41-1441(2), §41-1442(B)).
10. **E1.** Business card on file must use Stripe's off-session consent terms, stored as an agreement record (Stripe save-and-reuse docs).
11. **F1.** Post a CalOPPA-compliant privacy policy, plus the CTIA privacy statements (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §22575(b)).
12. **F3.** Disclose the AI and other service providers in the privacy policy. Strip photo metadata (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §22575(b)(1); CTIA 5.2.1).
13. **J1.** Publish the LLC notice 3 times within 60 days in Mohave County, or use a statutory agent in Maricopa or Pima County (A.R.S. §29-3201(G)).
14. **J3.** Get a city business license and, if home-based, a home-occupation review (lhcaz.gov business-license page; Home Occupations ordinance sheet).
15. **B3.** Route septic pumping only to haulers with an ADEQ septage-hauler vehicle license (A.A.C. R18-13-1100 et seq. via ADEQ [S]).
16. **D5.** Write the automatic-refund rules into the business terms word-for-word, then honor them (A.R.S. §44-1522; contract).
17. **G3.** Add a non-discrimination clause to the business terms (A.R.S. §41-1442(B)).
18. **C8.** Set a written retention rule: keep consent and opt-out logs at least 4 years after the last message (28 U.S.C. §1658(a)).

## 4. Anything we missed (K1)

- **Arizona Consumer Fraud Act for business-facing claims.** A.R.S. §44-1522 covers advertisement of "any merchandise", and business customers may be covered too [P/I]. Business-side marketing ("win jobs", conversion claims) needs substantiation. The FTC HomeAdvisor order (2023) [S] is the model risk.
- **"First win free" and automatic billing.** If any business plan ever becomes recurring or subscription, Arizona and FTC negative-option rules may apply. The FTC "click-to-cancel" rule was vacated by the 8th Cir. in 2025 [U]. Check before adding subscriptions.
- **Twilio and CTIA content rules for SHAFT and other restricted content.** Job descriptions from homeowners could include restricted content. Filter before forwarding [I].
- **Contractor-side recovery fund.** Explain to homeowners that ROC complaints and the Residential Contractors' Recovery Fund (A.R.S. §32-1131 et seq.) only cover licensed contractors [U — not opened today]. This supports the handyman disclosure.
- **Worker-classification and independence.** Keep Ask Hava from controlling how businesses do the work, to protect the independent-business statement [I].
- **Federal INFORM Consumers Act.** Applies to online marketplaces for consumer *products* by third-party sellers. Likely not this services model [U]. Confirm if goods are ever sold.
- **Spanish-language disclosures.** If the site or texts market in Spanish, give the key terms (fee, STOP/HELP, privacy) in Spanish too, to avoid a deception claim [I].
- **Public-records and robots.** Respect site terms and rate limits for ROC and city lookups (A6/J4).
- **Arizona data-broker or "sale" exposure.** None found in Arizona [S]. Never "sell" homeowner data; this also keeps CCPA "sharing" thresholds off [I].
