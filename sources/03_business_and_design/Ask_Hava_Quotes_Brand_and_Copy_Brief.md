# Ask Hava Quotes: brand and copy brief

**Owner:** Casey Solomon · **Date:** October 26, 2026 · **Status:** working draft

---

## 1. What it is

**One line:** Text Ask Hava what you need done, and Hava gathers quotes from local businesses so you can compare them and choose.

**One paragraph:** Ask Hava already helps people find what's going on and who to call in Lake Havasu. Now Hava can also do the calling around. Text one local number or use the chat on askhava.com, say what you need (a leaky faucet, a weekly pool service, a boat detail), and every local business that offers that service can send a quote. You see every quote on one private page, with each business's own wording, review links and state license status where a license applies. You pick. Your phone number is never shared, nobody calls you until you choose, and it's free for you. The business you pick pays Ask Hava a small fee.

**What it is not:**
- not a contractor;
- not a referral to a "trusted pro";
- not a ranking.

Hava gathers the quotes, and you decide.

## 2. How it fits the Ask Hava brand

| Ask Hava today | Ask Hava with quotes |
|---|---|
| Local directory and events concierge | The same concierge, now running errands too |
| "Who should I call?" | "Hava, get me quotes." |
| Listings, events, conditions | A **Get quotes** button on every service category and listing that opts in |

**Brand idea:** Hava is your local concierge. Concierges don't sell you things. They find out, line up options, and let you choose. That is exactly the quote service's promise.

## 3. Name options for the quote feature

| Option | Feel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask Hava** (no sub-name) | Simplest; the brand is the action | "Text Ask Hava" does the work; recommended for texts and ads |
| **Ask Hava Quotes** | Clear about what it does | Good for the website section and the business-facing side |
| **Hava Gets Quotes** | Friendly, verb-first | Good as a headline, weaker as a name |
| **Hava Pro Quotes** | Sounds premium | Avoid: "Pro" can suggest we vetted the businesses |
| **Ask Hava for Business** | For the business side | Sign-up page and pitch to service businesses |

**Recommendation:** use "Ask Hava" for homeowners and "Ask Hava for Business" for businesses. Use "quotes" as the verb and the feature, not as a separate brand.

## 4. Voice

- **Warm, local, short.** Like a well-connected neighbor who happens to be very organized.
- **Plain words.** "Get quotes," "choose," "free for you." Avoid "solutions," "vetted," "trusted pros," "best" and "#1."
- **Honest about what Hava is.** Hava is a service, and its texts are automated. Never write as if a person named Hava is personally calling around. The FAQ says it plainly: "Hava is the Ask Hava service. Our texts are automated; a real person reviews anything flagged."
- **Neutral.** Hava never recommends one business over another. Quotes appear in the order they arrived.

## 6. Headlines and page copy starters

**Home page hero**
> **Need something done? Ask Hava.**
> Text what you need. Local businesses send quotes. You see them all on one page and choose. Free for you, and your number stays private.
> [Text (928) 555-0100] [Start online]

**How it works (4 steps)**
1. **Tell Hava what you need.** Text or chat, and add a photo if you have one.
2. **Local businesses quote.** Every business that offers that service can send one.
3. **Compare on one page.** Prices in their own words, review links, license status.
4. **Choose.** Only the business you pick gets your name and address, and they call through Hava's number.

**Promises row**
- Free for you, always
- Your number is never shared
- Every quote shown, never ranked
- No spam. Reply STOP anytime.

**Service-category pages** (one line each)
- *Pool cleaning:* "Get weekly pool service quotes from local pool companies."
- *AC repair:* "AC down in July? Hava sends your request to every local HVAC company that wants it."
- *Boat detailing:* "Get your boat lake-ready. Compare detail quotes from Havasu detailers."
- *Home watch:* "Away for the summer? Get quotes from local home-watch services."

## 7. Text messages, in the Ask Hava voice

The required disclosures stay word for word; only the brand name changes.

- **First text (consent):** "Hi, this is Ask Hava. We gather quotes so you don't have to make the calls. It's free for you, and the business you choose pays us a small fee. Msg frequency varies by request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms and privacy: askhava.com/terms. Reply OK to start."
- **First quote:** "Ask Hava: your first quote for request 1042 is in, and more may come. See them all: askhava.com/q/…"
- **Update:** "Ask Hava: you now have 4 quotes for request 1042. askhava.com/q/…"
- **Picked:** "Ask Hava: Sample Plumbing has 2 business hours to confirm. We'll text you who will call and from what number."
- **STOP confirmation:** "Ask Hava: you won't get more texts. To use Ask Hava again, text START."

## 8. Business-facing copy (Ask Hava for Business)

**Hero**
> **Get local jobs. Pay only when you're picked.**
> Ask Hava sends you job requests from Havasu residents for the work you choose. Quote the ones you want. If a customer picks you and you confirm, the fee is $25, $75 or $199 depending on job size. Never for a lead you lose.

**Pitch lines**
- "Other lead sites charge for every lead. Ask Hava charges only for customers who already chose you."
- "$25 for a job under $500. About what one online lead costs, except this customer said yes."
- "Your first win is free."
- "Can't reach the customer? Automatic refund."
- "Repeat work from the same customer is always free."
- "Thousands of locals already use Ask Hava to find things to do and people to call." **Use this only once there's real usage data to back it.**

**Directory tie-in (factual badge):** "Quotes through Ask Hava." This means the business opted in to receive job requests. It's not an endorsement, rating or verification badge.

## 9. Ideas

**Built into Ask Hava**
1. **A "Get quotes" button** on every service category page and every listing that opted in. It opens the request chat with the category filled in.
2. **Seasonal prompts in the directory:**
   - AC tune-ups in May;
   - boat detailing in March;
   - home watch and turnover cleaning in October, as snowbirds arrive;
   - pool startup in spring.

   These are on-site banners only; never texts (no marketing texts).
3. **Events tie-in:** event pages for big weekends (the jet ski World Finals, spring break) show a "Need a rental cleaner or boat detail?" link to quotes.
4. **Conditions bar tie-in:** on 110°F days, a quiet "AC acting up? Ask Hava for quotes" link.
5. **"Request this business again"** from a resident's past jobs, which costs the business nothing.

**Getting the word out (no paid ads)**
1. Facebook group posts in Havasu community groups: one for homeowners, one for businesses.
2. An invite to every service business already listed on Ask Hava. That's about 690 businesses, contacted by email or a listing-page notice, never by unsolicited texts.
3. Small flyers or QR cards for vacation-rental owners and property managers. ("Turnover cleaner cancelled? Text Ask Hava.")
4. A simple "How Hava works" short video for Facebook.
5. A local news or radio angle: "A Havasu local builds a free way for neighbors to get repair quotes without phone tag." Use only if you're comfortable with your role being mentioned.

**Trust builders (only true ones)**
1. A public "How we make money" page: businesses pay $25, $75 or $199 when picked; homeowners never pay; no paid rankings.
2. A monthly public stats line once real data exists, for example "Average time to first quote: 2 hours."
3. A license line on every quote card where a license applies: "ROC # shows active on the state website as of [date]."

## 10. Things to keep straight

1. **The directory sells ads; the quote service never does.** Directory ads must never appear on quote pages or affect which businesses get job cards. That neutrality is the whole promise.
2. **Don't imply opt-ins are vetted.** The "Quotes through Ask Hava" badge means opted in, nothing more.
3. **Hava isn't a person.** Keep texts in the brand's voice without pretending a human is personally texting.
4. **Ask Hava's existing audience isn't counted as demand** in the financial plan; treat any lift from it as a bonus.

---

## 11. Protecting the name (not legal advice; ask the attorney)

- **A business idea can't be copyrighted.** Copyright protects the specific words, designs and code you create, and it applies automatically once they're written. You can register major works (website copy, the logo) with the U.S. Copyright Office for stronger protection.
- **The name and logo are protected by trademark,** not copyright. Ask the attorney to:
  1. search the USPTO for "Ask Hava" and similar "Hava" marks for services;
  2. file a federal trademark application for "Ask Hava" for the directory and quote services (and possibly the logo);
  3. confirm the LLC name or an Arizona trade name matches the brand.
- **Texting registration** needs the brand on record. Twilio's brand registration uses the legal business name from the EIN letter. Form the LLC as "Ask Hava LLC," or register "Ask Hava" as a trade name for the LLC, so texts, the website and the registration all match.
