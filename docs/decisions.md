## K2 (spec), October 27, 2026

- **D-001** Question: what format do requirements in `docs/spec/` take? The Kit Plan asks for IDs like REQ-MONEY-012 with Given/When/Then criteria (`sources/02_build_plan/Kit_Plan_Before_Coding.md:30`); the brief only says "numbered requirements, one file per area" (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:80`). Choice: one testable sentence per requirement, with a stable area-prefixed ID (for example `TXT-001`) and one or more `path:LINE` citations, checked by `scripts/trace_spec.py`. Given/When/Then cases are written per ID in K5 test design. Reason: the brief outranks the Kit Plan on how (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:7`); a one-sentence rule is the smallest testable unit and K5 owns test cases (`sources/02_build_plan/Kit_Plan_Before_Coding.md:34`).
- **D-002** Question: K2 is "one area per session" (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:595`); this task wrote every area in one session. Choice: accept one session for all areas, with the traceability script as the check. Reason: the runner assigned K2 as one task; the K3 conflict review (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:597`) re-reads every area with a fresh reviewer, which covers the risk the per-area rule guards against.
- **D-003** Question: quiet hours. v9.3 says 9 p.m.–8 a.m. (`sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:200`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:264`); the changes file says no non-urgent texts after 8 p.m. in the stricter of Arizona and area-code time (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:184`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:121`; `BUILD_BRIEF.md:446`). Choice: non-urgent texts are sent only when the local time is between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. in **both** Arizona time and the recipient's area-code zone. The v9.3 exceptions stay: urgent safety texts, STOP/HELP/START replies, pick codes, and direct replies within 15 minutes of the person's own text. Reason: the changes file wins on the window; it is silent on replies the person just asked for, so v9.3 still applies there.
- **D-004** Question: the "business clock". v9.3 runs timers on 8 a.m.–9 p.m. (`sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:179`), but texts may no longer go out after 8 p.m. (D-003) and the quote window counts every day (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:66`). Choice: business hours are 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. Arizona time, every day of the week; "business hour" timers (confirm, card fix, bank approval) count only time inside that window. Reason: a business can't be asked to act in a window when we can't text it; every-day counting follows the changes file.
- **D-005** Question: does an opt-out word close jobs? v9.3 says any opt-out word, including bare CANCEL, closes jobs not yet with a contractor (`sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:163`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:199`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:215`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:260`). The changes file says a single opt-out word is an opt-out, not a job cancel, and "cancel" alone never closes a job (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:159`). Choice: an opt-out stops all texts to that number and changes no job status; the homeowner's jobs carry on through the quote page and email and end by CLOSE, the close button, a cancel-intent message or their own timeouts. Reason: the changes file wins.
- **D-006** Question: are natural-language opt-outs ("please stop texting me") still honored, given the changes file defines single-word opt-outs (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`)? Choice: yes, as in v9.3 (`sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:261`); a message that merely contains an opt-out word in another sense ("can stop by at 3") is not an opt-out (`sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:188`). Reason: consent may be revoked by any reasonable means; honoring more revocations is the legally safest option.

- **D-010** Question: how is "4 months of their price" applied to a recurring price not stated per month (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:60`)?
