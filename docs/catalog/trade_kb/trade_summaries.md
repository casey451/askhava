# Ask Hava trade summaries

Short narrative per trade, taken from each file's `summary`. Ask Hava is not a contractor; it builds anonymous job cards for opted-in local businesses.

## Appliance Repair (`appliance_repair`)

Repair and install of refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges/ovens, microwaves, ice makers and disposals. ROC R-63 covers installing and repairing appliances but NOT gas, electrical or plumbing lines. Anything that connects to gas or propane is never handyman work (A.R.S. 32-1121(D)(2)). Refrigerant work needs EPA Section 608 certification. The model/serial label is the most useful thing for a quote: fridges usually inside the fresh-food side wall or ceiling; dishwashers on the inside door edge or tub edge; ranges behind the oven door or drawer; top-load washers under the lid; dryers inside the door opening (GE location guide). Havasu hard water is hard on dishwashers, ice makers and washer valves [judgment].

## Boat & dock services (`boat_dock`)

Boats and docks on and around Lake Havasu: boat engine and outboard/sterndrive repair, winterizing and tune-ups, trailer work, detailing and oxidation removal, upholstery, decontamination questions, boat lift repair, and dock repair or building. Dock work over the lake is regulated by several agencies (U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, BLM and/or Bureau of Reclamation for shoreline land, and Lake Havasu City for docks in its code), and most docks and lifts are at marinas with their own rules [judgment]. Ask Hava never says which permit a job needs; it gathers facts and sends a card.

## Cabinets & Countertops (`cabinets_countertops`)

Cabinet install, repair and refacing; countertop install and repair in granite, quartz (engineered stone), laminate, solid surface and tile. ROC R-60 Finish Carpentry lists cabinets and countertops; R-48 covers tile tops. Disconnecting and reconnecting sinks, faucets and disposals is plumbing work, and any gas cooktop reconnection is never handyman work. Cutting engineered stone makes silica dust (OSHA/NIOSH hazard alert), so shops fabricate off site and do minimal cutting in the home [judgment].

## Carpet Cleaning (`carpet_cleaning`)

Carpet, rug and upholstery cleaning, spot and pet-odor treatment, and tile-and-grout cleaning. Most carpet makers' warranties call for periodic hot water extraction by a professional [judgment; check the warranty]. No state license applies. Carpet soaked by a leak, flood or sewage is water-damage restoration (dry within 24-48 hours), not routine cleaning.

## Drywall (`drywall`)

Drywall patching, water-damage replacement, texture matching (knockdown and orange peel are common in Havasu [judgment]), new walls and ceilings, and popcorn removal. ROC R-10 covers gypsum board, tape, texture and non-load-bearing steel partitions. LHC needs no permit for minor hole patching but does list drywall work and wall removal/installation as permit work. Older textures and joint compound may contain asbestos; EPA says not to sand or scrape suspect material.

## Electrical (panels, outlets, wiring, lighting) (`electrical`)

Home electrical work on the owner's side of the meter: outlets, switches, GFCI/AFCI, lights and fans, breakers tripping, partial power loss, panel upgrades and change-outs, sub-panels, new circuits (AC, spa, shop, EV), rewires, smoke alarms (hardwired) and outdoor lighting. UniSource Energy Services owns the meter and the line to the house; Ask Hava routes outages and downed lines to UniSource and 911 [S24][S25]. Ask Hava learns what is happening, how much of the home is affected, the panel brand from a photo and the home's age, then writes a card. It never says what failed, that a panel is unsafe, or that work is needed. Licensed work is ROC R-11 Electrical / CR-11 [S2][S3]. Panel change-outs, electrical updates and rewires need an LHC permit [S17]. Hardwired or interconnected smoke alarms are never handyman work (A.R.S. 32-1121(D)(1)) [S4].

## Swamp coolers (evaporative coolers) (`evaporative_coolers`)

Evaporative or 'swamp' coolers on roofs, side walls, windows and patios: spring start-up, pad changes, pumps, motors, belts, float valves, leaks, fall shut-down and replacement. They work well in dry heat but lose cooling when monsoon humidity rises, and Lake Havasu City's very hard water (about 19 grains per gallon) builds scale fast on pads and pans [LHC-CCR][judgment]. Ask Hava learns where the cooler sits, what it is doing, its size and age, and whether the owner wants service, a seasonal visit or a new unit, then writes the card. It never says what is broken or what to buy. Licensed work is ROC R-39 (includes evaporative cooling) or the dual CR-39/CR-58/CR-79; small seasonal tasks may fit the handyman exemption [S2][S3][S4].

## Flooring (`flooring`)

Install and repair tile, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), laminate, hardwood, carpet, and concrete coatings. ROC R-8 Floor Covering covers carpet, tile, wood, linoleum, vinyl, asphalt, rubber and concrete coatings; R-48 covers tile; R-60 covers wood flooring. LHC needs no permit for floor covering. Old 9x9 inch vinyl tile and black mastic may contain asbestos; EPA says leave intact material alone and do not sand or scrape it. Slab homes are the norm in Havasu, so moisture and slab cracks matter for LVP and tile [judgment].

## Garage Doors (`garage_doors`)

Garage door springs, cables, rollers, tracks, panels, openers and remotes. Two spring types: torsion springs sit on a bar above the door and are wound under high tension; extension springs run along the side tracks and should have safety cables through them [judgment]. A broken spring often sounds like a loud bang and the door becomes very heavy [judgment]. Federal rules (16 CFR 1211) require openers to reverse and to have photo eyes or edge sensors, and require manuals to warn that springs, cables and hardware are under high tension and should be fixed by qualified people. Arizona has no separate garage door license class; door work fits ROC R-7 (carpentry, metal doors, hardware) and opener wiring R-11. Confirm with ROC.

## Gas lines (natural gas and propane piping) (`gas_lines`)

Natural gas and propane piping inside and outside the home: new lines for a range, dryer, BBQ, fire pit, pool heater, generator or patio heater, gas leak repair after UniSource red-tags, moving or capping a line, gas pressure tests, and flex connector or shutoff valve replacement. In Arizona this is plumbing-class work: R-37 covers 'water and gas piping systems' [S2]. It is never handyman work, at any price [S4]. Gas lines need an LHC permit [S17]. Ask Hava gives the gas leak safety step first, then learns the appliance, length and access. It never tells anyone a line or connector is safe, never sizes pipe, and never says whether gas can be turned back on.

## Generators and EV chargers (`generator_ev`)

Standby (whole-home) generators, transfer switches and inlets for portable generators, generator service, and home EV chargers (Level 2 circuits and charger installs). This is electrical work (R-11/CR-11); a natural gas or propane feed to a standby generator is gas piping work (R-37) and is never handyman work [S2][S4]. Ask Hava learns which device, fuel, panel photos and distance, then writes a card. It never sizes a generator or charger, never says a panel can handle a load, and never says whether a permit is needed beyond what the city lists.

## Golf cart repair (`golf_cart`)

Golf cart and low-speed vehicle repair: electric carts (lead-acid or lithium batteries), gas carts, chargers, motors and controllers, brakes, tires, lift kits, lights, seats, and street-legal upgrades. Common brands are Club Car, E-Z-GO, Yamaha, ICON and Evolution. Many Havasu carts are used in neighborhoods and to go to the lake [judgment]. Ask Hava gathers facts and photos; it never says what is wrong or if a cart is street legal.

## Gutter & Solar Panel Cleaning (`gutter_solar_cleaning`)

Gutter and downspout cleaning, solar panel washing, and bird (pigeon) critter-guard requests under panels. Many Havasu homes have few or no gutters, so solar washing and roof-debris cleanup are more common [judgment]. Panel makers' manuals generally say to use soft water and soft brushes and not to use pressure washers [judgment; check the panel manual]. Walking on tile roofs can crack tiles [judgment]. Cleaning needs no license; gutter install or repair is ROC R-45 Sheet Metal; any solar wiring or damaged panel goes to the solar installer (R-11).

## Handyman (`handyman`)

Small, casual repairs and installs around the house: patching, hanging things, assembly, caulking, minor fixture swaps, door and drawer fixes. In Arizona an unlicensed handyman may legally take a job only under the A.R.S. 32-1121(A)(14) 'handyman exemption': whole job under $1,000, casual or minor, no permit, not part of a bigger job and not split up, and ads must say 'not a licensed contractor'. Gas-connected work and hardwired/interconnected smoke alarms or sprinklers are never covered. Many requests that start as 'handyman' really belong to a licensed trade; this file is mostly about spotting those and routing them. Ask Hava does not decide whether a job qualifies; it tags the card so licensed and unlicensed businesses both see the facts.

## Home Inspection (`home_inspection`)

Buyer, seller (pre-listing) and pre-warranty home inspections, plus add-ons such as termite (WDIIR) reports, pool/spa, sewer scope and manufactured-home checks. Arizona home inspectors must be certified by the AZ Board of Technical Registration (BTR) under A.R.S. 32-122.02 and follow BTR standards of practice (A.A.C. R4-30-301.01): a visual, non-invasive look, not a code inspection. Termite (wood-destroying insect) reports must be done by an AZ Dept. of Agriculture pest management licensee (A.R.S. 3-3633). Ask Hava never says what an inspection will find.

## House Cleaning (`house_cleaning`)

Regular and one-time home cleaning: weekly/biweekly upkeep, deep cleans, move-in/move-out, post-construction dust, and short-term-rental (vacation rental) turnovers between guests. No state license applies to cleaning. Many Havasu homes are seasonal or vacation rentals, so turnover cleaning on fixed check-out/check-in windows is a big part of the local work [judgment]. Sewage, mold growth and hoarding-level cleanups belong to restoration or specialty services, not routine cleaning.

## Heating & cooling (AC, heat pumps, mini-splits, rooftop units) (`hvac`)

Central AC, heat pumps, gas or electric furnaces, rooftop package units and ductless mini-splits. In Lake Havasu City this is the most time-sensitive trade: summer highs often pass 115 F, and a home with no cooling can become dangerous for older adults, babies, people with medical needs and pets [judgment]. Ask Hava's job is to learn the system type, what it is doing, its age (from the data plate photo), the refrigerant on the label and the roof or yard access, then write a card a business can quote from. Ask Hava never says what is wrong, what it costs, or whether to repair or replace. Swamp coolers have their own file (evaporative_coolers). Licensed work is ROC R-39 / CR-39 / CR-79 (residential) and refrigerant handling needs EPA Section 608 certification [S2][S3][S38].

## Junk Removal (`junk_removal`)

Hauling away furniture, appliances, yard waste, construction debris, estate and garage cleanouts, and small demolition such as sheds or hot tubs. No state license is needed to haul. Demolition of structures is ROC R-57 Wrecking; disconnecting a hot tub or appliance from power, water or gas is licensed work. Fridges, freezers and AC units need refrigerant recovered before disposal under EPA Section 608. The Lake Havasu City landfill charges by weight, cash only; paint must be dried, and other household hazards go to collection days.

## Landscaping & irrigation (`landscaping_irrigation`)

Desert landscaping and watering systems: drip irrigation repair and installs, sprinkler and valve repair, controllers/timers, backflow devices, decorative rock and decomposed granite, desert plant installs, low-voltage landscape lighting, and yard cleanups. Havasu soil often has caliche (a hard, cement-like layer) that makes digging slow [judgment]. Ask Hava gathers the job size and photos; it never designs the yard or prices the work.

## Locksmith (`locksmith`)

House and car lockouts, rekeying after a move, lock installs and repairs, smart locks, safes, and mailbox locks. No Arizona state locksmith license was found (third-party sources only; treat as [unverified]). Door or frame replacement is ROC carpentry work (R-7/R-60). Wired access control or alarm work needs ROC R-67 and BTR alarm certification. A child or pet locked in a hot car is a 911 call first.

## Moving Help (`moving`)

Local and long-distance household moves, labor-only loading/unloading of trucks or PODS-style containers, single heavy items (piano, safe, gun safe), and in-home furniture moves. Arizona has no state mover license; AZ DPS tells consumers to check that an in-state mover is registered with the Arizona Corporation Commission, and A.R.S. 44-1613 bars an in-state mover from refusing to unload or threatening a lien once it has the goods. Interstate moves (Havasu to California or Nevada is interstate) need an FMCSA-registered mover with a USDOT number. Disconnecting gas appliances is licensed work and never handyman work. Summer heat is a real safety issue for crews.

## Painting (`painting`)

Interior and exterior painting, stucco and block wall painting, cabinet painting, staining, epoxy garage floors and wall covering. ROC R-34 Painting and Wall Covering covers paint, wallpaper, texture and liquid floor/wall coatings. LHC needs no permit for paint. In homes built before 1978, sanding or scraping may disturb lead paint, and EPA RRP rules apply to paid work. Desert sun breaks down exterior paint and caulk fast, especially on south and west walls [judgment].

## Pavers & concrete (`pavers_concrete`)

Flatwork and hardscape: concrete patios, walkways, driveways, slabs (RV pads, sheds), paver patios and pool decks, paver repair (sunken or lifted pavers), crack repair, decorative or stamped concrete, coatings, and short block garden walls. Caliche and expansive desert soils can affect digging and base prep [judgment]. Ask Hava collects size, use and photos only.

## Pest control (`pest_control`)

Pest management for homes: general pests (ants, roaches, crickets, spiders), bark scorpions, subterranean termites (treatment and wood-destroying insect inspections for home sales), pre-emergent and post-emergent weed control around homes and rock yards, bees and wasps (including Africanized honey bees), and pigeons, rats and mice (exclusion, trapping, baiting). In Arizona, pest work for hire is licensed by the Arizona Department of Agriculture, Pest Management Division, not the ROC. Ask Hava does not identify pests or say what treatment is needed.

## Plumbing (leaks, drains, fixtures, pipes) (`plumbing`)

Water supply and drain work inside the property line: leaks, burst pipes, slab leaks, clogs, sewer backups, toilets, faucets, shower valves, pressure, outdoor spigots, re-pipes and adding fixtures. Lake Havasu City water is very hard (about 19 grains per gallon) so scale on fixtures and valves is common, and many homes from the 1970s-90s may have older pipe types worth noting from photos [LHC-CCR][judgment]. Ask Hava sorts active water (urgent) from slow problems, learns where the issue shows up, how many fixtures are affected and what pipe is visible, then writes a card. It never says what failed or what to do. Water heaters, softeners/RO and gas piping have their own files. Licensed work is ROC R-37 / R-37R Plumbing / CR-37 / CR-77; drains and sewer lines also CR-80 [S2][S3].

## Pool cleaning (`pool_cleaning`)

Routine pool and spa service: weekly or every-other-week cleaning (skim, brush, vacuum, empty baskets), water testing and chemical balance, filter cleaning, green-pool cleanups, and acid washes or tile-line descaling. Havasu's hard water (about 19 grains per gallon) and hot summers drive calcium buildup and fast algae growth. Ask Hava collects pool size, filter type, sanitizer and condition, then sends an anonymous job card.

## Pool & spa repair (`pool_spa_repair`)

Repair of residential pool and spa equipment and surfaces: pumps and motors (Pentair, Hayward, Jandy), filters (cartridge, DE, sand), salt chlorine generators and salt cells, heaters and heat pumps, automation, leaks, lights, drain covers, and tile or surface problems. In Lake Havasu City the water is very hard (about 19 grains per gallon), so calcium scale on tile lines and salt cells is common. Ask Hava only gathers facts and photos and sends an anonymous job card; it does not figure out what is wrong or what it costs.

## Roofing (`roofing`)

Roof leaks, repairs, re-roofs and coatings. Lake Havasu roofs are mostly concrete or clay tile on pitched roofs, spray polyurethane foam (SPF) or elastomeric coatings on flat/low-slope roofs, some asphalt shingle, and metal or coated roofs on manufactured homes. On tile roofs the tile sheds most water and the underlayment underneath is the real waterproof layer (Tile Roofing Institute). In desert sun and heat, older felt underlayment often dries out and cracks long before the tile wears out [judgment]. Monsoon storms (June 15-Sept 30) bring wind-lifted tile, blown-off shingles and flat-roof ponding. Roofing work needs ROC R-42 (or CR-42, or a B general). Ask Hava does not diagnose a leak or say what a roof needs; it captures roof type, leak facts and photos.

## RV services (`rv_services`)

Mobile and shop RV services for motorhomes, travel trailers and fifth wheels: roof leaks and roof coatings, AC units (rooftop), slide-outs and awnings, water heaters, furnaces and fridges (propane/electric), generators, plumbing and tanks, batteries and solar, tires and brakes, detailing and wash. Lake Havasu has many winter visitors and RV parks, and summer heat is hard on rooftop AC and roof sealant [judgment]. Ask Hava never says what is wrong with the RV.

## Security & Alarm Systems (`security_alarm`)

Burglar alarm installs and repairs, monitoring setup, cameras and video doorbells, smart-home security, and fire/smoke alarm systems. Alarm businesses and agents must be certified by the AZ Board of Technical Registration (A.R.S. 32-122.05/.06; agents need a DPS fingerprint clearance card). ROC R-67 covers low-voltage alarm installs. The A.R.S. 32-101 'alarm business' definition leaves out fire alarm systems, which fall under ROC R-16 Fire Protection; hardwired or interconnected smoke alarms are never handyman work (32-1121(D)).

## Septic pumping & service (`septic`)

Septic tank pumping and related work: routine pumping, emergency pump-outs for backups, locating and digging up lids, riser installs, transfer-of-ownership inspections for home sales, baffle/filter cleaning, and septic repairs or new systems. Most of Lake Havasu City moved to city sewer under the Wastewater System Expansion Program (completed around 2011), but some homes and many nearby county areas still use septic [judgment: share not confirmed]. Pumping trucks are licensed by ADEQ; repairs and installs need ROC licenses and county permits. Ask Hava never says if a system is failing.

## Solar (PV panels, inverters, batteries, solar water heating) (`solar`)

Rooftop and ground-mount solar electric (PV) systems, inverters, monitoring, home batteries, panel removal and reinstall for roof work, critter guards, and solar water or pool heating. Arizona law makes it unlawful for a contractor to install, alter or repair a solar device without qualifying as a solar contractor, which means passing a solar exam tied to the license class [ARS-1170][ARS-1762]. Ask Hava learns whether it is a problem, an add-on, a removal or a new system, owned vs leased, system size and brand from photos, then writes a card. It never estimates savings, payback or production, and never says a system is underperforming. PV installs need an LHC permit [S17]. Panel washing routes to gutter_solar_cleaning.

## Tree & palm trimming (`tree_trimming`)

Tree and palm work: palm trimming (frond and seed-pod removal, 'skinning' trunks), mesquite and palo verde pruning and thinning, tree removal, stump grinding, storm cleanup after monsoon winds, and hauling. Work near overhead power lines is not sent to businesses: Ask Hava points the homeowner to UniSource Energy Services first. Ask Hava never judges if a tree is safe or sick.

## Water heaters (tank and tankless, gas and electric) (`water_heater`)

Repair, replacement and new install of tank and tankless water heaters, gas (natural or propane) and electric, plus heat-pump water heaters and expansion tanks. Lake Havasu City water is very hard (about 19 gpg); Rheem says scale builds in a tank unless water is soft (0-5 grains), and Rinnai's tankless water quality guide lists hardness up to 200 mg/L (about 11.7 gpg), below city water [RHEEM-UC][RINNAI-RE][LHC-CCR]. Ask Hava learns fuel type, tank vs tankless, size and age (from the label), location and the symptom, then writes a card. It never says what failed or whether to repair or replace. Gas water heaters are excluded from the handyman exemption (A.R.S. 32-1121(D)(2)) [S4].

## Water softeners, RO and filters (`water_treatment`)

Install, repair, service and removal of water softeners, reverse osmosis (RO) drinking water systems, whole-house filters and conditioners (salt-free systems). Lake Havasu City water averages about 19 grains per gallon hardness and about 753 ppm total dissolved solids, so softeners and RO are common; some outlying areas are on EPCOR or wells [LHC-CCR][EPCOR-LH]. Ask Hava learns the job (new, repair, service, remove), system type, where the loop or plumbing is, and the brand from a photo. It never says what water treatment a home should have or makes health claims about water. Licensed work is ROC R-54 Water Conditioning Equipment or CR-54 (also R-37/CR-37 plumbing) [S2][S3]. LHC lists softeners and RO as no-permit items [S17].

## Window Cleaning (`window_cleaning`)

Inside and outside window washing, screen and track cleaning, solar-screen removal and cleaning, and hard-water spot removal on glass. No state license applies. Havasu's hard water from sprinklers leaves mineral spots that regular washing may not remove; spot removal is a separate, slower service [judgment]. Ladder work near overhead lines follows the UES 15-foot rule.

## Windows & Doors (`windows_doors`)

Window replacement and repair, glass and dual-pane seal replacement, sliding door rollers and tracks, entry and interior doors, security screen doors, screens, window film, blinds and shutters. ROC R-7 covers carpentry incl. windows, metal doors and frames; R-60 finish carpentry covers door trim and metal doors; R-65 Glazing covers glass, window film, blinds and shutters. LHC requires a permit when the window size changes. In pre-1978 homes, window replacement is always covered by EPA RRP. CPSC says cordless window coverings are safest and screens do not stop child falls.
