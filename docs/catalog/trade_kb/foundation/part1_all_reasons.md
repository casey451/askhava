# Part 1: Every Reason People Reach Out (Ask Hava, Lake Havasu City)

Purpose: list what homeowners actually text or chat about, grouped by trade, so trade files cover real messages. Also covers emergencies (official safety steps only), "sounds urgent but usually isn't," and what we decline or redirect.

How to read this file
- [S#] = source in `sources_foundation.md`. **[judgment]** = our experience-based reading with no source behind it.
- Messages are paraphrased examples. Test IDs use REQ-#### only. No names, addresses, phones, plates or gate codes.
- Ask Hava never diagnoses, prices, promises results, ranks, or supervises. We ask questions, build an anonymous job card, and send it to opted-in local businesses. Quotes usually come within 24 hours, and the homeowner sees all of them, unranked.

## 0. Local context that shapes messages [judgment unless cited]
- **Extreme summer heat.** AC failure in summer is a health issue, not only comfort (see E4) [S27].
- **Monsoon season** (roughly summer): dust storms, wind, flash floods, power outages, roof and tree damage [judgment]. UES storm tips are at [S25].
- **Hard water and scale.** Softener, RO, water heater, pool tile and fixture messages are common [judgment]. The city's water comes from wells tied to the Colorado River and is treated for arsenic, manganese and iron [S19].
- **High water pressure zones.** City code requires pressure regulators; the city says pressure entering a home should be below 60 PSI [S19].
- **Seasonal residents.** Many homes sit empty for months, so we see "came back and found…" messages, house watching, pool startup, and AC checks before arrival [judgment].
- **Lake life.** Boats, lifts, docks, golf carts, RVs and toy-hauler garages [judgment].
- **Manufactured homes** are common. Installers are licensed by the AZ Dept of Housing [S11].
- **Scorpions** (bark scorpion), termites, pack rats and pigeons under solar panels [judgment; S32 for scorpions].
- **Utilities:** UES for electric and gas, emergency line [number removed, see source] [S21–S25]. The city runs water and sewer [S19][S20].

## 1. Reasons by trade

Format: reason → what we usually need to ask about (no diagnosis) → messy example.

### Systems

**HVAC (incl. swamp coolers and mini-splits): `hvac`**
- AC not cooling or blowing warm; AC won't turn on; thermostat blank; breaker trips when AC starts.
- Water dripping from the air handler or ceiling; condensate line clogged; ice on the lines.
- Strange noise (buzzing, banging, squealing); burning smell from vents (see E6).
- Old unit: wants replacement quotes, or a heat pump vs. gas furnace comparison.
- Maintenance: spring tune-up before summer, fall heating check, filter service plans.
- Swamp cooler: startup in spring, shutdown and winterizing, new pads, pump or float not working, leaking on the roof, switching to refrigerated AC.
- Mini-split for a garage, casita, shop or RV garage; mini-split not cooling.
- Ductwork: rooms uneven, ducts in a hot attic, duct cleaning, rodent damage.
- Snowbird: "turning the AC back on after the summer away" or "set it for vacancy."
- Messy: "ac blowin hot air its 112 in here help" · "the outside thingy is humming but fan not spinning" · "mi aire no enfría y tengo un bebé" · "swamp cooler pouring water down the side of the house."

**Plumbing: `plumbing`**
- Leaks: under the sink, in the wall, slab leak, water bill spike, meter spinning with everything off.
- Clogs: toilet, kitchen sink, tub, main line; sewer smell; gurgling drains.
- Sewer backup into the tub or shower (see E7).
- Burst pipe, or a hose bib or irrigation line split (see E3).
- Fixtures: faucet, toilet, garbage disposal, shower valve replacement.
- Pressure regulator (PRV) failing: banging pipes, toilets running, very high or low pressure [S19 context].
- Re-pipe quotes (old poly or galvanized lines) [judgment]. Water re-pipes need an LHC permit [S17].
- Gas line for a BBQ, fire pit or range (a gas line needs a permit and a licensed contractor) [S17][S4].
- Messy: "water coming up thru the floor tile in kitchen" · "my water bill was 300 something" · "toilet keeps running all night" · "hay agua saliendo del piso."

**Water heaters: `water_heater`**
- No hot water; not enough hot water; takes forever to get hot.
- Leaking tank, puddle under it, rust-colored water, popping or rumbling (scale) [judgment].
- Pilot out or won't light (gas); breaker trips (electric).
- Relief valve dripping or spraying (see U-list).
- Replacement: tank vs. tankless, gas vs. electric, moving to the garage.
- Flush and descale, anode rod.
- Messy: "theres water all around the water heater in the garage" · "pilot keeps going out" · "smell rotten eggs by the water heater" (treat as a possible gas leak; see E1).

**Water softeners / RO: `water_treatment`**
- Softener not regenerating, salt bridge, brine tank full of water, error code.
- White crust on fixtures, spots on dishes, "water feels hard again."
- RO: slow faucet, bad taste, filter changes, leaking under the sink, tank not filling.
- New system quotes; removing an old system; rental vs. purchase.
- Whole-house filter; arsenic or chlorine taste questions. Don't speak to water quality; mention the city water quality info [S19].
- Messy: "softener beeping and wont stop" · "my RO tastes funny" · "white stuff all over my shower glass."

**Electrical: `electrical`**
- Outlet or room dead; GFCI won't reset; breaker keeps tripping (NFPA lists frequent tripping as a reason to call an electrician [S29]).
- Lights flicker or dim (NFPA list [S29]); outlet warm or discolored (NFPA [S29]).
- Burning or rubbery smell, sparks from an outlet (NFPA [S29]; urgent, see E6).
- Panel upgrade: old panel, adding a sub-panel, solar or EV prep. Panel change-outs need an LHC permit [S17].
- New circuits: hot tub, shop, garage mini-split, RV pedestal.
- Ceiling fans, light fixtures, exterior and landscape lighting, holiday lights.
- Smoke alarm hardwired and chirping (see U-list); replacing hardwired alarms (never handyman work [S4]).
- Partial power loss after a monsoon storm (check for an outage with UES first [S25]).
- Messy: "half my house has no power but the neighbors do" · "outlet by the pool sparked" · "se fue la luz en la cocina nomas."

**Solar: `solar`**
- New system quotes; battery add-on; system expansion.
- System not producing; inverter error lights; monitoring app offline.
- Roof leak around panels; removal and reinstall for a reroof.
- Pigeons or pack rats under panels; critter guards (see also `pest_control`).
- Panel cleaning (route to `gutter_solar_cleaning`).
- Buying or selling a home with a leased or financed system (we don't give contract advice; we send it to installers for service questions only) [judgment].
- Messy: "solar app says zero all week" · "birds nesting under my panels so loud" · "need panels off for new roof."

**Generators / EV chargers: `generator_ev`**
- Standby generator install (natural gas or propane); portable generator transfer switch or inlet.
- Generator won't start or exercise; maintenance.
- EV charger (Level 2) install in a garage; panel capacity questions; golf cart charging outlet.
- Messy: "want a plug for my tesla in the garage" · "generator for when power goes out in monsoon."
- Safety: CDC says connecting a generator to home wiring without approved transfer equipment is illegal and a fire risk [S31]. Never run a generator indoors or near windows [S26][S31].

### Pools and outdoors

**Pool/spa repair: `pool_spa_repair`**
- Pump not running, humming, or loud; motor or capacitor; variable-speed upgrade.
- Filter: pressure high, sand or DE in the pool, cartridge replacement.
- Leak: water level dropping, wet equipment pad, leak detection.
- Heater or heat pump not working; spa won't heat; spa jets or blower.
- Lights out; GFCI tripping; automation or app not working (shock concerns: see E5).
- Resurfacing, replaster, pebble finish, tile and calcium removal, acid wash, deck coating.
- Green pool after vacancy; drain and clean.
- Pool barrier or fence questions; safety covers.
- Messy: "pool pump making a screaming noise" · "lost 2 inches a day" · "my spa says FLO error" · "felt a tingle in the pool" (see E5).

**Weekly pool cleaning: `pool_cleaning`**
- Start weekly or biweekly service; service while away for the summer; one-time green-to-clean.
- Chemical balance only; filter cleaning; salt cell cleaning.
- Switching providers; current service skipping visits (we don't judge; we just send a new request).
- Messy: "need someone to do my pool every week starting asap" · "pool guy ghosted me."

**Landscaping / irrigation: `landscaping_irrigation`**
- Drip system leaking or not running; timer or controller; valve stuck on; broken sprinkler head.
- Desert landscape install, rock and gravel refresh, turf removal, artificial turf.
- Weekly or monthly yard maintenance; weed pulling; cleanup after monsoon wind.
- Pre-emergent weed spraying (a pest-license job; route to `pest_control`) [S9].
- Low-voltage landscape lighting (R-21/R-67) [S2].
- Misting system install or repair (R-21 or R-54) [S2].
- Messy: "drip line spraying in the yard nonstop" · "need rocks redone in the front" · "weeds everywhere after the rain."

**Tree trimming: `tree_trimming`**
- Palm trimming (skinning, seed pods); mesquite or palo verde trimming; tree leaning after wind.
- Tree or limb down after a storm; tree on the roof, fence or car.
- Stump grinding; removal; roots in the pool or sewer line.
- **Limb near power lines** → UES (see E2 and R-list). UES says to keep at least 15 ft away and call UES before clearing foliage near the service line; UES will turn power off at no cost [S24].
- Messy: "palm tree needs a haircut" · "big branch hanging on the wires" (E2) · "storm knocked my tree into the block wall."

**Pavers / concrete: `pavers_concrete`**
- Pavers sinking or lifting; driveway cracks; new patio; extending a slab for an RV or boat.
- Pool deck resurface or coating; travertine.
- Block wall cracked or leaning; retaining walls (over 2 ft 6 in with slope, or over 3 ft flat, needs an LHC permit [S17]).
- RV pad and gate widening; curb cuts (city right-of-way permit [judgment]).
- Messy: "need slab for boat on side of house" · "pavers popping up by the pool."

### Interior and exterior

**Handyman: `handyman`**
- Small jumbled lists: hang TV, fix door, caulk tub, replace faucet, patch holes, install shelves, assemble furniture.
- Pre-sale punch lists; move-in or move-out fixes for rentals.
- Snowbird "while I'm away" fixes [judgment].
- Rules: handyman routing only when under $1,000, no permit, not part of a larger job, and no gas or fire-safety work [S4]. A lumped list may exceed $1,000 [judgment].
- Messy: "honey do list about 10 things" · "need a guy to fix a bunch of little stuff" · "necesito alguien para varias cositas en la casa."

**Roofing: `roofing`**
- Leak during a monsoon storm; water stain on the ceiling.
- Tile slipped or broken; underlayment worn out (tile roofs) [judgment]; flat-roof foam or coating.
- Wind damage; insurance inspection; reroof quotes.
- Swamp cooler leaking onto the roof; skylight leak.
- Messy: "water coming thru my ceiling when it rains" · "tiles blew off" · "roof is 25 yrs old need new one."
- LHC: roof covering replacement needs no permit; sheathing replacement does [S17].

**Painting: `painting`**
- Exterior repaint (sun fade, stucco); interior rooms; cabinets; garage floor epoxy (flooring overlap).
- Block wall painting; trim; pre-sale paint.
- Messy: "house paint is all faded" · "need 3 rooms painted."

**Drywall: `drywall`**
- Holes, cracks, water damage after a leak, texture matching, popcorn ceiling removal.
- Older popcorn ceilings can contain hazardous fibers; we don't advise on testing or removal. We ask whether the homeowner knows the home's age and add it to the card [judgment].
- Messy: "big hole in the wall from door knob" · "ceiling sagging after AC leak."

**Flooring: `flooring`**
- Tile, LVP, carpet, wood; tile cracked or hollow; floor lifted after a leak.
- Grout cleaning (overlaps carpet cleaning); epoxy garage floor.
- Messy: "want to rip out carpet put in vinyl plank" · "tiles cracking in the hallway."

**Cabinets / countertops: `cabinets_countertops`**
- Refacing or painting cabinets; new kitchen cabinets; quartz or granite tops; backsplash.
- Hinges, drawers, soft-close; vanity replacement.
- Messy: "want new counters in the kitchen and bathrooms" · "cabinet door fell off."

**Windows / doors: `windows_doors`**
- Fogged or broken dual-pane glass; drafty or hot windows; sun film; screens; sun screens.
- Sliding door hard to open or off the track; front or security door install; pet doors.
- Window replacement same-size (no permit) vs. size change (permit) [S17].
- Messy: "slider wont slide" · "window fogged up inside the glass" · "need sun screens on west side."

**Garage doors: `garage_doors`**
- Door won't open or close; spring broken (loud bang); cable off; off track; opener dead; remote or keypad.
- New insulated door (heat); RV-height door; opener with a battery backup.
- Messy: "garage door stuck halfway car inside" · "heard a huge bang in garage now door wont open." Broken springs are dangerous; don't suggest DIY [judgment].

**Appliance repair: `appliance_repair`**
- Refrigerator not cooling; ice maker; washer not draining; dryer no heat; dishwasher leaking; oven or range not heating.
- Gas range, dryer or oven: smell of gas (see E1).
- Install a new appliance (gas or water hookups are not handyman work [S4]; R-63 excludes gas, electric and plumbing lines [S2]).
- Messy: "fridge warm all my food going bad" · "dryer runs but no heat" · "la lavadora no saca el agua."

### Pests

**Pest control: `pest_control`**
- Scorpions inside (bark scorpions); recurring service; blacklight inspection; sealing [S32 for safety].
- Termites: mud tubes, swarmers, pre-sale termite inspection or report (wood-destroying insect).
- Pre-emergent weed control (needs a PMD license) [S9].
- Ants, roaches, spiders, crickets; pack rats or mice in the engine bay, garage or attic; pigeons under solar.
- Bees or wasps: a swarm or hive in a wall, eave or meter box (see E-list for stings).
- Messy: "found 3 scorpions this week one in my kids bed" · "mud tubes on garage wall" · "bees going into my water meter box."

### Cleaning
**House cleaning: `house_cleaning`**: recurring cleaning; move-out or move-in; deep clean; vacation rental turnovers; snowbird arrival or departure clean.
**Carpet cleaning: `carpet_cleaning`**: pet stains, move-out, tile and grout, upholstery, water extraction after a leak (if active water: route to plumbing first).
**Window cleaning: `window_cleaning`**: hard water spots on glass, screens, high windows, lake-view glass; sun screens.
**Gutter / solar-panel cleaning: `gutter_solar_cleaning`**: panel cleaning (dust storms, bird droppings); bird proofing (route to pest or solar); gutter cleanout (few homes have gutters [judgment]).
**Junk removal: `junk_removal`**: garage cleanout; estate cleanout; old hot tub removal; yard waste after a storm; appliance haul-away; construction debris.
- Messy: "need my house cleaned before we come back in october" (a month is fine; we don't write calendar dates into plans) · "old hot tub needs to go" · "solar panels filthy after the dust storm."

### Moving
**Moving help: `moving`**: local move labor; load or unload a truck or POD; in-home furniture moves; piano or safe; snowbird partial moves; long-distance (interstate movers need FMCSA registration [S36]).
- Messy: "need 2 guys to load a uhaul saturday" · "moving from havasu to phoenix."
- If a mover is holding goods for more money: AZ DPS moving hotline [number removed, see source] [S36] (see R-list).

### Vehicles and water
**Boat / dock: `boat_dock`**: boat won't start; winterize or summerize; impeller; detailing; upholstery; trailer lights or bearings; lift repair; dock repair; shore power to the dock.
- Electric shock risk near docks and in water (see E5). NFPA has electric shock drowning guidance [S50; search only].
- Messy: "boat lift wont go up" · "need boat detailed before memorial day" (a holiday name is fine).
**RV services: `rv_services`**: AC on the roof, slide-outs, awning, generator, water heater, roof reseal; RV pedestal or sewer hookup at home (electrical or plumbing trades).
**Golf cart: `golf_cart`**: batteries (lead-acid to lithium), charger, won't move, lights, street-legal upgrades, lift kits. Street use rules: we don't advise; LHC code points to state law [S43].
- Messy: "golf cart batteries dead need new ones" · "cart beeps when i plug it in."

### Home safety and inspection
**Home inspection: `home_inspection`**: buyer inspection; pre-listing; 4-point or insurance; pool or spa inspection; termite (route to pest); septic transfer inspection (route to septic) [S16].
**Locksmith: `locksmith`**: locked out of a house or car; rekey after moving in; smart lock install; safe opening; mailbox locks; garage and gate keypads (no codes on cards).
- Locked out with a child or pet inside a hot car: **call 911** [judgment; heat danger per S27].
**Security / alarm: `security_alarm`**: cameras, doorbell cams, monitored alarms, takeover of an old system, panel beeping, false alarms.
- Fire alarms and sprinklers are not handyman work [S4].
**Septic: `septic`**: pumping; alarm on an aerobic system; slow drains and soggy ground; transfer-of-ownership inspection for a sale [S16]; new system or repair (R-41 plus a Mohave County permit) [S14].
- Messy: "septic alarm going off red light outside" · "selling house need septic inspection."

## 3. Emergency list (official safety steps only)

Rule: when an emergency signal appears, send the official safety step **first**, in plain words, then ask if they'd still like a request sent. Never say Ask Hava will send help or that anyone will arrive. Steps below are quoted or closely paraphrased from the cited source.

**E1. Gas smell, hissing, or suspected gas leak** (natural gas or propane). Source: UES [S23]
1. "Leave the building immediately and take others with you."
2. "Do not create any sparks or flames." No smoking or matches. Don't start a car or use phones, flashlights or electrical equipment that could spark.
3. "Leave the door open as you exit."
4. "Call 911 and UniSource's emergency number, [number removed, see source], from a safe location away from the area."
5. "Do not return to the building until firefighters or other public safety officials declare it safe."
- Signs (UES): rotten-egg smell; hissing, whistling or roaring sound; dead or discolored plants; bubbling water; dirt blowing from the ground [S23].
- After a shutoff, only UniSource employees may turn gas back on or relight pilots [S25].
- Propane homes [judgment]: the same leave-and-call steps apply. Call 911 and the propane supplier from outside.

**E2. Downed or sparking power line; tree or limb on a line.** Source: UES [S24][S25]
- "If you EVER see a downed power line, call 911 immediately; don't get near it."
- "Stay away from downed power lines. Never touch them."
- "If a power line comes into contact with your vehicle, stay inside the vehicle until help arrives."
- "Never try to remove tree limbs or other debris that may have made contact with downed power lines."
- Report outages and emergencies to UES: [number removed, see source].

**E3. Burst pipe or major active leak.** Sources: CDC [S31] for standing water and electricity; city water info [S19][S20]
- If water is near outlets or electrical equipment: CDC says, "If you have standing water in your home and can turn off the main power from a dry location, then go ahead and turn off the power ... If you must enter standing water to access the main power switch, then call an electrician." "NEVER turn power on or off yourself or use an electric tool or appliance while standing in water."
- Water shutoff: the home side of the line starts at the city water meter [S19]. Shutting the home's main valve is a common first step [judgment; based on the city leak guidance S19 snippet]. If the leak is at the city meter or in the street, call Lake Havasu City (Water/City Hall [number removed, see source]).

**E4. AC out in extreme heat, especially with an infant, older adult, someone ill, or pets.** Source: NWS/CDC heat illness [S27]
- **Heat stroke** signs: confusion, slurred speech, body temperature above 103°F, hot red dry or damp skin, fainting. Steps: "Call 911 or get the victim to a hospital immediately." Move the person to a cooler, preferably air-conditioned place. Cool them with cool cloths or a bath. "Do NOT give fluids." [S27]
- **Heat exhaustion**: move to a cooler place, loosen clothing, cool wet cloths, sip water. Get medical help if they vomit or symptoms last more than 1 hour [S27].
- NWS notes a fan alone may not prevent heat illness at very high heat index [S27].
- Then: "Would you like me to send an urgent AC request to local businesses now?" Never promise timing.

**E5. Shock or tingling in or near a pool, spa or lake dock.** Source: CPSC "Don't Swim with Shocks" [S30]
- If you feel a shock in the water: "Move away from the source of the shock. Get out of the water. If possible, exit without using a metal ladder."
- If someone else is being shocked: "Immediately turn off all power." "Call or have someone else call 9-1-1." Don't jump in. The CPSC page cites the Red Cross on using a fiberglass rescue hook [S30].
- Signs (CPSC): tingling, muscle cramps, a swimmer who can't move or seems stuck, or underwater lights acting strange (on when they should be off, flickering) [S30].

**E6. Burning smell, sparks, smoke from an outlet, panel or appliance.**
- If there's fire or smoke: NFPA, for smoke alarms sounding: "Get out, call 9-1-1, and stay out." [S28]
- No fire or smoke: NFPA lists "a burning or rubbery smell coming from an appliance," "sparks from an outlet," and "discolored or warm wall outlets" as reasons to call a qualified electrician [S29]. UES: most panels have a main switch; "Use it to cut all power ... in case of fire or shock" [S24]. Only if it's safe to reach; never while standing in water [S31].

**E7. Sewage backing up into the home.** Sources: CDC [S31]; city lateral rules [S20]
- CDC: floodwater and sewage can make you sick. If water is near electrical equipment, follow the E3 power steps. Wear rubber boots and gloves, keep kids and pets away, and wash hands after any contact [S31 and CDC cleanup guidance; CDC floodwater page linked from S31].
- City sewer main in the street: call LHC Wastewater at [number removed, see source] [S20]. The home side of the line is the owner's responsibility [S20].

**E8. Carbon monoxide alarm or CO symptoms.** Source: CPSC [S26]
- "If the alarm signal sounds do not try to find the source of the CO": "Immediately move outside to fresh air." "Call your emergency services, fire department, or 911." Do a head count. "DO NOT reenter the premises until the emergency services responders have given you permission."
- No alarm but symptoms (headache, dizziness, nausea, shortness of breath, tiredness): get to fresh air right away and call the fire department or 911 from outside or a neighbor's home [S26].
- Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside, in the garage, or near windows [S26][S25][S31].

**E9. Smoke alarm sounding (three loud beeps) or fire.** Source: NFPA [S28]
- "A continued set of three loud beeps ... means smoke or fire. Get out, call 9-1-1, and stay out."

**E10. Scorpion sting.** Source: Arizona Poison and Drug Information Center [S32]
- First aid: clean the site with soap and water, cool compress, keep the limb in a comfortable position, and over-the-counter medicine as needed.
- "Call [number removed, see source] for our poison experts to determine if you need to go to the hospital." Small children are at the highest risk; signs include jerky body and wild eye movements [S32].
- Then offer a pest control request.

**E11. Bee swarm attacking / many stings.** No official AZ source fetched this pass. Interim wording **[judgment, needs official source]**: "If anyone is having trouble breathing or has many stings, call 911." For a single sting, call Poison Control at [number removed, see source] [S32 lists the number; bee guidance not fetched].

**E12. Flash flood or water rising around the home.** Source: CDC [S31] (power steps). NWS "Turn Around Don't Drown" was not fetched this pass **[judgment: cite NWS before using any driving wording]**.

**E13. Child or pet locked in a hot car.** **[judgment]**: "Call 911 now." Heat risk per NWS/CDC [S27]. Then offer a locksmith request, without implying it is faster than 911.

**E14. Tree or structure fell on the home; ceiling collapse; someone hurt.** **[judgment]**: "If anyone is hurt or you smell gas or see wires down, call 911 and follow the gas or power-line steps (E1/E2)." Then offer a request.

**E15. Garage door fell or spring snapped with someone hurt.** **[judgment]**: call 911 if anyone is hurt; keep away from the door; don't try to lift it.

For every emergency [judgment]:
- Safety step first, then the card.
- Mark the card "urgent" with the facts only (e.g. "No AC, indoor temperature reported high, infant in home"). Never record a name or address on the card.
- Never say "help is on the way," "someone will be there in X," or anything that sounds like dispatch.

## 4. "Sounds urgent but usually isn't" [mostly judgment; sources noted]
These need a calm reply and the normal card flow. If any emergency sign from section 3 appears, use section 3 instead.

| Message | Why it's usually not an emergency | Calm reply idea |
|---|---|---|
| Smoke alarm **chirps** once every 30–60 seconds | NFPA: "A single chirp every 30 or 60 seconds means the battery is low and must be changed." Chirping after a new battery means the alarm is at end of life; replace alarms over 10 years old [S28]. | Share the NFPA line. Hardwired alarms: "Only qualified electricians should install hardwired smoke alarms." [S28] Offer an electrician request. |
| GFCI outlet won't work in the bathroom or garage | Often a tripped GFCI elsewhere [judgment]. NFPA says to test GFCIs monthly [S29]. | Ask: "Have you tried the reset button on nearby GFCI outlets?" Only if no water or heat is present. |
| Breaker tripped once | NFPA flags *frequent* trips [S29]. | Ask if it trips again; offer an electrician request. |
| Water heater relief valve dripping | Often a pressure or temperature issue to have checked [judgment]. | Ask about puddle size; plumbing or water heater card. If spraying hot water, keep away [judgment]. |
| AC running but not keeping up on the hottest afternoon | Often normal at extreme highs [judgment]. Not urgent unless there are health risks (E4). | Ask indoor temp, who's home, and whether air is cool at all. |
| Swamp cooler dripping off the roof | Usually a float or overflow [judgment]. | Normal card. Ask if water is entering the home. |
| Garage door stuck **open** | Security issue, not usually a safety issue [judgment]. | Normal card, marked same-day if they ask. |
| Pool green after vacancy | Not an emergency [judgment]. Pool barrier still matters for kids. | Pool cleaning card. |
