# Spec: Fees, billing, refunds and ledger

Prefix: `FEE` · Modules: `payments`, `catalog` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **FEE-001** The introduction fee is $25 when the fee basis is under $500.00, $75 when it is $500.00 up to and including $1,500.00, and $199 when it is over $1,500.00 (D-009). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`.
- **FEE-002** No fee is ever a percentage of the job, and there is no percentage cap (D-357). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Ask Hava is not a contractor." and "Ok fix everything we need to." on fix round 11 (`docs/decisions.md:477`, `docs/decisions.md:480`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`.
- **FEE-003** The fee basis of a range quote is its midpoint. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:61`.
- **FEE-004** A diagnostic-visit quote (a visit fee plus an estimate on site) exists only in a category whose quote format is `diagnostic_visit` (PICK-053) and uses the set fee tier stored for its category, which is $25 by default and can never be set above $75 (D-399, D-418, D-441). - **FEE-005** The fee tier is set when the homeowner picks, from the chosen quote alone, and no AI flag changes it (D-009). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`.
- **FEE-006** The fee is charged when the business confirms the introduction and is never refunded or adjusted because of the final bill; it pays for the introduction service only and doesn't depend on whether the work is done or paid for (TERMS-026) (D-357). - **FEE-007** A logged owner-portal switch changes the fee mode between tiers and a flat fee per job type with no code change; in flat mode each job type's fee defaults to its category's set tier, and after any mode change a business must accept a new card-authorization version before its next charge (D-072). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:63`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:464`.
- **FEE-008** Fees are tax-included: no tax is ever added on top of $25, $75 or $199. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:248`.
- **FEE-009** Homeowners are never charged anything. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:247`.
- **FEE-010** Only businesses that join within the first 90 days after launch, or no later than 90 days after a category they signed up for is switched on (including a waitlisted business that joined earlier), get a free first win, and a business joins on the date its sign-up form was submitted (D-019, D-152, D-195). - **FEE-011** A free first win is allowed once per entity key (ROC number, city-list entity, Stripe card fingerprint, the identity-code contact on the record, or the owner name on the city list), checked at the moment the win is used (D-151).
