# Spec: Fees, billing, refunds and ledger

Prefix: `FEE` · Modules: `payments`, `catalog` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **FEE-001** The introduction fee is $25 when the fee basis is under $500.00, $75 when it is $500.00 up to and including $1,500.00, and $199 when it is over $1,500.00 (D-009). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`.
- **FEE-002** No fee is ever a percentage of the job, and there is no percentage cap. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`.
- **FEE-003** The fee basis of a range quote is its midpoint. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:61`.
- **FEE-004** A trip-fee-plus-estimate quote uses the set fee tier stored for its category. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:61`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:35`.
- **FEE-005** The fee tier is set when the homeowner picks, from the chosen quote alone, and no AI flag changes it (D-009). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:59`.
- **FEE-006** The fee is charged when the business confirms the introduction and is never refunded or adjusted because of the final bill. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`.
- **FEE-007** A logged owner-portal switch changes the fee mode between tiers and a flat fee per job type with no code change. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:63`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:464`.
- **FEE-008** Fees are tax-included: no tax is ever added on top of $25, $75 or $199. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:248`.
- **FEE-009** Homeowners are never charged anything. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:247`.
- **FEE-010** Only businesses that join within the first 90 days after launch get a free first win (D-019). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:67`.
- **FEE-011** A free first win is allowed once per entity key (ROC number, city-list entity or Stripe card fingerprint), checked at the moment the win is used. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:116`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:344`.
- **FEE-012** A free first win is recorded as a $0 "free first win" ledger entry with the waived amount. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:325`.
- **FEE-013** Repeat work from the same customer is always free: a homeowner's pick of a business they were already introduced to is charged $0. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:69`.
- **FEE-014** Cards are collected only by Stripe Elements or Checkout and saved with a SetupIntent for off-session use, so card data never touches Ask Hava's servers. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:212`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:110`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:363`.
- **FEE-015** The business accepts the card authorization ("I authorize Ask Hava to charge this card $25/$75/$199 by quote tier, ongoing services counted at 4 months, each time I confirm a homeowner's pick; first win free if I join in the first 90 days; refunds per the Terms"), and its version and timestamp are stored. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:212`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:252`.
- **FEE-016** Each charge attempt uses its own idempotency key (for example pick-1042-try1, then try2), so an attempt can never charge twice and a failed attempt never blocks a later one. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:327`.
- **FEE-017** Before every charge attempt, including retries, the app checks that the job is still waiting for payment and the pick is still current. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:203`.
- **FEE-018** If Stripe has been unreachable for 4 hours, the pick is released and the homeowner is offered the next quote. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:203`.
- **FEE-019** If no Stripe notice arrives 30 minutes after a charge, the app asks Stripe directly, and asks again before giving up on a card that needed approval. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:202`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:224`.
- **FEE-020** A card that fails or needs bank approval gets a text plus 1 business hour, after which the homeowner is offered the next quote. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:191`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:328`.
- **FEE-021** A payment that succeeds after the job moved on is refunded automatically and Casey is told. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:191`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:203`.
- **FEE-022** A business is refunded automatically when there is no connection (a person answering for 45 seconds or more) after 2 call attempts at least 3 hours apart within 72 hours, decided at 96 hours. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:334`.
- **FEE-023** A business is refunded automatically when the homeowner cancels before any call. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:335`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:98`.
- **FEE-024** A cancel refunds the business only if it has not talked to the homeowner. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:98`.
- **FEE-025** Anything charged after a cancel is refunded. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`.
- **FEE-026** Each automatic refund happens exactly once; a failed refund is retried hourly with an alert, and a refund on a disputed charge is blocked and flagged instead. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:330`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:204`.
- **FEE-027** A bank dispute alerts Casey with evidence from the audit log, and the ledger records it once as a dispute of the amount plus the $15 Stripe fee. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:337`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:346`.
- **FEE-028** The ledger is append-only, with entry types charge, refund, credit, free first win, dispute and payout. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:94`.
- **FEE-029** A nightly reconcile reads Stripe's event list and auto-matches each unknown charge to a job or auto-refunds it (always refunding when the job is closed), keeping the alert. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:118`.
- **FEE-030** The owner money view and every business statement are built only from the ledger and match Stripe to the cent. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:348`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:361`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:152`.
- **FEE-031** A receipt is emailed for every charge, and the charge description names the request number. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:252`.
- **FEE-032** Money moves only through the `payments` module. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:152`.
- **FEE-033** The owner portal flags businesses whose quotes keep landing just under $500 or $1,500. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:62`.
- **FEE-034** Fee calculation is unit-tested from the K2b worked examples. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:471`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:536`.
