# Spec: Quotes, quote page and pick

Prefix: `PICK` · Modules: `quotes`, `picks`, `portals` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **PICK-001** Each business may quote for 24 hours after its own job card is actually sent (after any quiet-hours hold), counted every day of the week, and no quote is accepted once the picked business has tapped Confirm (D-011, D-042, D-043). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:83`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:66`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:85`.
- **PICK-002** A quote stores its type (firm, range or trip fee), amount, high amount, original wording, AI-read flag, business-confirmed flag, earliest arrival and expiry. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:92`.
- **PICK-003** The business's original wording is always stored and shown next to any AI-formatted summary. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:200`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:92`.
- **PICK-004** An AI summary that adds a price, scope, warranty or other claim not in the original wording is rejected and not shown. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:200`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:245`.
- **PICK-005** A business can view its quote card exactly as homeowners see it. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:245`.
- **PICK-006** A price the AI read from a text quote is always shown back to the business on the quote form to confirm before the homeowner sees it, whatever the AI's confidence, and a quote read with less than 80% confidence is never shown before that confirmation (D-149). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:192`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:292`.
- **PICK-007** A quote containing a phone number, a link, spelled-out numbers or "text me / call me" wording bounces back to the business. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:292`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`.
- **PICK-008** The quote page shows every eligible quote, numbered in arrival order with no sorting or filtering, a quote's number never changes, and a quote whose business became ineligible shows "no longer available" and can't be chosen (D-084). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:123`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:189`.
- **PICK-009** The quote page keeps adding quotes as they arrive, each with a Choose button. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:98`.
- **PICK-010** The quote page link carries an unguessable 128-bit token, the page is hidden from search engines, and it shows no names, phone numbers or addresses. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:310`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:311`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:427`.
- **PICK-011** Quote-page tokens stop working 30 days after the job closes. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:314`.
- **PICK-012** The homeowner can pick any quote from the first quote onward. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:82`.
- **PICK-013** Picking on the page requires a 6-digit Twilio Verify code sent to the homeowner's phone, valid 10 minutes, with 5 tries and then a 15-minute cooldown (D-015). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:312`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:190`.
- **PICK-014** The code field allows paste and one-time-code autofill. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:313`.
- **PICK-015** A text of "<number> YES" (for example "2 YES" or "2 yes please") from the homeowner's own phone picks that quote. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:220`.
- **PICK-016** The quote page shows "By choosing, you agree to the Terms" with a link before any pick. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:213`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:244`.
- **PICK-017** The pick has a review step showing exactly what happens next, with "Go back" before the final confirm. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:406`.
- **PICK-018** At the pick the homeowner chooses how the business reaches them: a call through the masked number or an email address they choose to share. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:408`.
- **PICK-019** After a pick, the business has 2 business hours (a setting), counted from delivery of the pick text, to tap Confirm, and without a Confirm the job returns to Quotes ready (D-148, D-192). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:273`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:174`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:221`.
- **PICK-020** The business's license and account status are re-checked at the pick (with the one live license lookup) and again at Confirm (from the stored results), and an ineligible business can't confirm (D-056). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:222`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:65`.
- **PICK-021** The business sees the fee before tapping Confirm, on a review step with "Go back". Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:406`.
- **PICK-022** The homeowner's first name, address and full photos are released, and the masked call line opened, only after Stripe confirms payment or a free win is recorded. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`.
- **PICK-023** The homeowner's phone number is never given to the business. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:410`.
- **PICK-024** The details text prints the homeowner's first name once and adds "Tip: on your first call, ask which number to text." (D-201). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:282`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:124`.
- **PICK-025** After a pick on a rental property, one text asks the homeowner who will meet the business. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:614`.
- **PICK-026** When the picked business's payment or free win is recorded, every other business that quoted gets "The homeowner picked another quote for job <number>. <k> businesses quoted; the picked quote was <lower than / about the same as / higher than> yours." when at least 3 quotes (a setting) were received, and otherwise the first sentence only; when the job reaches a final status, every business that quoted, including any that quoted after the pick, except the business whose pick was connected, gets "This job is no longer active." once; and a business whose quote is refused after Confirm gets it at once (D-044, D-150, D-193). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:274`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:85`.
- **PICK-027** A business that asks about a live pick gets a short reply with the Confirm link and what happens next. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:90`.
- **PICK-028** Quote, pick and quote-page link IDs are derived from what they identify plus a server secret. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:109`.
- **PICK-029** When Ask Hava can't text the homeowner (opted out, or texting switched off or suspended), the pick code goes by email to an address the homeowner gave in their own text or web-chat conversation, with the same limits as PICK-013, and an email typed on the quote page never receives codes (D-050). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:312`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:177`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:590`.
- **PICK-030** A "<number> YES" text from an opted-out homeowner's own phone still picks, and the confirmation goes by email and on the quote page (D-050). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`.
- **PICK-031** A "<number> YES" text picks only when the homeowner has exactly one job in Quotes ready; with more than one, Ask Hava first asks which job, and a text pick uses the masked call unless the homeowner changes it on the quote page before the business confirms (D-068). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:408`.
- **PICK-032** A "<number> YES" for a different quote while the first pick waits for Confirm replaces that pick, and the first business is told the pick was withdrawn; once the business has tapped Confirm, the reply says the pick is confirmed and how to close the request (D-147). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:82`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:220`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:221`.
- **PICK-033** YES followed by the job number (or YES alone when that business has exactly one pick waiting) from a phone linked to the picked business gets "Fee $<amount>. Reply CONFIRM <number> to accept, or open <link>.", and CONFIRM <number> is a Confirm only from the owner's phone or a phone with the "Confirm and billing" role when a card and the current card authorization are on file; from any other linked phone, or without them, the reply is the Confirm link (D-148, D-191). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:272`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:406`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:90`.
- **PICK-034** For a recurring service, the pick text and the Confirm review step show how the fee was set, for example "Fee $75 (ongoing services count as 4 months: $125 x 4 = $500)" or "Fee $75 (ongoing services count as 4 months, capped at $75)" (D-148, D-196). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:277`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:60`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:212`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:343`.
- **PICK-035** A business that answers or opens a pick after its Confirm window ended is told the pick expired and its quote can't be picked again for this job (D-148). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:174`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:90`.
- **PICK-036** A "<number> YES" from a homeowner with no job waiting for a pick gets a reply saying where each of their open requests stands (D-147). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:220`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:90`.
- **PICK-037** The pick text goes to the owner's phone and every "Confirm and billing" phone, and by email to the owner and those members, and if no Confirm has happened 60 minutes (a setting) after delivery, one reminder goes the same way, with quiet hours still applying (D-191). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:272`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:174`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:90`.
- **PICK-038** For the PICK-026 comparison, a firm quote compares on its amount and a range on its midpoint (as FEE-003), "about the same" means within 10% (a setting) of the receiving business's quote, a trip-fee quote compared with any other quote type gives "different quote types" instead, and no amount or business name is ever disclosed (D-193). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:274`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:61`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:85`.
- **PICK-039** The quote form shows the fee tier that applies to a trip-fee quote in that category before the business submits, for example "Trip-fee quotes in this category have a $25 fee." (D-185). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:266`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:61`.
