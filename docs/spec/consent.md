# Spec: Consent and opt-out

Prefix: `CON` · Modules: `intake`, `messaging`, `businesses` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **CON-001** The first message to any new number is the full consent text on every path, except that on the emergency path the safety text (INT-004), and for a heat match the heat-safety text (INT-031), is sent first and the consent text immediately after it (D-008, D-274), a first message matching the business-intent list gets one text with the business sign-up link, the Ask Hava name and the STOP/HELP line and opens no job (D-132), and a first message that is only HELP, INFO or AYUDA gets the HELP reply, with the consent text following when the person sends a request (D-131). - **CON-002** The consent text names Ask Hava and includes "Msg frequency varies by request", "Msg & data rates may apply", "Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help", and links to the Terms and the privacy policy. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:237`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:239`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:240`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:241`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:242`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:179`.
- **CON-003** The consent text says the business the homeowner picks pays Ask Hava a small fee. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:81`.
- **CON-004** The consent text links the homeowner Terms, says "Reply OK to agree to the Terms and start", and says the user must be 18 or older (D-052). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:213`.
- **CON-005** Consent is any reply to the consent text whose first word, ignoring case, punctuation and accents, is ok, okay, yes, y, yep, yeah, sure, fine, claro, dale, listo, sí or si, or that starts with "go ahead", "sounds good", "de acuerdo" or "está bien" (so "yes please", "Y" and "si" count), any further words in that reply are handled as part of the request, and the time, the exact reply and the consent-prompt version are stored (D-021, D-127, D-237). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:325`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:107`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:265`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:608`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:183`.
- **CON-006** Consent is handled before any other reply, so "Yes" sent to the consent question never confirms a pending pick. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:608`.
- **CON-007** No job card is sent for a request until the homeowner's consent is recorded. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:170`.
- **CON-008** A text that is only one of stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel or unsubscribe, or the Spanish parar, alto, cancelar or baja (any case), opts that number out of all texts at once (D-020). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:114`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:443`.
- **CON-009** An opt-out gets exactly one confirmation, sent within 5 minutes, with no marketing, that says "To use Ask Hava again, text START." (D-007). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:261`.
- **CON-010** Natural-language revocations such as "please stop texting me", and their Spanish equivalents, are honored as opt-outs (D-006). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:261`.
- **CON-011** A message that merely contains an opt-out word in another sense (for example "can stop by at 3") is not an opt-out. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:188`.
- **CON-012** An opt-out never closes or cancels a job; the homeowner's jobs continue through the quote page and email (D-005). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:159`.
- **CON-013** After an opt-out, no text of any kind is sent to that number until it texts START, except the one opt-out confirmation (CON-009) and the HELP reply to a HELP, INFO or AYUDA text (CON-018, D-131). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:213`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:259`.
- **CON-014** A text that fails with carrier error 21610 (recipient opted out) is never retried. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`.
- **CON-015** A number that opted out and texts again after START is treated as a new request, starting with the consent text, and no text goes to it before START (D-053). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:95`.
- **CON-016** An opted-out homeowner who writes again without START is emailed if an email is on file, and otherwise is listed in Casey's weekly digest (OPS-030) with no call-back, while an emergency keyword in that message still raises the CON-017 critical alert (D-226). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:314`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:213`.
- **CON-017** A possible emergency from an opted-out number raises a critical alert to Casey. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:213`.
- **CON-018** HELP, INFO or AYUDA gets a reply within 5 minutes, at any hour and also from an opted-out number, naming Ask Hava, the help email and askhava.com/help, saying "A person reads HELP messages within 4 days, or within 24 hours if your job is urgent. This number is text-only.", "Can't open links? Reply LIST to get your quotes by text." and that Canadian numbers may pay roaming charges, and the STOP instruction, in Spanish for AYUDA; it gives a voice number only when a help line separate from the texting number is set, and the help-phone setting can never be the texting number; the person's next message after a HELP is queued for Casey with that due date, urgent ones in the OPS-032 push, and a message to the help email gets an automatic reply with the same targets and is listed in the weekly digest; "help" with other words (for example "I need help with my sink") is a request, not a HELP keyword (D-022, D-066, D-131, D-289, D-290, D-295). - **CON-019** Business sign-up includes an unchecked checkbox that gives written E-SIGN consent to job-card texts and automated calls at the number entered, and stores its wording version and timestamp; each team member's phone is added only after that member ticks the same unchecked box, with its own version, timestamp and STOP (D-191). - **CON-020** A business gets a welcome text naming Ask Hava with HELP and STOP instructions when activated, and gets no job card before that text has gone out. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:257`.
- **CON-021** Only people who texted Ask Hava, started a web request or signed up are ever texted; tenants and on-site contacts are never texted. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:258`.
- **CON-022** Consent records, opt-in wording versions, STOP/HELP/START events and message logs are kept 5 years after the last message to that number. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:183`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:356`.
- **CON-023** Consent is given only to Ask Hava, for its own texts about the person's own request, and no phone number or opt-in is ever shared, sold or transferred. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:167`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:168`.
- **CON-024** The owner dashboard shows each week's opt-out rate by audience and alerts at 3% of texted homeowners or 5% of texted businesses. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:269`.
- **CON-025** On the web-chat path the full consent text is shown first and accepted by typing OK or pressing an agree button, and a phone number entered there gets the full consent text as its first text and no other text until it replies OK by text (D-051). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:113`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:213`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:445`.
- **CON-026** The consent text is sent in Spanish when the first message reads as Spanish, and otherwise in English (D-065). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:266`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:88`.
- **CON-027** Any reply to the consent text that is not consent, an opt-out or a HELP keyword gets the consent text once more as a direct reply, in Spanish when that reply reads as Spanish, and with "Ask Hava is a local service. Our texts are automated. A person reads HELP messages within 4 days, or within 24 hours if your job is urgent." on top in the same text when the reply asks who this is or whether it is a person; a request still waiting for consent 24 hours after the first consent text gets the consent text one last time, inside quiet hours; nothing else is sent before consent except the CON-001 safety or heat-safety text, and the request closes silently at 72 hours (D-095, D-127, D-237, D-274, D-289). - **CON-029** A sole-word CANCEL or CANCELAR opt-out from a number with a live job marks that job "paused by the homeowner" without changing its status: a pick waiting for Confirm is withdrawn and that business is told, no new pick, Confirm, charge or release happens, quotes still arrive and timers keep running, and the pause ends when the homeowner picks or closes on the quote page or by email (D-129).
