# Spec: Job lifecycle

Prefix: `JOB` · Modules: `jobs` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **JOB-001** Every job has exactly one status: Intake, Open, No quotes yet, Quotes ready, Awaiting confirm, Payment pending, Connected, Done, Closed, Closed early, Not covered or Cancelled. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:155`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:158`.
- **JOB-002** A status moves only along the defined arrows, and any other move is refused and logged as a bug. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:155`.
- **JOB-003** Every waiting status has a timeout. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:111`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:136`.
- **JOB-004** Each status change takes a row lock and applies only if the job is still in the expected status. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:689`.
- **JOB-005** Every status change writes an audit row. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:150`.
- **JOB-006** Timers live in the database, survive a restart, and check the current status first, doing nothing if the job has moved on. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:179`.
- **JOB-007** "Business hours" for timers are 8:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. Arizona time, every day of the week (D-004). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:179`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:66`.
- **JOB-008** Intake moves to Open only after consent, intake questions, the safety check, the area check and the duplicate check. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:170`.
- **JOB-009** An Open job moves to Quotes ready when its first quote arrives (D-011). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:82`.
- **JOB-010** An Open job with no quote by 6 p.m. the next day moves to No quotes yet, and the "no quotes yet" text, sent at 6 p.m., says some small or unusual jobs get no quotes and offers "1) Keep waiting 2) Add details 3) Close", where 2 adds the homeowner's next message to the job page (D-030, D-241). - **JOB-011** A No quotes yet job moves to Quotes ready when a quote arrives and to Closed after 5 days with none, and the homeowner is told it closed because no business quoted and that they can text any time to try again (D-241). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:329`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:172`.
- **JOB-012** A Quotes ready job with no pick for 3 days moves to Closed. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:173`.
- **JOB-013** Awaiting confirm returns to Quotes ready when the business does not confirm within 4 business hours (PICK-019), within the extended time after a HOLD (PICK-043) or a BIZ-034 away time, or within 60 clock minutes on an emergency job (PICK-044) (D-252, D-270, D-285, D-345). - **JOB-014** Payment pending returns to Quotes ready when no payment succeeds on the Confirm page within 1 business hour after a decline or bank check (FEE-020), or within 60 clock minutes on a job chosen as an emergency (PICK-044) (D-270, D-365). - **JOB-015** A Connected job moves to Done on the homeowner's DONE (or LISTO) reply and to Closed on NO or PROBLEM at day 3 (counted from the later of the release and the picked quote's earliest visit date) or day 14, with the JOB-028 replies also accepted; when the homeowner chose a call, it returns to Quotes ready if the business never called within 72 hours; when the homeowner chose email, the 72-hour call check does not apply and a day-3 NO records a no-show and returns the job to Quotes ready and the check-in text's first line reads "Reply DONE, PROBLEM, or NO if they never reached you. - **JOB-017** A review request is sent only after Done, and asks for private feedback to Ask Hava that is never shown publicly or on quote cards and never used to order quotes (D-083). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:177`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:189`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:454`.
- **JOB-018** The homeowner can cancel a job from any status before Done. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:155`.
- **JOB-019** A job closes on "CLOSE" plus its number (for example "CLOSE 1042"), the close button on the quote page, or a longer cancel-intent message in English or Spanish (for example "please cancel my request", "never mind", "no longer need", "ya no"). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:159`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`.
- **JOB-020** The word "cancel" alone never closes a job. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:159`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:444`.
- **JOB-021** A cancel-intent message from a homeowner with more than one open job gets "Close which?" with each job numbered plus an "all" option, and closes nothing until answered. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`.
- **JOB-022** A cancel-intent message from a homeowner with one open job closes that job and says so. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:215`.
- **JOB-023** A message that merely mentions "cancel" without cancel intent gets one reply explaining how to close: "To close request <number>, reply CLOSE <number>." Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:199`.
- **JOB-024** A job number that was texted to a homeowner is never reused for anyone else. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:481`.
- **JOB-025** Each entry into Quotes ready starts a fresh 3-day no-pick timer, and the failed pick's quote shows "no longer available" and can't be chosen again; the job closes, and the homeowner is told, only when no choosable quote is left and every job card's 24-hour quote window has ended, and otherwise it waits in Quotes ready and the homeowner is told more quotes may still come (D-045, D-140). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:173`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:111`, `sources/01_spec/hhq_twin/app.py:1131`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:83`.
- **JOB-026** A Connected job with no DONE, NO or PROBLEM reply 7 days after the day-14 check-in moves to Closed, with no review request (D-046). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:176`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:111`.

Text us any time to start again.", and when the homeowner chose email it applies only if the homeowner did not reply YES to "Ask Hava: Did <business> email you and agree a time? Reply YES or NO.", sent at 72 hours (D-142, D-243, D-286). - **JOB-028** At the day-3 or day-14 check-in, LATER (or MÁS TARDE) moves a day-3 check to day 14, ONGOING (or CONTINUO) marks a recurring service as started and moves the job to Done with no more check-ins, and OTHER (or OTRO) means the homeowner hired someone else and closes the job without recording a no-show (D-240).
