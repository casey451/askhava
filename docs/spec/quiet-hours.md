# Spec: Quiet hours

Prefix: `QH` · Modules: `messaging` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **QH-001** A non-urgent text is sent only when the local time is between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. in both Arizona time and the recipient's area-code time zone (D-003). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:184`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:121`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:446`.
- **QH-002** Arizona time is computed as America/Phoenix, which keeps the same UTC offset all year. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:184`.
- **QH-003** The recipient's time zone is derived from the phone number's area code; every area code in the table has a zone, and a number whose area code is not in the table is never texted (D-100, D-139). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:121`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:537`.
- **QH-004** The only texts allowed outside the window are urgent safety texts (including the heat-safety text, INT-031), STOP/HELP/START replies, pick codes, direct replies within 15 minutes of the person's own inbound text, alerts to Casey (never texts, OPS-035, and sent only as OPS-029 says), and, for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, its job cards and confirm requests (at night only to businesses that ticked "after-hours emergencies"), its quote texts (the first-quote text and quote-count updates), the questions INT-029 asks after the cards, and the homeowner's pick, confirm and connect texts, including the who's-calling (TXT-027), missed-call (TXT-030) and PICK-044 texts, and the INT-033 texts (D-003, D-054, D-076, D-136, D-164, D-208, D-270, D-271, D-314). - **QH-005** Quiet hours apply to every recipient, homeowners and businesses alike, including job cards and confirm requests, and apply to business emails and pick calls exactly as to texts: every channel of one business notice is sent, or held, together, so its clock has one start, except as QH-004 allows (D-054, D-308). - **QH-006** A text held by quiet hours is sent at the start of the next allowed window. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:212`.
- **QH-007** A held text is dropped if its job closed before the send time. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:212`.
- **QH-008** Retries of failed texts follow the same quiet-hours rules as first sends. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:200`.
- **QH-009** Quiet-hours decisions are covered by unit and property tests across both time zones and the daylight-saving changes of the recipient's zone. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:471`.
