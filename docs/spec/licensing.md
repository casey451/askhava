# Spec: Categories, licensing and routing

Prefix: `LIC` · Modules: `catalog`, `licensing` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **LIC-001** Each category stores its job types, license rule (none, ROC class or other state agency), recurring flag, intake questions (each marked whether its multiple-choice answer is a job-card field), fee rule with a set tier for trip-fee quotes, after-hours emergency flag and keyword list (D-202). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:283`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:35`.
- **LIC-002** Adding or switching off a category is a logged settings change with no code change. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:35`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:464`.
- **LIC-003** A category that isn't confirmed in the K2b list stays switched off. Source: `sources/02_build_plan/Build_Plan.md:34`.
- **LIC-004** Childcare, home care, massage, mobile hair and nails, mobile vets, catering, towing and property management are not offered at launch. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:34`.
- **LIC-005** Garage doors and junk removal are off at launch, while estate cleanouts and donation pickup stay on. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:240`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:249`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:460`.
- **LIC-006** A business's license class comes from the state record at sign-up and is never typed in by the business. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:115`.
- **LIC-007** ROC licenses are checked against the ROC's daily downloadable lists plus a throttled single live lookup at each pick (D-017). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:38`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:65`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:250`.
- **LIC-008** A failed lookup (site down, CAPTCHA or page change) is "unknown": the business stays as it was for up to 72 hours (a setting), is warned at 24 hours (a setting) and is then paused; while its status is unknown it can't Confirm; a pause that clears when the list loads needs no review and is shown to Casey as a daily count; and a misread page never changes a verified status (D-199). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:280`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:194`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:130`.
- **LIC-009** A license-end or bond-cancellation date on the state record pauses the business on that date. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:129`.
- **LIC-010** ROC lists and the city license list are used for verification only, never for recruiting. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:250`.
- **LIC-011** A business in a no-license category must match Lake Havasu City's published active business-license spreadsheet on its listed business name, trade name (DBA) or owner name, may enter its city license number when the list has one, and a business not yet on the list waits as pending and is activated automatically, with the identity code, when a later daily list shows a match, keeping its join date (D-059, D-177, D-255). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:343`), Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:258`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:37`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`.
- **LIC-012** Pest control and any weed job involving pre-emergents or advertised weed control go only to Arizona Department of Agriculture pest-management licensees, with the license number shown. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:203`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:46`.
- **LIC-013** Alarm installs and monitoring go only to Board-certified alarm businesses that also hold an ROC low-voltage class. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:204`.
- **LIC-014** Camera installs go only to R-67 (or broader) licensees, or to handymen under $1,000 with no permit. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:204`.
- **LIC-015** Home inspections go only to Board-certified inspectors. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:204`.
- **LIC-016** Septic pumping goes only to haulers with an ADEQ septage-hauler vehicle license. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:205`.
- **LIC-017** Moves that start or end outside Arizona are refused (D-082). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:206`.
- **LIC-018** Home watch and turnover cleaning never include rent collection, finding tenants or leasing. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:207`.
- **LIC-019** A handyman quote for construction-type work whose total (labor plus materials, top of any range) is $1,000 or more is refused with an explanation of Arizona's handyman limit. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:41`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:345`.
- **LIC-020** The $1,000 limit does not apply to non-construction services such as detailing or moving. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:45`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:208`.
- **LIC-021** Gas work, hardwired smoke alarms and fire sprinklers are never sent to handymen at any price. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:42`.
- **LIC-022** Permit-type jobs (water heater replacement, panels, new circuits, re-roofs and similar) are never sent to handymen. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:43`.
- **LIC-023** A split-job check runs when a handyman's quote is submitted and blocks it when the same business has another handyman job at the same address within 30 days and the two jobs share a job type or together reach $1,000 (D-027, D-081, D-154). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:208`.
- **LIC-024** Every handyman quote card shows "Not a licensed contractor. Only for jobs under $1,000 total with no permit." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:44`.
- **LIC-025** Handymen certify on each quote that it is a full-job price under $1,000 with no permit and no gas, smoke-alarm or sprinkler work, and a business that breaks this is removed. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:241`.
- **LIC-026** A licensed-trade quote card shows "ROC #<number> showed Active on the ROC website when we checked on <date>. Check it yourself." with a link. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:192`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:144`.
- **LIC-027** At sign-up the business hears a read-back of what its license covers, for ROC and other state-agency licenses alike, and may add extras only if the license covers them or they need no license (D-267). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:355`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:94`.
- **LIC-028** Dryer-vent cleaning needs no license, while rerouting or replacing a vent is handyman work under the $1,000 cap. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:240`.
- **LIC-029** Plug-in temporary holiday lights need no license, while any new outlet, circuit or hardwiring goes only to R-11 electricians. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:240`.
- **LIC-030** Freestanding appliance repair goes to R-63 licensees or to handymen under $1,000, and never includes gas, plumbing or electrical lines. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:240`.
- **LIC-031** Weekly pool cleaning needs no license; pool repair or equipment work goes to R-6 or a handyman under $1,000, and gas heaters only to licensed businesses. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:240`.
- **LIC-032** Insurance for no-license categories is shown but never required. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:48`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:64`.
- **LIC-033** Licensing code keeps at least 95% branch coverage, and mutation tests turn red when the license filter is broken. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:481`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:472`.
- **LIC-034** A quote card for a license from another state agency shows "<Agency> license #<number> showed Active on the <agency> website when we checked on <date>. Check it yourself." with a link (D-057). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:192`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:203`.
- **LIC-035** A sub-license receives only cards whose job types all fall within its scope: R-39R only air-conditioning job types and R-37R only drain and sewer job types (D-058). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:136`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:137`.
- **LIC-036** Handyman quotes are taken through the portal quote form, whose required certification box sits on the same screen as the price and is ticked on every quote, and a handyman's text quote gets a link to that form (D-081, D-186). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:267`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:241`.
- **LIC-037** A licensed-trade quote card shows "ROC license since <year>", or "<Agency> license since <year>" for another state agency's license, from the original issue date on the state record, when the record has one (D-190, D-267). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:355`), Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:271`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:192`.
- **LIC-038** A no-license quote card shows "City business license on file since <year>" when the city list gives a date, and otherwise "City business license on file", after the LIC-024 handyman line, which stays first (D-190). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:271`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:37`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:44`.
- **LIC-039** When a license list fails to load for 12 hours (a setting), Casey gets an alert listed for his next window, and while it fails the throttled single live lookup runs for each active business on that list so verified statuses keep updating under LIC-008 (D-230). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:318`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:194`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:38`.
- **LIC-040** The catalog gives these categories multiple-choice intake questions marked as job-card fields: pool and spa repair and weekly pool cleaning (pool size band, surface, water condition), roof repair (roof type, stories, leak location), local moving (bedrooms, stairs or elevator, distance band), boat detailing and boat repair (length band; in the water, on a lift or on a trailer), freestanding appliance repair (appliance type, brand from a list, error code showing or not), window cleaning and solar screens (count band, stories) and home watch (visit schedule); free-text model or serial numbers are never card fields (D-257). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:345`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:35`.
- **LIC-041** The plumbing repair, heating and cooling, electrical, roof repair and painting categories split their job types into repair, replace and new-install job types where the license rule allows, so a business can untick some at sign-up or later (BIZ-004) (D-257).
