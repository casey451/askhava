# Spec: Business sign-up and accounts

Prefix: `BIZ` · Modules: `businesses`, `portals` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **BIZ-001** Sign-up creates a Stripe customer and needs no card. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:324`.
- **BIZ-002** Identity is checked by a chain: a text code to the phone on the state record, then a voice call reading the code (landlines), then email to the address on the state record, then an automatically mailed letter (Lob) as the last resort; every code or letter goes to a contact from a public record (the city license list for no-license businesses), never one the business typed, and with no usable record contact the sign-up waits in needs review (D-059). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:37`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:93`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:428`.
- **BIZ-003** Owner approval never activates a business by itself; the identity code is still required, and unlicensed businesses also need a city-license-list name match. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`.
- **BIZ-004** A business ticks a job-type checklist at sign-up and can change it any time. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:116`.
- **BIZ-005** A business can switch on "also send me jobs nobody else covers". Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:104`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:125`.
- **BIZ-006** A business can tick "We speak Spanish", which is shown on its quote card. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:88`.
- **BIZ-007** A business can tick "after-hours emergencies". Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:87`.
- **BIZ-008** A business can record insurance (yes or no), which is shown on no-license quote cards as business-reported and is never verified, tracked for expiry or used to pause (D-061). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:48`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:190`.
- **BIZ-009** A business account is pending, active, paused (with a reason), waitlist, needs review or removed. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:90`.
- **BIZ-010** A business can pause itself, change its towns and job types, and set its own quiet times, which can only narrow the app-wide window, in the portal; there is no weekly card limit (D-028, D-085). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:396`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`.
- **BIZ-011** A complaint gets an apology, an explanation that the business is independent, the business's own phone, the job details and the ROC complaint link through askhava.com. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:91`.
- **BIZ-012** A business is paused only after 3 complaints in 90 days, and every complaint alerts Casey (D-016). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:91`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:462`.
- **BIZ-013** The business portal shows how the homeowner chose to be contacted after a pick. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:409`.
- **BIZ-014** The business portal can never release contact details without a confirmed pick. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:315`.
- **BIZ-015** The business portal shows billing (charges, free first win, refunds, credits, statements as PDF and CSV, payment methods) built from the ledger. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:393`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:361`.
- **BIZ-016** A business is activated automatically when its identity code is completed and its license or city-list match passes with no flag (record or name mismatch, the letter path, a fraud signal, or switched-off categories only); a flagged sign-up waits in needs review until Casey approves it, his approval never activates without the code and the match, he can pause any business afterwards, and every automatic activation is listed for him in the owner portal (D-145). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:617`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:375`.
- **BIZ-017** A business whose owner phone opts out of texts is paused with the reason "texts stopped" and is emailed how to resume, while an opt-out from a team member's phone stops texts to that phone only and the owner is emailed (D-062, D-198). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:279`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`.
- **BIZ-018** Business texts are in English unless Spanish is picked for that phone in the portal settings, and the language is set per phone (D-063, D-191). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:272`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:416`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:88`.
- **BIZ-019** Business sign-up requires an email address, every text to a business is also shown in its portal, and while texting is off or suspended business texts go by email (D-144). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:177`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:209`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:393`.
- **BIZ-020** A business whose sign-up waits in needs review is told its status by email and by text to the record contact within 1 business day of starting to wait, and at least every 7 days until it is decided (D-145, D-178). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:259`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:617`.
- **BIZ-021** Sign-up starts with the category list, each marked "Open now" or "Coming later" (pest control only as a neutral item in that list), before asking for a license number; a business choosing only coming-later categories joins the waitlist before entering a license and is told why and "Your join date is kept." (D-145, D-175, D-176). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:256`, `docs/decisions.md:257`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:35`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:90`.
- **BIZ-022** A business may write an "About us" line of up to 140 characters, shown on its quote cards as written; it is checked like a quote (PICK-007) and against the WORD-003 claims list (and "licensed" on a no-license card), and a line that fails is returned to the business with the reason (D-190). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:271`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:194`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:200`.
- **BIZ-023** The owner can add team members, each with an email for sign-in, an optional phone and the roles "Cards and quotes" and/or "Confirm and billing" (the owner always has both), and adding or removing one is logged and emailed to the owner (D-191). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:272`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:374`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:315`.
- **BIZ-024** Anyone may fill in the sign-up form for a business, the identity code still goes only to the record contact, and the screen says "Someone else filling this in? The owner just reads you the code." (D-179). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:260`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:93`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`.
- **BIZ-025** The business portal has a "Your numbers" panel showing this month's cards, quotes, picks, confirms, fees and fee per win, plus the typical (median) number of quotes per job in each of its trades (D-193). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:274`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:393`.
- **BIZ-026** Before sending a code, sign-up shows the masked record contact and its kind (mobile, landline, email or mailing address), calls a landline by voice first, and offers "That number is old", which explains how to update the ROC or city record, saves the sign-up and resumes it automatically when the next daily list shows a new contact (D-178). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:259`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:93`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:37`.
- **BIZ-027** A sign-up waiting in needs review gets Casey's decision within 1 business day (a setting), shown as a service target in the owner portal that blocks nothing (D-204). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:285`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:115`.
- **BIZ-028** From a linked business phone, PAUSE pauses job cards until RESUME, and BUSY <n> pauses them for n days (1 to 60) with the portal showing "Booked until <date>"; each keyword has an English and a Spanish reply template, and these words are never read as an opt-out nor an opt-out word as one of them (D-198). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:279`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:158`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:396`.
- **BIZ-029** Each complaint is shown in the business portal with its kind and date, without homeowner details, with a reply box open for 5 days, and the reply is shown to Casey with the complaint; the 3-in-90-days pause (BIZ-012) is unchanged (D-200). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:281`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:91`.
- **BIZ-030** Sign-up shows a plain-words summary of fees, refunds and card use above the Terms link, including "It's an introduction fee for a homeowner who chose you." and "A new fee never applies until you accept it.", the business page shows the same summary as "How you're charged", and a test checks its numbers against the fee settings (D-173, D-205). Source: Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:254`, `docs/decisions.md:286`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:212`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`.
- **BIZ-031** The sign-up done screen says "You're one of <n> businesses in <trade> so far. We'll text you once when requests in your trade start.", with n counted as in CARD-019, and that one text is an account notice under the business's consent (CON-019), never marketing (D-187).
