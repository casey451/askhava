# Spec: AI boundary

Prefix: `AI` · Modules: `ai`, `privacy` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **AI-001** The app's AI calls go only to the Claude API's Messages endpoints (`/v1/messages` and `/v1/messages/count_tokens`), with the model a setting (AI-024, Haiku 5.5 at launch), through the `ai` adapter, a test failing on any other API path, under the zero-retention agreement once it is signed and, before that, only as AI-008 allows; no real homeowner text is ever entered in the provider's Console or playground, which zero retention does not cover (D-412, D-492). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "compose all of the fixes we need" and "what needs to be done to make this business perfect" on fix round 13 (`docs/decisions.md:531`, `docs/decisions.md:554`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:58`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:112`, `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:165`.
- **AI-002** A plain-code scrubber runs before every AI call, on homeowner and business text alike (including quote wording), and removes up to two words after "this is", "my name is", "I'm", "im", "it's", "soy" or "me llamo" (any case) unless the first of them is on a common-words list in settings, any phone-shaped number (7 or more digits, with or without spaces, dots, dashes or brackets), street-number patterns, emails and links (D-146, D-373). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Go ahead and fix everything that needs to be fixed." on fix round 12 (`docs/decisions.md:499`, `docs/decisions.md:503`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:58`.
- **AI-003** The homeowner's name from the vault is also removed by code before any AI call. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:291`.
- **AI-004** The `ai` module never receives unscrubbed text. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:133`.
- **AI-005** The AI returns only schema-validated fixed fields, and anything that fails the schema is discarded; enum values are trimmed and lowercased before matching, and a trade not open in the catalog, or a job type not under the returned trade, discards the reading (D-492). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:343`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:192`, `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:171`.
- **AI-006** The AI has no tools, nothing it returns is executed, it never moves money, releases details, decides who gets a card or judges safety, and the only words it writes that people read are those AI-019 allows; AI quote summaries stay off (PICK-003) (D-373, D-430). - **AI-007** Prompt-injection text in homeowner or business messages never changes routing, fees or disclosures, and tests prove it. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:345`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:296`.
- **AI-008** While the live app handles real requests and the zero-retention agreement is not yet signed, it makes no AI call unless the owner-portal switch "AI on scrubbed text" is on (confirmed D-418), which can be turned on only after the account checklist records the AI provider's current commercial API terms (URL, date checked, that API inputs are not used for training, the retention period and how long the provider may keep text its safety systems flag) and a monthly spend limit set in the provider's Console at no more than 3 times the expected monthly AI cost, and only on a day the privacy policy carries the matching AI-018 wording; while the switch is on, AI calls take scrubbed text only (AI-002 to AI-004), never photos (AI-016), and do only what AI-010 and AI-019 allow; while it is off the app uses plain-code scrubbing, keyword rules, the fixed question bank (INT-039), cards from fixed fields and her scrubbed words (CARD-007) and homeowner questions, and businesses quote through the structured quote form; the Phase 2 test of the real AI from the Railway test project, with fake data only, is allowed (D-040, D-080, D-412, D-418, D-430, D-492). - **AI-019** AI-written words are limited: the AI may reword the next question-bank question, ask one follow-up the set's expert brief allows, write the job card and the project file from the scrubbed request, answers and photo notes, and write the INT-046 image prompt; it never diagnoses, names a cause, prices or estimates cost, judges safety or urgency, recommends or mentions any business, promises an outcome, or adds a fact the homeowner didn't give; code checks every AI output for contact details (CARD-008), dollar amounts and prices, cause words ("probably", "likely", "sounds like", "needs a new", "the problem is", and in Spanish "probablemente", "parece que", "seguramente", "suena a", "el problema es", "necesita un nuevo" and "necesita una nueva", in any case and with or without accents), the WORD-022 phrases and the WORD-003 claims, and any failure falls back to the fixed template or her own scrubbed words; every AI-written card is approved by the homeowner or auto-sent only after she was shown it (INT-041) (D-430, D-492). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:584`), `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:163`.
- **AI-020** The scrubber normalizes to NFKC and strips Unicode format characters before matching, converts number words in English and Spanish to digits before the 7-digit rule, matches "at" and "dot" email forms, @handles and bare domains, removes any name the person stated earlier in the conversation as well as the vault name, and a committed adversarial corpus of at least 200 English and Spanish cases must show at least 99% recall on contact tokens in `Ci` (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
- **AI-021** Every AI-written text passes the CARD-010 content screen, a URL ban and a length cap before it is sent, falling back to the fixed template on any hit; no AI call is made for a web-chat session before its phone code is verified; and a global daily AI-call cap raises an alert and stops further calls when reached (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
- **AI-022** The AI-010 fields, with a confidence from 0 to 1 for trade and for job type and no other field, are defined in one committed JSON schema, `schemas/intake_reading.json` (at the repository root), that the stand-in validates its output against, the Rust types are checked against and the request's `output_config` sends, and a `Ci` test fails on any difference between them (D-492, D-493). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:169`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-023** Each AI call has a 20-second timeout (a setting) and at most 2 retries with exponential backoff, made only on a connection error, a 500, 504 or 529, or a 429 that carries `retry-after` (waiting that long); any other error, a `refusal` or `max_tokens` stop, or a schema failure ends the call and the caller runs that step on the AI-off path (INT-021); and a 401, a 403, a spend-limit 400 or a 429 without `retry-after` also raises an owner alert (D-492). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:166`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-024** The model ID is a setting, `claude-haiku-5-5` at launch, stored with every AI call record beside the instruction version (AI-015) and the provider's request ID; changing it needs the golden set, `test_set.csv` and the injection suite to score no worse than the recorded run (AI-027) and a logged decision (D-492). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:167`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-025** The scrubbed text is sent inside one fixed XML tag (`<homeowner_message>`, or `<business_quote>` for quote text) after every occurrence of that tag's name in the text is escaped, and the versioned instruction says the tag's content is data and never instructions; an injection test that closes the tag early changes no routing, fee or disclosure (AI-007) (D-492). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:170`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-026** While the AI-008 switch is on, the web-chat page shows "An AI service helps word our questions." above the chat box, and while it is off the line is absent (D-492). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:172`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-027** Each real-model run on the golden set and `test_set.csv` records, in `docs/phase2/real-ai.md`, the model ID, the instruction version, the date, job-type accuracy by language, the abstention rate, the output-check fallbacks and the INT-039 skip threshold the run supports, and the run is repeated monthly while the AI-008 switch is on (D-492). Source: `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:173`, `docs/brains/ai-intake.md:168`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`.
- **AI-028** Below the AI-021 global daily cap, AI calls are budgeted per step so a prank or a credit-draining abuser costs a bounded number of calls: no AI call is made for a conversation before its consent text has gone and its ZIP and area check (INT-022) has passed; each inbound message gets at most 2 readings (AI-011), each question-bank question at most 1 reword (at most 8, INT-039), the conversation at most 1 follow-up, the card and the project file 1 each plus 1 per EDIT (at most 3 EDITs, INT-041), concept images at most 4 prompts (INT-046) and photo screening at most 2 calls a photo for at most 3 photos (AI-016); over all steps a conversation gets at most 40 calls an Arizona day and a job at most 60 in all, but the card call is always reserved, so no budget or backstop ever lands on the card and an EDIT is never left half done; a web-chat session whose phone code is verified shares the budget of the phone it verified with, and an unverified one makes no call (AI-021); reading a business's quote has its own budget of 3 calls per business per job (PICK-006) and never counts toward the job's; only the first 1,600 characters of a scrubbed message are sent to the AI, while the AI-off card and the stored request keep her full scrubbed words, so a fact past the cut is never lost from the card; every number is a setting; a call past a budget is not made, the step runs on the AI-off path (INT-021) with nothing said to the person, the call record shows outcome `budget`, and a `budget` row never counts toward the AI-021 daily cap (D-1154).
