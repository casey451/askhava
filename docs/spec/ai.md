# Spec: AI boundary

Prefix: `AI` · Modules: `ai`, `privacy` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **AI-001** The app's AI calls go only to the Claude API (Haiku 5.5), through the `ai` adapter, under the zero-retention agreement once it is signed and, before that, only as AI-008 allows (D-412). - **AI-002** A plain-code scrubber runs before every AI call, on homeowner and business text alike (including quote wording), and removes up to two words after "this is", "my name is", "I'm", "im", "it's", "soy" or "me llamo" (any case) unless the first of them is on a common-words list in settings, any phone-shaped number (7 or more digits, with or without spaces, dots, dashes or brackets), street-number patterns, emails and links (D-146, D-373). - **AI-003** The homeowner's name from the vault is also removed by code before any AI call. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:291`.
- **AI-004** The `ai` module never receives unscrubbed text. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:133`.
- **AI-005** The AI returns only schema-validated fixed fields, and anything that fails the schema is discarded. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:343`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:192`.
- **AI-006** The AI has no tools, nothing it returns is executed, it never writes homeowner texts, moves money or releases details, and while AI quote summaries are off (PICK-003) it writes nothing any homeowner or business reads (D-373). - **AI-007** Prompt-injection text in homeowner or business messages never changes routing, fees or disclosures, and tests prove it. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:345`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:296`.
- **AI-008** While the live app handles real requests and the zero-retention agreement is not yet signed, it makes no AI call unless the owner-portal switch "AI on scrubbed text" is on (default pending Casey), which can be turned on only after the account checklist records the AI provider's current commercial API terms (URL, date checked, that API inputs are not used for training, and the retention period) and only on a day the privacy policy carries the matching AI-018 wording; while the switch is on, AI calls take scrubbed text only (AI-002 to AI-004), never photos (CARD-013), and only classify and choose the next fixed question; while it is off the app uses plain-code scrubbing, keyword rules, the question bank (INT-039) and homeowner questions, and businesses quote through the structured quote form; the Phase 2 test of the real AI from the Railway test project, with fake data only, is allowed (D-040, D-080, D-412).
