# Spec: Texts and templates

Prefix: `TXT` · Modules: `messaging` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **TXT-001** Every text kind has an English and a Spanish template, and a test fails if any text kind lacks a Spanish template. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:88`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:416`.
- **TXT-002** A homeowner whose language is Spanish gets every text from the Spanish template. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:266`.
- **TXT-003** Spanish templates are marked as drafts for native-speaker review. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:266`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:146`.
- **TXT-004** Every text is built from a fixed template selected by template ID and language; the AI never writes texts to homeowners. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:286`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`.
- **TXT-005** Texts use plain words, carry no meaning only through emoji, and show full links. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:415`.
- **TXT-006** Every link inside a text points to askhava.com, and no public link shortener is ever used. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:29`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:263`.
- **TXT-007** The ROC complaint link in texts is askhava.com/help/roc-complaint, which links onward to the ROC. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:117`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:91`.
- **TXT-008** Homeowner texts are only about the homeowner's own request, with no promotions, cross-selling or "save this number" wording. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:181`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:262`.
- **TXT-009** No text is sent to a past customer unless they write first. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:262`.
- **TXT-010** No template says "reply CANCEL"; texts tell homeowners to "Reply CLOSE" plus the job number. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:199`.
- **TXT-011** The outbox never sends the same message twice: every send carries an idempotency key derived from what it identifies. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:140`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:149`.
- **TXT-012** Inbound messages and delivery-status notices are de-duplicated by Twilio's message ID. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:201`.
- **TXT-013** Delivery status (queued, delivered, failed, undelivered) is tracked for every text. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`.
- **TXT-014** Important texts (quote link, confirm request, who's calling, missed call) are retried up to 3 times over 30 minutes, then sent by email and shown in the portal (D-236). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:324`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`.
- **TXT-015** Every failed text is retried, and when a homeowner's texts fail 3 times with no email on file, Casey gets a call-back alert, due as INT-023 sets (D-226). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:314`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:211`.
- **TXT-016** The homeowner is texted when the first quote arrives, with that quote's type, amount and earliest visit and a note that more may come and that they can pick any time (D-239). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:327`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:82`.
- **TXT-017** Quote-count update texts (for example "You now have 4 quotes") go out at most once per 3 hours per job, measured from the delivery time of the previous update (D-012). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:84`.
- **TXT-018** The first status text offers "Want updates by email too?". Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:209`.
- **TXT-019** The message log stores a vault reference instead of the phone number, plus kind, template ID, language, request ID, sent time, Twilio status and attempts. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:95`.
- **TXT-020** Message bodies are reduced to their template ID 30 days after sending, while the log entry is kept for the consent-log period (D-013). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:95`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:438`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:356`.
- **TXT-021** A texting on/off switch in the owner portal (web-and-email mode) stops all outbound texts, and updates then go by email and the quote page. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:177`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:464`.
- **TXT-022** A suspended Twilio number pauses all texting and switches to email plus the portal, and no second number is ever used to get around a suspension. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:267`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:75`.
- **TXT-023** During a texting suspension, everyone who gave no email (new texters and open jobs needing an update) goes on Casey's call list in the owner portal. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:209`.
- **TXT-024** While texting is off or suspended, any message with an emergency keyword raises a critical alert to Casey and gets the safety text by email when an email is on file and in the web chat, and, when the homeowner's own message sent during the suspension matched an emergency keyword, one automated voice call from the Ask Hava number reads the safety text (CALL-019) whether or not an email is on file, never to a number that opted out; the site status banner turns on automatically, and texting is switched back on only by Casey after Twilio confirms the number is restored (D-144, D-206, D-210). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:298`), Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:287`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:134`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:149`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:209`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:213`.
- **TXT-025** Update texts for the same job and recipient that quiet hours held are merged into one text at the start of the next allowed window (D-158). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:84`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:212`.
- **TXT-026** A business with a released job and no call attempt gets a reminder 24 and 48 hours after the release, "Ask Hava: Job <n>: no call yet. Call within 72 hours of the release, or the fee can't be refunded.", naming the homeowner's call window (PICK-018) and, when the homeowner chose email, adding "The homeowner chose email; your 2 call attempts still count for the refund.", from an English and a Spanish template (D-174, D-235, D-266). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:323`, `docs/decisions.md:354`), Casey Oct 27, 2026: "Make all the changes you recommend" on the business-side fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:255`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:176`.
- **TXT-027** The who's-calling text names the picked business, the masked number and the homeowner's call window, and says "If you miss it, call them back on this number. It works for 30 days." and "Ask for the price and any changes in writing before work starts. Don't pay in full up front." (D-213, D-235). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:301`, `docs/decisions.md:323`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:187`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:189`.
- **TXT-028** After a RENT answer, the homeowner gets one text, "Ask Hava: If you rent, your landlord may be responsible for this repair. Check your lease or ask your landlord before you choose.", from an English and a Spanish template marked for attorney review (D-216). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:304`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:89`.
- **TXT-029** A PROBLEM reply gets "Ask Hava: Thanks. A person reads every problem report within 7 days. Ask Hava can't settle disputes about work or price. Licensed trades: you can also file with the state at askhava.com/help/roc-complaint." from an English and a Spanish template (D-222). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:310`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:91`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:117`.
- **TXT-030** After a business's first call attempt that no person answers, the homeowner gets one text under quiet hours, "Ask Hava: <Business> tried to call you about request <n>. Call them back on <masked number>.", or the same message by email when it can't be texted and an email is on file (D-236). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:324`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:181`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:334`.
- **TXT-031** A homeowner text that is only LIST or LISTA gets the job's choosable quotes in arrival order, one line each with its number, business name, quote type and amount and earliest visit, at most 10 quotes per text, ending "To choose, reply the number and YES, for example 2 YES.", and with more than one job in Quotes ready it first asks which job (D-239). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:327`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:82`.

We'll text you when the first one is in.", saying "at 8 a.m." when quiet hours hold the cards, and each quote-count update says "<k> of <N> businesses have quoted so far" (D-241).
