# Spec: Wording and claims

Prefix: `WORD` · Modules: `messaging`, `site`, `portals` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **WORD-001** The brand in every template, page and document is "Ask Hava" (and "Ask Hava for Business"), and the legal entity is "Ask Hava LLC". Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:24`.
- **WORD-002** The terms, the quote page and the site state "Ask Hava is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:237`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:188`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:453`.
- **WORD-003** No template or page uses "vetted" or "background-checked", calls or ranks any business "best" (the neutral homeowner prompt in INT-014 is exempt, D-029), and a test scans every template and page for these claims. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:194`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:454`.
- **WORD-004** The fee is called an "introduction fee" (by job size) and never a "commission" or "share". Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:238`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:455`.
- **WORD-005** The quote page says "Every eligible quote shown, in the order received." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:189`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:452`.
- **WORD-006** No-license quote cards say "Insurance (business-reported): Yes" or "Insurance (business-reported): No" (D-023). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:190`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:452`.
- **WORD-007** Privacy copy says "Your number is never shared" together with "Your first name and address go only to the business you pick." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:191`.
- **WORD-008** All copy uses the service voice ("Ask Hava: ...") and never a first-person Hava, and every text in either language begins "Ask Hava:" (D-064). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:28`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:145`.
- **WORD-009** Texts and help copy disclose "Ask Hava is a local service. Our texts are automated.", and anyone who asks whether they are talking to a person gets a truthful answer. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:197`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:28`.
- **WORD-010** Quote refusals are worded as eligibility rules ("this business isn't eligible for this job type"), never as Ask Hava judging the work. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:237`.
- **WORD-011** No feature prices, bids, negotiates, schedules, supervises or guarantees work on Ask Hava's behalf. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:237`.
- **WORD-012** Business-side claims such as "$25 is about what one online lead costs" appear only with a source cited on the page. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:193`.
- **WORD-013** Key terms (fee, STOP/HELP, privacy) also appear in Spanish wherever Spanish copy markets the service. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:195`.
- **WORD-014** Quote cards say "Independent business, not affiliated with Ask Hava." Source: `sources/03_business_and_design/Ask_Hava_Prototype_Revision_Notes_for_ChatGPT.md:45`.
- **WORD-015** The Stripe account is described as "local service marketplace: introduction fees charged to service businesses." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:224`.
