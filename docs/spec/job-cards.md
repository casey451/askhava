# Spec: Job cards and who receives them

Prefix: `CARD` · Modules: `quotes`, `catalog`, `licensing`, `privacy` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **CARD-001** A job card goes to every active, verified business serving the job's town whose license covers every remaining candidate job type as the license map says (LIC-043) and which ticked at least one of them, skipping a business whose largest-job answer is below a size or budget band the homeowner gave, or which answered no to recurring work for a recurring job type (BIZ-049) (D-392, D-393). - **CARD-002** The same license and checklist rules apply when a job is offered to businesses in neighboring towns, which are Arizona towns only (INT-022) (D-382). - **CARD-003** There is no cap on how many businesses receive a job card or join a trade (D-028). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:13`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:113`.
- **CARD-004** When no business's checklist covers the job, the card goes to licensed businesses that left "also send me jobs nobody else covers" switched on. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:125`.
- **CARD-005** A job card never contains a homeowner name, phone number, email, street address or on-site contact. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:81`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:91`.
- **CARD-006** A card shows the town and the kind of place only (for example house, marina, storage lot or ramp). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:49`.
- **CARD-007** A card's description is the job card the homeowner approved (INT-041): while AI is on, the AI-written card (AI-019) from her scrubbed words, answers, photo notes and project file; with AI off, her scrubbed description plus fixed fields; either way it carries the card-field answers (LIC-040), the INT-030 timing answer (with "Needed by <day>" for answer 4), the photos (INT-044), any concept image with its label (INT-046) and, when the 911 text was sent, "911 text sent: <gas / CO alarm / smoke or fire / electrical / power line>", and it passes the CARD-008 gate last (D-202, D-208, D-394, D-430, D-434, D-436).
