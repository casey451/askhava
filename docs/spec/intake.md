# Spec: Intake and conversation

Prefix: `INT` · Modules: `intake`, `messaging` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **INT-001** A homeowner can start a request either by texting the Ask Hava number or by web chat on askhava.com. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:43`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:177`.
- **INT-002** A plain emergency keyword list, matched by word stem, runs on every inbound message before any AI call or other handling, after street-address patterns are removed and never on the answer to the name-and-address question (so "Smoketree Ave" is not "smoke"), and contains at least smell, burning, alarm, smoke, spark, flood, dizzy, head hurts, buzz, melt, sizzl, propane, carbon monoxide, "co alarm", "co detector", nausea and the Spanish olor, quema, humo, chispa, inund, mareo, zumb, derret, monóxido and náusea, plus "hot" and "caliente" only in a message that also names an outlet, plug, cord, switch, breaker, panel, wire or meter (or their Spanish words) (D-137, D-165, D-272). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Ok fix everything we need to." on fix round 8 (`docs/decisions.md:363`, `docs/decisions.md:367`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:607`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:130`.
- **INT-003** Any emergency keyword match sends the safety text within 5 minutes, whatever the AI returns. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`.
- **INT-004** The safety text says why it was sent (for example "a rotten-egg smell can mean a gas leak") and contains "If you smell gas, leave now and call 911 and your gas company from outside. Ask Hava is not an emergency service.", for a gas match it also says "Don't flip switches or light anything. Call your gas company now, before getting quotes. Keep the appliance off until a licensed pro checks it."; for a carbon-monoxide match (carbon monoxide, co alarm, co detector, monóxido) or a symptom keyword (dizzy, head hurts, nausea, mareo, náusea) it puts first "Carbon monoxide has no smell. If a CO alarm is sounding, or anyone has a headache, dizziness, nausea or confusion, get everyone outside to fresh air now and call 911 from outside. Don't go back in until responders say it's safe." and then "Once everyone is outside, call your gas company too. Keep the appliance off until a licensed pro checks it.", which replaces the gas match's "Call your gas company now" sentence when both match; and the electrical and water lines follow INT-034 (D-209, D-272). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Ok fix everything we need to." on fix round 8 (`docs/decisions.md:363`, `docs/decisions.md:367`), Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:297`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:196`.
- **INT-005** An emergency request never produces a job card before the homeowner replies that they are safe. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-006** After the homeowner replies that they are safe, or answers "Today, it's urgent" to the INT-030 question, Ask Hava lets them choose emergency or regular, saying between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. "Emergency also lets businesses that take after-hours calls reach you tonight. After-hours work usually costs more." and between 8 p.m. and 8 a.m. first giving the INT-033 after-hours count, and the choice is not offered when that count is 0 (D-208, D-271, D-292). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Ok fix everything we need to." on fix round 8 (`docs/decisions.md:363`, `docs/decisions.md:366`, `docs/decisions.md:387`), Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:296`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-007** A message of one or two words never opens a new job (for example "ME", a bare ZIP code or "ok sure"), except a message with an emergency keyword, which opens a job whatever its length (D-138). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:214`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-008** A cancel-like message never opens a new job. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`.
- **INT-009** At most one active job exists per homeowner, property and trade. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:98`.
- **INT-010** A second message about the same thing within 2 hours joins the existing job even if read as a different trade; after 2 hours, a same-trade message joins only if its description mostly matches, otherwise it opens a new job. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:98`.
- **INT-011** An answer to an intake question counts only for a job that is still open, and is matched exactly (so "ok" inside "broke" is not an answer). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:214`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:626`.
- **INT-012** Word answers to intake questions are accepted (for example "the AC one", "Yes!", a first name, or "my email is ..."). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:122`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:646`.
- **INT-013** When the trade is in doubt, the homeowner is asked a numbered trade question such as "Is this mainly 1) plumbing or 2) heating/AC?", and the candidate job types follow the chosen trade. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:120`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:644`.
- **INT-014** When the candidate job types fall under different licenses, the homeowner is asked "Which fits best?" with numbered options. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:121`.
- **INT-015** An intake question that gets no usable answer is asked once more and then a fallback is applied, so intake never freezes. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:644`.
- **INT-016** A request that fits no open category gets an immediate reply and is logged as recruiting demand, and the category check is the first intake step after consent, before the ZIP, own-or-rent and name-and-address questions (D-242). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:330`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:50`.
- **INT-017** When no licensed business in the town covers the job, the homeowner is told right away and the request is logged as recruiting demand. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:126`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:648`.
- **INT-018** Intake stores the candidate job types, the answers to intake questions, and the rental, major, urgent and language flags for each request. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:108`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:304`.
- **INT-019** A job waiting in Intake with no homeowner reply gets one reminder at 24 hours and is closed at 72 hours with a text saying "Text us any time to restart"; while consent is still pending the 24-hour reminder is the consent text itself (CON-027) and the close sends nothing (D-095, D-127). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:111`.
- **INT-020** A homeowner message that shows business intent (for example "get on your list") is detected by the widened intent phrase list plus an AI second opinion on scrubbed text, run only on a message that would otherwise open a new request, and is answered with the business sign-up link, also when it is the number's first message (CON-001, D-132). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:92`.
- **INT-021** While the AI is down, the homeowner gets "we have your message" once and the system retries the reading every 30 minutes. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:192`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:200`.
- **INT-022** A request outside the service area (a towns list in settings, starting with Lake Havasu City), for a trade that is not open, or that duplicates an open job moves from Intake to Closed early (D-086). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:162`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:170`.
- **INT-023** Every new request hears back within 24 hours, by text, email or a call-back alert to Casey, and a call-back for a homeowner who can't be reached by text or email is due within 24 hours for a job marked "Today, it's urgent" or emergency and within 4 days (a setting) otherwise; a request with an emergency keyword counts as urgent before INT-030 is answered, and an urgent call-back goes in Casey's OPS-032 push (D-226, D-277). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Ok fix everything we need to." on fix round 8 (`docs/decisions.md:363`, `docs/decisions.md:372`), Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:314`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:538`.
- **INT-024** An emergency is never a reason to close a request early; the request continues once the homeowner replies that they are safe (D-055). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:162`.
- **INT-025** The safety text ends "Reply SAFE when you are safe."; safe, seguro, segura and "we're safe", "we're ok" or "we're fine" count as the safe reply, and so does a plain OK or Yes once consent is recorded, and when one reply records consent while the safety question is still open, one direct reply asks "Are you safe now? Reply SAFE." (D-128). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`.
- **INT-026** Where AI is allowed (AI-008), the AI emergency flag also runs on the scrubbed first message before consent, and a flag sends the safety text within 5 minutes as a keyword match would (D-137). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:58`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-027** The homeowner's first name and street address are asked as the last intake question, after consent and the area check (for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, right after the job cards go out, INT-029), the question says "Only the business you pick gets this, after you pick it. We never ask about immigration status or ID.", and that answer is stored only in the vault and never sent to the AI (D-146, D-164, D-238). - **INT-028** When the AI rental reading is missing or unsure, or no AI is used, intake asks "Do you own or rent this place?" with the answers own, rent, "own the home, rent the lot" and "second or winter home" (the last two counting as own), before the job opens, or, for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, right after the job cards go out (D-155, D-164, D-246). - **INT-029** For a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, the job cards go out once consent, the safe reply, the urgent choice, the ZIP and the area check are done; the own-or-rent and name-and-address questions follow the cards; if the picked business confirms before the address is in, the homeowner is asked for it again at once and nothing is released until it arrives; and a later RENT answer sends "Landlord approval may be needed." to every business that got the card (D-164) Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:87`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`.
- **INT-030** Every request asks "How soon do you need this? 1) Today, it's urgent 2) In the next few days 3) No rush 4) By a certain day" right after the category check, stores the answer as the urgent flag and a job-card field (CARD-007), an answer of 1 leads to the INT-006 emergency-or-regular choice, and an answer of 4 asks which day and stores "Needed by <day>" as a job-card field, the job otherwise being handled as answer 2 (D-208, D-292). - **INT-031** A message naming an air conditioner, AC, cooler or swamp cooler together with a heat word (hot, warm, no AC, not cooling, not cold, stopped, died, won't turn on, broke, or a temperature of 85 or more, and their Spanish words), or an answer of 1 to INT-030 on an AC or cooler job, gets the heat-safety text within 5 minutes at any hour, before the consent text on a new number (CON-001), once per job, from an English and a Spanish template: "Ask Hava: No AC in this heat can be dangerous. If anyone is confused, very weak or fainting, call 911 now. Go somewhere with AC if you can; fans don't cool enough in extreme heat. Drink water. Ask Hava is not an emergency service."; it names no hotline or cooling site, never holds the request, and is followed by the INT-006 choice (D-208, D-274). - **INT-032** A first message that names two trades starts a job for the first and replies "Ask Hava: That's 2 jobs. We'll start with <first>. Reply ALSO to start <second> too.", and a reply of ALSO (or TAMBIÉN) starts the second job (D-246). - **INT-033** Between 8 p.m. and 8 a.m. Arizona time, before the INT-006 choice, the homeowner gets "Ask Hava: <N> <trade> businesses on Ask Hava take after-hours calls.", N being the active, unpaused businesses in that trade that ticked after-hours emergencies (BIZ-007); when N is 0 the choice is not offered, the job continues as regular and the text is "Ask Hava: No <trade> business on Ask Hava takes after-hours calls yet. Your request goes to businesses at 8 a.m. If anyone feels unwell or it isn't safe, call 911."; and a job chosen as an emergency at night with no quote 60 minutes (a setting) after its first card was sent gets one text, "Ask Hava: No after-hours business has quoted yet. Your request also goes to every <trade> business at 8 a.m. If anyone feels unwell or it isn't safe, call 911.", and its cards held by quiet hours go to every other eligible business at 8 a.m. (D-271). - **INT-034** For an electrical match (spark, burning, smoke, buzz, melt, sizzl, chispa, quema, humo, zumb, derret, or "hot" with an outlet, plug, cord, switch, breaker, panel, wire or meter word) the safety text adds "If you see smoke or flames, get everyone out and call 911 from outside. Don't touch a sparking or hot outlet, cord or panel; stop using it and call a licensed electrician.", for a water match (flood, inund) it adds "Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords or appliances. Don't stand in water to reach the breaker panel. If you can do it safely, shut off the main water valve.", both from English and Spanish templates marked for the same safety desk check as the heat-safety text, and every safety text keeps the INT-004 gas line (D-273).
