# Spec: Intake and conversation

Prefix: `INT` · Modules: `intake`, `messaging` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **INT-001** A homeowner can start a request either by texting the Ask Hava number or by web chat on askhava.com. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:43`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:177`.
- **INT-002** A plain emergency keyword list, matched by word stem, runs on every inbound message before any AI call or other handling, after street-address patterns are removed and never on the answer to the name-and-address question (so "Smoketree Ave" is not "smoke"), and contains at least smell, burning, alarm, smoke, spark, flood, dizzy, head hurts, buzz, melt, sizzl, propane, carbon monoxide, "co alarm" and the Spanish olor, quema, humo, chispa, inund, mareo, zumb and derret, plus "hot" and "caliente" only in a message that also names an outlet, plug, cord, switch, breaker, panel, wire or meter (or their Spanish words) (D-137, D-165). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:607`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:130`.
- **INT-003** Any emergency keyword match sends the safety text within 5 minutes, whatever the AI returns. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`.
- **INT-004** The safety text says why it was sent (for example "a rotten-egg smell can mean a gas leak") and contains "If you smell gas, leave now and call 911 and your gas company from outside. Ask Hava is not an emergency service." Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:196`.
- **INT-005** An emergency request never produces a job card before the homeowner replies that they are safe. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-006** After the homeowner replies that they are safe, Ask Hava asks whether the repair is urgent, says after-hours work usually costs more, and lets them choose emergency or regular. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-007** A message of one or two words never opens a new job (for example "ME", a bare ZIP code or "ok sure"), except a message with an emergency keyword, which opens a job whatever its length (D-138). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:214`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-008** A cancel-like message never opens a new job. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:112`.
- **INT-009** At most one active job exists per homeowner, property and trade. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:98`.
- **INT-010** A second message about the same thing within 2 hours joins the existing job even if read as a different trade; after 2 hours, a same-trade message joins only if its description mostly matches, otherwise it opens a new job. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:98`.
- **INT-011** An answer to an intake question counts only for a job that is still open, and is matched exactly (so "ok" inside "broke" is not an answer). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:214`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:626`.
- **INT-012** Word answers to intake questions are accepted (for example "the AC one", "Yes!", a first name, or "my email is ..."). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:122`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:646`.
- **INT-013** When the trade is in doubt, the homeowner is asked a numbered trade question such as "Is this mainly 1) plumbing or 2) heating/AC?", and the candidate job types follow the chosen trade. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:120`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:644`.
- **INT-014** When the candidate job types fall under different licenses, the homeowner is asked "Which fits best?" with numbered options. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:121`.
- **INT-015** An intake question that gets no usable answer is asked once more and then a fallback is applied, so intake never freezes. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:644`.
- **INT-016** A request that fits no open category gets an immediate reply and is logged as recruiting demand. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:50`.
- **INT-017** When no licensed business in the town covers the job, the homeowner is told right away and the request is logged as recruiting demand. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:126`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:648`.
- **INT-018** Intake stores the candidate job types, the answers to intake questions, and the rental, major, urgent and language flags for each request. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:108`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:304`.
- **INT-019** A job waiting in Intake with no homeowner reply gets one reminder at 24 hours and is closed at 72 hours with a text saying "Text us any time to restart"; while consent is still pending the 24-hour reminder is the consent text itself (CON-027) and the close sends nothing (D-095, D-127). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:111`.
- **INT-020** A homeowner message that shows business intent (for example "get on your list") is detected by the widened intent phrase list plus an AI second opinion on scrubbed text, run only on a message that would otherwise open a new request, and is answered with the business sign-up link, also when it is the number's first message (CON-001, D-132). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:92`.
- **INT-021** While the AI is down, the homeowner gets "we have your message" once and the system retries the reading every 30 minutes. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:192`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:200`.
- **INT-022** A request outside the service area (a towns list in settings, starting with Lake Havasu City), for a trade that is not open, or that duplicates an open job moves from Intake to Closed early (D-086). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:162`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:170`.
- **INT-023** Every new request hears back within 24 hours, by text, email or a call-back alert to Casey. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:538`.
- **INT-024** An emergency is never a reason to close a request early; the request continues once the homeowner replies that they are safe (D-055). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:162`.
- **INT-025** The safety text ends "Reply SAFE when you are safe."; safe, seguro, segura and "we're safe", "we're ok" or "we're fine" count as the safe reply, and so does a plain OK or Yes once consent is recorded, and when one reply records consent while the safety question is still open, one direct reply asks "Are you safe now? Reply SAFE." (D-128). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:515`.
- **INT-026** Where AI is allowed (AI-008), the AI emergency flag also runs on the scrubbed first message before consent, and a flag sends the safety text within 5 minutes as a keyword match would (D-137). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:290`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:58`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`.
- **INT-027** The homeowner's first name and street address are asked as the last intake question, after consent and the area check (for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, right after the job cards go out, INT-029), and that answer is stored only in the vault and never sent to the AI (D-146, D-164). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:291`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:58`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:133`.
- **INT-028** When the AI rental reading is missing or unsure, or no AI is used, intake asks "Do you own or rent this place?" before the job opens, or, for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, right after the job cards go out (D-155, D-164). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:89`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:614`.
- **INT-029** For a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, the job cards go out once consent, the safe reply, the urgent choice, the ZIP and the area check are done; the own-or-rent and name-and-address questions follow the cards; if the picked business confirms before the address is in, the homeowner is asked for it again at once and nothing is released until it arrives; and a later RENT answer sends "Landlord approval may be needed." to every business that got the card (D-164) Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:86`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:87`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`.
