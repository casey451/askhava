# Spec: Privacy, the contact vault and retention

Prefix: `PRIV` · Modules: `privacy` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **PRIV-001** Homeowner names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, on-site contacts and original photos are stored only in the contact vault, and every other record refers to them by ID. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:81`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:85`.
- **PRIV-002** Vault fields are encrypted field by field with AES-GCM under a key kept separate from the database credentials. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:348`.
- **PRIV-003** Every vault read is audit-logged. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:349`.
- **PRIV-004** Only the messaging and release code can read the vault. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:85`.
- **PRIV-005** No homeowner contact detail leaves the vault except to the business on a confirmed, paid pick (or a recorded free win). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`.
- **PRIV-006** Job cards, logs, dashboards and AI prompts never contain vault fields. Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:81`.
- **PRIV-007** Homeowner contact details are purged 180 days after the job closes, unless a dispute or complaint is open, except the phone number, which stays encrypted with its consent and opt-out records for the consent-log period and is used only for them (D-013, D-079). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:354`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:356`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:183`.
- **PRIV-008** Job text is kept with contact details removed, for 3 years (D-013). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:355`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:437`.
- **PRIV-009** Photos are deleted 30 days after the job closes (D-013). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:358`.
- **PRIV-010** Ledger and payment records, and the audit log, are kept 7 years. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:357`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:359`.
- **PRIV-011** "Delete my data" removes the vault entry and photos and replaces names with "deleted", while ledger and tax records stay without personal details, and the delete confirmation says "We keep your phone number, locked, for 5 years only as proof of your texting permission and opt-out." (D-214). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:302`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:443`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:385`.
- **PRIV-012** The app never collects SSNs, driver's licenses or tax ID images, and uses EIN or ROC number instead. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:223`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:361`.
- **PRIV-013** Photos are optional, asked for as INT-044 says, with size and type limits checked from the file bytes rather than the name (D-432). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:586`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:338`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-014** Every photo is re-encoded at upload with all metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC and maker notes) stripped before it is stored, screened by any check, sent to any AI step or placed on any card, the uploaded bytes with metadata are never stored (the vault copy, PRIV-001, is the stripped one), and photos are stored privately and served only by the app's own photo route through short-lived signed links, never a direct storage link (D-383, D-454). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Go ahead and fix everything that needs to be fixed." on fix round 12 (`docs/decisions.md:499`, `docs/decisions.md:513`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:339`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-015** The photo request and the upload page say "Please leave people, house numbers and license plates out of photos." (D-432). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:586`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-016** Logs carry request IDs only and never phone numbers. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:373`.
- **PRIV-017** Sentry runs with send_default_pii=False plus a scrubber. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:372`.
- **PRIV-018** Backups are encrypted. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:349`.
- **PRIV-019** Code, tests, fixtures, logs and screenshots use fake data only, and the PII scanner fails the build on real-looking personal data. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:263`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:298`.
- **PRIV-020** The homeowner's phone number is never shared, and the homeowner's first name and address go only to the business they pick. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:191`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`.
- **PRIV-021** A homeowner can review and change their data (properties, language, texts on or off) in the homeowner portal. - **PRIV-023** Every photo is encrypted by the app with AES-GCM under the vault key, with associated data naming its job and photo ID, before it is written to object storage, so the storage bucket never holds a readable photo (D-454).
