# Spec: Privacy, the contact vault and retention

Prefix: `PRIV` · Modules: `privacy` · Format and checks: [index.md](index.md)

- **PRIV-001** Homeowner names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, on-site contacts and original photos are stored only in the contact vault, and every other record refers to them by ID; a card photo (CARD-014) is a separate, screened copy, kept and encrypted by `privacy` like the vault but not a contact field (D-477, D-478). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:81`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:85`.
- **PRIV-002** Vault fields are encrypted field by field with AES-GCM under a key kept separate from the database credentials. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:348`.
- **PRIV-003** Every vault read is audit-logged. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:349`.
- **PRIV-004** Plaintext vault reads go only through `privacy.api`, each audit-logged with its purpose, by the readers D-093 lists (`messaging`, `picks` for the release, `calls`, the `portals` homeowner and owner realms, and `privacy` itself); `quotes` reads only card photos (CARD-014) for the card email and the business job page, never a contact field; and the owner realm reads, besides the contact on a call-back alert, a help request's own conversation on its page (OPS-036) (D-093, D-348, D-477, D-478). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:85`.
- **PRIV-005** No homeowner contact detail leaves the vault except to the business on a confirmed, paid pick (or one with a recorded $0 fee decision: a free first win, a repeat customer under FEE-013 or a fee-free job type under FEE-045, D-477). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`.
- **PRIV-006** Job cards, logs, dashboards and AI prompts never contain contact fields or original photos; a job card may carry card photos (CARD-013, CARD-014), the screened copies `quotes` reads through `privacy.api` (PRIV-004) (D-477, D-478). Source: `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:81`.
- **PRIV-007** Homeowner contact details are purged 180 days after the job closes, unless a dispute or complaint is open, except the phone number, which stays encrypted with its consent and opt-out records for the consent-log period and is used only for them (D-013, D-079). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:354`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:356`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:183`.
- **PRIV-008** Job text is kept with contact details removed, for 3 years (D-013). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:355`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:437`.
- **PRIV-009** Photos are deleted 30 days after the job closes (D-013). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:358`.
- **PRIV-010** Ledger and payment records, and the audit log, are kept 7 years. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:357`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:359`.
- **PRIV-011** "Delete my data" removes the vault entry and photos and replaces names with "deleted", while ledger and tax records stay without personal details, and the delete confirmation says "We keep your phone number, locked, for 5 years only as proof of your texting permission and opt-out." (D-214). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "do whatever research you need to to make the most ideal changes" on the three-angle fix plan (`docs/decisions.md:294`, `docs/decisions.md:302`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:443`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:385`.
- **PRIV-012** The app never collects SSNs, driver's licenses or tax ID images, and uses EIN or ROC number instead. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:223`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:361`.
- **PRIV-013** Photos are optional, asked for as INT-044 says, with size and type limits checked from the file bytes rather than the name (D-432). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:586`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:338`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-014** Every photo is re-encoded at upload with all metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC and maker notes) stripped before it is stored, screened by any check, sent to any AI step or placed on any card, the uploaded bytes with metadata are never stored (the vault copy, PRIV-001, is the stripped one), and photos are stored privately and served only by the app's own photo route through short-lived signed links, never a direct storage link (D-383, D-454). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "Go ahead and fix everything that needs to be fixed." on fix round 12 (`docs/decisions.md:499`, `docs/decisions.md:513`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:339`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-015** The photo request and the upload page say "Please leave people, house numbers and license plates out of photos." (D-432). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:586`), `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:222`.
- **PRIV-016** Logs carry request IDs only and never phone numbers. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:373`.
- **PRIV-017** Sentry runs with send_default_pii=False plus a scrubber. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:372`.
- **PRIV-018** Backups are encrypted. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:349`.
- **PRIV-019** Code, tests, fixtures, logs and screenshots use fake data only: phones in the fictional 555-0100 to 555-0199 block, email addresses at example.com, example.org or the reserved `.test`, `.example` and `.invalid` domains, names from the fixture generator's fake list and streets such as "1 Test Way"; `scripts/pii_scan.py` fails the build on a phone, email address, street address, labelled name or key-shaped string outside those forms, on an image under `tests/` or `docs/design/` carrying EXIF GPS or camera-device metadata, and on any file it could not scan unless that file is on its allow-list (D-485). Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:263`, `BUILD_BRIEF.md:298`.
- **PRIV-020** The homeowner's phone number is never shared, and the homeowner's first name and address go only to the business they pick. Source: `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:191`, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:225`.
- **PRIV-021** A homeowner can review and change their data (properties, language, texts on or off) in the homeowner portal. Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "as well packaged as possible" and "Do everything that you recommend for all the following questions." on fix round 14 (`docs/decisions.md:581`, `docs/decisions.md:594`), `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:385`, `sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:218`.
- **PRIV-022** Privacy-module code keeps at least 95% branch coverage. Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:481`.
- **PRIV-023** Every photo is encrypted by the app with AES-GCM under the vault key, with associated data naming its job and photo ID, before it is written to object storage, so the storage bucket never holds a readable photo (D-454). Source: Casey Oct 28, 2026: "all data we keep will be encrypted" (`docs/decisions.md:611`, `docs/decisions.md:613`), `BUILD_BRIEF.md:348`.
- **PRIV-024** A key inventory in the runbook lists every secret (`VAULT_KEY`, `VAULT_INDEX_KEY`, `ID_SECRET`, the photo-link key, the export key, vendor keys) with its owner, storage, offline escrow (sealed on paper, apart from the password manager) and rotation trigger; vault encryption is AES-256-GCM with a random 96-bit nonce from the OS source and the key version in the associated data; blind-index keys are derived per purpose (`homeowner-phone|v1`, `sender-dir|v1`, `email|v1`); the nightly export is encrypted to an offline recipient key whose private half never sits on the host; and a rotation drill re-encrypts the vault and rebuilds the blind index from v1 to v2 with both versions readable during the move (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:156`.
- **PRIV-025** Photo decoding runs with explicit limits (8,000 px per side, 40 megapixels, 256 MiB) behind a decode semaphore and a timed `spawn_blocking`, with a per-route body limit; only the primary frame is kept and orientation is applied before metadata is stripped; HEIC is not decoded in the app at launch (a HEIC upload gets a plain reply asking for a JPEG or PNG, which phones send by default), so no native-code decoder runs in the process that holds the keys (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:168`.
- **PRIV-026** Inbound email (business replies, help emails, email opt-outs) reaches the app only through the email provider's signed inbound webhook into `privacy::api::read_inbound_email()`, its raw body is stored only in `privacy.message_body` under the TXT-020 cut, the mailbox itself keeps nothing beyond 7 days, SPF, DKIM and DMARC results are recorded with each message, a sender that fails them may only opt out and never acts on or changes data, and a per-purpose HMAC email blind index matches senders to records (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:174`.
- **PRIV-027** Homeowner data leaves the vault only by the listed readers: the Stripe dispute evidence pack carries IDs and timestamps and never contact fields or help-message text; card emails carry no image bytes, only a link to the signed-in job page; Twilio message bodies are deleted through its API 31 days after receipt; and Cloudflare and every other processor that sees plaintext in transit is named in the privacy policy's processor list (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
- **PRIV-028** Contact fields deserialize straight into `Redacted<String>`, request tracing logs only the matched route template and never the URI, photo and action links carry their token in the path and never the query string, audit rows hold record IDs and never contact values (a homeowner's portal edit logs the field name only), and `intake.property` holds only the vault contact ID and the town, with a schema test (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
- **PRIV-029** The app's object-storage token is write-only and prefix-scoped, the bucket has versioning and object lock for at least the export window, the audit stream carries a hash chain verified nightly, the request log is kept 30 days, and `ai.reading` and the off-database AI stream hold enumerated fields only, with every AI-written text stored where PRIV-008 and TXT-020 retention applies (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
- **PRIV-030** A HELP notification to Casey and the CON-016 digest lines pass the AI-002 scrubber and then the CARD-008 gate before sending, so no email address, street address or phone number reaches the alert mailbox (D-480). Source: `BUILD_BRIEF.md:293`, `docs/audit/prebuild-audit-2026-10-28.md:183`.
