# Ask Hava Pilot Privacy Notice, version <date>

*For homeowners during the hand-run test (D-470). Link it from the top of the request form. It describes only what the test actually does; the full privacy policy (`docs/legal/privacy-policy.md`) replaces it when the real service launches. Replace `<date>`, `<postal address>` and `<help email>` before publishing.*

---

**What we collect during this pilot.** What you type into our request form: what you need done, the kind of work, how soon, the town, your first name, the job address and your email, and how you heard about us. Any photos you email us. The quotes and emails needed to run your request.

**What businesses see before you pick one.** Local businesses that do that kind of work get a short description of the job, without your name, street number or email.

**What the business you pick gets.** After you pick a business and say yes, we send that one business your first name, the job address and your email, so it can contact you about this job. During this pilot we never ask for or share your phone number. If the pick happens in a paid week, the business gets your details only after it pays Ask Hava its introduction fee.

**Who handles your information.** We use Google (Forms, Sheets and Gmail) to receive and keep your request, and Stripe to take businesses' fees. Stripe never gets your details. We don't use AI tools on your name, address, email or photos, and we don't sell or rent anyone's information.

**How long we keep it.** We delete your name, address and email 90 days after the pilot ends, unless we need them longer for a payment, refund, dispute or legal reason. We keep the rest without anything that identifies you.

**Security.** Only the owner can see the records. The accounts use two-step sign-in, and nothing is shared. No method of storage or sending is completely secure. We look into any suspected unauthorized access, and we tell affected people and the authorities when the law requires it.

**Your choices.** Email `<help email>` to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop at any time.

**Don't send sensitive information.** Please don't put bank or card numbers, ID numbers or passwords in your request.

**Ask Hava is not a contractor.** You agree with the business you pick directly, and Ask Hava isn't part of that agreement.
