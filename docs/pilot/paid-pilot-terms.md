# Ask Hava Paid Pilot Terms, version <date>

*For businesses during the hand-run test (D-470). Send this to every signed-up business before the first paid week. A business must accept it on the acceptance form before it gets a job card in a paid week. Replace `<date>`, `<postal address>` and `<help email>` before sending. Keep each accepted version.*

---

**Ask Hava is not a contractor.** Ask Hava is an introduction service run by Ask Hava LLC in Lake Havasu City, Arizona. It sends you anonymous job cards from homeowners who asked for quotes. You decide whether to quote and what to charge. If a homeowner picks you, you agree with the homeowner directly. Ask Hava doesn't prepare your quote, set your price, negotiate your scope, schedule or supervise your work, pick materials, get permits, direct your workers, take payment for your work, or judge whether your work is good. Ask Hava is not your employer, partner, agent, subcontractor or guarantor.

**What's free.** Getting job cards and sending quotes is free.

**The introduction fee.** From `<date>`, if a homeowner picks your quote, you pay a fixed introduction fee before Ask Hava gives you her details. The fee pays Ask Hava for introducing you to the homeowner who chose you. It is not a share of your price, not payment for your work, and not a sale of data or a license to a list. It depends only on the size of your quote:
- $25 when your quote is under $500 (and for a visit-fee quote);
- $75 from $500 up to $1,500;
- $199 over $1,500.

A range counts at its middle. Ongoing service counts as 4 months of the monthly price, and its fee is never more than $75. The job card shows the fee before you quote. Any tax Ask Hava owes on the fee is included in it; nothing is added on top.

**What you get for it.** Once you pay, Ask Hava emails you the homeowner's first name, the job address and her email, so you can contact her about this job. Ask Hava never shares her phone number. Use her details only for this job and follow-up about it: no marketing lists, no resale, no sharing.

**What Ask Hava doesn't promise.** The homeowner may not answer, may not hire you, or may change her mind. The fee doesn't depend on whether the work is done, finished or paid for. Ask Hava makes no promise about how many job cards you'll get or how often they turn into work.

**Refunds.** Ask Hava refunds the fee in full if you email the homeowner twice, at least 3 hours apart, within 72 hours, and she never replies; tell us within 4 days of paying. Ask Hava may also refund any charge at its own choice. No refund is owed because the work wasn't done or paid for.

**If you don't pay.** If you don't pay within 4 business hours of being picked, the pick may go to the homeowner's next choice. Nothing is owed.

**Your license.** When you quote, you confirm you hold every Arizona license the work needs, that it's active and covers the job, and that you include your ROC number on your bids and estimates where Arizona law requires it. Ask Hava may stop sending you cards if it can't confirm a license the job needs. Handyman work without a contractor license is only for jobs Arizona's handyman exemption allows: under $1,000 in total, with no permit. A project is never split up to fit under the limit.

**No ranking, no paid placement.** Every eligible quote goes to the homeowner in the order it came in. Nobody can pay to be shown first.

**Stopping.** You can stop getting job cards at any time by replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to any job card, or emailing `<help email>`. Ask Hava can stop sending you cards at any time.

**Liability.** To the extent the law allows, Ask Hava's total liability to you is limited to the introduction fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim. Arizona law governs these terms.

**Changes.** A new version of these terms applies only after you accept it.
