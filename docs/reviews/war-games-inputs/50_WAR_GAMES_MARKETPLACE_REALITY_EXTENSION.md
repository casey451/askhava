## War-game scorecard and rollout gates

Use the same market-pocket lens as WG-03 (Phoenix, Scottsdale, Goodyear and other Phoenix-area cities, with Lake Havasu City as an explicit comparison). Keep trade, job type, service radius, homeowner persona, provider supply and actual lawful jurisdiction independently configurable. Separate **simulation observations** from claims about real markets. Never invent population, contractor, income or conversion facts; use sourced data for external assumptions or label synthetic distributions.

For every run, capture: total *eligible* invitees, actually delivered cards, providers opening, valid quotes, **genuinely comparable** quote count, first-valid-quote latency, homeowner selection, business confirmation, authorized payment, authorized contact release, completed scheduling (only if that branch exists), cancellations/no-shows, disputes, customer re-contact, contractor repeat paid participation, and precise operator minutes. Also measure all-in cost by tier ($25/$75/$199 tax-included), segment-level contribution margin, free-job leakage, adverse/false-positive fraud actions, missing communications, customer clarity/accessibility and founder hands-on minutes. Report both totals and per-market distributions; report confidence intervals when meaningful and never treat agent-created demand as forecast proof.

**Pass/fail rules:**

1. **Invariant failures = FAIL even once**: unauthorized contact release, incorrect charge or waiver, forbidden production send, privacy leak, misleading synthetic quote, unapproved fee change, lost paid-ledger fact, false claimed appointment, or tampered results.
2. **Service-quality findings = score, not invented pass**: quote coverage, response delay, quote comparability, selection rate, support minutes, worker participation, provider dropout and unit economics. Record observed values by persona/market and recommend a target only after baseline evidence; no made-up guaranteed speed or coverage.
3. **Feature-not-built = NOT_APPLICABLE / CONDITIONAL**, never marked `PASS`: calendar/OAuth appointment booking, public review integrations and future price benchmarks do not become MVP requirements just because they appear here.
4. **Decision disputes = OPEN**, never 'solved by AI': business fee exceptions, refunds/replacement after paid release, free first jobs WG-01, pricing incentives, scope splitting, insurance/guarantee responsibility or calendar booking terms require existing approved policy or founder/legal signoff. The simulation can recommend alternatives and show tradeoffs, not choose them.
5. **Budget and quality gates:** stop at existing D-1154 AI budget ceiling; log spend, model versions, seeds, mocks and test environment. Show unknowns, actual result artifacts and independent replay. Every failure becomes a referenced existing task or a draft task **through approved repo governance**; do not invent assigned task IDs.
