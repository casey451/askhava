# Accessibility plan and the Gate B VoiceOver script

T115 (A11Y-019, A11Y-020, A11Y-022; D-317, D-379, D-3155, D-4240). The statement itself is
[`docs/legal/accessibility-statement.md`](legal/accessibility-statement.md), served at
`GET /accessibility`; this file only links it.

## 1. The plan

- **Target.** WCAG 2.2 Level AA on every page (A11Y-001). Any axe-core violation at 320, 768 or
  1280 px fails `Ally` (`tests/a11y/`); every GET route has a state in `tests/a11y/page_states.ts`.
- **What machines check.** Landmarks, one `h1`, the skip link and `lang`; targets and contrast;
  reflow and text spacing; keyboard journeys J1 and J2; the design tokens.
- **What people check.** Gate B: Casey runs the VoiceOver script below on an iPhone in Safari before
  launch, and again after any change to a flow it covers (`docs/brains/accessibility-forms.md`
  section 9, last box).
- **Emails (A11Y-019).** Real text, never an image; every email has a plain-text part; link text
  names the action and the job ("Confirm job 1042"), never "click here".
  `tests/docs/test_email_accessibility.py` checks every email literal and email source.
- **Statement (A11Y-020).** The conformance target, known limits, and contact by text, email or the
  help page's form, with the relay line and the usual 2-day reply; no phone line while the number
  is text-only (CALL-020). `tests/docs/test_accessibility_statement.py` scans the document and
  the page's templates against D-4240's closed lists; `tests/a11y/statement.spec.ts` checks the
  served page holds every line of the document.
- **Known limits** are kept in the statement, not here, so there is one copy.

## 2. Gate B VoiceOver script (A11Y-022)

Set up: an iPhone with VoiceOver on (Settings, Accessibility, VoiceOver), Safari, the staging site,
fake details only. Use swipe right to move, double-tap to act, and the rotor (two-finger turn) for
headings, links and form controls. Write down each step that fails, with the words VoiceOver said.

### Homeowner flow

1. Open the home page. VoiceOver reads the page title, then "Skip to main content" first.
   Double-tap it: focus lands on the main region.
2. Rotor to headings: there is one heading level 1, and the order makes sense.
3. Open the request page. Each question is read with its label and hint before the field.
4. Submit with a field left empty. The title starts with "Error:", focus moves to the error
   summary, and each error link moves to its field.
5. Finish the request. The status message is read without moving focus.
6. Open the quote page link from the fake text. Each quote card is read in order: business name,
   price, what it covers.
7. Choose Pick on one quote. The review step reads the fee line and "Go back" before the final
   button.
8. Enter the code from the fake text. The field accepts paste and autofill, and a wrong code is
   read as an error with what to do.
9. Choose how the business may reach you, then confirm. The result is read as a status message.
10. Turn the phone to landscape and back: nothing is cut off and focus stays put.

### Business card-entry step

1. Open the fake pick email for a business and follow its Confirm link.
2. The Confirm page reads the job, the introduction fee by job size, the refund rule and "Go back"
   as plain text before the card form.
3. Move into Stripe's embedded card form. Each field (card number, expiry, CVC, ZIP) is read with
   its own label. Enter the Stripe test card; a wrong number is read as an error.
4. Move out of the card form with a swipe: focus returns to the page, not to the top.
5. Confirm. The result is read once, and the page says what happens next.

### Pick email: confirm or hold

1. Open the fake pick email in the Mail app. VoiceOver reads it as text, not as an image.
2. Each link names its action and the job ("Confirm job 1042", "Hold job 1042").
3. Follow Confirm: the page says what it will do before it does it, and the Confirm window shows as
   an end time, not a countdown (D-317).
4. Go back to the email and follow Hold: the single Hold acts at once and the page says it is held.
5. Open the plain-text version (Mail, View, Message, Plain Alternative on a Mac, or a client that
   shows it): every link and fact is there in words.
