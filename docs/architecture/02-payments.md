# Architecture section 2 notes: `payments`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `payments`, in their original order.

- A $0 Confirm after a PaymentIntent was opened, and a refused paid event (T754, D-4763, D-5158, D-5171, D-5133; FEE-010, FEE-013, FEE-016, FEE-021, PICK-022, SEC-049): the press that records a $0 decision calls `payments::api::end_intent_unless_charging` on its own transaction before `press_move` commits it, with no Stripe call for the ended intent; when that answers false (a charge in flight) the whole press transaction, $0 row included, is rolled back and the press answers the attempt's state. `payments::api::no_fee_release` clears a pick whose intents have all ended with no attempt in `sent`, `processing`, `succeeded`, `needs_action` or `unreachable`; `held_release` and `from_stored_success` fall to it for such a pick (the paid path, or `PickEnded`, when it finds no $0 decision). The Stripe webhook's `release` raises one High alert per pick and reason through `ops::api::raise_alert_once` (subject `payments.pick_intent`, kinds `payments.success_refused_wrong_amount`, `_wrong_pick`, `_not_paid`, `_wrong_intent`) when `paid_release` refuses a verified success that names the pick's own stored PaymentIntent; a success naming another intent, or a pick with none, is only info-logged.
- Pilot scorecard money, pick and quote inputs (T477, D-3920 to D-3922; OPS-039, OPS-047, PICK-052, FEE-046): read-only readers in `payments::scorecard_inputs`, `picks::scorecard_inputs` and `quotes::scorecard_inputs`, each taking job IDs and a window; `quotes::api::jobs_by_trade(to)` gives every job carded before `to` per trade (first sent card's category), request-level quote inputs are windowed on the job's first card and count quotes received before `to`, and `picks` takes its qualifying job IDs from `quotes` readers. PICK-052's returned notes are `quotes.note_returned` audit rows (`quotes::api::record_note_returned`, field = the reason's code name, never the note), read back through `ops::api::audit_subjects` (each row's subject, field code name and time, D-4037). FEE-046's set-aside rate is `ASKHAVA_SALES_TAX_SET_ASIDE_BASIS_POINTS` (default 860), passed to `LedgerTotals::net_per_introduction_cents`.
