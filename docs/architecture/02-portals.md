# Architecture section 2 notes: `portals`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `portals`, in their original order.

- Security-event cap and webhook refusals (T319, D-2207, D-3445, D-3469 to D-3473): `portals::api::admit_security_event`, an in-process counter checked before any pool connection, lets at most N rows a minute per (kind, SEC-040 client address, route) and per (kind, route) across addresses reach `record_security_event`; the rest are counted and logged once per minute (count only, no address), the 403 unchanged; a minute's count is logged when the next event of a later minute arrives, with no timer (D-3472). N is `ASKHAVA_SECURITY_EVENTS_PER_MINUTE` (`core` settings), raised to `EVENTS_PER_HOUR_ALERT` (50, the default) by `configure_security_events`, which `server`'s `main` calls once at start-up with the `ClientIpPolicy`. `server::webhook_refusals::layer` is a route layer on the Stripe, Twilio text, Twilio voice and Twilio call-callback (`voice_status`, D-3473) routers only: each 403 they answer (a source or signature refusal, the only 403 they give) records one `webhook_signature_failed` event (route, request ID, no actor) under the same cap.
- Confirm's $0 decision and release (T494, D-3824; FEE-010, FEE-012, PICK-022): `portals::confirm_waiver` is the one place the Confirm route judges a free first win: `join_dates` builds `payments::api::JoinDates` from `businesses::api::join_facts`, `catalog::api::trades_opened` (a new `portals` to `catalog` call) and the launch-date setting the caller passes (no sign-up row, or no launch setting, is `Timestamp::MAX`: no win); `decide_at_confirm` runs `waive_at_confirm` on the caller's Confirm transaction; after it commits, `release_no_fee` takes `no_fee_release` for the pick's own business and job and hands it to the `payments::api::ReleaseSink` (`server`'s `PickReleaser`, which releases once).
- Pilot scorecard panels (T622, D-4280, D-4639; OPS-046, OPS-047): `portals::scorecard_panels` returns view structs, never HTML, and computes no input itself: `email_panel` reads `messaging::api::email_days` (shown only inside the six-week window, alert open when the day before had deferrals above 5%, never calling `check_deferrals`), and `trade_figures` reads `quotes::api` (`jobs_by_trade`, `median_quote_minutes`, `quotes_per_business`), `picks::api::diagnostic_share`, `payments::api::refund_reasons` and `ops::api::not_a_fit_counts`; counts, code names and IDs only.
- Pilot scorecard page (T158, D-4702; OPS-039, CALL-024, OPS-046, OPS-047): `portals::scorecard::owner_scorecard_page` renders `templates/scorecard.html` from the scorecard readers of `quotes`, `picks`, `payments`, `jobs`, `intake`, `calls`, `businesses` and `ops` plus T621's judge and T622's panels, computing no input itself; per trade (job IDs from `quotes::api::jobs_by_trade`) and in total, for the Arizona week and the pilot to date; the serving route (T305) passes `ScorecardInputs` (lines, set-aside rate, pilot start, pushed trades). `owner_record_minutes` is the page's one write, through `ops::api::record_minutes`. A GET writes nothing and no color acts (D-396).
- Owner portal tools (T108, D-4282; OPS-003, FEE-007, LIC-002, OPS-001, OPS-035, CON-024, BIZ-027, SEC-043): `portals::owner_portal` builds the owner home (`/owner/home`) and settings (`/owner/settings`) pages and parses and applies their `POST`s (`/owner/pause`, `/owner/fee-mode`, `/owner/category`, `/owner/texting`, `/owner/banner`, `/owner/alert-ack`; fixed paths, IDs in the body, D-3890) through `ops::api::set_switch`, `payments::api::switch_fee_mode`, `catalog::api::set_category_enabled`, `messaging::api`'s mode controls, `ops::api::clear_banner` and the new `ops::api::acknowledge_pushed_alert`; the fee mode and every settings change need the 5-minute factor, a pause or acknowledgement does not. Reads: `ops::owner_alerts` (`pushed_alerts`, `alert_subject`, `waiting_alerts`, T779) and `businesses::review_queue` (`needs_review_signups`). `server::owner_portal` serves them behind the owner session (T310, D-4619).
- Owner refunds and audit log (T205, D-4481, D-4506; FEE-026, FEE-050, SEC-006): `portals::owner_portal` builds pick P's refund review step (`/owner/refunds/{pick}`) and its presses (`/owner/refunds/{pick}/refund`, `/owner/refunds/{pick}/evidence`) and the audit log viewer (`/owner/audit-log`). Calls: `payments::api::refund_state`, `disputes_of`, `request_refund` (`RefundTrigger::Owner`) and `record_pick_owner_submission`, never `list_disputes` or `record_owner_submission`; `ops::api::audit`, `audit_once` and `export_audit`. Both presses need the 5-minute factor. No route serves them until T304 (D-2128).
- Owner calls, opt-out and Stripe answer (T484, D-4581 to D-4584; OPS-028, OPS-035, CON-035, PICK-048, SEC-043): `portals::owner_portal` adds the bridge press (`/owner/call-homeowner`), the owner-recorded opt-out (`/owner/opt-out`, `messaging::api::suppress_logged` with `RevokeChannel::Owner` and one owner audit row per filing since T738, D-4727) and the Stripe-answer filing (`/owner/stripe-answer`, new `quotes::api::file_stripe_answer`, `latest_stripe_answer`); `portals` now calls `calls` (`OWNER_BRIDGE_KIND`). The press queues `calls.owner_bridge`; `server::register_owner_calls` registers it and `calls.owner_alert_call` (T204's) in the Twilio branch over the new `ASKHAVA_OWNER_PHONE` setting (required in test and live). New `calls::owner_calls` (`place_owner_alert_call`, say-only, keyed per alert; `bridge_owner`, her phone read in `calls`, then `ring_owner`; `alert_facts`) and `messaging::alert_contact` (`messaging.inbox_contact`'s contact ID for an inbox ID). `server::owner_portal` serves the three `POST`s (T310, D-4619).
- Owner release button (T540, D-4687, D-4688; PICK-022, PICK-023, SEC-006, TD-PICK-41): `portals::owner_release` builds `/owner/release` (job number and a one-time press ID in the body) behind `owner_power(OwnerPower::Release)`; it resolves the number through the new `jobs::api::job_by_number` and calls the new `picks::api::resend_release`, which reads the job's newest `picks.release` row and queues one `messaging.business_notice` (`release_notice` payload, dedupe key `messaging.business_notice.release_resend.<pick>.<press>`) and one `picks.release_resend` audit row in one transaction; it never calls `picks::api::release` and writes no release, charge or fee decision. `server::owner_portal` serves the `GET` and `POST`.
- Owner help page (T530, D-4733; OPS-036, SEC-005, TD-OPS-23): `portals::owner_help` builds and serves `/owner/help` (one request by `?request=`), `/owner/help/reply` and `/owner/help/close` behind `owner_principal`; `server` merges `owner_help_routes` with its session mint and the webhooks' `ID_SECRET`. Calls: `ops::api::open_help_requests`, `help_request`, `add_owner_reply`, `set_help_closed`; `privacy::api::problem_words` (owner proof), `scrub`, `keep_problem_words`; `quotes::api::final_gate`; `intake::api::may_text` and `rules::wanted_language`; `messaging::api::enqueue` (`template_id` `owner_reply`, outside the registry, `Relation::OpenRequest`, under quiet hours). A reply goes only by text to a homeowner `may_text` allows; any other asker is refused (email replies are T740's).
- Owner digest page (T815, D-5318, D-5319, D-5799; OPS-030, OPS-033, PRIV-006, SEC-005): `portals::owner_digest_page` serves `GET /owner/digest` (`OWNER_DIGEST_ROUTE`) behind `owner_principal` and `no-store`, rendering `digest_flags::owner_digest_view` under the session's owner proof (`owner_digest.html`, linked from the owner home); the owner's view then calls `ops::api::latest_digest` and `digest_opened`. `server` merges `owner_digest_routes` in `owner_pages` with its session mint, `recovery`'s restore actions and the flag numbers from `RouterParts::with_owner_flag_limits` (`Settings::owner_flag_limits`).
- Get help and the help form (T200, D-3155, D-4293, D-4372 to D-4375; BIZ-047, OPS-036, CON-035, PRIV-031, SEC-014): `portals::help` holds `GET_HELP_ROUTE` (`/b/help`) and `HELP_FORM_ROUTE` (`POST /help`), served by T309. `send_get_help` opens or joins the business's request through `intake::api::business_help` and queues the answer email with `messaging::api::queue_account_email`; `admit_help_form` counts through `intake::api::admit` (5 per IP /64 an hour, 200 a day); `send_help_form` calls `messaging::api::suppress` (`RevokeChannel::HelpForm`) for an opt-out with a number and opens the request through `intake::api::receive_help_email`. `business_layout.html` fills `base.html`'s `get_help` header block.
- Owner health page (T476, D-4624; OPS-008, OPS-009, SEC-002, SEC-036): `portals::health_page::owner_health_page` renders `/owner/health` (`OWNER_HEALTH_ROUTE`; T304 serves it) from T475's readers only (`messaging::api` text, email, sent-count and timing figures, `quotes::api::card_figures` and `spot_check_cards`, `payments::api::payment_errors`, `ai::api::ai_figures`; `portals` now depends on `ai`), plus a `HealthInputs` the route passes in: email limits, quote limit, `Settings::spot_check_every_days` (`ASKHAVA_SPOT_CHECK_EVERY_DAYS`, above 0, default 30, D-4625), web time since start, the `/healthz` worker state, one `SecretLine` (name, present, parsed, environment-matched; no value) per required secret and the four SEC-036 `AccountIds`. Windows end at `now`: last 7 days, Arizona day and month, and the last N days for the 10-card spot-check. Last backup reads "Not yet recorded" until T109.
- Intake report and money view pages (T434, D-4858; OPS-040, FEE-046, FEE-028): `portals::intake_report::intake_report_page` renders `/owner/intake-report` from `quotes::api::intake_report_ids` (new `quotes::intake_report`: the month's no-quote cards' jobs and the not-withdrawn quotes with `confirm_on_site` or `diagnostic_visit`, IDs only, D-3155), `jobs::api::report_facts` (new `jobs::report_facts`: each job's latest `jobs.job_facts` row by `seq`), `catalog::api::category` and `question_bank`, and `intake::api::golden_results` against the new setting `ASKHAVA_GOLDEN_TARGET_PERCENT` (default 90, `Settings::golden_target_percent`). `portals::money_view` renders `/owner/money`, takes the expense `POST /owner/money/expense` and the CSV `/owner/money/csv` from T210's `payments::api` only. Fixed paths, the month a `?month=YYYY-MM` value (D-3890); T305 serves them.
- PROBLEM counted as a complaint (T721, D-4423, D-5377, D-5378; JOB-016, BIZ-011, BIZ-012, BIZ-029, OPS-005, SEC-006): `portals::owner_complaint::count_problem_as_complaint` reads the PROBLEM alert (`ops::api::alert_subject`), its job and kept words (`privacy::api::problem_words`, owner session only), the business of the job's newest `paid`, `free` or `no_show` pick (`picks::api::settled_pick`, `picks::settled_pick`) and calls `businesses::api::count_problem` (`businesses::problem_complaint`), which locks the business row, answers `Already` when a `businesses.complaint` row exists for the business and job, else runs `record_complaint` as a savepoint and writes one `businesses.problem_counted` audit row by the owner. `problem_reports` lists the last 30 days' PROBLEM alerts (`ops::api::digest_alerts`, `jobs::api::job_number`, `businesses::api::counted_jobs`), no contact field. No new table; no homeowner text (T713).
- Owner PROBLEM reports page (T712, D-4423, D-5569; JOB-016, BIZ-012, SEC-006): `portals::owner_complaint::owner_problem_reports_page` renders `problem_reports`' rows (`owner_problem_reports.html`, linked from the owner home); `server::owner_portal` serves `GET /owner/problem-reports` and `POST /owner/problem-complaint` (alert ID and kind in the body, no fresh factor) behind the owner session, reading the session's owner proof through `portals::api::current_session` and `session_minter`; a press calls `count_problem_as_complaint` and answers 303 to the list.
- Weekly digest and owner flags (T524, D-3880, D-3882, D-4932, D-5318, D-5319, D-5704, D-5706; OPS-027, OPS-030, OPS-031, OPS-033, OPS-034, OPS-041, BIZ-054, CON-016): `portals::digest_flags` builds `weekly_digest` (each OPS-030 item as a count with job numbers or code details, read only through other modules' readers; complaints list each job number with `replied` or `no reply` from `businesses::api::for_casey`, and self-acknowledged alerts include text-delivery alerts, D-5706; unreadable business texts have no reader and are shown as not counted), `owner_flags` (crowded trades last Arizona month with quotes per win, trades over the NO-or-OTHER share in each of the last months, lapsed-pick businesses and repeat requesters' counts, from `core::api::OwnerFlagLimits`, the `ASKHAVA_FLAG_*` settings; no flag acts) and `owner_digest_view` (both, plus help requests due in 4 days and PROBLEM words only under an `OwnerSessionProof`; T815 serves it at `OWNER_DIGEST_ROUTE`). `server::digest_email` registers the daily `ops.weekly_digest` task (from `register_email_due`; with no email provider a run does nothing), first queued by `worker.rs`'s `schedule_checks` when email is ready; a run records a digest row and queues the email to `messaging::api::OWNER_INBOX` once 7 days passed, or re-sends an unopened one once after 4 days (`REMINDER_TEMPLATE`); restore actions are `recovery::api`'s.
- Notice and backup address changes (T542, D-3890, D-3892, D-5081, D-5298 to D-5302; SEC-038, SEC-014, BIZ-040, BIZ-043): new `portals::address_changes` (`request_address_change`, `confirm_address_change`, `remove_backup_address`, `address_change_page`, `templates/address_change.html`) calls `require_step_up` first, mints the typed address's mailbox with `privacy::api::mailbox_for` and calls `businesses::api::request_notice_change`, `confirm_mailbox`, `request_backup`, `confirm_backup`, `remove_backup` and `members` (new `portals` to `privacy` and `businesses` calls). New `server::address_changes` serves `GET /b/addresses` and `POST /b/addresses/request`, `/confirm` and `/remove-backup`, always merged in `business_pages`; `RouterParts::with_address_changes` (from `address_parts`: the vault, `ASKHAVA_ID_SECRET` and the shared email provider's `MailboxCodeSender`) lets a change go ahead. New limits `server.address_codes_per_member` (5 a day) and `server.address_tries_per_member` (10 an hour), after `CODE_SENDS_PER_IP` and `CODE_TRIES_PER_IP`. New `businesses::api::EmailCheckError::MailboxTaken`.
- Owner's waiting-phones page (T397, D-3081, D-3744, D-3889, D-4726, D-5293; BIZ-033, BIZ-038, SEC-038, SEC-042, SEC-048): new `portals::phone_approval` (`phone_approval_page`, `posted_phone`, `PHONES_ROUTE`, `APPROVE_PHONE_ROUTE`, `REJECT_PHONE_ROUTE`, `templates/phone_approval.html` on `business_layout.html`). New `server::phone_approval` serves `GET /b/phones` and `POST /b/phones/approve` and `/reject`, always merged in `business_pages` with the portal's vault (`RouterParts::with_vault`, else the webhooks'); it calls `businesses::api::waiting_phones`, `approve_phone`, `reject_phone` and `sign_in_lines`, `portals::api::require_step_up` (`SensitiveChange::ApprovePhone`) and `privacy::api::read_business_mailbox` under `BusinessPurpose::PhoneApproval` (new `server` to `privacy` read purpose in use). No new `RouterParts` part.
- Business team page (T544, D-3890, D-3892, D-5567, D-5722; BIZ-023, FEE-041, SEC-038, SEC-019): new `portals::team_page` (`team_page`, `may_see_team`, `ShownMember`, `TEAM_ROUTE`, `templates/business_team.html` on `business_layout.html`); `server::business_step_up` serves `GET /b/team` behind `business_principal`, `no-store`, calling `businesses::api::members` and `privacy::api::read_business_mailbox` under `BusinessPurpose::AccountEmail` with the portal's vault (`business_step_up::routes` gains a vault argument; no new `RouterParts` part). Its forms post to T565's `/b/team/role`, `/b/team/remove` and `/b/billing/limit`; `/b/billing` links to it.
