# Architecture section 2 notes: `ai`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `ai`, in their original order.

- AI-008 gate: in live, `ai::api::read_intake` calls the AI only when a zero-retention signing date is recorded or the owner's "AI on scrubbed text" switch is on with the newest `ai.provider_terms` row complete (training-free, flagged-content retention, a Console spend limit at most 3 times the expected cost) and today's `ai.privacy_policy_version` holding the AI-018 sentence; otherwise it returns `AiOff::SwitchOff` without a call and intake runs keyword-only. Each reading is stored in `ai.reading` with no text column and its trade, job type and flags are queued for the off-database stream in the call record's transaction; `ai::api::weekly_counts` gives the weekly summary its counts (T025, D-841 to D-843).
- Owner AI page (T737, D-4727, D-5534; AI-008, AI-018, SEC-006, SEC-043, A11Y-030): the new `ai::checklist` (`record_provider_terms`, `record_policy_version`: one insert and one IDs-only `Actor::Owner` audit row per record, in one transaction; insert only) and the new read-only `ai::api::missing_records` (every `Missing` in the gate's order and whether the switch is on, from `readiness`) and `zero_retention_in_force` (whether a zero-retention date in force keeps live AI on whatever the switch says, D-5690) feed `portals::owner_ai_switch` (`owner_ai_page`, `owner_ai_change`, behind `OwnerAction::AiSwitch`'s fresh factor, calling `ai::api::set_ai_switch`), served by the new `server::owner_ai_routes` at `/owner/ai`, `/owner/ai/terms`, `/owner/ai/policy` and `/owner/ai/switch` behind the owner session; the owner home links to it ("AI switch").
