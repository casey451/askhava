# Architecture section 2 notes: `ops`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `ops`, in their original order.

- Not a fit and the weekly minutes (T479, D-3753, D-3883, D-4152): `ops::scorecard_inputs` is the only writer and reader of `ops.not_a_fit_answer` (one row per business and job) and `ops.minutes_entry` (append-only, the latest entry per Arizona week counts), migration 0200, app role SELECT and INSERT only. `portals::not_a_fit` stores an answer through `ops::api::record_not_a_fit` under `quotes::api::job_trade` (the first sent card's category, only for a card holder) and touches no routing or fee; `business_actions::job_details_page_choice` shows the four one-tap reasons posting to `NOT_A_FIT_ROUTE` (`/b/not-a-fit/{object}/{token}`, served by T306), and every job page shows `businesses::api::business_statement()` under the card (CARD-020, D-3889). T158 reads `not_a_fit_counts` and `minutes_for_week`.
- Safe mode (T487, D-3878, D-4000): `ops`' `ops.safe_mode_check` task (`SAFE_MODE_KIND`, queued at worker start, re-queued every minute) sets the `ops.safe_mode` flag from open pushed alerts that are critical or High of one of `SAFE_MODE_KINDS` (D-4310); `payments::handlers::charges_paused` calls `ops::api::safe_mode_on` (the flag, or a push unacknowledged 24 hours by the database's `now()`) beside the `charging` and `all` switches, and `server`'s release gate reads it too (T408, D-3144, D-4026): `release_sink::PickReleaser::settle_and_release` holds a cleared pick by queuing one `payments.held_release` task (`server::release_hold`, due every 15 minutes while safe mode lasts), which once it ends clears the pick again through `payments::api::held_release` and releases through the same settle (FEE-017). Nothing else reads safe mode, so texts never pause for it (D-133).
- Alert pushes (T204, D-2207, D-3880, D-4044, D-4084): `ops::api` decides what pushes (`alerts_to_push`: critical, a health alert open 10 minutes, a ledger alert on its second night, `PUSH_AT_ONCE` kinds; `daytime_push_due` for the 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. Arizona slots over `DAYTIME_KINDS` alerts and `ops.push_item` rows) and records it (`ops.alert_push`, `ops.push_slot`); `messaging`'s `messaging.alert_push` (every minute) emails each to `ASKHAVA_ALERT_EMAIL` through T469's provider and queues `calls.owner_alert_call` for a critical one; `messaging.hourly_checks` runs `check_webhook_silence` and the email-use check (`ops.email_limit`, Normal, once a day). `acknowledge_cleared` clears `ops.vendor_errors`, `ops.health_check_failed` and, from a matched `reconcile`, `payments.ledger_mismatch`. Migration 0193 adds `ops.owner_away` (OPS-026), `ops.push_item`, `ops.push_slot` and `ops.owner_digest` (T528 writes it).
- Weekly digest rows (T528, D-3882, D-3883, D-4044, D-4202, D-4197; OPS-030): `ops::digest_rows` is the reader and writer of `ops.owner_digest` (`digest_sent` inserts, `digest_opened` keeps the first open, `digest_reminded` marks one reminder on an unopened digest, each one statement; `latest_digest` reads the newest) and reads, without writing, `ops.alert` with `ops.alert_ack` (`digest_alerts`: given kinds raised or resolved in `[from, to)`, self-acknowledged when the acknowledgement is by `system`), `ops.coverage`'s trade rows (`trade_active_counts`) and `ops.audit_log` (`system_status_changes`: one subject kind's system-actor status and field rows in `[from, to)`). Results are IDs, code names, counts and times only. T524 builds the digest from them; `businesses`, `intake` and `picks` counts are T605's.
- Help requests (T151, D-4275 to D-4277; OPS-036, PRIV-026, PRIV-030, CON-034): `ops::help_requests` is the only writer and the main reader of `ops.help_request` (the second reader is `ops::help_request_keys`' `help_requests_of_homeowner`, her request IDs for `privacy::api::delete_my_data`, T757, D-4765), `ops.help_message` (a `privacy.message_body` inbox ID, never text) and `ops.help_keyword` (`migrations/0194_help_requests.sql`): `open_help_request` joins an open request of the same asker opened within 1 hour, else opens one notified at once unless 10 were notified that Arizona day, and adds one OPS-032 push item (`ops.help_request`, shown from due less 24 hours, or at once when held back); `open_help_requests`, `help_request`, `add_owner_reply` and `set_help_closed` serve T524 and T530. `intake::handlers` is the entry for every kind: `note_help` (a HELP keyword, from `server`'s `inbound_text.rs`, records the time only), `help_answered` and `help_follow_up` (T482's `receive_reply`), `business_help` (a business text that gets the auto reply), `run_help_request_opened` (`portals::api::HELP_REQUEST_KIND`) and `receive_help_email` (T133 hands it `privacy::api::read_inbound_email`'s output; a DMARC, SPF or DKIM failure opens nothing). Each keeps the scrubbed words with `privacy::api::keep_problem_words` (her job, else the request's own ID), then for a request notified now queues `messaging::api::queue_help_notice` (`messaging.help_notice`, deduped per request) holding the job fields and the words through `quotes::api::final_gate`; `messaging::help_requests` composes and emails it to the OPS-035 alert address with `HelpSender`, built by `server` next to the alert sender (none in dev and Ci).
