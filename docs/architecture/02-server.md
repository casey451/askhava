# Architecture section 2 notes: `server`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `server`, in their original order.

- Twilio webhook routes (T241, D-1307 to D-1309): `server`'s `inbound_text::routes` (`POST /webhooks/twilio/sms` and `POST /webhooks/twilio/status`), merged by `build_router` only when `server::webhooks` built them (D-1310, D-1311) (`ASKHAVA_PUBLIC_BASE_URL` set; unset is no routes in dev and CI and a start-up error in test and live). Each delivery is checked by `adapters::api::verify_webhook` over the public base URL plus the route path and query (403 and one log line otherwise, SEC-016) and then runs in one transaction, answered 200 unless the database failed (500). An inbound text: `receive_inbound` (a repeat MessageSid stops), `privacy::api::read_inbound` (the raw body goes no further), `on_inbound_text` (an opt-out stops), `reply_to_help` (a HELP text, also from a listed number, stops after its reply; any other text from a listed number stops), then for a number not in the sender directory `intake::api::receive_reply`; a business phone's STOP also calls `businesses::api::phone_stopped`, and its other texts queue `business_auto_reply` when `businesses::api::auto_reply_due` holds (T256, D-2039). A racing first text from a new phone continues with the contact that won the unique phone index, or rolls back with a 500 (D-1751). A text a non-database module error stops after the repeat check (`Halt::Failed`) is still answered 200 and raises one High `messaging.inbound_text_failed` alert about its inbox row, the failed step in the alert's `ops` audit row, in the delivery's transaction, with no body, phone or error text (T325, D-2207, D-3107). A status callback: `receive_status`.
- Web security (T051, D-943, D-944, D-945, D-946): `server::build_router`'s outermost layer is `portals::api::security_headers`, so every response, a 404 included, carries the SEC-012 CSP (own origin only, no inline or eval script, `object-src 'none'`, `frame-ancestors 'none'`), one-year HSTS with `includeSubDomains` and no `preload` (SEC-054), the Permissions-Policy, `nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; `no_store` goes on contact and photo pages (SEC-034). Portal and site routers (never webhooks) layer `refuse_cross_site`, which refuses a state-changing request by `is_cross_site` (D-4292): a present `Sec-Fetch-Site` alone decides (`same-origin` passes whatever `Origin` says, since under `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` a browser's own form post sends `Origin: null`; any other value is refused); without it `Origin: https://<Host>` passes, `Origin: null` is left to the token check, and any other `Origin` or none is refused; POST handlers read their form through `CsrfForm`, whose `csrf` field must equal `csrf_token(&jar)`: the SHA256 of a label and the `__Host-askhava-session` cookie's token, or with no session the `__Host-askhava-pre` cookie's, so no key or table is needed. `start_session` deletes the browser's earlier `portals.session` row and drops the pre-session cookie (SEC-042). Each refusal is a bare 403 and a `record_security_event`: one log line and one `portals.security_event` row (kind, route template, request ID, and the session's row ID as actor reference when the browser is signed in); the event that brings its kind to 50 within an hour raises one High `ops` alert unless one was raised (flagged `raised_alert`) within that hour, so a steady attack raises at most one per kind per hour (SEC-048). Portal templates extend `app/site/templates/base.html` through `app/portals/askama.toml` (D-491).
- Web chat client IP (T646, D-4292, D-4880, D-5612; SEC-014, SEC-040): `server::chat_codes::guard`'s token layer reads a passing `POST`'s client IP with `portals::api::security_event_client` (the edge's `ASKHAVA_CLIENT_IP_HEADER`, never the socket peer) and hands it to `site` as the extension `site::api::ChatClientIp`, only when it is not unspecified; `site::chat::post` (and `carry`) key `intake`'s new-request limit (`Key::Ip`) on it, and without it the limit gets no IP (`site` may not call `portals`).
- Email action links (T301, D-1955, D-3669, D-3675): `server::action_links` serves `GET` and `POST` on `portals::api::ACTION_ROUTE` (`/b/a/{kind}/{object}/{token}`), merged with `checkin` in `build_router`, under `refuse_cross_site` with `no-store` outermost. A `GET` reads only (`open_action_link`; the person, where needed, through `use_action_link` in a transaction always rolled back) and shows `portals::api::action_page` or, for a Quote link, `quote_form_page_priced` from `quotes::api::quote_rules`, `sent_card`, `job_page` and `last_facts`. Only the Quote `POST` acts: `admit_quote`, `parse_price`, `parse_facts`, `largest_job_warning` (`businesses::api::capabilities`), `parse_quote` and `submit_quote_with_facts` in one transaction; Pause, Resume and Ask about a charge act with a business session (T455, D-3839): `use_action_link` in the action's transaction, the signed-in person's own link and `session_may`, then `businesses::api::pause_self` or `resume_self`, or `payments::api::receipt_for` and `portals::api::ask_about_charge` (signed out: a 401 before any token read; a refused pause or resume a 409 that rolls back); Approve (T399, D-3081, D-3744) acts with the link's own owner's session and `portals::api::require_step_up` (`SensitiveChange::ApprovePhone`) checked before its transaction, then `use_action_link`, `businesses::api::waiting_phones` and `approve_phone` naming the phone's `number_set_at`, only when `ActionGrant::issued_at` is not before it; Hold (T456, D-3890) acts by the link alone: `open_action_link`, `picks::api::hold_ask`, `use_action_link` with the pick's facts and `picks::api::hold_pick` in one transaction (a refusal a 409 that rolls back); every other kind's `POST` is the bare 404 until its task (T457, T458, T463). A used link's repeated `POST` (T814, SEC-026, D-5316) is answered after the refused use rolled back: `portals::api::used_action_link`, on a bare connection, names the person, business and object of a used link within its window whose member still has the role; a Quote link then shows `job_details_page_choice` again, a Confirm link the 303 to its Confirm page, every other kind `portals::api::action_done_page` (a session kind only for its own signed-in person); anything else stays the bare 404. A Confirm link sent by text (T714, SEC-004, PICK-033, D-4425, D-5628) is minted by `portals::api::mint_text_confirm_link` on the same route, stored as the SHA256 of `action-text:confirm:<object>:<token>` in `portals.login_link` (no new column or migration); `open_action_link`, `action_link_business` and `use_action_link` refuse a token that is not 64 lower-case hex characters before any read, then try the email binding and, for Confirm, the text one; a text-started grant's `confirm_link_session` lasts `portals::api::TEXT_CONFIRM_SESSION_LIFE` (30 minutes) instead of `BUSINESS_SESSION_LIFE` (30 days).
- Link this phone action (T458, D-3675, D-5173; CALL-011, CALL-012, BIZ-033): `server::action_links` serves the Link this phone `POST` with a business session through `portals::api::use_action_link` and `calls::api::link_by_action`, which calls `businesses::api::link_pending_phone` (the owner's phone approved; a member's waiting, with `complaints::phone_link_notice` queued), `messaging::api::sender_put_pending` and, for a member's link, `messaging::api::sender_hold`. The served quote form calls `quotes::api::record_note_returned` on its own connection when it returns a note (D-3877, D-5175).
- Safety key kept (T571, D-4051, D-4088; CON-035): `server::voice_status`'s key route, when `calls::api::safety_key_once` fails, queues the core task `calls.safety_key_retry` (`calls::safety_key_retry`, IDs, arrival and key only; up to 8 claims, then one High `calls.safety_key_lost` alert about the safety alert); when the queue fails too it answers 200 with a 2-second `<Pause>` and a POST `<Redirect>` to itself carrying `Digits` and `attempt` (at most 3), then the spoken STOP line and the same alert if it can. Registered in `register_safety_call` with the safety parts' vault.
- Health probe and liveness exits (T106, D-3144, D-3881, D-4044, D-4224; OPS-007, OPS-049): `server::healthz` serves `GET /healthz` (merged first in `build_router`, under every layer): `liveness::round_trip`'s schema-less `SELECT 1`, then `core::api::queue_health` (the oldest task due with no `done_at`, `dead_at` or live lease and with attempts left, T778, and the newest finish or lease start), with a 5-second limit on each; 200 unless the database fails or due work has waited past `ASKHAVA_WORKER_DOWN_SECS` (default 600), then 503; a queue read that fails or times out after a good round trip also answers 503, as `database` up and `worker` unknown (`HealthState::worker_unknown`, T778, D-5081 F36); `no-store` JSON naming `status`, `database`, `worker` and `last_activity_secs` only. `healthz::check` has no HTTP type (T518 calls it); `healthz::web_health` gives T476 the start time and that state. `server::liveness` checks every 10 seconds, in `web` the round trip and in `worker` that and a new `ClaimTick` pass (`Worker::run_watched`); after `ASKHAVA_LIVENESS_LIMIT_SECS` (default 180) of failures in a row the binary returns an error and exits non-zero for the host's On Failure restart.
- Health check alert (T624, D-4280, D-4463; OPS-004, OPS-023): `server::health_watch` registers `ops.health_check` in `worker_registry` (limits from `HealthLimits::from_pairs`); `worker`'s `schedule_alert_checks` seeds it with the no-handler check. Once per 5-minute slot it runs `healthz::check`, then in one transaction queues the next slot (`max(own slot, slot of now) + 1`, key `ops.health_check.<slot>`, payload `health_failed` and `slot`) and, in test and live, raises one High `ops.health_check_failed` (`ops::api::raise_kind_once`) on a second failure in a row or clears it on a pass (`ops::api::acknowledge_cleared`).
- Stripe-or-webhook-errors alert (T518, D-3877, D-4880, D-5670, D-5876, D-5908; OPS-004, OPS-023): `server::vendor_alerts::layer` covers the five `/webhooks` routers (merged in `lib.rs`'s `webhook_routes`) and writes `ops` audit rows `server.webhook_error` (each 5xx or unanswered request) and `server.webhook_ok` (a route's first 2xx per 5-minute window, `audit_once`); its 5-minute task `ops.vendor_errors_check` (seeded in `schedule_alert_checks`) reads them through `ops::api::audit_subjects` with `payments::api::stripe_calls`, tracks error runs per source in its payload and raises or clears `ops.vendor_errors` (`raise_kind_once`, `acknowledge_cleared`).
- Help forms served (T309, D-2128, D-3890, D-4293, D-5866; BIZ-047, OPS-036, PRIV-031, SEC-014, SEC-034, SEC-042, SEC-048): new `server::help_forms`. `routes` serves `GET`/`POST /b/help` behind `portals::api::business_principal` (none: `signed_out`, 401) and `POST /help` with the pre-session CSRF cookie, `admit_help_form` first (refused: 429 "Too many messages"), then `send_help_form` with the portal's vault, else the webhooks'; all `no-store`, `refuse_cross_site` outermost, `CsrfForm` for the token. `guard` wraps `site::api::help_routes` so `GET /help` gets `ensure_pre_session` and `site::api::HelpFormCsrf`, `no-store`. New `RouterParts::with_help_mail` (`help_forms::help_mail`: the email provider's `ASKHAVA_ID_SECRET` and postal line) queues the Get help answer through `send_get_help`; `lib.rs`'s `vault_pages` merges it beside `phone_approval`.
- Watch checks (T525, D-4434, D-4435; SEC-047, OPS-052): `server::watch_checks` registers two worker tasks in `worker_registry`, each first queued by `worker.rs`'s `schedule_checks` under its hour's or UTC day's dedupe key and re-queued by each run: `privacy.read_rate_check` (hourly, `privacy::api::check_read_rates` for the hour in its payload, then queues the next hour due when that hour ends, so a late run skips no hour, D-4435) and `ops.volume_check` (daily, `ops::api::check_volume`, which reads `pg_database_size(current_database())` and raises through `raise_kind_once` `ops.volume_low` (`normal`) from 70% and `ops.volume_high` (`high`, daytime push, never safe mode) from 85% of the setting `ASKHAVA_DB_VOLUME_BYTES`). The setting is optional in `core`'s `Settings`; the registration refuses a missing one in test and live, and in dev and Ci the volume check raises nothing.
- Homeowner portal served (T307, D-3967, D-4025, D-4010; SEC-008, SEC-014, SEC-034, SEC-042, SEC-046, PRIV-021, CON-035): `server::homeowner_portal` serves `GET`/`POST /h/sign-in`, `POST /h/sign-in/code`, `GET /h` and `POST /h/texts-off`, always merged by `build_router` (in `business_pages`), with the Twilio webhooks' vault and ID secret and the optional `RouterParts::with_homeowner_verify` part (`homeowner_verify` in `main`; none: no send, no check, never a 5xx); the code is sent in a spawned task after the limits admit it, so the code page never waits on Verify, D-4013). Calls: `portals::api` (`admit_code_send`, `admit_code_try`, `send_sign_in_code`, `finish_sign_in`, `OnFile::read`, `HomeFacts::read`, `turn_texts_off`), `privacy::api::find_by_phone` (index only, no plaintext read) and `intake::api::conversations_of_contact`/`conversation` for the language. `HomeFacts::read` in `portals` reads her phone and address through `read_own_contact` and calls the new `messaging::api::texts_on` (`messaging::held_messages`, a new `portals` to `messaging` read), which derives the suppression digests as `optout.rs` does. The data routes (T561, D-3967, D-3877, D-4887; PRIV-031, PRIV-011): `GET /h/my-data` answers her file (`portals::api::download_file` over her requests and consent records through `intake::api::conversations_of_contact`, `conversation` and `consent_records`, her jobs' shown quotes through `jobs_of_contact`, `quotes::api::shown_quotes` and `page_quotes` with `businesses::api::public_names`, and her held text words through the new `messaging::api::held_words`, the outbox bodies still kept for her with phones and emails masked by `core::api::scrub_text`); `POST /h/my-data/request`, `/h/my-data/resend` and `/h/my-data/code` call `open_request`, `resend_request_code` and `confirm_request` after `admit_code_send` (IP, then her contact) or `admit_code_try`; a confirmed copy answers the same file, a delete `DELETE_CONFIRMATION`; a refused or impossible send is a 429 that sends nothing. The opted-out link (T804, D-5281, D-5339, D-5340, D-5747 to D-5751; PRIV-031, SEC-014, SEC-026, SEC-037): for a number on the suppression list (`portals::api::number_listed`, over `messaging::api::texts_on`) `POST /h/my-data/request` sends no code; after the same `admit_code_send` it calls `portals::api::send_request_link`, which in one transaction writes the received audit row (`data_download::open_link_request`), stores the link's salt in the new `portals.data_request_link` (migration 0217: `request_id`, `salt`, `sent_at`, `used_at`; UPDATE on `used_at` only) and queues the email through `messaging::api::queue_data_request_link` (a new `portals` to `messaging` call), rolling back when she has no confirmed update address (the answer then says to email help@). `GET /help/my-data/{object}/{token}` (`open_data_link`) reads only; its `POST` (`confirm_data_link`) uses the link with one guarded UPDATE and acts through `data_download::act_once`, the act the code path shares; a copy answers only the sign-in link. The footer postal line is `RouterParts::with_data_link_postal` (`data_link_postal` in `main`, the update emails' rule).
- Owner reports served (T305, D-5772 to D-5776; OPS-039, OPS-040, FEE-046, SEC-005, SEC-034, SEC-042, SEC-048): `server::owner_reports::routes` serves `/owner/scorecard` with `POST /owner/scorecard/minutes`, `/owner/intake-report`, `/owner/money` with `POST /owner/money/expense` and the CSV `/owner/money/csv` behind `require_owner`, `no-store`, `refuse_cross_site` and the CSRF form check; `owner_pages` merges it. `main` passes `ReportSettings::from_settings` through `RouterParts::with_report_settings` (set-aside basis points, `ASKHAVA_GOLDEN_TARGET_PERCENT`, the scorecard lines, the launch date as the pilot start, else this Arizona week's Monday, and the new optional setting `ASKHAVA_PILOT_PUSHED_TRADES`, `Settings::pilot_pushed_trades`); without it the defaults.
- Opted-out text pick (T741, D-4727, D-5670, D-5794; PICK-030, CON-013, CON-016): `server`'s opt-out screen (`inbound_text::screen`, the `Outcome::Suppressed` arm) queues the wrote-again follow-up for every listed homeowner text that is not HELP, as before, and, for one with no provider keyword and no 911-list match that `picks::api::rules::read_text_pick` reads, calls new `intake::api::answer_opted_out_pick` (`app/intake/src/text_pick_reply.rs`) in the delivery's transaction: her open request's live jobs, each pause by `paused_under_lock`, then `picks::api::opted_out_yes` as `Actor::Homeowner` (channel `page`, no text). The delivery then ends as `suppressed`.
- Business settings routes (T733, D-4727, D-4822, D-5318, D-5787; BIZ-010, BIZ-058, SEC-027, BIZ-046, SEC-019, A11Y-030): new `server::business_settings_routes`, merged in `build_router` (`business_pages`), serves `GET`/`POST /b/settings/service-area` (`portals::service_area_settings_page`, `change_service_area`; owner or "Confirm and billing" only), `POST /b/shared-inbox` (`set_inbox_shared`, 303 to `/b` or the step-up page) and `GET /b/contact-card.vcf` (`contact_card` over the voice hooks' masked pool), each behind `business_principal`, `no-store`, `refuse_cross_site` and CSRF on `POST`. No new table, library or task kind.
- Call texts in her language (T590, D-4052, D-5540; TXT-027, TXT-030, TXT-039): `server` passes `intake::api::job_language` as a `calls::api::JobLanguage` fn pointer to `calls::api::run_whos_calling_in` and `picks::api::run_unreachable_step_in`, since neither `calls` nor `picks` may call `intake`; the who's-calling text, the missed-call text and its email copy follow it, English with no source or a failed read.
