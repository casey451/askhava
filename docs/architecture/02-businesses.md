# Architecture section 2 notes: `businesses`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `businesses`, in their original order.

- Deliverability re-tests (T686, D-4361, D-5007; BIZ-042): the daily `businesses.deliverability_retests` (`businesses::retest_run`, registered by `server::register_license_reapply`, first queued by the worker's `schedule_checks`, each run queuing the next at 9 a.m. Arizona time) calls `queue_pick_retests` and `queue_emergency_retests` at the worker clock's now with the 90-day Yes lives; each clears the Yes it re-tests and queues one `businesses.sample_pick` or `businesses.emergency_test` email in one transaction.
- Unknown license status (T655, D-4310, D-5184 to D-5186; LIC-008): the `businesses.license_standing` chain (`businesses::license_standing`, registered by `server::register_daily_lists` with the list run's settings, first queued by the worker's `schedule_start`) runs 5 minutes after each slot of the daily list grid. It reads `licensing::api::roc_standing` and `recheck_paused`, queues one `businesses.license_unknown` owner email per unknown spell (a `NOTICE_KINDS` kind), pauses for `license` at the latest Active check's `checked_at` plus 72 hours and resumes with no review on a later Active check.
- Record resume (T690, D-4361, D-5217 to D-5219; BIZ-026, BIZ-002, QH-001): the daily `businesses.record_resume` run (`businesses::record_resume`, registered by `server::register_record_resume`, first queued by the worker's `schedule_licenses`, due 30 minutes after the ROC list's run hour) queues, once per newly recorded ROC list, one `businesses.record_resume_one` task (payload the business ID) per sign-up waiting on "That number is old", at most `ASKHAVA_BIZ_RESUME_BATCH` (default 25), in turn by business ID from the last one queued, which the chain's payload carries with the last list acted on. Each task makes one `licensing::api::read_own_record` through `signup_record`, and in one transaction `resume_on_new_record` and `identity::send_code_seen` with the record held in memory; outside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Arizona, or a text step outside the record phone's area-code window (`messaging::api::window`), it re-queues under `.held.<business>.<list date>.<until>`.
- Weekly digest counts (T605, D-4202, D-4218; OPS-030, OPS-041, BIZ-054): read-only readers over a half-open window (`from` counted, `to` not), returning IDs, codes, counts and job numbers only. `businesses::digest_counts` gives `leaving_answers` (business, who asked, choice or `None`, never the "Something else" words) through the crate-private `leaving::answers_between`, so `leaving.rs` stays the only file naming the answers table (TD-BIZ-32), and `license_paused` (paused with reason `license`, by `status_changed_at`); `intake::not_covered_counts::not_covered_by_trade` counts distinct requests per trade from `intake.recruiting_demand` by `created_at`; `picks::digest_counts::no_shows` reads `no_show` picks by `ended_at` with `jobs::api::job_number`. T524 and T478 call them.
- Business texting follows the stored switch (T526, D-3881, D-3882, D-5070; TXT-035, BIZ-036, WEB-024): new optional setting `ASKHAVA_BUSINESS_NUMBER` (`Settings::business_number`; the business line's own sending number). New module `businesses::texting_switch` (`business_texting_on`, a new `businesses` to `messaging::api::load_lines` call, for `site`). `server` (`/b/notices`, the sign-up), `intake` (`cards_out`) and `messaging`'s notice worker and outbox sender read `load_lines` with that number; texting is on only while the stored switch is on and the number is set.
- Home texting number (T725, D-4709, D-5279, D-5402, D-5347; WEB-017, CON-005, WORD-013): `businesses::texting_switch::homeowner_texting_number` (a new `businesses` to `messaging::api::load_lines` call, for `site`) answers the homeowner number only from `ASKHAVA_LAUNCH_DATE` on while the homeowner line's `sender()` is open; `server` passes `ASKHAVA_HOMEOWNER_NUMBER` (`server::homeowner_number`, now public) and the launch date through `SitePages::with_homeowner_texting`; `/` and `/es` show the number with its consent line together, else as before.
- Away until (T543, D-3890, D-4865 to D-4867; BIZ-034, BIZ-038, PICK-043): migration 0206 adds `businesses.business.away_until` and `busy_until` (T463's). `businesses::away_busy::set_away` stores an end the caller bounded with `picks::api::away_until`/`away_for` (owner or "Confirm and billing", one audit row); `away_until` reads it while ahead. New cross-module call: `picks::handlers::notice_sent` reads `businesses::api::away_until` and starts a non-emergency window at `window_start(sent, away, emergency)`, marking `picks.pick.away_delayed`; `hold_pick` queues one `messaging.business_notice` (`hold_end_notice`, dedupe `messaging.business_notice.hold_end.<pick>`). `server::action_links` acts on the Away link's `POST` with the link's own person's business session (`portals::api::away_page`, one hours field).
- Busy for <n> days (T463, D-3675, D-3890, D-4939; BIZ-028, BIZ-038, BIZ-010): `businesses::away_busy::set_busy` (1 to 60 days; owner or "Confirm and billing") pauses through `pause_self`, stores `businesses.business.busy_until` and queues one `businesses.busy_end` task at the end (payload: business, the pause's `status_changed_at`, the end), all in one transaction; `run_busy_end`, registered in `server::register_business_handlers` with the worker clock, resumes through `resume_self` only while the row is still self-paused with that pause time and end. `server::action_links` acts on the Busy link's `POST` with the link's own person's business session and answers with `portals::api::busy_until_line`.
- Late cards on eligibility (T677, D-4360, D-5075, D-5076; CARD-018, CARD-024, CARD-020): `businesses::service` queues one `businesses.became_eligible` core task (`{"business": id}` only, dedupe per business and minute, due at the next minute) in the same transaction as a move into a status that `receives_cards`, or, on such a business, a towns or job-type edit that adds one or an overflow or after-hours tick turned on (`businesses::api::queue_eligible`). `server::register_late_cards` runs `quotes::api::run_became_eligible` on the worker clock: `quotes::late_cards` lists the jobs with a sent card (`quotes.shown_card`) in the business's categories that it does not hold and, per job under `jobs::api::lock_for_quote_change`, while it `takes_late_cards`, builds `card_pool` from the stored card's town (towns only, no band floor: the ZIP and band answer stay in `intake`, D-5075) kept to the job's card holders and the payload business, and sends nothing to a business with a largest-job limit (D-5077), records cards through `send_late_cards_in_reach` and queues `queue_card_notice` or `queue_emergency_card`. Before the welcome email (`may_get_cards` false) it queues itself 5 minutes on, for up to 24 hours after the status move (D-5076).
- Business text-consent page (T522, D-3879, D-3881, D-5913; CON-019, BIZ-036, TXT-035): new `businesses::text_lines` (`member_text_lines`, the phones one member linked with `number_end`, number version and `may_text`), new `portals::text_consent_page` (`text_consent_page`, `record_text_consent` over `record_consent_for`) and template `text_consent.html` (extends `business_layout.html`), new `server::text_consent`, merged in `build_router` (`business_pages`), serving `GET`/`POST /b/texts` behind `business_principal`, `no-store`, `refuse_cross_site` and CSRF on `POST`; the switch is `messaging::api::load_lines`' stored business line. `notice_settings.html` links the page when the channel is text or both; `not_a_fit.html` now extends `business_layout.html` (D-5567). No new table, library or task kind.
