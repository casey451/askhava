# Architecture section 2 notes: `quotes`

Moved here verbatim from `docs/architecture.md` (D-6022), whose index lists every part. Until T885 makes `docs/architecture/` a shared path, a task adds new notes to the index, at the end of the section this part came from, and the next plan change moves them here.

The section 2 notes ("Processes and code layout") whose first named crate is `quotes`, in their original order.

- Owner flag counts (T619, D-4280, D-4311; OPS-027, OPS-034, PICK-019): read-only readers, IDs and counts only, each taking its numbers and window as arguments. `quotes::flag_inputs::month_quotes(jobs, (from, to))` gives, for one trade's jobs from `quotes::api::jobs_by_trade`, the jobs first carded in the month, their median confirmed quotes per job in tenths (a job with none counts 0) and their confirmed quotes; `picks::flag_inputs` gives `lapsed_businesses` (`expired` picks per business, `min` or more), `wins` (`paid` or `free` picks on given jobs) and `picked_jobs` (given jobs with any pick row), windowed on `picked_at`; `intake::flag_inputs::repeat_requesters` gives the vault contact IDs with `min` or more requests opened in the window and no pick on any, reading picks only through `picks::api::picked_jobs`. T524 holds the settings and the flags.
- Request minimum (T679, T710, D-4360, D-4408, D-4421; LIC-046, INT-017, OPS-006): `quotes::api::request_coverage(conn, map, ((town, zip), facts), band_floor)` reads `businesses::api::active_in_category` (`businesses::active_count`, the active businesses with a ticked job type in the category, any town; paused and removed not counted) and applies `licensing::api::request_intake` with `map.opening(category)` and `quotes::api::MIN_ACTIVE_BUSINESSES` (1, a constant, not yet a setting; D-4421, was 2); only `Open` loads `card_pool` and counts as `coverage_in_reach`; `TooFewBusinesses`, `WaitlistOnly` and `NotRouted` are `Coverage::NotCovered`. Caller: `intake::order_dispatch` (T671).
- Download contents (T820, D-5399, D-5526, D-5709, D-5804 to D-5806; PRIV-031, CON-022): her file has seven sections (`requests`, `quotes`, `messages`, `consent`, `jobs`, `answers`, `feedback`). `server::homeowner_portal::quote_items` adds `quotes::api::withdrawn_quotes` (`app/quotes/src/withdrawn_quotes.rs`) as "Withdrawn: " items; `portals::api::more_held_items` reads each job's description (`quotes::api::description`), answers (`intake::api::job_answers`, worded from the catalog's question bank), feedback (`jobs::api::feedback::of_job`, `app/jobs/src/feedback_of_job.rs`), the opt-out and START records (`messaging::api::optout_records`, `app/messaging/src/optout_records.rs`: digests by `privacy::api::phone_digests`, from `ops::api::contact_created_at` on) and HELP (`ops::api::help_keyword_of`, `app/ops/src/help_keyword_of.rs`). Every section but `requests` and `consent` is masked.
