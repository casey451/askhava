How it works

Start in the chat on this site. You don't need to know which trade to ask for.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need.

    Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.

  2. 2

    Havasu businesses quote.

    It goes to Havasu businesses that take this kind of job. Each one has 24 hours to quote. Every quote lists the same details: the price, what's included and when they can come. Not every business answers.

  3. 3

    You pick one, or none.

    Check their license and reviews, then choose. You hire the business directly.

How we keep it fair

  • Your number stays with us.

    Businesses see the job, not you. Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick.

  • Nobody pays to be first.

    Quotes show in the order they came in. Laftly doesn't rank or rate businesses, and no business can pay for a better spot.

  • Asking costs you nothing.

    Laftly is a quote-request service; the business you choose pays Laftly a set introduction fee. That's $25, $75 or $199, by quote size. You pay Laftly nothing. If no quote fits, close the request.

For businesses

Quote for free. Pay only if a homeowner picks you.

When a homeowner sends a job you do, we email you the details and you decide whether to quote. Every card says how many businesses got it. You quote once. No bidding rounds, no paid placement.

If the homeowner picks you, you pay a fixed introduction fee by job size, never a share of the job.

Who runs Laftly

Laftly is run by Laftly LLC, a Lake Havasu City business. Its owner lives in Havasu. The owner has a full-time day job. Sign-up reviews are usually answered within 4 days, help and charge questions usually within 2 days, and this page shows when the owner is away.

In an emergency, call 911

Laftly isn't an emergency service and doesn't judge what needs an emergency response. If your message mentions gas, smoke, fire, a CO alarm going off or a downed power line, we send one automatic reminder to call 911. Don't rely on Laftly in an emergency: call 911.
