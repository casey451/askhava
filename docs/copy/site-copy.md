# Site copy

The approved wording for laftly.com, block by block (WORD-029, D-474). Page templates take their words from here; `scripts/check_copy.py` checks every block not marked `fixed`, and the copy panel (`docs/copy/copy-panel.md`) reviews every change. Blocks marked `fixed` are wording the spec sets word for word, cited by requirement.

**Status: home page per D-3331 (Casey's V5 guide, November 12, 2026), confirmed by Casey as the replacement of the October 28 draft (D-4098).** The texting number shows only after Gate C (WEB-017); until then the start line names the chat only.

Market names: each block below reads as today's one market renders it. On the page, "Lake Havasu City", "Arizona" and "Havasu" come from `docs/catalog/markets.yaml` (its `name`, `state_name` and `short_name`), never typed into the template (D-1515, INT-048). The page title ("Local quotes for Havasu homes") and the numbers heading ("Havasu requests so far") take `short_name` the same way. The "Who runs Laftly" block is fixed and keeps its words.

## Home · start line (homeowner)
> Start in the chat on this site. You don't need to know which trade to ask for.

## Home · step 1 (homeowner)
> Tell us what you need. Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.

## Home · step 2 (homeowner)
> Havasu businesses quote. It goes to Havasu businesses that take this kind of job. Each one has 24 hours to quote. Every quote lists the same details: the price, what's included and when they can come. Not every business answers.

## Home · step 3 (homeowner)
> You pick one, or none. Check their license and reviews, then choose. You hire the business directly.

## Home · fair kicker (homeowner)
> How we keep it fair

## Home · fair 1 (homeowner)
> Your number stays with us. Businesses see the job, not you. Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick.

## Home · fair 2 (homeowner)
> Nobody pays to be first. Quotes show in the order they came in. Laftly doesn't rank or rate businesses, and no business can pay for a better spot.

## Home · fair 3 (homeowner)
> Asking costs you nothing. Laftly is a quote-request service; the business you choose pays Laftly a set introduction fee. That's $25, $75 or $199, by quote size. You pay Laftly nothing. If no quote fits, close the request.

## Home · business title (business)
> Quote for free. Pay only if a homeowner picks you.

## Home · business text (business)
> When a homeowner sends a job you do, we email you the details and you decide whether to quote. Every card says how many businesses got it. You quote once. No bidding rounds, no paid placement.

## Home · business fee (business)
> If the homeowner picks you, you pay a fixed introduction fee by job size, never a share of the job.
> $25 for a quote under $500. $75 for $500 to $1,500. $199 for over $1,500.

## Home · business line (business, fixed)
> Job requests and picks come by email. We don't text or call businesses. (WEB-024)

## Home · who runs Laftly (homeowner, fixed)
> Laftly is run by Laftly LLC, a Lake Havasu City business. Its owner lives in Havasu. The owner has a full-time day job. Sign-up reviews are usually answered within 4 days, help and charge questions usually within 2 days, and this page shows when the owner is away. (WEB-022, WEB-021)

## Home · 911 note (homeowner, fixed)
> Laftly isn't an emergency service and doesn't judge what needs an emergency response. If your message mentions gas, smoke, fire, a CO alarm going off or a downed power line, we send one automatic reminder to call 911. Don't rely on Laftly in an emergency: call 911. (WEB-022)

## Footer · tagline (homeowner, fixed)

## Footer · not a contractor (homeowner, fixed)
