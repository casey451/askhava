# Site copy

The approved wording for askhava.com, block by block (WORD-029, D-474). Page templates take their words from here; `scripts/check_copy.py` checks every block not marked `fixed`, and the copy panel (`docs/copy/copy-panel.md`) reviews every change. Blocks marked `fixed` are wording the spec sets word for word, cited by requirement.

**Status: home page draft, after the copy panel's first review (October 28, 2026). Waiting for Casey's OK before the rest of the site is written to match.** The texting number shows only after Gate C (WEB-017); until then the start line names the chat only.

## Home · kicker (homeowner)
> For homes in Lake Havasu City

## Home · headline (homeowner)
> Get quotes from Havasu businesses without giving out your number.

## Home · lede (homeowner)
> Tell us what needs doing, and we'll ask around for you. Havasu businesses that do that work send their quotes, and you see them on one page. Pick one, or none.

## Home · buttons (homeowner)
> Get quotes. See how it works.

## Home · start line (homeowner)
> Start in the chat on this site, or text the Ask Hava number. That number is text-only.

## Home · texting consent line (homeowner, fixed)
> Texting Ask Hava is your OK to get automated texts about your own request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Terms and Privacy. (CON-005; carrier rules)

## Home · cost line (homeowner, fixed)
> Free for you. The business you pick pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee. (CON-003)

## Home · step 1 (homeowner)
> Tell us what you need. Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.

## Home · step 2 (homeowner)
> Havasu businesses quote. It goes to Havasu businesses that take this kind of job. Each one has 24 hours to quote. Every quote lists the same details: the price, what's included and when they can come. Not every business answers.

## Home · step 3 (homeowner)
> You pick one. Check their license and reviews, then choose. You hire the business directly, or you can pick none.

## Home · fair kicker (homeowner)
> How we keep it fair

## Home · fair 1 (homeowner)
> Your number stays with us. Businesses see the job, not you. Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick.

## Home · fair 2 (homeowner)
> Nobody pays to be first. Quotes show in the order they came in. Ask Hava doesn't rank or rate businesses, and no business can pay for a better spot.

## Home · fair 3 (homeowner)
> Asking costs you nothing. Ask Hava is a quote-request service; the business you choose pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee. That's $25, $75 or $199, by quote size. You pay Ask Hava nothing. If no quote fits, close the request.

## Home · coverage title (homeowner)
> Pools, AC, boats, yards and more

## Home · coverage line (homeowner)
> We're adding more. Send us any job and we'll tell you if we cover it. See the full list.

The coverage list itself comes from the catalog's open categories, never typed by hand, and never names pest control on the home page (WEB-010).

## Home · business title (business)
> Quote for free. Pay only if a homeowner picks you.

## Home · business text (business)
> When a homeowner sends a job you do, we email you the details and you decide whether to quote. Every card says how many businesses got it. You quote once. No bidding rounds, no paid placement.

## Home · business fee (business)
> If the homeowner picks you, you pay a fixed introduction fee by job size, never a share of the job.
> $25 for a quote under $500. $75 for $500 to $1,500. $199 for over $1,500.

## Home · business line (business, fixed)
> Job requests and picks come by email. We don't text or call businesses. (WEB-024)

## Home · who runs Ask Hava (homeowner, fixed)
> Ask Hava is run by Ask Hava LLC, a Lake Havasu City business. Its owner lives in Havasu. The owner has a full-time day job. Sign-up reviews are usually answered within 4 days, help and charge questions usually within 2 days, and this page shows when the owner is away. (WEB-022, WEB-021)

## Home · 911 note (homeowner, fixed)
> Ask Hava isn't an emergency service and doesn't judge what needs an emergency response. If your message mentions gas, smoke, fire, a CO alarm going off or a downed power line, we send one automatic reminder to call 911. Don't rely on Ask Hava in an emergency: call 911. (WEB-022)

## Footer · tagline (homeowner, fixed)
> Local quotes. Your choice. (WEB-004)

## Footer · not a contractor (homeowner, fixed)
> Ask Hava is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work. We pass along your request and show you quotes from independent local businesses, as they wrote them. You choose, and you hire the business directly. (WORD-002, WEB-015)
