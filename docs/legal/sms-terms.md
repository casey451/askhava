# Ask Hava SMS Terms

These terms cover texts between you and Ask Hava LLC about your own request for quotes. They are part of the Ask Hava Homeowner Terms.

## 1. The program

Ask Hava is a quote-request service; the business the homeowner chooses pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee. You text the Ask Hava number (or start on askhava.com) to ask for quotes on a job at your home. We send automated texts about that request only: confirmation that it was sent, when quotes arrive, a link to your private quote page, confirmation of the business you choose, the name of the business that will call you, check-ins and reminders about that request. We send no marketing texts. Businesses never text you from the Ask Hava number.

## 2. How you agree

Your first message gets our consent text:

> Ask Hava: Got your message. Ask Hava LLC is a local quote service in Lake Havasu City, not a contractor. We pass your request to local businesses, and you hire the one you choose directly. Free for you; the business you choose pays Ask Hava a set fee of $25 to $199, by quote size. Our texts are automated. Msg frequency varies by request. Msg & data rates may apply. You must be 18 or older. Reply OK to agree to the Terms (askhava.com/terms) and to get automated texts about your request. Privacy: askhava.com/privacy. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Replying OK (or yes, sure and similar) agrees to the Terms and to get automated texts about your request. We keep the time, your exact reply and the version of the consent text you saw. If your message mentions danger words, we send an automatic safety reminder first. Don't rely on Ask Hava in an emergency: call 911.

You must be 18 or older. Consent is not a condition of buying anything; Ask Hava is free for you, and you can also use askhava.com and email instead of texts.

## 3. How often

Message frequency varies by request. Most requests get a handful of texts over a few days. Texts that aren't urgent wait for 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; safety texts and texts on a job you marked as an emergency may come at any hour.

## 4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Ask Hava charges you nothing. Canadian numbers work, and roaming charges may apply.

## 5. Help

Text HELP (or INFO, or AYUDA in Spanish) at any time, even after opting out. The reply:

> Ask Hava (Ask Hava LLC), Lake Havasu City: Tell us what's wrong in one text. The Ask Hava owner gets HELP messages right away and usually replies within 2 days, or within 24 hours if your job is urgent. Help: askhava.com/help or help@askhava.com. Can't open links? Reply LIST to get your quotes by text. This number is text-only. If anyone is in danger, call 911. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.

## 6. Stopping texts

Text STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE (or PARAR, ALTO, CANCELAR or BAJA) on its own, or tell us in your own words, such as "please stop texting me". You'll get one reply and nothing more:

> Ask Hava: You're unsubscribed. You won't get more texts or automated calls from Ask Hava at this number. To use Ask Hava again, text START.

You can also opt out by emailing help@askhava.com, replying to any Ask Hava email, using the help form on askhava.com, or switching texts off on your quote page. Every opt-out goes on one list at once and is never applied later than 10 business days. Opting out doesn't close your request; it continues on your quote page and by email.

## 7. Starting again

Text START or UNSTOP. A "yes" alone does not restart texts. The reply:

> Ask Hava: You can text us again. Send your request to start. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Your next request starts with the consent text.

## 8. Your number

Ask Hava never gives your phone number to any business, and never sells or shares phone numbers or texting permission for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

## 9. Carriers

Carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages. If texting is down, a banner on askhava.com says so, and you can use the chat on askhava.com and your quote page.
