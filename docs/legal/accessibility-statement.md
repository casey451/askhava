# Ask Hava Accessibility Statement

## Our target

Ask Hava aims for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.2 Level AA, on askhava.com, the homeowner quote pages and the business portal. Every page is tested with automated checks (axe-core) at phone, tablet and desktop widths, and with keyboard-only use, before it goes live.

## How the site is built

- Pages are built to work without JavaScript, with screen readers, when zoomed and with larger text spacing, and these are checked automatically.
- Every business notice is an email with a plain-text part and also shows in the portal, so no notice needs a phone call or audio.
- Homeowners can use Ask Hava by text, by the chat on askhava.com, or by email and their quote page.
- Masked calls between a homeowner and the business they chose work with 711 relay services.

## Known limits

- The Ask Hava number is text-only. A call to it hears a short recorded message asking you to text; there is no voicemail.
- Spanish pages and texts are drafts until a native speaker reviews them.
- [Any other known limit found in testing before launch.]

## Contact us

Tell us about any barrier by text (text HELP to the Ask Hava number), by email at help@askhava.com, or with the help page's form. If you use a relay service, you can reach us by text or email. The Ask Hava owner usually replies within 2 days, the same as our HELP replies.
