# Ask Hava Homeowner Terms

These terms are between you and Ask Hava LLC, a Lake Havasu City business ("Ask Hava", "we", "us"). They cover your use of Ask Hava by text, on askhava.com and on your quote page.

## 1. What Ask Hava is

Ask Hava is a quote-request service; the business the homeowner chooses pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee.

Ask Hava is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work. We pass along your request and show you quotes from independent local businesses, as they wrote them. You choose, and you hire the business directly.

The business you choose pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee ($25, $75 or $199, by quote size). You pay Ask Hava nothing.

We don't recommend, rank or rate any business, and we don't price, schedule, supervise, inspect or guarantee any work.

## 2. Who can use Ask Hava

- You must be 18 or older.
- Ask Hava is for jobs at homes in the Arizona towns we list on askhava.com, starting with Lake Havasu City.
- You may ask for quotes for a home you live in or look after. If you give us someone else's contact details, make sure they're OK with it.

## 3. How it works

1. **You send a request** by text or on askhava.com. We ask a few questions about the job.
2. **We send a description of the job,** without your name, phone number, email or street address, to local businesses that do that kind of work and have signed up.
3. **Businesses quote in their own words.** Your quote page shows every eligible quote, numbered in the order received; the number is arrival order, not a ranking. We never change a quote's amount or words. A quote is "eligible" when it follows our rules: no contact details in it, the business still holds any license the job needs, a handyman quote stays under Arizona's handyman limit, and it arrived inside the quote window.
4. **You choose one quote,** or none. Choosing is free, and you don't have to choose.
5. **The business confirms, and pays its introduction fee when one is due.** Only then does Ask Hava give that one business your first name and the job address, as you asked when you picked, plus your email if you chose to share it.
6. **The business contacts you** through a masked Ask Hava number, between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. Arizona time (any hour on an emergency job), and you two set the time. Ask Hava doesn't schedule anything. Ask Hava never gives your number to any business. If a business asks for it on the call, that's your choice.
7. If the business doesn't confirm in time, or it falls through, you can choose another quote.

## 4. Your agreement is with the business

You and the business make your own agreement; Ask Hava is not part of it and doesn't guarantee the work.

- Get the price, the scope and the start date in writing from the business.
- Arizona law requires a licensed contractor to give you a written contract for many jobs over $1,000 (A.R.S. 32-1158). Ask for one.
- Businesses marked "Not a licensed contractor" may only do jobs under $1,000 total (labor and materials) that need no permit and involve no gas, hardwired smoke alarms or fire sprinklers (A.R.S. 32-1121(A)(14)).
- You pay the business directly for the work. Ask Hava never handles payment for the work.

## 5. What we check, and what we don't

- **Licensed trades:** we check the business's license status on the state's records each day and show the date we last checked, for example "ROC #<number> showed Active on the ROC website when we checked on <date>. Check it yourself."
- **We don't check** workmanship, background, references, reviews or insurance. Google and Yelp links on a quote are links the business gave us; we don't rate or rank anyone. Insurance shown on a quote is what the business told us ("Insurance (business-reported)").
- Check any business yourself before you hire it. For licensed contractors, the Arizona Registrar of Contractors takes complaints; our help page links to its complaint form at askhava.com/help/roc-complaint. Handymen are not licensed by the Registrar.

## 6. Not an emergency service

Ask Hava is not an emergency service and does not watch messages around the clock. If your message mentions danger words, we send an automatic safety reminder. Don't rely on Ask Hava in an emergency: call 911. If you smell gas, see smoke or anyone is in danger, leave and call 911.

## 7. Texts and emails

Our texts are automated. When you text Ask Hava, or give us your mobile number in the chat on askhava.com and type the code we send to it, you agree to get automated texts about your own request; you don't need to reply to agree. Quote updates come by text unless you switch to email, or both, on your quote page, and every update links to your private quote page. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time. You can also opt out by emailing help@askhava.com, using the help form, or switching texts off on your quote page. The details are in the Ask Hava SMS Terms.

## 8. Electronic signatures

You accept these terms only when you pick a quote, on your quote page or by text. On the quote page, next to the Pick button the page says "By picking, you agree to the Terms", with a link to them. A text that lists your quotes ends with the same line and a link to askhava.com/terms; you pick by texting back the quote's number and YES, for example 2 YES.

Your first text, our consent text and any other reply never accept these terms. Picking a quote after that line is an electronic signature with the same effect as signing on paper, under the Arizona Electronic Transactions Act (A.R.S. 44-7007) and the federal E-SIGN Act. We keep a record of the terms version you accepted, its exact text, the language, whether you picked on the page or by text, when, and for which request.

## 9. No warranties

Ask Hava gives no warranty of any kind. The service is provided as it is. We don't promise that a business will quote, confirm, call, show up or do good work, or that the service will always be available. Nothing in these terms is a home warranty, protection plan or service contract.

## 10. Limit of liability

**Limit of liability.** To the extent the law allows, Ask Hava's total liability to you for any claim about the service is limited to $100. This limit does not apply to gross negligence, intentional misconduct, or anything the law does not allow us to limit.

Nothing in these terms releases or waives any claim for personal injury.

## 11. Disputes

- Arizona law governs these terms.
- Any court case goes to the courts in Mohave County, Arizona. Either of us may use small claims court.
- Before going to court, please write to us at help@askhava.com so we can try to fix it.
