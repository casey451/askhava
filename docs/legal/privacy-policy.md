# Laftly Privacy Policy

Effective [date]. Laftly LLC, [mailing address: P.O. box or registered agent, never a home address], Lake Havasu City, Arizona. Contact: help@laftly.com.

This policy is also our privacy notice. It is for the people who use Laftly.

## 1. Who we are

Laftly is a quote-request service; the business the homeowner chooses pays Laftly a set introduction fee.

Laftly is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work. We pass along your request and show you quotes from independent local businesses, as they wrote them. You choose, and you hire the business directly.

## 2. What we collect

- **From homeowners and tenants:** your phone number and what you tell us about the job. We also collect your town and ZIP, your first name and the job address, and, if you choose, your email, photos of the job and pictures of the look you want.
- **From businesses:** business and license details from public records. Team members' names, emails and phones. Notice settings and payment details. Our payment processor, Stripe, holds card details, not Laftly.
- **Automatically:** records of messages and calls (who, when and how long). We never record calls. For businesses, when each notice email was sent and delivered, and when each of its own links was first opened. Basic website logs. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or email open tracking.

We never ask for Social Security numbers, driver's licenses, immigration status or ID images.

## 3. How we use it

We use it to run your request. We send a description of the job to local businesses and show you their quotes. We pass your choice to the business you choose. We send you texts and emails about your request. We also use it to keep the service safe, handle help requests and disputes, and meet legal duties. We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't send marketing texts.

## 4. Who sees what, and when

### When you pick a business

When you send a request, Laftly sends a description of the job, with your name, phone number, email and street address removed, to local businesses that do that kind of work. If you send photos, those businesses also see them, with location data removed; we ask you to send only photos that show no person or house number, and once our AI photo screening is on, any faces, house numbers, license plates or readable text are blurred as well. Only after you pick a business, and it confirms (and pays its introduction fee, when one is due), does Laftly give that one business your first name and the job address, plus your email if you chose to share it and your photos at full size, with location data removed. Laftly never gives your phone number to any business, and never sells or shares phone numbers or texting permission for marketing.

### Who sees what at each step

| Who | What they see | When |
|---|---|---|
| Local businesses in that trade | The job description with your name, phone, email and street address removed. Your town and timing. Your answers to fixed questions. Any photos you sent, with location data removed. Once AI photo screening is on, faces, house numbers, license plates and readable text are blurred. | When the request goes out |
| Anyone on laftly.com | Your feedback, with your first name and town, only if you ticked the box allowing it | After your job is done |

Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick. Laftly never gives your number to any business. If a business asks for it on the call, that's your choice.

The business you pick may use your details only for your job and follow-up about it. Our business terms forbid marketing lists and resale.

**Vendors** work under contract, only to run Laftly. They provide texting and calls (Twilio), email (Resend) and one-time codes sent by email (Twilio SendGrid, which sees the email address we send each code to). They also provide payments (Stripe), hosting and its 30-day logs (Railway) and encrypted backup storage (Backblaze B2). Other vendors provide error monitoring (Sentry), letters for business identity checks (Lob) and website delivery and security (Cloudflare). Our AI vendor provides automated text reading (Anthropic; see section 6) only once it is switched on.

**Legal demands.** We'll tell you about a legal demand for your data unless the law forbids it.

## 5. Text messages

You choose to text us: your first text, or giving us your mobile number in the chat on laftly.com and typing the code we send to it, is your OK to get automated texts about your own request, and we text you only about that request. We keep the time of that first message and the version of our first reply as proof. Quote updates come by text unless you switch to email, or both, on your quote page; if you give an email for updates, we use it only for your request and never share it with businesses unless you choose email contact when you pick. Every update email has an unsubscribe link. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time.

You can also opt out by emailing help@laftly.com, replying to any Laftly email, using the help form on laftly.com, or switching texts off on your quote page. Every opt-out goes on one list at once and stops all texts and automated calls from Laftly to that number. To use Laftly by text again, text START.

Your phone number and your texting permission are never shared with any business or third party, and your texts are never used for marketing.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Laftly never sells or rents lists of people, phone numbers or texting consent.

## 7. Photos

Photos are optional; a request goes out the same way without them. When you send one, our software removes its hidden data (location, device and time) before it is stored or shown to anyone. Our own server, not an outside AI, checks it for faces, people, house numbers and license plates. It blurs or leaves out what it finds. You see each photo as businesses will see it and can remove it before your card goes out. Photos you keep go on your job card to the businesses that get it. Please leave people, house numbers and license plates out of photos. A photo sent by text is copied to our storage and deleted from our texting provider.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Your contact details** (name, email, address): deleted 180 days after your job closes, longer only while a complaint or dispute is open.
- **Photos:** deleted 30 days after your job closes.
- **Job descriptions** with contact details removed: 3 years.
- **Your phone number, locked (encrypted), with your texting permission and opt-out records:** 5 years after our last message to that number, only as proof of your texting choices. An opted-out number is kept as long as needed to honor the opt-out.
- **Payment, ledger and tax records, and our audit log:** 7 years.
- **Records of which terms you accepted, and each version of the terms:** 7 years after the last pick or charge made under them, with no contact details.
- **Website request logs** (request IDs, pages and status codes, no contact details): 30 days, in our own logs and our host's.
- **Business records, team phones and call logs:** [days, set before launch and shown on the business data page].

## 9. Your choices

You can see and change your details (properties, language, texts on or off) on your homeowner page. You can also get a copy there: "Download my data" gives you a file of your requests, quotes, messages and the texting-permission records we still hold. You can ask us to delete your data there or by emailing help@laftly.com. Before we act on a delete or copy request sent by email or the help form, we text a code to the number on file and you type it back, so no one else can ask in your name. We answer each request within 30 days. When we delete, we keep only what the law or proof of your texting choices requires, without your other details. We keep your phone number, locked, for 5 years only as proof of your texting permission and opt-out.

## 10. How we protect it

We encrypt contact details field by field, and photos, before they are stored. We limit which parts of our software can read them and log every access. We keep backups encrypted and use multi-factor sign-in for our own accounts. The business you pick sees your first name and the job address only after it signs in to its Laftly account. We never put them in an email or a text. Our website uses an encrypted connection. Ordinary text messages and email are not end-to-end encrypted. Your mobile carrier, our texting provider or your email provider may keep copies. Please share sensitive details on laftly.com rather than by text. Card numbers are held by Stripe, not by Laftly. No system is perfect. We look into any suspected unauthorized access to personal information. We tell affected people and government authorities when the law requires it. We may also tell you when we think it would help protect you.

## 11. Other people's details

If you give us someone else's contact details, make sure they're OK with it. We use them only for your job, never text them, and delete them with your other details.

## 12. Children

Laftly is only for people 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has contacted us, we stop the request. If we learn we have information from a child under 13, we delete it, except the record of a texting opt-out.

## 13. Do Not Track

We don't track you across other websites. So a browser's Do Not Track signal changes nothing: we don't track anyone across other websites.

## 14. Businesses

The business data page explains what we keep about businesses and team members, and for how long. It also covers who can see it and the notice-email and timing records we keep for each business. Call logs hold no recordings, and Laftly never asks about workers. Business data goes to police or others only when a law requires it. The business data page says: We'll tell you about any legal demand for your business's data unless the law forbids it.

## 15. Changes

We'll post any change here with its effective date. For an important change, we'll tell you before it applies to information we already have. We never start using information you already gave us for a new purpose, such as training AI, without telling you and asking first.
