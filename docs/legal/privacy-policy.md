# Ask Hava Privacy Policy

Effective [date]. Ask Hava LLC, [mailing address: P.O. box or registered agent, never a home address], Lake Havasu City, Arizona. Contact: help@askhava.com.

This policy also serves as Ask Hava's privacy notice to the people who use it.

## 1. Who we are

Ask Hava is a quote-request service; the business the homeowner chooses pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee.

Ask Hava is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work. We pass along your request and show you quotes from independent local businesses, as they wrote them. You choose, and you hire the business directly.

## 2. What we collect

- **From homeowners and tenants:** your phone number; what you tell us about the job; the town and ZIP; your first name and the job address; and, if you choose, your email, an on-site contact and photos.
- **From businesses:** business and license details from public records, team members' names, emails and phones, notice settings, and payment details (card details are held by our payment processor, Stripe, not by Ask Hava).
- **Automatically:** message and call records (who, when, how long; we never record calls), the times a business's notice emails were sent, delivered and their own links first opened, and basic website logs. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or email open tracking.

We never ask for Social Security numbers, driver's licenses, immigration status or ID images.

## 3. How we use it

To run your request: send a description of the job to local businesses, show you their quotes, pass your choice to the business you choose, send you texts and emails about your request, keep the service safe, handle help requests and disputes, and meet legal duties. We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't send marketing texts.

## 4. Who sees what, and when

When you send a request, Ask Hava sends a description of the job, with your name, phone number, email and street address removed, to local businesses that do that kind of work. Only after you pick a business, and it confirms and pays its introduction fee, does Ask Hava give that one business your first name and the job address, plus the on-site contact and email if you chose to share them. Ask Hava never gives your phone number to any business, and never sells or shares phone numbers or texting permission for marketing.

| Who | What they see | When |
|---|---|---|
| Local businesses in that trade | The job description with your name, phone, email and street address removed; town; timing; your answers to fixed questions | When the request goes out |
| The one business you pick | Your first name and the job address, plus the on-site contact and email if you chose to share them | Only after you pick it and it confirms and pays its introduction fee |
| No business, ever | Your phone number | Never. Calls go through a masked Ask Hava number |
| Vendors that run Ask Hava for us | Only what each needs to do its job | While providing the service, under their data-processing terms |
| Police, courts or others | Only what a law requires, such as a subpoena or warrant | Only when the law requires it |

Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick. Ask Hava never gives your number to any business. If a business asks for it on the call, that's your choice.

The business you pick may use your details only for your job and follow-up about it. Our business terms forbid marketing lists and resale.

**Vendors** (under contract, only to run Ask Hava): texting and calls (Twilio), email (Resend), payments (Stripe), hosting (Railway), error monitoring (Sentry), letters for business identity checks (Lob), and, only once it is switched on, automated text reading (Anthropic; see section 6).

**Legal demands.** We'll tell you about a legal demand for your data unless the law forbids it.

## 5. Text messages

You choose to text us, and we text you only about your own request. Message frequency varies by request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out at any time.

You can also opt out by emailing help@askhava.com, replying to any Ask Hava email, using the help form on askhava.com, or switching texts off on your quote page. Every opt-out goes on one list at once and stops all texts and automated calls from Ask Hava to that number. To use Ask Hava by text again, text START.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Ask Hava never sells or rents lists of people, phone numbers or texting consent.

## 7. Photos

Photos are optional. When you send one, our software removes its hidden data (location, device and time) before it is stored or shown to anyone. Please avoid photos of people. Photos aren't placed on job cards until the AI screening above is in use.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Your contact details** (name, email, address, on-site contacts): deleted 180 days after your job closes, longer only while a complaint or dispute is open.
- **Photos:** deleted 30 days after your job closes.
- **Job descriptions** with contact details removed: 3 years.
- **Your phone number, locked (encrypted), with your texting permission and opt-out records:** 5 years after our last message to that number, only as proof of your texting choices.
- **Payment, ledger and tax records, and our audit log:** 7 years.
- **Business records, team phones and call logs:** [days, set before launch and shown on the business data page].

## 9. Your choices

You can see and change your details (properties, on-site contacts, language, texts on or off) on your homeowner page, and ask us to delete your data there or by emailing help@askhava.com. When we delete, we keep only what the law or proof of your texting choices requires, without your other details. We keep your phone number, locked, for 5 years only as proof of your texting permission and opt-out.

## 10. How we protect it

We encrypt contact details field by field, limit which parts of our software can read them, log every access, keep backups encrypted, and use multi-factor sign-in for our own accounts. Card numbers are held by Stripe, not by Ask Hava. No system is perfect. If your information is exposed in a way that could harm you, we'll tell you by email or text.

## 11. Other people's details

If you give us someone else's contact details, such as an on-site contact or a landlord, make sure they're OK with it. We use them only for your job, never text them, and delete them with your other details.

## 12. Children

Ask Hava is only for people 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has contacted us, we stop the request. If we learn we have information from a child under 13, we delete it, except the record of a texting opt-out.

## 13. Do Not Track

We don't track you across other websites, so we treat browsers' Do Not Track signals the same as everyone else's: no cross-site tracking.

## 14. Businesses

The business data page explains what we keep about businesses and team members and for how long, who can see it, that call logs hold no recordings, that Ask Hava never asks about workers, and the notice-email and timing records we keep for each business. Business data goes to police or others only when a law requires it, and the business data page says: We'll tell you about any legal demand for your business's data unless the law forbids it.

## 15. Changes

We'll post any change here with its effective date, and for an important change we'll tell you before it applies to information we already have. We never start using information you already gave us for a new purpose, such as training AI, without telling you and asking first.
