# Ask Hava Business Email Notice Terms

These terms explain the emails Ask Hava LLC sends to businesses that signed up for job requests. They are part of the Ask Hava Business Terms.

## 1. Your consent

At sign-up you tick an unchecked box that gives your consent, as an electronic record under the federal E-SIGN Act and the Arizona Electronic Transactions Act, to get job and account notices by email. We store the box's wording version and when you ticked it. Each team member gives the same consent at first sign-in. Ask Hava never places recorded or automated calls to you.

Job requests and picks come by email. We don't text or call businesses. If Ask Hava ever offers business texting, you'll choose email, text or both, and texts go only to phones whose owner ticks their own box.

## 2. What we send

| Email | Who gets it | When |
|---|---|---|
| Job card | The owner and every "Cards and quotes" member | When a matching request goes out (held to 8 a.m. outside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., except EMERGENCY cards to people who turned on "Emergency emails at any hour") |
| Open-cards email | Each person who gets cards, at up to two times a day they choose | Only when the business has cards not yet quoted, or a pick waiting |
| Pick notice | The owner and every "Confirm and billing" member | When a homeowner chooses your quote |
| Confirm reminders | The same people | Every business hour until you confirm or the window ends, each with the Hold link |
| Release (job confirmed; the homeowner's details are only in your signed-in portal, never in the email) | The owner and every "Confirm and billing" member | After you confirm and pay |
| Receipts, refunds and charge answers | The owner and every "Confirm and billing" member | With each charge or refund |
| Account notices | The owner | Sign-up, license, pause, complaint and terms changes |
| Monthly missed-cards email | The owner | On the 1st, only when the business got at least 3 cards last month and half or more ended without a quote |

Every notice email comes from "Ask Hava" <jobs@notify.askhava.com>, has a plain-text part, and carries a footer with Ask Hava LLC's postal address, a "Manage email notices" link and the line "You get these emails because your business signed up for job requests from Ask Hava." Notice emails never contain promotions.

Ask Hava emails come only from addresses ending in askhava.com, never ask for your card or a code, and every button goes to askhava.com. Opening a link only shows a page; nothing happens until you press that page's button.

## 3. Managing notices

- In the portal, each person can set their open-cards times, add a backup address, mark an inbox as shared and turn on "Emergency emails at any hour".
- Each person can turn job-card emails off only while the business still gets them another way (another team member, or text).
- Pausing job cards or setting "Busy" stops cards for your business. Turning off an email is never an opt-out of anything else.
- Changes take effect at once.

## 4. Making sure our emails reach you

- At sign-up we send a sample card and a sample pick and ask whether they arrived and whether your phone alerted you, with Do Not Disturb or Sleep on. Skip this and you may miss picks. A pick you don't confirm in 4 business hours may go to another business.
- In your notice log: Delivered means your mail server accepted it; it may still be in Spam.
- If emails to an address bounce or are marked as spam, we stop sending to it until it is verified again, show a banner in the portal and email your other addresses.

## 5. Records we keep

For each notice we keep its send time, the provider's delivery status, and when its own link was first opened (we never use tracking pixels or click redirects; a first opening may be a mail scanner), and when you quoted or confirmed. You see your own records in your notice log. The business data page says how long we keep them.

## 6. Recruiting emails

Ask Hava never texts or calls a business to recruit it. If we email a business that hasn't signed up, the email says who we are, carries our postal address and has a working way to stop further recruiting emails, honored at once.
