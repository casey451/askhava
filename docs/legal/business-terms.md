# Ask Hava Business Terms

These terms are between your business ("you") and Ask Hava LLC, a Lake Havasu City business ("Ask Hava", "we", "us"). They apply when your business signs up for job requests from Ask Hava.

Ask Hava is not a contractor. It passes along this request and the homeowner's choice. If the homeowner picks you, they hire you directly; Ask Hava doesn't direct, schedule, supervise or pay for the work.

## 1. What Ask Hava is

Ask Hava is a quote-request service; the business the homeowner chooses pays Ask Hava a set introduction fee.

Businesses on Ask Hava are independent. Ask Hava never directs, controls, schedules, supervises, inspects, warrants or pays for your work, never sets or changes your prices, and never negotiates for you or the homeowner. There is no subcontract, employment, agency, partnership or joint venture between you and Ask Hava. You and the homeowner make your own agreement, and Ask Hava is not part of it. The introduction fee pays only for the introduction service: a fixed amount by job size ($25, $75 or $199), never a percentage or share of the job's price, and it doesn't depend on whether the work is done, finished or paid for. The refunds are the ones these terms list, and Ask Hava may also refund any charge at its own choice. No refund is owed because the work wasn't done or paid for.

## 2. Joining

- Joining is free. No card is needed at sign-up.
- Your license class comes from the state record, never typed in. We confirm your identity with a code sent only to a contact on the public record (the state license record, or the Lake Havasu City business-license list for trades with no state license).
- A business in a trade with no state license must match the city's active business-license list.
- **Trades licensed by the Arizona Department of Agriculture:** you state at sign-up that you hold the active Department business license and give its number. Ask Hava does not check it, and homeowners are told so. A false statement gets you removed.
- **Handymen** (not licensed contractors) certify on each quote that it is a full-job price under $1,000 with no permit and no gas, smoke-alarm or sprinkler work. A business that breaks this is removed.
- You add your own team members and choose their roles. You're responsible for what your team does on Ask Hava.

## 3. Job requests and quotes

- Job requests come by email (and by text only if Ask Hava offers business texting and you choose it). Ask Hava never places recorded or automated calls to you. The Ask Hava Business Email Notice Terms explain the emails.
- Ask Hava never buys, imports, rents or resells requests from another site or list. Every job card comes from a homeowner who contacted Ask Hava directly by text or web chat.
- Each job card is anonymous: no homeowner name, phone, email or street address. Every card says how many businesses got it.
- You quote once. No bidding rounds, no paid placement.
- You may quote for 24 hours after your card is sent. A quote must not contain a phone number, a link, an email address or "call me" wording; such a quote bounces back to you.
- The homeowner sees every eligible quote, numbered in the order received, with your own words as you wrote them. Ask Hava never edits, rounds or converts your amount, never suggests a price and never passes a counter-offer. Only you can change your quote.
- Every quote for a job uses that job type's format (a range, a monthly price, up to 3 tiers, a firm price, or a diagnostic visit fee on repairs), so the homeowner can compare quotes side by side. Notes (up to 300 characters) must be about the job; notes with contact details, other channels or off-platform payment offers are sent back to you.
- You may edit or withdraw a quote until the homeowner picks.
- Your Google Business Profile link, Yelp link and license numbers, if you give them, show on every quote.
- Everything in your quote, your notes, your "About us" line and your quote facts must be true.

## 4. Picks and Confirm

- When a homeowner picks your quote, the owner and each "Confirm and billing" member get a pick email: "Ask Hava: A homeowner picked your quote for job <n>. Accept by <time>." To accept the introduction, tap Confirm and pay. If you can't take the job, tap "Can't take it": no fee, and the homeowner's backup choice, if any, gets the pick.
- On a regular job you have 4 business hours (8 a.m. to 8 p.m., every day) to confirm, counted from when the pick email is sent. Reminders go every business hour until the window ends. You can use "Hold for 2 more hours" once per pick. On a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, the window is 60 minutes at any hour.
- If you don't confirm in time, the homeowner may choose another quote.
- **Confirm and pay is one step.** The Confirm page shows the job number, the fee, its tier and how the fee basis was set, the refund line and the terms version, and one "Confirm and pay $<fee>" button ("Confirm" alone for a free first win). Your Confirm counts only when the payment succeeds.

## 5. The introduction fee

- **$25** when the fee basis is under $500.00; **$75** from $500.00 up to and including $1,500.00; **$199** over $1,500.00.
- The fee basis is your quote's amount; for a range, its midpoint; for an hourly rate, the midpoint of the rate times your estimated hours. A site-visit-needed quote (a trip fee plus an estimate on site, or an hourly rate with no estimate) pays the set tier for that category, which is $25 and never more than $75.
- A one-time setup charge in an ongoing-service quote is shown to the homeowner as you wrote it and is never part of the fee basis.
- **No introduction fee** for a home inspection, or a termite report, for a home sale, purchase or refinance. The Confirm page says "No introduction fee for this job."
- **Ongoing services:** a price with a period (weekly, monthly and so on) counts as 4 months of that price, and the fee is never more than $75.
- The fee is set from the chosen quote when the homeowner chooses, charged when you confirm, and never changed by the final bill.
- **Tax included.** Any tax Ask Hava owes on the fee is included in it. No tax is ever added on top.
- Launch offer: your first win is free, on any job size, when you join in the first 90 days or within 90 days after your trade opens. One free first win per business.
- Repeat picks from the same homeowner are free for 180 days after the job closes.
- **Price lock:** a business that joined in the first 90 days after launch pays no more than the fee amounts in effect on its join date for 6 months. After that, fees follow the published schedule, and a change needs your OK first. A new fee never applies until you accept it.
- **Monthly fee limit:** you may set one. A Confirm that would go over it is not charged, and that pick ends.

## 6. Card and charge authorization

Card details are entered in Stripe's card form on the Confirm page and are held by Stripe, not by Ask Hava. Only cards are accepted. Saving a card is optional.

By pressing "Confirm and pay" (and, if you tick "Save this card for next time", by saving it), you accept this authorization:

> I authorize Ask Hava LLC to save this card with Stripe for introduction fees. I'm charged only when I press Confirm and pay for a homeowner's choice of my quote, or when a homeowner picks a quote I set to auto-confirm, and the fee is shown before I press it or set it: $25, $75 or $199 by job size, ongoing services counted at 4 months and capped at $75. First win free if I join in the first 90 days or within 90 days after my trade opens. Refunds per the Terms. I can remove this card at any time.

Ask Hava never charges a card without your press of "Confirm and pay", and never retries a charge on its own. The one exception is optional and yours to choose: once Ask Hava offers it, you may tick "Auto-confirm and pay if picked" on a quote, which accepts this authorization for that quote only:

> I authorize Ask Hava LLC to charge my saved card $<fee> for this quote's introduction if the homeowner picks it, without another tap. I can untick this until the homeowner picks.

If that charge fails or your bank asks for approval, the pick goes to the normal Confirm page and window. If a card fails or needs bank approval, the Confirm page shows it at once and you can try again or use another card for 1 business hour (60 minutes on an emergency job).

## 7. Refunds

Refund if you can't reach them: 2 call attempts at least 3 hours apart, inside their window, within 72 hours. They can be on different days.

These are the automatic refunds, and each happens once, to the card that paid (Ask Hava may also refund any charge at its own choice, section 1):

1. **Couldn't reach the homeowner.** Decided at 96 hours after the release, when all of these are true: you made 2 call attempts at least 3 hours apart, within 72 hours, inside the window (8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Arizona time, any hour on an emergency job); no call in either direction was answered by a person; Ask Hava sent or attempted the missed-call text to the homeowner (a delivery failure never counts against you); the homeowner has not replied DONE or PROBLEM; and, when the homeowner chose email, the homeowner did not reply YES to the 72-hour "Did <business> email you and agree a time?" question. The decision email says which condition was or wasn't met.
2. **Homeowner cancelled first.** The homeowner cancels before you have talked to them (any call in either direction answered by a person counts as talking).
3. **Charged after a cancel or after the job moved on.** Anything charged after a cancel, or a payment that succeeds after the job moved on, is refunded.
4. **Our mistake.** If a charge was our mistake, it is refunded in full.

**No refund** when you never call within 72 hours of the release (when the homeowner chose a call), or when the homeowner reports at the day-3 check-in that you never reached them (when the homeowner chose email). That is recorded as a no-show.

**Questions about a charge:** use "Ask about a charge" in your portal or reply to any Ask Hava email. The Ask Hava owner usually replies within 2 days. A refund on a charge you've disputed with your bank is handled through that dispute instead.

## 8. The homeowner's details

As part of the introduction, Ask Hava gives you the homeowner's first name and the job address so you can contact them about this job. Use them only for this job and follow-up about it. No marketing lists, no resale.

- The homeowner's phone number is never given to you. Call on the masked Ask Hava number, and only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. Arizona time (any hour on an emergency job). You and the homeowner set the time of the visit; Ask Hava schedules nothing. Calls are never recorded.
- Don't pass the homeowner's details, or the job, to another business without the homeowner's OK.
- A homeowner can report a misuse of their details by replying PROBLEM.

## 9. Your responsibilities as an independent business

You alone are responsible for:

- your quotes, your prices and your agreement with the homeowner;
- every license, permit, bond and insurance the job needs, and keeping your license current;
- putting your ROC license number on every bid and contract, as A.R.S. 32-1124 requires, and giving a written contract where A.R.S. 32-1158 requires one;
- your workmanship, your workers, your taxes and your compliance with the law.

**Non-discrimination.** You may not refuse, price or treat a job differently because of a homeowner's race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, familial status, age or any other characteristic protected by law, or because the homeowner rents.

## 10. Indemnity and liability

- **Indemnity.** You will defend and indemnify Ask Hava against third-party claims arising from your work, your quotes and statements, your license status, your handling of homeowner details, or your breach of these terms. This does not apply to the extent a claim results from Ask Hava's sole negligence.
- **No warranties.** Ask Hava gives no warranty of any kind about the service, the number of job requests, or any homeowner. Ask Hava doesn't promise any number of job cards, quotes, picks or jobs.
- **Limit of liability.** To the extent the law allows, Ask Hava's total liability to you is limited to the introduction fees you paid in the 12 months before the claim. This limit does not apply to gross negligence, intentional misconduct, or anything the law does not allow us to limit.

## 11. Complaints, pauses and leaving

- If a homeowner complains, the complaint shows in your portal with its kind and date (no homeowner details) and a reply box open for 5 days. The homeowner gets an explanation that you are independent, your business phone and the Registrar of Contractors complaint link.
- Your business is paused only after 3 complaints in 90 days.
- A lapsed license or bond pauses you on that date. While a state lookup fails, you stay as you were for up to 72 hours but can't confirm a pick while your status is unknown.
- You can pause job cards, set "Busy for <n> days" or leave at any time in the portal. Leaving withdraws your open quotes, ends any waiting pick (the homeowner's backup choice applies), removes your saved cards and ends every sign-in; we keep billing records for 7 years.

**When Ask Hava may end your account.** Ask Hava may end your business's account for a false certification or statement, misuse of homeowner details, discrimination, unpaid charges, or a confirmed breach of the handyman certification. We email you the reason. When an account ends, these still apply: fees you owe, the limits on using homeowner details (section 8) and the indemnity (section 10).

## 12. Electronic records

You agree to receive these terms, receipts and notices electronically, and your clicks in the portal (accepting these terms, "Confirm and pay") are electronic signatures under the Arizona Electronic Transactions Act (A.R.S. 44-7007) and the federal E-SIGN Act. We keep a record of each version you accepted and when, and of each charge.
