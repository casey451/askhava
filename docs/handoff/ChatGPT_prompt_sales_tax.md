# Research task: Is Ask Hava's introduction fee subject to Arizona sales tax (TPT)?

You are a careful Arizona tax researcher. I need a clear answer, backed by the actual statutes, rules and ADOR rulings, on whether my business owes Arizona Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT) on the fee it charges. I'm not hiring a lawyer or CPA, so treat this as the research a tax professional would do, and show your sources.

## The business (facts to assume)

- **Name and place:** Ask Hava, an Arizona LLC (being formed) in Lake Havasu City, Mohave County, Arizona. It's online only, with no storefront and no visitors.
- **What it does:** a homeowner texts or web-chats a description of a home job, such as an AC repair or a pool cleaning. Ask Hava emails an anonymous job card (job type, general area, timing, no name or address) to local businesses that signed up. Businesses reply with quotes in their own words. Ask Hava shows the homeowner every quote, unchanged and unranked. The homeowner picks one.
- **The fee:** the business the homeowner picked confirms and pays Ask Hava a **fixed introduction fee**: $25 for quotes under $500, $75 for $500–$1,500, and $199 over $1,500. Ongoing services are valued as 4 months and capped at $75. Only after paying does the business get the homeowner's first name and address, plus a masked phone line to talk to them.
- **Who pays:** homeowners never pay Ask Hava anything. Businesses pay nothing to sign up, and no subscription. The only charge is the fee per introduction.
- **What Ask Hava never does:** it never sets or changes prices, never bids, never schedules or supervises work, never guarantees work, never handles the homeowner's payment for the job, never sells or rents lists or data, and never advertises any particular trade. The homeowner hires and pays the business directly.
- **Payment processing:** Stripe charges the business's card.

1. **Bottom line:** does Ask Hava owe Arizona TPT (state, Mohave County or Lake Havasu City) on these fees? Answer yes, no, or "unclear", and give your confidence level.

   - retail

   - online marketplace/remote seller rules (LR 17-011)
   - any advertising or publication classification

How strong is that argument? What wording or process changes would make it weaker? For example: describing the fee as an introduction service fee, or releasing contact details only as part of setting up the conversation.
4. **Similar businesses:** how do lead-generation or referral services like Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor and Networx handle Arizona TPT on their fees to service providers? Is there any public evidence, such as their tax help pages, rulings or court cases?
5. **What I should do:** if it isn't taxable, do I need to file anything at all? If the answer is unclear, what's the cheapest safe option?
