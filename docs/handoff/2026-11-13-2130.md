## Lessons (new)
- **Plan PRs that regenerate every prompt** (D-3474, D-3530) conflict with any plan PR that regenerates a single prompt. To resolve:
  1. Merge main.
  2. Take either side.
  3. Rerun `gen_prompts.py` with `kit/tasks.json` saved and restored, so stored budgets don't churn.
  4. Revert every regenerated prompt except the conflicted one.
- **`merge_main.py` refuses** a `kit/prompts/*.md` conflict by design. When resolving by hand, also move the branch's own D entry after main's new entries, or `check_pr_change` fails "append-only".
- **Resuming builders and reviewers** with SendMessage for fix rounds and round-2 reviews kept their context and cost far less than fresh agents.
