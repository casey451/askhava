## Lessons
1. **A flaky test burns attempts across unrelated tasks.** When two tasks fail on the same test they don't own, fix the test in a harness task at once (T559). Don't triage each task separately.
2. **Plan PRs chase main.** With 7 lanes, main moves every 10–20 minutes. `merge_main.py`, then merge as soon as CI is green and the edited tasks are still QUEUED. Waiting for "nothing MERGE_PENDING" never comes, so the gate was applied as "no edited task is in flight".
3. **A fix to a claimable task can lose the race** (T105 was claimed before #880 merged). Decide quickly, or plan it as the `--plan-fix` for the next attempt.
4. **Multi-round money reviews keep finding real bugs** (D-3999 rounds 1 and 2). Keep at least two rounds, and a third when round 2 finds something.
5. `delete_trigger` on a send_later check is refused by the auto-mode classifier. Use `update_trigger enabled=false` instead.
