## Lessons
1. **Plan gaps cluster right after merges that change shared readers or tests.** T487's scan cost T408 an attempt, and T477's reader cost T523 one. After a merge that adds a scan, an allow-list or a narrow reader, re-scout the QUEUED tasks that touch the same area.
2. **Failed attempts leave no branch on the remote** (T111 and T523: `pr: None`, `git ls-remote` empty). Every retry starts from scratch, so budget and size estimates must assume a full rebuild.
3. **"Order-only" dependencies often also encode ownership, Cargo or migration order.** Dropping three raw gave 56 `validate_tasks` errors. Always add replacement dependencies, and replay the migration ancestor check.
4. **Open-ended negative rules can't be satisfied by a word list.** Close the list in the plan, and tell the reviewer what counts as a finding.
5. **Fable as triage-plus-builder** in one agent (T523) was fast, and the Opus review still caught a wrong test entry point. Keep the independent review.
6. A QUEUED task picks up a merged plan fix at its next claim. `--requeue` is only for BLOCKED tasks.
