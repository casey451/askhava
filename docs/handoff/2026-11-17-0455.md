## Lessons
1. **Pre-claim checks of newly added tasks pay off at once.** 7 of 15 new audit tasks had gaps. T584 started before its fix landed and stopped cheaply (under 1M tokens) on exactly the predicted gap.
2. **A VM loss leaves the step `in_progress` and later steps `pending`, and the job log is often 404.** The runner returns the task to QUEUED with no attempt lost, but the session's tokens are gone. Count such losses with `list_workflow_runs` on backup-runner.yml (`failure` conclusions).
3. **When lanes sit idle with claimable tasks and the keeper sleeps for hours, dispatch `backup-runner.yml` once by hand.** That also clears dead leases.
4. **A dependency pin with a new advisory fails every hand-in that uses the crate.** Read the Checks job log (`cargo audit` lines) before triaging a "Ci/Checks failure" as the task's own fault.
5. **Docs/architecture.md section-11 edits on main can resync an in-flight task** that edits the same row (T221 after D-4070). That is cheap, but prefer leaving a row the in-flight owner will rewrite anyway.
