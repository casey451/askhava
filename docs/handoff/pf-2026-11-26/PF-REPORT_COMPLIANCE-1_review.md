## 1. TCPA, revocation and quiet hours

**Current law:** FCC 24-24 was published March 5, 2024. The [October 11, 2024 effective-date notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-10-11/html/2024-23605.htm) says the relevant provisions took effect “April 11, 2025.” [DA 25-312](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-312A1.pdf) deferred only the cross-category portion to April 11, 2026, and [DA 26-12](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-26-12A1.pdf) extended that to January 31, 2027. However, [FCC 26-67, 91 FR 67214](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/html/2026-21821.htm), published October 26, expressly states “Effective November 25, 2026.” It supersedes that deferred date. The amended rule permits designated exclusive revocation methods with disclosure; without designation, reasonable means still must be accepted. Informational revocation may be category-specific; marketing revocation covers future marketing. Laftly deliberately keeps broader global suppression. No universal every-text STOP instruction follows from this FCC amendment where an exclusive reply-text method is not selected; carrier rules remain separate.
