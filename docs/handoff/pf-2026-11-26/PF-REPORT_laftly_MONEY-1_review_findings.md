- **Ordinary repeated press:** `charge.rs:141–189,214–237,266–283` uses the pick-derived intent key, returns an existing intent, and reads a repeated press's attempt rather than intentionally sending another confirm. This is not proof of every concurrent interleaving.
- **Attempt persistence:** `charge.rs:617–641` writes the sent attempt, audit and scheduled check before the network request; `charge.rs:224–234` drops the connection before calling Stripe.

- **Paid release proof validation:** `repo/app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:216–225,415–438` checks stored intent ID, fetches Stripe, requires succeeded status, a valid charge ID, matching mode, USD amount and pick metadata. The ordinary webhook path's recovery gap is finding 1.
- **Late-success end in payments:** `repo/app/payments/src/handlers.rs:347–374` and `repo/app/payments/src/late.rs:75–110` check the payment-side end and enqueue a deduplicated refund/alert. The sink also handles a moved-on pick at `repo/app/server/src/release_sink.rs:167–175,231–239` when it receives a valid clearing.

This is sound for the ordinary known-dispute/known-refund path, not proof of every out-of-order event.
- **Unreachable refund eligibility (pure rules):** `repo/app/payments/src/refund_rules.rs:160–216` checks calls within 72 hours, calling hours/emergency exception, at least two attempts three hours apart, no human answer, required notice, and 96-hour decision time. `refund_rules.rs:236–256` separately implements cancel-before-talk and charge-after-cancel decisions.
