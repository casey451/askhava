# T026: messaging: English and Spanish template registry, the Spanish conversations setting and template scans

Task T026 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: messaging-templates.
Branch: `claude/task/T026-messaging-templates`

## Goal

`messaging` builds every text from a fixed English or Spanish template chosen by ID and language (Spanish only while the owner's Spanish conversations setting is on, drafts marked for review), every text begins "Ask Hava:", and a template scan fails on emoji, shortened or off-domain links, promotion wording or "reply CANCEL" (BIZ-018, TXT-001 to TXT-008, TXT-010, TXT-039, WORD-008).
