## Goal

`messaging` builds every text from a fixed English or Spanish template chosen by ID and language (Spanish only while the owner's Spanish conversations setting is on, drafts marked for review), every text begins "Ask Hava:", and a template scan fails on emoji, shortened or off-domain links, promotion wording or "reply CANCEL" (BIZ-018, TXT-001 to TXT-008, TXT-010, TXT-039, WORD-008). This task creates `app/messaging/templates/en.json` and `es.json` (shared paths: every later task adds only its own keys) and records a `segments` count beside each template for TXT-044.
