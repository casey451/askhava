## Goal

Register row IC-95, the email's Approve link (D-3081; BIZ-033, BIZ-038, SEC-038, SEC-004, SEC-025, SEC-026, SEC-048; D-1955, D-2128). After T396 (`businesses::api::approve_phone`), T364 (the served business step-up page) and T306 (the last earlier owner of `app/server/src/action_links.rs`). The owner email's Approve action link (`ActionKind::Approve`, `app/portals/src/action_links.rs:59`, served by T301) only uses up the link: nothing approves the phone. (1) `app/server/src/action_links.rs`: the Approve link's `POST`, with a business session of that link's own owner and a step-up passed in the last 5 minutes (`require_step_up` with `SensitiveChange::ApprovePhone`, checked before the transaction), calls `approve_phone` for the link's object (the line `businesses.phone_linked` names) with the phone's current `number_set_at` in the transaction that uses the link, and only when the link was issued at or after that `number_set_at`: a link issued before the last number change approves nothing and stays unused, and the page says a newer email is on its way (the change queued one, T398); an expired link gets the one not-found answer and approves nothing; without that session it sends the person to sign in, without the step-up to the step-up page, and in both cases the link stays unused and nothing is approved; a refused approval (not waiting, taken, unknown) leaves the link unused and says the phone could not be approved; every other kind is unchanged. (2) Tests in `app/server/tests/approve_link_route.rs` (TD-SEC-66) against the assembled router, links seeded as `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs` seeds them (D-1955's hash), fake data only. Update `app/server/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-3744: `portals.login_link.created_at` is not the issue time: `mint_action_link` stores it `LINK_LIFE` back so action links never count toward the sign-in link limits (`app/portals/src/action_links.rs:9`, `:268`), `ActionGrant` carries no time, and `server` may not read `portals` tables. So this task owns `app/portals/src/action_links.rs` (after T148 and T199, done; T457, its only queued owner, follows it through T463 and T456) and `app/portals/src/models.rs` (T084, T086 and T272, all done): a new `models` function beside `use_link` (which `app/portals/src/business_auth.rs:1166` also calls, unchanged) uses the link and returns its `created_at`, and `ActionGrant` gains the issue time (`created_at + LINK_LIFE`) behind a new `issued_at()` accessor, so `use_action_link` and `open_action_link` keep their signatures and no other caller changes. The Approve route compares `issued_at()` with `number_set_at` in the transaction that used the link and rolls it back when the link is older, so the link stays unused. TD-SEC-66 seeds its links with `portals::api::mint_action_link`, never raw SQL, so each link's stored time is the backdated one production writes. Plan change D-3889 (Fable's pre-claim check, Nov 16, 2026): a signed-out Approve `POST` now answers "sign in" (`signed_out()`, `app/server/src/action_links.rs:605`), which the unowned `td_sec_44_every_other_kind_post_is_not_found_and_uses_nothing` (`app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs:490` to `:506`) forbids, so this task owns that file (its later owners T456, T463, T457 and T458 all follow it) and adds `ActionKind::Approve` to `SESSION_ACTS` (`:487`), changing nothing else there. `app/server/CLAUDE.md` is at its 8,192-byte cap: its action-link line ("every other kind's `POST` is the bare 404") is reworded in place to name Approve among the session kinds without growing the file, and the detail goes in `app/server/NOTES.md`.
