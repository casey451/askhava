# T092: privacy: 180-day contact purge, holds, photo and message deletion, delete my data

Task T092 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: privacy-retention-purge.
Branch: `claude/task/T092-privacy-retention-purge`

## Goal

A daily `privacy` job purges contact details 180 days after close unless a hold exists (the phone kept encrypted with its consent records), photos at 30 days, scrubbed job text at 3 years, and ledger and audit records at 7 years; "Delete my data" removes the vault entry and photos and confirms what is kept (PRIV-007 to PRIV-011, PRIV-027). `privacy` calls only `adapters` and `ops`, so the purge runs on `core.task` events: `app/jobs/src/purge_events.rs` enqueues each job's close date for `privacy`; at purge time the `payments` handler this task owns turns an open dispute (T066's record) into a `privacy.retention_hold` through `privacy::api`; `businesses` may not call `privacy`, so `app/businesses/src/complaint_holds.rs` enqueues a hold event for each open complaint (T083's record) and a release event when it closes, which `privacy`'s handler applies; and the `intake`, `messaging` and `payments` handlers delete or keep their own rows (scrubbed job text, `messaging.inbox`, ledger rows without names) when `privacy` enqueues the purge (D-491). Terms-acceptance records and published terms versions are kept 7 years after the last pick or charge under them (PRIV-010, D-492).
