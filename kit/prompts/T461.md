## Goal

Plan change D-3676 (split from T302; BIZ-016, BIZ-019, BIZ-040, BIZ-049, BIZ-058, SEC-004; D-2128, D-3262, D-3602, D-3641): the rest of sign-up up to activation, in T302's `app/server/src/business_signup.rs`, on the existing route constants only (no new open path, so D-3641's list is unchanged). A correct email code activates an unflagged sign-up at once (`businesses::api::confirm`, `app/businesses/src/email_verification.rs:401`, calls `identity::settle`), and sign-up cannot finish without towns, a base ZIP and a radius (BIZ-058, `docs/spec/businesses.md:62`), so the order is: (1) `SERVICE_AREA_ROUTE` `POST` (the base ZIP and radius, `businesses::api::save_service_area`, BIZ-058, BIZ-049), allowed once the identity step is done; (2) `EMAIL_CHECK_ROUTE` `POST` (`/b/signup/email-code`), the emailed code step, refused until the service area is saved (`businesses::api::service_area` answers one) and before identity: `start_signup_check` sends it, `confirm_mailbox` (`email_verification.rs:363`'s `confirm`) checks it, a refused or expired code showing `Screen::EmailCode` with its problem; `EMAIL_RESEND_ROUTE` resends under the same rule. (3) Activation (BIZ-016): when the email step's `confirm` leaves the business `Active`, that response clears the `__Host-askhava-signup` cookie (`Max-Age=0`, same name and attributes), sends the owner the normal sign-in link to the verified sign-up email through the server's sign-in mailer (`business_signin::SignInMailer`; link plus code, SEC-004; no session is started without it) and shows the done screen (`Screen::Done` with `portals::api::done_facts`); when it leaves the business in `NeedsReview` (a flag), the done screen says so and the cookie stays, so nothing more is asked. Every later step reads the sign-up only from that verified cookie (T459's), never from a posted or path ID, and checks that the sign-up's stored state (its identity step, `businesses::api::Step`, code-sent and email-verified facts) allows that step now, and that the business's stored status (`businesses::api::AccountStatus`) is `Pending` or `NeedsReview`; a missing or bad binding (a wrong or altered MAC, ID or salt), a step out of order or a business in any other status is refused like any refusal (the screen again, no detail), changes nothing, sends nothing and calls no Verify (D-3641). The inbox check (BIZ-042) is not served here: it needs the owner's session and is T462's. Its rows in `docs/architecture.md` section 13 are marked `yes`. Tests: cases in `app/server/tests/business_signup_route.rs` (TD-SEC-45: the email code step is refused before the service area and before identity, accepts the right code and refuses a wrong one; the activating response clears the cookie and sends one sign-in email; a step taken after activation with a still-valid sign-up cookie is refused and changes nothing). Update the `server` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
