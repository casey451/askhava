## Goal

War games concurrency and overload (D-2054, from Casey's shared AI-capacity review, November 8, 2026): `wargames/overload.py` (Python, standard library only) runs one scenario on T234's local lane only (the stand-ins and fake data, so no real AI call and nothing of D-1154's $40 AI cap is spent), reusing T234's drivers, chaos injectors and latency recorder: at least 100 intake conversations at once, mixed web chat and inbound texts, with duplicate and replayed inbound webhooks among them, against at least 2 web processes and 2 worker processes (`app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`) sharing one database when the environment allows it (otherwise the summary records the process counts used and why). AI faults: the module starts a fault proxy on a loopback address in front of the whole stand-in server (`stand_ins/server.py`); every path passes through unchanged except the AI path (`/anthropic/v1/messages`), where, in bursts, it delays the reply or adds the stand-in's `x-stand-in-fault` header for a 429 with and without `retry-after`, a 529, a 500 or a 504, so no stand-in file changes. The app reaches the proxy through T298's `ASKHAVA_AI_BASE_URL` (D-2065), pointed at the proxy instead of the stand-in server (the stand-in client takes only an `http` loopback base URL, `app/adapters/src/ai.rs`, which the proxy keeps). T299 builds the AI client at start-up and calls it from the text and web-chat paths (IC-23, D-2065), and T237, which runs this scenario, waits for it; until T299 is on `main`, the AI-fault part and the AI figures are reported "not reachable" with that reason (as D-2030 (2) records what J1 cannot reach), and the rest of the scenario still runs on the AI-off path. Each run reports: the most AI calls in flight at once and the AI calls that reached the provider (both counted at the proxy) against the calls refused on a budget (outcome `budget` in `ai.call_stat`); turns that fell back on the AI-023 reply budget; p50, p95 and p99 (nearest rank) of the time to the next question; the database pool's highest use and p95 acquire wait (from T416's `GET /__test/perf/db` snapshot, one per process: the scenario adds up the snapshots of all its web processes, D-3292); the task-queue depth and oldest task age (`core.task`); and every duplicate outbound text and every duplicate AI call, both of which must be zero, any other count being an anomaly logged as T234 logs one (a duplicate AI call is an AI call caused by a duplicate or replayed delivery (TXT-012 de-duplicates by Twilio's message ID): per Twilio message ID, the AI calls beyond those its first delivery made, never counting an AI-023 retry or AI-011's second reading; calls are tied to their message through the call records' `subject_id`, or the count is "not exposed"). A figure the app does not expose is reported "not exposed", never guessed (T242 lists the smallest instrumentation for each). Fake data only (BUILD_BRIEF.md:66, PRIV-019): no record holds a homeowner message body, name, phone, email or address; run logs go under the git-ignored `wargames/runs/`; the tests run offline in `Ci`.

Acceptance: `python3 -m wargames.overload --target local` runs the scenario and exits 0 with a summary holding every figure above (each a number, "not exposed" or "not reachable" with its reason), the process counts and both duplicate counts; `tests/wargames/test_overload.py` proves the p99 on planted timings (1 to 100 ms give 99), that the proxy passes a non-AI request through unchanged and answers the AI path with a planted 429 with and without `retry-after`, a 529, a 500 and a delayed reply, that a planted duplicate text and a planted extra AI call for one Twilio message ID are anomalies while an AI-023 retry and AI-011's second reading are not, and that a record holding a planted homeowner message, name or phone fails; `scripts/pii_scan.py` passes on the module and the logs.
