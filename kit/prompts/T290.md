# T290: worked examples: pin D-033 for one-cent-wide recurring ranges at the $500 and $1,500 edges

## Goal

D-033 says the fee basis is computed exactly and rounded down to the whole cent once, before the tier is chosen; `four_months` (`app/payments/src/rules.rs:290`) does exactly that for a recurring range, from the sum of its two ends, and no worked example has a recurring range whose midpoint is a half cent, so nothing pins it (third external audit, which read the rule as "floor the midpoint first"; that reading was rejected, D-1874). This is not a fee change. Add rows to `docs/catalog/worked_examples.yaml` after W40 (W41 onward): for each recurring period (weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly, yearly) and each edge ($500.00 and $1,500.00), the one-cent-wide range (low L, high L + $0.01) whose exact 4-month basis is the nearest reachable on each side of the edge (the highest basis below it and the lowest at or above it), plus, where a one-cent-wide range reaches it exactly, the basis a cent above the edge. Worked from `four_months` (`app/payments/src/rules.rs:290`) on `main`: only yearly reaches a cent below, at and a cent above both edges ($1,499.97–$1,499.98 → $499.99, $1,500.00–$1,500.01 → $500.00, $1,500.03–$1,500.04 → $500.01; $4,499.97–$4,499.98, $4,500.00–$4,500.01 and $4,500.03–$4,500.04 for $1,500); quarterly reaches a cent below and at but not a cent above ($374.99–$375.00 → $499.99, $375.00–$375.01 → $500.00; $1,124.99–$1,125.00 and $1,125.00–$1,125.01 for $1,500); weekly, every two weeks and monthly reach none of the three exactly, so their rows are the nearest on each side (weekly $28.84–$28.85 → $499.98 and $28.85–$28.86 → $500.15, $86.53–$86.54 → $1,499.94 and $86.54–$86.55 → $1,500.11; every two weeks $57.68–$57.69 → $499.93 and $57.69–$57.70 → $500.02, $173.07–$173.08 → $1,499.98 and $173.08–$173.09 → $1,500.07; monthly $124.99–$125.00 → $499.98 and $125.00–$125.01 → $500.02, $374.99–$375.00 → $1,499.98 and $375.00–$375.01 → $1,500.02); 22 rows in all, numbered W41 to W62 in this order: weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly, yearly, each period's $500 rows then its $1,500 rows in rising basis (W41–W44 weekly, W45–W48 every two weeks, W49–W52 monthly, W53–W56 quarterly, W57–W62 yearly); the four whose basis is above $1,500 are W44 (weekly, $1,500.11), W48 (every two weeks, $1,500.07), W52 (monthly, $1,500.02) and W62 (yearly, $1,500.01), each $75 under the cap and $199 without it; each row's `fee_basis` and `fee` are worked from D-033 (exact sum of the ends times the period's monthly factor times 4, divided once, rounded down to the cent), with the recurring cap of $75 applying above $1,500 (FEE-039). Example: every two weeks, $57.69 to $57.70: (5,769 + 5,770) × 26 × 4 ÷ (12 × 2) = 50,002.33 cents, basis $500.02, fee $75 (flooring the midpoint first would give $499.98 and $25). Rows a period cannot reach are listed in the decision with the nearest reachable amounts instead. `scripts/check_catalog.py` and `app/payments/src/tests/worked_examples.rs` (`tests::worked_examples`) must pass with their logic unchanged. Two DONE tests pin the recurring rows by data (D-1400), so a new row fails them until their data follows (D-2386): this task also owns `app/payments/src/tests/worked_examples.rs` (T058's), where it adds the new row ids to the `RECURRING` list (`:18`) and its length, and `app/payments/src/tests/recurring.rs` (T188's and T244's), where `checked == 14` (`:130`) rises to exactly 14 plus the rows added (36 for the 22 rows above), the uncapped list `["W20", "W33"]` (`:122`, the rows that pay $199 with the cap removed) gains exactly W44, W48, W52 and W62, and the doc comment above the test (`:98`) names the new rows. Data changes only: no other line of either file changes, no assertion is removed or loosened, and the counts are exact. This is a harness task because the worked examples are a locked catalog file no queued task owns and it takes over two DONE tasks' test files for data only. Update `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-FEE-50 passes: `python3 scripts/check_catalog.py` and `cargo test -p askhava-payments -- worked_examples recurring` pass with every new row W41 to W62; the only lines changed in the two test files are the new ids in `RECURRING` and its length, the exact new `checked` count, W44, W48, W52 and W62 added to the uncapped list, and the doc comment naming the new rows; the plan lock is rewritten with the decision entry.
