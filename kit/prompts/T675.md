## Goal

Plan change D-4360 (orphan 1, steps 9 and 10; round-1 review findings 4 and 5; INT-044, INT-045, INT-046, PRIV-014, PRIV-025, SEC-041). Nothing asks for photos: `photos::photo_text` (`app/intake/src/photos.rs:205`) has no caller, `privacy::api::take_picture_text` (`app/privacy/src/media_fetch.rs:168`) has none, and the inbound text route (`app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`) reads no `NumMedia` or `MediaUrl<n>` field; `RawReply` and `TextIn` carry no media. A Twilio fetch and provider delete inside the webhook's transaction would break on a rollback or retry (a photo deleted at Twilio and not kept) and can pass Twilio's webhook timeout, so: (1) `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs` (after T573, the last queued owner before T299 and T402) commits the text as today and, in that same transaction, queues one `intake.picture_text` `core.task` per homeowner text with media, its payload the inbox row's ID, the Twilio message SID and up to 3 media SIDs only (no URL kept beyond what the SIDs rebuild, no phone, no words, no image); a business phone's media is ignored as today. (2) A new `app/intake/src/picture_text.rs` handles that task (registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`): outside any held transaction it fetches each media through `take_picture_text` with `fetch_twilio_media` (SEC-041: Twilio's hosts only, size cap, timeout), stores it, deletes it at the provider only after the store committed, and then, under the homeowner lock, calls the dispatcher's photo step for that request; a rerun takes nothing twice (dedupe key per media SID). (3) `app/intake/src/order_dispatch.rs` asks step 9 after the trade questions (`photo_text` with the job type's asks, at most `MAX_PHOTO_ASKS`, the upload link `issue_link` gives for T674's page, PRIV-015's line); never while a 911 text waits for SAFE; each stored photo is shown as it will appear on the card and needs her YES, which records the same `photo_ok` row T674's "Send this photo" writes, REMOVE (or QUITAR) taking it off; a photo with no `photo_ok` row is left off the card with a note (INT-044); one re-ask only for a file that would not decode or is under the size setting; "no" or any other text moves on; and step 10 in a `design: true` category (`design::is_design_category`, `app/intake/src/design.rs:47`): the look-pictures question, handled as photos, and, while the AI may not read photos, the describe-the-look question; the concept image only while the owner's "Concept images" switch is on (off at launch), through `design::concept_step`. Step 11 then follows. (4) T672's `tests/integration/tests/intake_order_timing.rs` and T673's `intake_order_flow.rs` script replies from the trade questions to name and address; with the photo step between them they are extended here, add-only: a "no photo" reply (and in the flow test one confirmed picture text) is inserted where the order now asks, and the photo ask is added to the expected texts; no assertion is removed or loosened. Tests (TD-INT-49) in the new `tests/integration/tests/intake_order_photos.rs`, PostgreSQL, the Twilio media stand-in and fake images only: a picture text queues one task whose payload holds only IDs; the task stores, confirms by YES and puts it on the card, then deletes at the provider; a rerun fetches nothing twice; a store failure deletes nothing at the provider; REMOVE takes it off; an undecodable file re-asked once; a HEIC gets the PRIV-025 reply; no photo asked while SAFE is awaited; a design category asks the look question; a media URL off Twilio's hosts is refused and nothing fetched. The route's done tests pass unchanged.

Acceptance: TD-INT-49 passes against PostgreSQL with the stand-ins; no Twilio fetch or delete runs inside the webhook's transaction; picture texts are fetched only from Twilio's hosts and deleted there only after the store committed; no photo goes on a card without its `photo_ok` row; the flow and timing tests change only by adding the photo step; every existing inbound-route test passes unchanged.
