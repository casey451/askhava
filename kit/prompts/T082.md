## Goal

Business sign-up carries the unticked E-SIGN consent box naming each notice kind, an activated business gets the welcome email before its first card, STOP from a business phone stops only texts, unexpected business texts get the portal-link auto-reply (BIZ-032 here is that auto-reply only; the help request is T151's, the channel preference T147's and "Ask about a charge" T148's, D-492), and Ask Hava never texts or calls to recruit (BIZ-017, BIZ-032, BIZ-048, CON-019, CON-020); the sign-up done screen's email-only notices are tested here once T193's screen exists (TD-BIZ-17). The SEC-028 email footer is T094's (it runs earlier); the channel preference and the business-texting switch are T147's, which runs later, so the welcome text and the PAUSE, RESUME and BUSY texts are T147's and T148's cases; this task records the E-SIGN consent and the welcome notice request (D-491). Each recruiting email says in its first line that it is an advertisement for Ask Hava (BIZ-048, D-492).
