## Goal

`messaging::rules` sends a non-urgent text only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in both Arizona time and the recipient's area-code zone, never texts an area code missing from the table, lets only the listed exceptions through at night, holds and merges texts to the next window (dropping a held text when its job closed, except the closing text), and applies the same rule to retries and business emails (QH-001 to QH-009, TXT-025).
