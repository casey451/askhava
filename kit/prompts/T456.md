## Goal

(1) `migrations/0202_picks_businesses_hold_away.sql` adds `picks.pick.confirm_due` (`timestamptz NULL`) and `away_delayed` (`boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`), and `businesses.business.away_until` and `busy_until` (`timestamptz NULL`; `busy_until` is T463's). (2) A new `app/businesses/src/away_busy.rs`, through `businesses::api`: `set_away(conn, by, until, now)` stores an end the caller already bounded with `picks::api::away_until` (D-285), for the owner or a "Confirm and billing" member, with its audit row; `away_until(conn, business, now)` reads it (none once past). (3) `app/picks/src/handlers.rs`: `notice_sent` starts the window at `window_start(sent, away_until, emergency)` and stores `away_delayed` (a non-emergency pick arriving while the business is away starts its clock when away ends, with no text to the homeowner; an emergency pick never waits, BIZ-034); `start_window` writes `confirm_due`; a new `picks::api::hold_pick(conn, pick, ask, now, settings)`, in one transaction, locks the waiting pick, computes the new end with `picks::api::hold(ask, (now, confirm_due), rules)` (refused when `confirm_due` is unset, the pick was away-delayed or held, or the job is an emergency, PICK-043), stores it once through `extend_once` (a second Hold changes nothing), writes it to `confirm_due`, restarts `jobs`' backstop and queues `picks.confirm_ends` at it as `start_window` does (the earlier timer then finds a moved `timer_from` and does nothing). (4) `app/server/src/action_links.rs`: the Hold link's `POST` (the link alone, `UseBy::LinkAlone`) acts once through `use_action_link` and `hold_pick` with the pick's `HoldAsk`; the Away link's `POST`, with a business session `portals::api::session_may` allows, bounds the posted time with `away_until` and calls `set_away`; a refusal shows the page again with its reason and leaves the link unused. Tests: `tests/integration/tests/hold_away.rs` (the stored end, a Hold moving the window end once, the away-delayed start and its Hold refusal, roles) and cases in `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs` (TD-SEC-44).
