## Goal

Plan change D-4727 (D; INT-010, INT-022, INT-043; D-485). `rules::follow_up_joins` (`app/intake/src/rules.rs:375`), `joins_open_job` and `conversation::joining_job` (`app/intake/src/conversation.rs:209`) have no production caller; the dispatcher opens a job only by `opens_job` (`app/intake/src/order_dispatch.rs:168`), so a later message of another trade after two hours, or a same-trade one sharing few words, is taken as an answer to whatever step is current. In `order_dispatch.rs` (after T719, the last queued owner before T299, which now follows this task): when a clear homeowner text in an open request reaches the dispatcher with no step of hers waiting for an answer (every job of the request past the card step, or a step-marker showing nothing is asked), `follow_up_joins` decides with: the last homeowner message to her newest job (the time `joining_job` reads), the request's window setting (2 hours default) and share (`JOIN_SHARE_PERCENT`), `same_trade` from the stored keyword reading of this text against the job's trade, and the job's scrubbed description; joining keeps today's path (the text stays on that job); otherwise the text opens a new job in the request through the dispatcher's own `open_job` path as ALSO does (T717: property `none`, no INT-009 lookup), with its coverage line and its own step order; a text while a step waits is that step's answer, as today. No AI reading is used (AI-008 off path). Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/intake_follow_up_joins.rs` (TD-INT-58): 1:59:59 joins another trade, 2:00:00 same trade 5 of 10 words joins, 49 of 100 opens a new job, another trade after the window opens a new job, a text while a question waits is its answer; TD-INT-35's unit cases pass unchanged. Update `app/intake/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-5567 (pre-flight job 31): "the time `joining_job` reads" is the `last` that `models::newest_job` returns (`app/intake/src/conversation.rs:218`; `joining_job` itself returns only the job ID, and only inside the window); the dispatcher reads `models::newest_job` itself (`pub(crate)`, `app/intake/src/models.rs:656`, taking the homeowner ID that `models::conversation_state` gives, as `joining_job` does; not the private `newest_job` in `replies.rs`) for the newest job; D-5670 below replaces the `last` it passes. Plan change D-5670 (outside pre-flight, checked on `main` 52bc3014): `last` is the later of the newest job's opening and the latest `heard` row of its request other than this message's (`inbox_id <> msg.inbox_id`), read in `order_dispatch.rs`; do not pass `models::newest_job`'s `last`, which already includes this message: `hear` runs first in `replies::in_request` (its `heard` row, `conversation::hear`), before `receive_text` reaches the dispatcher, so that `last` is the message's own time and every text would join (`rules::joins_open_job`, `rules::follow_up_joins`; TD-INT-58, `docs/test-design.md:938`). `models.rs` and `conversation.rs` are T745's (in progress): find `newest_job`, `hear` and `joining_job` by name.
