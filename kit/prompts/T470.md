## Goal

Plan change D-3750 (D-3263, D-3186; SEC-025, OPS-038, SEC-018, PRIV-016, BIZ-019): emails stored in `messaging.email_outbox` by `queue_email` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:343`) and by the business notice sender (job cards, pick and money notices, `app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs`), the receipts and the homeowner emails are never sent: only the account mailers call `send_email` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:454`), for their own row, and no task sends a queued row to a provider (D-3263 parked T222's email checks for it). Only the account mailers store a credential mark in place of a credential (`LINK_MARK`, `app/messaging/src/signin_mail.rs`; T468's `CODE_MARK`) and add it only on the way to the provider; business notices store real per-recipient sign-in links in their bodies (`app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs:24`, `:389`), so a stored notice is itself a credential for its recipient. (1) `migrations/0205_messaging_email_lease.sql` (headed `messaging`): `messaging.email_outbox` gains `claimed_at timestamptz` (the drain's lease) and the status `dropped` (a row never to be sent; its subject and bodies are cleared in the same statement, as a sent row's are, so the status CHECKs extend to it); no old migration is edited. Its number follows T456's `0204` (T470 depends on T456 for that order only, V9) and T236's migration moves to `0206`. (2) `app/messaging/src/email_due.rs` (new; exported through the shared `api.rs` and `lib.rs`): the `messaging.email_due` worker task. Each run, at the worker clock's now, leases a bounded batch, oldest first, in one short committed statement under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` that sets `status = 'sending'` and `claimed_at = now` together: it takes `queued` rows whose `not_before` passed and whose `claimed_at` is null or stale, plus `sending` rows only when their lease is stale (`claimed_at` older than `STALE_AFTER`, 5 minutes, as `outbox_sender`'s, or unset 5 minutes past `not_before`), because `claim_email` itself takes `queued` or `sending` (`app/messaging/src/models.rs:764`) and a non-429 provider error leaves a row `sending` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:515`). A row a worker leased is therefore `sending` with a fresh lease, which no second lease takes, whether the two runs overlap or follow one another, so each row gets one provider call per lease, under its stored idempotency key (SEC-018: a retry after a stale lease is the same email to Resend). `app/messaging/src/models.rs` (owners done): `hold_email` (`:819`), which puts a row back to `queued`, also clears `claimed_at` and never touches a `dropped` row (`status NOT IN ('sent', 'dropped')`), so a held row is leased again once its `not_before` passes. Each leased row is then sent through `send_email` with the provider, email key and `DailyLimit` it is handed (T469's `EmailProvider`), so OPS-038's bulk hold and the daily quota (SEC-025) hold as for every other email. It takes only templates no other code sends: never a template whose own handler calls `send_email` (the sign-in link, the email code, the welcome and phone-stopped, the coverage notice and the business event emails), listed by template ID in one constant; and, as a backstop, a leased row whose subject or body holds `LINK_MARK` or `CODE_MARK` is `dropped` with one High alert naming the row ID only and never sent, so a literal mark never reaches anyone. A homeowner row's address is read through `EmailTo::Homeowner` (the vault, under `messaging`'s send grant); a business row's comes from a resolver trait the composition supplies, which answers the address, or `Gone` when the row's business has left (BIZ-051: leaving stops all notices) or its mailbox is no longer a current member's (a removed member, SEC-024), and a `Gone` row is `dropped` unsent, since it holds that person's sign-in link. With no provider handed in, a run does nothing: it leases, sends and marks nothing and queues no next run; with one, it queues its next run, as `send_due` does (`app/messaging/src/outbox_sender.rs`). No transaction is open during the provider call, and no address, body or key is logged (PRIV-016). (3) `app/privacy/src/business_contacts.rs`: `BusinessPurpose::Notice` ("the business notice and card emails: the address only"), in `ALL`, with its audit label (SEC-047). (4) `app/server/src/email_due.rs` (new): the resolver (the current-member and left-business checks through `businesses::api`, then `read_business_mailbox` under `BusinessPurpose::Notice` on a bare connection) and the handler, registered in `worker_registry` (the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`) always, with T469's provider when it exists and with none otherwise, so `tests/integration/tests/task_registry.rs` (TD-OPS-36, built from the dev stand-in pairs with no Resend key) passes unchanged; the first run is queued at start only when a provider exists.
