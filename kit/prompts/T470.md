Each run, at the worker clock's now, leases a bounded batch, oldest first, in one short committed statement under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` that sets `status = 'sending'` and `claimed_at = now` together: it takes `queued` rows whose `not_before` passed and whose `claimed_at` is null or stale, plus `sending` rows only when their lease is stale (`claimed_at` older than `STALE_AFTER`, 5 minutes, as `outbox_sender`'s, or unset 5 minutes past `not_before`), because `claim_email` itself takes `queued` or `sending` (`app/messaging/src/models.rs:764`) and a non-429 provider error leaves a row `sending` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:494`). Each leased row is then sent through `send_email` with the provider, email key and `DailyLimit` it is handed (T469's `EmailProvider`), so OPS-038's bulk hold and the daily quota (SEC-025) hold as for every other email. It takes only templates no other code sends: never a template whose own handler calls `send_email` (the sign-in link, the email code, the welcome and phone-stopped, the coverage notice and the business event emails), listed by template ID in one constant; and, as a backstop, a leased row whose subject or body holds `LINK_MARK` or `CODE_MARK` is `dropped` with one High alert naming the row ID only and never sent, so a literal mark never reaches anyone. A homeowner row's address is read through `EmailTo::Homeowner` (the vault, under `messaging`'s send grant); a business row's comes from a resolver trait the composition supplies, which answers the address, or `Gone` when the row's business has left (BIZ-051: leaving stops all notices) or its mailbox is no longer a current member's (a removed member, SEC-024), and a `Gone` row is `dropped` unsent, since it holds that person's sign-in link. With no provider handed in, a run does nothing: it leases, sends and marks nothing and queues no next run; with one, it queues its next run, as `send_due` does (`app/messaging/src/outbox_sender.rs`). No transaction is open during the provider call, and no address, body or key is logged (PRIV-016).
