## Goal

Phase 2 war games (Casey, October 31, D-1154), part 1: `wargames/` (Python, standard library only) drives the Railway test site through its real entry points (signed inbound texts and status callbacks to the texting webhook, the web intake and web chat, signed inbound business email replies, the homeowner, business and owner portal pages) and, for faults the sandboxes cannot make, a local lane (`--target local`: the stand-ins and the test-control router, as `Ci`'s journeys run). A persona library (`wargames/personas.py`) holds both sides: ordinary, confused and vanishing homeowners; Spanish speakers; tenants; STOP and START mid-job; pranksters and AI-credit drainers (photo spam, bursts of requests, endless rambling intake, re-entry loops, opt-out and opt-in churn, one- and two-word messages); people posing as businesses; contractors gaming quotes (bounced contact details, edits, double picks, after-hours claims, never confirming). Chaos injectors (`wargames/chaos.py`): duplicate and replayed webhooks, out-of-order texts, the clock at the 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. quiet-hour edges and at the 24-hour and 4-business-hour timer edges (local lane), a vendor outage mid-flow and the AI returning junk (local lane, through the stand-ins' fault and plant headers). The campaign session plays personas live too, improvising beyond the library, and adds each new one to it. Fake data only (BUILD_BRIEF.md:66, PRIV-019): every persona's name, phone (555-0100 to 555-0199), email (example.com) and street comes from the fixture generator, and no real number, key or person is ever used; signing secrets and test keys are read the way T129's sandbox mode reads its credentials (from the build environment's secrets, never printed, logged or committed); an entry point whose secret this session cannot reach runs on the local lane only and the run log says so; timers on the test site are shortened only through settings, as T136 does, and each simulated business day is recorded with its wall-clock span. `wargames/anomalies.py` checks after every step, from outside effects only (docs/test-design.md section 1 rule 1): no duplicate outbound text, email or charge; consent text first on every path; an opt-out word stops every text; no text outside quiet hours; no homeowner contact detail outside the vault or on any text to a business; every status change has an audit row; and every anomaly is logged as one JSON line with the request, job and message IDs (`wargames/runs/<run>.jsonl`, fake IDs only). The tests under `tests/wargames/` run offline in `Ci` (no test site, no network, the stand-ins at most) and run logs `wargames/runs/*.jsonl` are git-ignored (`.gitignore`), so only the reports carry results; persona addresses use fake street names such as "1 Test Way", since `scripts/pii_scan.py` scans `wargames/`. On the test site the AI-008 switch is on during the money run and the rounds, so real Claude calls are made on fake data from the test project's Console key (T137's), and the campaign's AI spend is capped at $40 in all (a test-project daily AI-call cap, AI-021, of 600 calls, and the Console spend limit Casey set at the account checkpoint, whichever is lower); outbound texts use Twilio's test credentials and magic numbers only, so no real phone receives anything (BUILD_BRIEF.md:190).

Acceptance: `python3 -m wargames run --target local --smoke` runs one scenario of every persona class and every chaos injector against the local lane and exits 0 with an anomaly log, and `--target test-site --smoke` does the same against the Railway test site (or records which entry points ran on the local lane only and why); `tests/wargames/test_wargames.py` proves each anomaly check fires on a planted defect (a duplicate send, a text at 20:00:00 Arizona time, a sentinel phone on a business text, a status change with no audit row) and stays quiet on a clean run; `scripts/pii_scan.py` passes on the persona library and the logs.
