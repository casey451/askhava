## Goal

Plan change D-5081 (external code review of Nov 20, 2026, finding F19; INT-044, PRIV-014). `privacy::api::take_picture_text` leaves the media at the texting provider when storing the photo fails, and answers `DeletePending` when the store worked and the delete did not; T675's caller in `app/intake/src/picture_text.rs` does not say what then happens, so a homeowner's photo can stay at the provider with no retry. In `picture_text.rs`: a `DeletePending` answer queues a delete-only task under its own dedupe key (the media's ID) that retries with backoff until the provider confirms, and raises one alert after its last attempt; and when the store has failed on its last retry the media is deleted at the provider too, nothing being kept, as on the 72-hour path. `media_fetch.rs` is read, not edited. Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/picture_text_delete.rs`, PostgreSQL with the Twilio stand-in, fake data only: a `PictureSource` whose `take` stores through the real path but answers `MediaState::DeletePending` and leaves the media at the stand-in (its `PictureTextMedia::delete_media` fails; `DeletePending` comes from `take_picture_text` at `app/privacy/src/media_fetch.rs:186` to `:189`), and whose `discard` reports failure once and then calls the real Twilio stand-in, so the retry deletes and a later fetch of the media gets 404; storage fails on every retry and the media is then deleted at the stand-in with no photo row; a media that deletes at once queues no task. T675's own tests pass unchanged. Build notes go to the end of `app/intake/NOTES.md`; update `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-5550 (pre-flight job 23; INT-044, PRIV-014): every provider delete this task adds (the retry after `DeletePending`, and the delete after a store that failed on its last retry) goes through T675's `PictureSource::discard(url)` (T675's goal, D-5337: `intake` may not name an `adapters` type, `kit/layers.json:117` to `:127`), never an adapter call. When the `discard` after a store that failed on its last retry answers false, it queues the same delete-only task (dedupe key: the media's ID), retried with backoff and alerting after its last attempt: the backoff and dead-lettering are `core`'s task queue (`app/core/src/tasks.rs:166`, `backoff`; dead-lettered after `max_attempts`, `DEFAULT_MAX_ATTEMPTS` 5 at `:45`), and the alert after the last attempt is T366's dead-letter alert (`app/server/src/no_handler_alert.rs`, `alert_dead_day` at `:112`), so this task adds no alert kind of its own; the delete-only task's handler returns an error whenever `discard` answers false, so the queue retries it (an `Ok` would end it). Tested: storage fails on every retry and the first `discard` fails, then the retry deletes and the media answers 404. The Twilio stand-in cannot fail a media delete (`stand_ins/twilio.py:285` to `:291`; `/_stand_in/faults`, `stand_ins/server.py:25` to `:31`, fails only sends) and `stand_ins/` is protected, so the failed delete comes from the test's own `PictureSource`, as above; nothing in `stand_ins/` changes. Plan change D-5646 (pre-flight, checked on main d615c36; INT-044, PRIV-014): the `Hold::Discard` branch of the `intake.picture_text` handler in `picture_text.rs` (a closed request, the 72-hour hold, no request) calls `discard` for each media and ignores its `false`, leaving the media at Twilio with no retry. When `discard` answers `false` there, queue the same delete-only task as the `DeletePending` path (its dedupe key the media's ID), whose retry counts the provider's 404 as success (`app/adapters/src/base.rs`, `delete`). The test file adds a fake source whose `discard` fails once on the `Hold::Discard` path and then succeeds on the retry.

Acceptance: `tests/integration/tests/picture_text_delete.rs` passes against PostgreSQL with every listed case; T675's tests pass unchanged; `app/privacy/src/media_fetch.rs` is not in the diff. On the `Hold::Discard` path a failed `discard` queues the delete-only task, and its retry deletes the media (D-5646).
