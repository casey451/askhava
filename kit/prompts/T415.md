## Goal

Plan change D-3262 (BIZ-037, BIZ-040, BIZ-051, SEC-024, PRIV-002, PRIV-016; D-1724, D-3186, D-963), the composition half of T412's store. (1) `app/businesses/src/contact_ids.rs`, through the shared `businesses::api`: `contact_ids_in_use(conn, since)` returns, for businesses whose status is not `removed` (they left, BIZ-051), the mailbox of every current member (`removed_at` null), of every backup address not removed of a current member, and of every open `businesses.mailbox_check` row for the sign-up email or a current member (`remove_member`, `app/businesses/src/team.rs:441`, `:449`, leaves a removed member's backup and check rows behind, so they count only through a current member), and the ID of every `businesses.staff_phone` of such a business; and also every mailbox that stopped being used at or after `since`: a removed member's (by its `removed_at`, which also dates that member's leftover backup and check rows), a removed backup's (its own `removed_at`), a `mailbox_check` row's, which has no closed time (`migrations/0142_businesses_email_verification.sql:15` to `:30`), by its `verified_at`, else its `sent_at`, and a removed business's (its status change time). (2) `messaging::api::unsent_business_mailboxes(conn)` in the shared `app/messaging/src/api.rs`: the `recipient_id` of every `messaging.email_outbox` row with `recipient_kind` `business`, `status` not `sent` and `queued_at` within the last 7 days (`migrations/0089_messaging_retries_email.sql:39`), so a row stuck in `sending` cannot keep an address forever. (3) `app/server/src/business_contacts.rs`: `ContactDirectory`, the production `businesses::api::SenderLink` (`app/businesses/src/team.rs:223`), which calls `messaging::api::sender_add` then `privacy::api::store_staff_phone` for the same line in the caller's transaction, so the digest and the sealed number move together; and `sweep_business_contacts(pool, now)`, run on `jobs`' daily `SWEEP_KIND` task in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` after the request sweep: it takes `contact_ids_in_use(conn, now - 30 days)` and `unsent_business_mailboxes` together as the in-use list and calls `privacy::api::forget_unused` with `before` 2 days earlier than now. So a mailbox is kept while it is in use, for 30 days after it stops being used, and while any unsent business email names it: the old address must still get SEC-024's change notice, and T196's `mark_returned` counts soft bounces 14 days apart (BIZ-040), so a bounce must still find its mailbox for at least that span, with margin; a mailbox `mailbox_for` touched in the last 2 days is never removed, so a sign-up or address change in progress is safe; a line's number goes at the first sweep after the line is unlinked (no notice goes to a phone after unlinking). Each step deletes only what is absent from the list read in that run, so a rerun or a crash between steps is harmless. (4) Tests in `app/server/tests/business_contacts.rs` (TD-PRIV-29), fake data only. Update the `businesses`, `messaging` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files, add this task's line to the contact vault notes of `docs/architecture.md` (found by grep, never loaded whole; D-3079), and `docs/decisions.md`.
