## Goal

Each business may quote for 24 hours after its own card was actually sent; a quote stores its category's format and amounts (hourly quotes with hours, read back to text quoters), the form shows the fee it would carry before submit, the business can see its card as homeowners do, and a quote with a phone number, link or contact wording bounces (PICK-001, PICK-002, PICK-005 to PICK-007, PICK-039, FEE-043).
