# T046: quotes: quote window, quote fields, bounces and the all-quotes-in trigger

Task T046 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: quotes-intake.

## Goal

Each business may quote for 24 hours after its own card was actually sent; a quote stores its category's format and amounts (hourly quotes with hours) with a confirmed flag, no quote reaches the homeowner before the business confirms it, and a quote with a phone number, link or contact wording bounces (PICK-001, PICK-002, PICK-007); the business's preview of its card is T186's (PICK-005) and the read-back screen T148's (PICK-006) (D-493). When every card's 24-hour window has ended on a Quotes ready job with no pick, `quotes` calls `jobs::api::all_windows_ended(job, quoted, sent)` (T032's), which sends the TXT-042 text and closes a job with no choosable quote left (JOB-025's every-window-ended half) (D-491). `quotes` keeps no fee code: the fee preview on the quote form is T148's, through `payments::api` (FEE-043, D-491).
