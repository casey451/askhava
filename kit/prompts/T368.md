(F1) `checklist_offer` (`app/businesses/src/capability.rs:168`) offers only the job types `ClassMap::job_types` gives the ROC classes, plus the no-license categories, so a pest-management attester, a Board-certified home inspector or an ADEQ septage hauler is offered none of its own job types, and a handyman on the city list is never offered a handyman-allowed job type (faucet replacement, camera install), against `docs/catalog/matching_examples.yaml` M05 and M09; `save_capability` then refuses those ticks as `NotCovered` and stores nothing. (F5) sign-up's `category_list` (`app/businesses/src/signup.rs:109`) reads `ClassMap::opening` from the compiled categories file (`app/licensing/src/class_map.rs:373`, `:378`), and `checklist_offer` (`capability.rs:171`) and `license_change` (`:368`) read the file's `enabled` flag, while intake reads the stored switch (`catalog::api::is_enabled`, `app/intake/src/routing.rs:252`), so a category switched off in settings is still offered at sign-up, in the checklist and in a license-change email. Fix: (1) the checklist is built from everything the business holds (its ROC classes, its non-pest agency licenses, its pest attestation with its record categories, whether it signed up as a handyman, and its city-list standing): a job type of a category whose license kind is not `none` is offered licensed and pre-ticked exactly when `licensing::api::eligibility` gives that holder a card for it (eligibility gives `Card` for every no-license job type of a city-list business, `app/licensing/src/rules.rs:80`, so a no-license row is never pre-ticked); a job type is offered unticked as a handyman extra when eligibility gives a handyman card and the job type itself is not gas, smoke-alarm, sprinkler or permit-type work (LIC-021, LIC-022); the no-license extras stay unticked as today, except that any row for which eligibility gives `NotHandyman` or `RefusedJobType` is left out (the handyman category is license kind `none` and `handyman_allowed`, `docs/catalog/categories.yaml:165`, so only a handyman sees it); so the checklist and the matching cannot disagree; the holder comes from T374's `businesses::api` holding read; (2) the enabled set is the stored switch (`catalog::api::enabled_categories`): a switched-off category leaves the sign-up list, the checklist and `reapply_license`'s offered job types; a stored-on category keeps the file's `launch_open` label. The existing pure functions keep their signatures and give the same answer for an ROC-only holder, so every TD-BIZ case passes unchanged; the new entry points (holder and stored set in, offer out) are what the served sign-up (T302) calls. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/capability_offer.rs` (TD-BIZ-46, TD-LIC-27), each failing on today's `main`: a septage hauler, a certified inspector and a pest attester (general pest service and scorpions; termites only with the wood-destroying organism record category) get their job types pre-ticked and `save_capability` stores them; a city-list handyman gets faucet replacement and camera install unticked, never gas line work or a gas pool heater, and saving them stores them; an R-37 business's offer is unchanged; an R-11 business is not offered the handyman category; a city-list business with no license gets no pre-ticked row; a category switched off through `catalog::api::set_category_enabled` leaves `category_list`, the checklist and `reapply_license`'s offer, and switching it on brings it back.
