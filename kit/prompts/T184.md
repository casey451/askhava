# T184: quotes: overflow to 'jobs nobody else covers', pauses and late eligibility

Task T184 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: quotes-overflow-late-eligibility.

## Goal

When no checklist covers a job the card goes to licensed businesses with "jobs nobody else covers" on; a paused business, or one under an owner pause for its town or trade, gets no card; and a business that becomes eligible while the job is Open or No quotes yet gets the card then (CARD-004, CARD-016, CARD-018). With matching in place, this task also adds to intake's `routing.rs` (T043's, extended here) the INT-017 count line, N counted through `quotes::api` as CARD-001 would send it then, and its Not covered branch when no active business in the town covers the job, and INT-032's SPLIT offer when no business's license covers every candidate job type, with SPLIT or DIVIDIR making two requests (TD-INT-25, TD-INT-29, D-491).
