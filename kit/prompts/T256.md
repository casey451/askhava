# T256: server: two first texts from one new phone both reach intake; a business phone's STOP reaches businesses

Task T256 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: inbound-first-contact-race.
Branch: `claude/task/T256-inbound-first-contact-race`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, recovery, compliance).

## Goal

Two texts with different MessageSids from a phone the vault does not know yet can race in `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`: both record their inbox row, both find no contact, one stores it, and the other's `store_contact` answers `VaultError::PhoneTaken`, which `vault_halt` turns into `Halt::Failed`; the delivery then commits its inbox row and answers 200 without reaching intake, and a resend stops as a repeat, so the text is lost (D-752 with D-1311; external audit A01, D-1751). (1) In `homeowner`, on `PhoneTaken` look the phone up again with `privacy::api::find_by_phone` (inside the same delivery transaction; the vault's contact savepoint was rolled back, the competing insert has committed or is waited on by the unique index) and continue with that contact. If the second lookup still finds none, answer `Halt::Database` so the whole delivery rolls back (inbox row included) and Twilio sends it again; never commit the inbox row of a text that did not reach its handler because of a contact race. The contact uniqueness constraint stays; any other non-database error keeps D-1311's 200. (2) D-1715's inbound wiring: after `messaging`'s `Outcome::OptedOut` for a number `sender_business` knows, call `businesses::api::phone_stopped` for that phone in the same transaction, and when `businesses::api::auto_reply_due` is true for a business sender's text, queue `business_auto_reply` through the outbox (T082's functions; no new template). Update the `server` `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-TXT-32 and TD-BIZ-39 pass in `tests/integration/tests/inbound_first_contact.rs` against PostgreSQL, with two signed deliveries held at the absent-contact lookup by two connections (a barrier, not a sleep); every case in T241's `tests/integration/tests/inbound_text_route.rs` still passes unchanged.
