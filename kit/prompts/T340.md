## Goal

Seventh audit N7-01 (D-2331; FEE-045, FEE-005, FEE-004, FEE-007; sets aside only D-2210's default facts and closes its known gap). Today `fee_inputs` (`app/picks/src/service.rs:408`) prices every pick with default facts: not fee-free, `VisitTier::DEFAULT`, `RecurringCap::DEFAULT`, recurring when the quote has a period. So a buyer inspection (a `fee_free_job_types` entry, `docs/catalog/categories.yaml:665`) is charged, against FEE-045. After T339 and T251 (which record each job's codes), T343 and T404 (the DONE tests' fixtures record facts; D-3118 gave T404 the facts row of `tests/integration/tests/email_pick_code.rs`, T095's, whose `ready` fixture (`:84` to `:124`) records none and whose `opted_out_yes` case (`:244` to `:256`) picks a non-emergency job through `page_pick` (`app/picks/src/email_code.rs:241`)) and T295 and T318 (the queued owners of `service.rs`): (1) `picks` reads the job's latest facts row through `jobs::api::job_facts` and passes the codes with the chosen quote to `payments`; `app/payments/src/fee_facts.rs` resolves them from `catalog::api` (payments may call catalog; picks may not): the category's set visit tier, whether the pick is fee-free and whether it is one-time or recurring. (2) Several candidates (INT-040's "Job type not certain", or an emergency job whose type has not settled; D-2331): the category decides the visit tier; if any remaining candidate is fee-free, the pick is fee-free ($0, a `fee-free job type` ledger entry, no free first win used), never charged; the pick is one-time only if every remaining candidate is one-time, otherwise the quote's period decides as today. (3) A non-emergency job with no row, an unknown code, or candidates spanning more than one category is refused at the pick (`PicksError::Quotes`), storing nothing (fail closed). (4) An emergency job (`jobs::api::current`'s `emergency`) is never refused for its facts: with no row, an unknown code or candidates spanning categories, the pick proceeds on the catalog's default facts for a one-time job (`VisitTier::DEFAULT`, not fee-free, one-time) and raises one High alert `picks.fee_facts_unsettled` per pick through `ops::api::raise_alert`, naming the job number and the pick only, never homeowner details. The money reasoning (D-2331): Confirm (T261) charges exactly the fee stored at the pick and nothing recomputes it (D-1871), so the alert is the control that lets the owner check that fee before the business confirms. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/fee_facts_at_pick.rs` through the real page-pick and text-pick paths with a catalog fixture (TD-FEE-62). Money: the overseer runs an independent money review of the hand-in. Update the `picks` and `payments` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`, and add this task's line to the off-database records and recovery notes of `docs/architecture.md` (found by grep, never loaded whole). Any test this task writes that builds an `adapters::api::PaymentIntent` (a fake `IntentStripe` or `PaymentsAdapter` answering an intent) builds it with `serde_json::from_value` (the type derives `Deserialize`), never a struct literal, and gives a succeeded intent a fake `ch_` `latest_charge`, so T352's new field (`#[serde(default)]`) breaks no file T352 does not own (D-3134).
