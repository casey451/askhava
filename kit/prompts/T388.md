## Goal

Split from T232 by plan change D-3055 (PICK-042, PICK-054's email and page half; D-244, D-466, D-493). After a Confirm the homeowner gets a copy of the accepted quote (type, amount, quote facts and the business's own words) by email when an email is on file, offering "Add Ask Hava to your contacts" and no reward, and the quote page shows it until its token expires (PICK-011). Three gaps the split closes: nothing queues the email, the page's view has no accepted-quote fields, and `messaging` may not read `quotes`, `picks` or `intake` (`kit/layers.json`). (1) The trigger, `app/picks/src/settle.rs` (T254's): the Confirm is the settle's move from awaiting confirm to `paid` or `free` (`SettleStep::Move`, which happens once per pick under the pick's row lock); in that branch, in the caller's transaction, `settle` queues one `picks.accepted_quote` task (a constant exported through `picks::api`) whose payload holds the pick, job and business IDs only, with the dedupe key `picks.accepted_quote.<pick>`; `Settled`, the signature and every other branch are unchanged, so `Was` and `Left` queue nothing. (2) The handler, `app/intake/src/accepted_quote.rs` (`intake` may call `picks`, `quotes` and `messaging`), registered in the shared `register_handlers` (`app/server/src/lib.rs:379`): it reads the job's homeowner contact and language from `intake`, the accepted quote through `quotes::api` (a read added to the shared `api.rs`, as `earliest_visit` is at `app/quotes/src/api.rs:343`, or the existing card reads), and hands the values to `messaging::api`; a pick no longer `paid` or `free`, or a homeowner with no email on file, sends nothing and the task ends done. (3) `app/messaging/src/accepted_quote_email.rs` composes and queues the email to the homeowner's email on file through the existing homeowner path (`EmailTo::Homeowner`, the address read from the vault under `messaging`'s send grant, `app/messaging/src/email.rs:547`), never into a log, payload or error. Its words are built in Rust and rendered inside `templates/email_layout.html`, as the receipt (`app/messaging/src/receipt_email.rs:98`) and check-in (`app/messaging/src/checkin_email.rs:125`) emails are, with a `words(language, ...)` for English and Spanish (Spanish a draft, TXT-003), HTML and plain text carrying the same lines and the homeowner footer; nothing is added to the text registry (`app/messaging/templates/en.json` and `es.json`), whose done tests fill every key and require every homeowner template in T318's `NOT_UPDATE_TEXTS` (`app/messaging/src/tests/template_registry.rs:27`, `app/intake/src/tests/intake_timeouts.rs:68`, `app/quotes/src/tests/update_texts.rs:133`); the email carries the contact card offer (askhava.com and, once it exists, the texting number) and no reward, and no text carries it (TXT-008, TXT-009). (4) The quote page: `app/portals/src/quote_page.rs`'s `PageView` and `load` gain the accepted quote while the job's pick is `paid` or `free` and the token works, and `quote_page.html` shows it (its number, type, amount, facts and the business's own words, marked as the quote she accepted) above the list, with the same "Add Ask Hava to your contacts" offer; nothing else on the page changes, the pick forms keep their rules and the page shows no name, phone or address (PICK-010). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/accepted_quote_email.rs` (TD-PICK-42), fake data: a settle that moves a pick queues one task and a second settle of the same pick queues none; the handler queues one email holding the type, amount, facts and words and the contacts offer and no reward, in the homeowner's language; with no email on file no email is queued; the quote page (through `quote_page_routes`) shows the accepted quote after the Confirm and still at 29 days 23:59 after close, and is gone (404) at 30 days. Every done test of `settle`, the release and the quote page passes unchanged; a done test that breaks is a `PLAN GAP:`, never an edit. Update the `picks`, `intake`, `messaging` and `portals` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
