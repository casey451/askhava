## Goal

askhava.com serves /terms, /privacy, /sms-terms, /your-data and /business-data in English from the drafted legal documents in `docs/legal/`, rendered at build time by `app/site/build.rs` with `pulldown-cmark` (allowed by D-491) into Askama pages, the build failing if a document is missing; the two data pages also exist in Spanish (TERMS-021, TERMS-023; the terms, privacy policy and SMS terms have no Spanish source yet); each page carries the WORD-002 footer statement, the data pages and the help-page link hold the AI-018 switched-off sentence while the AI switch is off, and the consent text's links resolve (WEB-014, TERMS-021, TERMS-023, AI-018). The Stage 0.5 static set is T116's (D-491). Each published version of the four documents is kept once in `docs/legal/published/versions.json` with its full text and SHA-256, and a docs test fails if a published version's text no longer matches its hash (TERMS-034, D-492).
