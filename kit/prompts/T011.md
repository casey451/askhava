## Goal

`core` gives every crate validated settings (the app refuses to start on a missing value, a live key in a test environment, a test key in live, or a vendor account identifier off that environment's allow-list), an injectable clock with Arizona time and the 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. business clock, and logging that carries request IDs and route templates only, with Sentry's default PII off and contact fields held as `Redacted<String>` (JOB-007, PRIV-016, PRIV-017, PRIV-028, SEC-001 to SEC-003, SEC-036).
