## Goal

Plan change D-4280 (Opus pre-claim scout b, Nov 18, 2026, checked on `main` 7f322437): split from T523 (D-3880, D-3881), whose change could not fit the build-task limit. The owner-portal flags OPS-027 and OPS-034 need counts no `api` gives. Each reader below is a new module re-exported by one line in its module's shared `api.rs`, takes its numbers and window as arguments (the settings are T524's, which divides the confirmed-quote count by the wins for quotes per win), and returns counts and record IDs only, never a phone, name or address (PRIV-028). (1) `app/quotes/src/flag_inputs.rs`: for given job IDs and a calendar month, the median number of confirmed quotes per job and the trade's confirmed-quote count. (2) `app/picks/src/flag_inputs.rs`: the business IDs with N or more lapsed picks (PICK-019; `picks.pick.status = 'expired'`) in a window, with their counts; for given job IDs and a month, the number of wins (picks `paid` or `free`); and which of given job IDs have any `picks.pick` row. (3) `app/intake/src/flag_inputs.rs`: homeowner IDs (the vault ID, never a phone) with N or more requests in a window and no pick, "no pick" coming from the `picks::api` reader in (2), so `intake` queries no `picks` table. T523 keeps the `jobs` check-in recorder and its connected and NO-or-OTHER reader and owns `tests/integration/tests/flag_inputs.rs`; this task does not touch them. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/flag_inputs_counts.rs` against real PostgreSQL, fake data only: each reader on planted rows, its boundary (median 4 quotes against 3, 3 lapsed picks against 2, 3 requests against 2), and no contact field in any result. Update the `quotes`, `picks` and `intake` `NOTES.md`, the readers' lines in `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
