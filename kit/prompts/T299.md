- **AI-029** No database connection or open transaction is held while an AI provider call is awaited: a caller takes a connection only to read what the call needs (the AI-008 switch, the AI-021 admission and the AI-028 budgets, each on its own short transaction) and releases it, awaits the provider for up to the AI-023 timeout, retries and backoff included, with no connection held, then takes a connection again to record the call and to apply the reading, which is applied only if the conversation step it was asked for is still current; a reading that returns after its step moved on is recorded and discarded. Because no connection is held, each AI-028 budget check is, like the AI-021 admission (D-1757), one statement that reserves the call only while the budget has room, so concurrent calls for one message, conversation or job (a duplicate or replayed webhook among them) can never pass a budget together; a refused reservation makes no call and is recorded with outcome `budget`. The inbound-text webhook commits its durable record of a text before any AI call is made for it and never awaits the AI inside its transaction, so a slow or retrying provider can neither hold a webhook's transaction open nor use up the database pool (D-2038). (`docs/spec/ai.md:33`)
- **AI-021** Every AI-written text passes the CARD-010 content screen, a URL ban and a length cap before it is sent, falling back to the fixed template on any hit; no AI call is made for a web-chat session before its phone code is verified; and a global daily AI-call cap raises an alert and stops further calls when reached (D-480). - **AI-023** Each AI call has one time budget, and its retries and backoff run inside it: a call a homeowner's reply waits on (every intake step's call, on web chat or in reply to an inbound text: the reading, a reword, the follow-up, the card, the project file, a photo screen and a concept prompt) has 3 seconds by default, a setting allowed from 1 to 5 seconds and never more; a call no homeowner reply waits on (reading a business's quote, PICK-006) may use its own setting, 20 seconds by default, 1 to 120. Everything fails closed: a schema failure is discarded (AI-005), a word check failure falls back to the template or her own scrubbed words (AI-019, AI-021).
