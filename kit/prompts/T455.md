## Goal

Plan change D-3675 (split from T301; BIZ-028, BIZ-032, BIZ-038, SEC-025; D-1955, D-225): T301 serves the action route with `POST` acting only for Quote (Approve is T399's). This task makes the Pause, Resume and Ask about a charge links act once, in T301's `app/server/src/action_links.rs`, each through `use_action_link` in the caller's transaction, with a business session `portals::api::session_may` allows. (1) Pause and Resume: `businesses::api::pause_self` and `resume_self` (BIZ-028, BIZ-010). (2) Ask about a charge: the link's object is the charged pick (`payments.charge_evidence` is keyed by pick); `payments::api::receipt_for(conn, pick, business)` (`app/payments/src/receipts.rs:84`) gives its job and tier (none for another business: the one not-found answer); `portals::api::ask_about_charge` is called with `ChargeAsked { business, charge: pick, job }` and the page answers with `charge_answer` and the `/b/charges/{pick}` link (BIZ-032, D-225). A refused action (a business not active, a pause not its own) leaves the link unused, changes nothing and says why. Hold, Away and Busy keep T301's answer (T456 and T463 make them act). (3) Tests: cases in `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs` (TD-SEC-44: each acts once, a used link acts no more, a cross-site or wrong-CSRF `POST` is refused, changes nothing and is a security event) and TD-SEC-67 in `tests/e2e/action_links_served.spec.ts` with links seeded through `psql` (D-1955's hash): the quote form and the Hold page (its `GET`) in a browser, and a quote form `POST` past `admit_quote`'s per-business or per-IP limit stores nothing (D-2206); the Confirm link's browser case is T457's (TD-SEC-68). Update the `server` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
