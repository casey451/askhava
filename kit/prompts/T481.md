## Goal

Plan change D-3753: T110's replay (OPS-015 to OPS-017) must mark an outbox row sent with the SID it found in Twilio's sent log and rebuild charges from Stripe's records, but `messaging::models::mark_sent` (`app/messaging/src/models.rs:278`) is `pub(crate)` and moves only a `sending` row, and `payments` has no charge rebuild (`book_success`, `app/payments/src/charge.rs:687`, is `pub(crate)`). (1) `app/messaging/src/replay_sent.rs`, through `messaging::api`: `mark_replayed_sent(conn, row, listed, sent_at)` (exported through `messaging::api` and so callable by any higher crate, but its one intended caller is T110's replay in `recovery`; its doc says so) marks an outbox row that is not yet `sent` (`queued`, `sending`, `unknown` or `failed`; never `blocked` or `expired`) sent with the listed message's SID, its body cleared, under the row's lock, once (a second call or a row already sent changes nothing and says so); it refuses a SID already stored on another outbox row and a listed message whose `To` is not the row's recipient (resolved as the sender resolves it, through `privacy::api`) or whose `From` is not one of Laftly's sending numbers, and writes one `ops` audit row of IDs only; it sends nothing. (2) `app/payments/src/charge_rebuild.rs`, through `payments::api`: `rebuild_charge(conn, charge, at)` takes one succeeded charge as `list_charge_page` lists it (`app/adapters/src/payments.rs:435`) and, when its PaymentIntent's label names a pick and try (`pick-<id>-try<n>`, `intent_label`/`attempt_label`) that `payments` holds and its PaymentIntent ID equals the one stored for that pick in `payments.pick_intent`, books it through `book_success` under its derived `charge_key` (one evidence record, one `charge` ledger entry, its receipt task), never calling Stripe to charge; a charge already booked answers `AlreadyBooked` and changes nothing; one whose pick is unknown or whose PaymentIntent ID differs answers `Unmatched` with its `ch_` ID, for T110 to refund and flag (TD-OPS-04); a charge Stripe shows refunded (fully or in part) answers `Refunded` and books nothing, for T110 to list. Money code: 95% branch coverage. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/replay_helpers.rs` against PostgreSQL and the stand-in Stripe, fake data only: each status marked once, a `blocked` row, a SID already on another row and a wrong recipient refused; a lost charge rebuilt once with exactly one ledger row, a second rebuild adding nothing, an unknown pick and a mismatched PaymentIntent answered unmatched, a refunded charge booked nowhere. Update the `messaging` and `payments` `NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-3844 (the overseer's pre-claim scout, Nov 15, 2026): corrects the matching above. Stripe's charge list returns no idempotency key or `pick-<id>-try<n>` label (`ListedCharge`, `app/adapters/src/payments.rs:171` to `:185`, carries only the charge, its PaymentIntent and `metadata[pick_id]`), so `rebuild_charge` matches on `pick_id` and the PaymentIntent stored for that pick in `payments.pick_intent` only, and takes `try_no` from the pick's newest `payments.charge_attempt` row. `book_success` (`app/payments/src/charge.rs:687`) books nothing and still returns `Ok` when `write_evidence`'s join on that attempt (`:761`) finds no row, so a pick with no attempt row (lost in the restore) answers `Unmatched`, never a silent success, and the rebuild reports booked only when the evidence record was written. Money work: an Opus review in two rounds (the second on the head the first round's fixes produce). T481 defines the listed-message struct (SID, To, From) in `replay_sent.rs`. Plan change D-3889 (Fable's pre-claim check, Nov 16, 2026, revised after the plan change's review): (3) for T110's replay, which can read neither `messaging.outbox` nor the vault, and with no recipient number ever leaving `messaging`: `messaging::api::replay_window(conn)` gives the sending numbers and the earliest and latest send time of the outbox rows not yet `sent` (the statuses `mark_replayed_sent` takes), with no recipient; `messaging::api::replay_match(conn, vault, listed)` takes the messages T110 listed from Twilio (SID, `To`, `From`, body, send time; a `messaging` type) and, inside `messaging`, matches each unsent row by its recipient (resolved under `messaging`'s send grant), sender, the body's SHA-256 (a redacted body matches on recipient, sender and send time within the window) and send time, answering only row IDs with a matched SID, ambiguous or none; no number is returned or logged. `tests/integration/tests/replay_helpers.rs` covers both.
