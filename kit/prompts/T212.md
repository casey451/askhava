# T212: intake: photo asks with on-server checks and preview, inspiration pictures and the labeled concept image

Task T212 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: intake-photo-asks-preview.
Branch: `claude/task/T212-intake-photo-asks-preview`

## Goal

Intake asks for the job type's photos after the trade questions, fetches picture texts from allowed hosts only, strips metadata and checks each photo on Ask Hava's own server, shows the homeowner each one before the card goes, and for design categories takes inspiration pictures and offers a labeled concept image only while its switch is on (the switch stays off until the account checkpoint chooses the adapter; this task builds the switch-on path against the stand-in adapter, 3 change rounds, the label and the project file only, and tests it in TD-INT-28; T137 runs it against the live adapter, D-492) (INT-044 to INT-046, SEC-041, PRIV-015; the photo ask and upload page say "Please leave people, house numbers and license plates out of photos." and a picture text's media is deleted from the texting provider once stored, D-491). Picture-link fetching and the concept-image call live in `privacy` (which calls `adapters`); the upload page is a portals page; the photo and concept-image slots on the business job page are filled here: `quotes::job_page` (`job_page.rs`) supplies the data only and the page is rendered by the portals route `business_actions.rs` with `business_actions.html` (T148's), both extended here (D-491, D-492). The photo and design steps are placed in the conversation's step order in `app/intake/src/conversation.rs` (T040's and T103's, extended here, before T162 replaces the step sequence), and the design cases are tested in `tests/integration/tests/intake_photos.rs` with the photo cases (D-493).
