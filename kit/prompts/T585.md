## Goal

Plan change D-4052 (Casey's outside code audit, batch B, Nov 17, 2026; each item re-checked on `main` 67f601a before planning): (B28, D-3911's first follow-up, never scheduled) `reapply_license` (`app/businesses/src/capability.rs:626` to `:661`) drops job types through `models::replace_job_types` with no coverage report, so a trade a license change leaves with no active business does not alert until another change touches it (OPS-006; the gap is stated at `app/businesses/src/service.rs:13` to `:14`). It reports as `set_job_types` does: `service::report_edit` (`:209`) and `coverage_keys` (`:150`, for the before keys) made `pub(crate)`, with the before and after keys, in the same transaction, only when the business is active (a pause already reports through `apply`); the `service.rs` gap line is removed. (B10) `card_facts` (`app/licensing/src/card_facts.rs:67` to `:95`) keeps the ROC row only when its latest check showed Active (`roc_facts`, `:97`), and when none is current it falls through to `NoLicense`, so a quote card on an ROC or state-agency category whose license lapsed after the card went out shows the no-license city-list lines (LIC-038's lines are for work that needs no license). (D-4064, licensing, the Opus pre-claim check, narrowing D-4052) Only a real lapse changes: when the business's latest `licensing.license_check` row exists and is not Active, `card_facts` on an ROC kind returns `CardFacts::NoLicense` with `city_listed: false` and `city_since_year: None`, so `portals::quote_cards` drops the city line (`app/portals/src/quote_cards.rs:137`) and a handyman still shows LIC-024's line (`docs/spec/licensing.md:28`); a business with no ROC check row keeps today's path, since handyman and city-listed businesses quote ROC kinds that allow handymen (`plumbing_repair`, `docs/catalog/categories.yaml:73` to `:80`) and TD-PICK-62 expects their lines (`tests/integration/tests/quote_page_cards.rs:259` to `:270`). Returning `Ok(None)` for every licensed kind with no current license, as first planned, would drop LIC-024 from handyman cards; state-agency kinds keep today's path. The doc says why (no false claim either way; PICK-041 shows only checked facts). Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/license_change_coverage.rs`, fake data only: a class change that leaves a trade with no active business records 0 active for it and alerts (TD-OPS-01's shape; the case seeds an `ops.trade_request` row for the current month, since a trade alerts only with one, `app/ops/src/alerts.rs:263` to `:272`, and `report_edit` counts at `Timestamp::now()`); one that leaves at least 2 active businesses in that trade and in each of the business's towns raises no alert (OPS-006 alerts below 2, and `report_edit` reports the towns too, `service.rs:209` to `:217`); an ROC category whose latest check is not Active gives no-license facts with no city line, a handyman with such a check still gets LIC-024's line, a business with no check row keeps today's facts, and a no-license category still gives its city line. `capability` and `card_facts` tests pass unchanged. The second D-3911 follow-up (a town no business has served) stays unowned and is listed in D-4052.

Acceptance: `tests/integration/tests/license_change_coverage.rs` passes against PostgreSQL with every listed case, the coverage and lapsed-license cases failing on `main` before the change; existing `capability`, `card_facts`, coverage and `tests/integration/tests/quote_page_cards.rs` (TD-PICK-62) tests pass unchanged.
