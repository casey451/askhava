# T021: catalog: load categories, job types, license and fee rules, settings and switches

## Goal

`catalog` loads `docs/catalog/categories.yaml` into its tables: each category's job types, license rule, recurring flag and switch; a category not confirmed in the K2b list, or on the not-at-launch list, stays off; the AI never sets a license class; and switching a category on or off is a logged settings change with no code change (LIC-001 to LIC-005, AI-017). The card-field flag of each intake question is T177's (TD-LIC-24); the not-offered reply is T043's intake behaviour (TD-LIC-01 is shared with T043, D-491).
