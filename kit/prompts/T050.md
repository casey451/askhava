## Goal

An opt-out never changes a job's status: the job carries on through the quote page and email with the CON-028 notice, "cancel" alone never closes a job, and a sole-word CANCEL from a number with a live job only marks it paused by the homeowner (CON-012, CON-028, CON-029, JOB-020). Intake records the pause and enqueues the opt-out event; withdrawing a waiting pick is T054's, the still-open line by email T094's and on the quote page T052's, and the Confirm refusal T224's (D-491).
