## Goal

An opt-out never changes a job's status: the job carries on through the quote page and email with the CON-028 notice, "cancel" alone never closes a job, and a sole-word CANCEL from a number with a live job only marks it paused by the homeowner (CON-012, CON-028, CON-029, JOB-020).
