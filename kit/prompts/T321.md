## Goal

Fourth external audit F32 (D-2208; CON-022, CON-036, D-079): CON-022 keeps consent records, opt-in wording versions, STOP/HELP/START events and message logs 5 years after the last message to that number, and the privacy policy promises the same (`docs/legal/privacy-policy.md:91`), but nothing ever deletes them: `intake.consent_record` and the `messaging` keyword and message-log rows are append-only (`migrations/0053_intake_tables.sql:62`) and `app/intake/src/purge.rs` says they are never deleted there. (1) A daily `messaging.consent_sweep` (registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`, queued once a day) finds each number whose last inbound or outbound message is more than 1,827 days old (5 years, never short by a leap day) and deletes, in one transaction per number: its rows of every table CON-022 names, through `app/messaging/src/consent_purge.rs` for `messaging`'s and `intake::api` (`app/intake/src/consent_purge.rs`) for `intake.consent_record`, each with one `ops` audit row of IDs only; `migrations/0190_consent_five_year_purge.sql` grants the app role DELETE on exactly those tables and nothing else (no UPDATE, so they stay append-only for every other change). (2) The opt-out is never weakened: a number on `messaging.suppression` keeps its suppression row, the `messaging.optout_event` row that row points to (`event_id`, `migrations/0044_messaging_optout.sql:33`; digest-only, no phone) and its locked phone in the vault, because the suppression digest is keyed `homeowner-phone|v<n>` and PRIV-024's key rotation rebuilds it from the vault plaintext (D-079), so clearing the phone would lose the opt-out at the next rotation; only its other CON-022 rows (consent records, keyword events, message logs) go at 5 years. A number not on suppression loses its CON-022 rows and then its locked vault phone, cleared through `privacy::api` (`app/privacy/src/consent_phone.rs`). The DELETE grant on `messaging.optout_event` is limited to rows no suppression row references (a policy or a trigger that refuses the rest). That an opted-out number's phone stays locked past 5 years is listed for Casey, with whether the privacy policy should say so (D-2208). (3) Tests in `tests/integration/tests/consent_purge.rs` (TD-CON-30): a number last messaged 1,826 days ago keeps everything; at 1,827 days a number not on suppression loses its rows and its vault phone, with audit rows of IDs only; an opted-out number keeps its suppression row, the opt-out event it points to and its locked phone, loses its other rows, and is still refused a text after the purge and after an index-key rotation; deleting an opt-out event a suppression row references is refused; a message in between restarts its clock. The rotation case builds its two-version `VAULT_INDEX_KEY` the way `tests/integration/tests/vault.rs` does (its `key(version, byte)` helper at line 32 and the v1-plus-v2 key set of `the_rotation_drill_moves_every_row_to_v2`, TD-PRIV-09) and rotates with `privacy::api::rotate_batch` as that test and `tests/integration/tests/inbound_scrub_context.rs` do; it is not skipped. Update the `messaging`, `intake` and `privacy` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `docs/decisions.md`.
