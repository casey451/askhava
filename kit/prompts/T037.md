# T037: licensing: the agency license line and insurance line on cards

Task T037 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: licensing-card-lines.

## Goal

Quote cards show the license line with its check date and link (ROC or other agency), the license-since year from the state record, the city-license line for no-license businesses and business-reported insurance where it applies, and never a rating, review count or ranking from Ask Hava (LIC-026, LIC-032, LIC-034, LIC-037, LIC-038, WORD-016). The "About us" line (BIZ-022) is T081's and the quote page's arrival order T052's (D-491).
