Acceptance: TD-WEB-05 passes on PostgreSQL: the English and Spanish business pages show the switch-on WEB-024 line only when the business-texting switch is on and a business number is set, and the switch-off line otherwise, including on a read error; every existing TD-WEB case passes unchanged.
