## Goal

An Intake job with no reply gets one reminder at 24 hours and closes at 72 hours with the INT-019 text; and every new request hears back within 24 hours by text, email or a call-back alert to Casey (INT-019, INT-021, INT-023). Intake's inbound handler also routes LIST and LISTA to `quotes::api`'s LIST lines (T047) and texts them back (TD-INT-08, D-491). While the AI is down or a call fails, that step runs at once on the AI-off path, with no hold and no 30-minute retry (INT-021, D-492, D-493).
