# T210: ops and payments: the monthly intake accuracy report and the money view's sales-tax set-aside and expenses

Task T210 (build, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: intake-report-money-view.

## Goal

The monthly report lists no-quote cards and "need more info" quotes by category and question set with the golden-set share; and the money view shows the 8.6% sales-tax set-aside and Casey's manual expenses beside the ledger totals, never entering the ledger (OPS-040, FEE-046). The manual expenses list is its own table `payments.expense` (this task's migration; architecture section 3 names it, D-491). The money view's page and the expense-entry POST are the portals route `app/portals/src/money_view.rs` rendering `money_view.html` from `payments::api` (`app/payments/src/money_view.rs` computes the figures); the intake report's words sit in `intake_report.html` (D-492). The golden-set share comes from the stored runs T156 makes (`intake::api`) (D-493).
