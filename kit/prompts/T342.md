## Goal

Seventh audit N7-03 and N7-04's quote side (D-2334, D-2333; PICK-007, REC-010). (1) `gate_once` (`app/quotes/src/gate.rs:63`) gates each line on its own, so "555\n123\n4567" in a quote's notes, original wording or a tier name passes `text_bounce` (`app/quotes/src/intake.rs:369`). `text_bounce` also gates a second view of the whole field with every whitespace run (line feed, carriage return, tab, no-break space) folded to one space, and bounces when either view is cut; `gate.rs` is unchanged, so a card keeps its lines. After T293 (the brand owner of `intake.rs`) and T164 (the form and the stored column). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/quote_whole_field_gate.rs` (TD-PICK-58): 555/123/4567 across `\n`, `\r\n`, a tab and a no-break space; an email or domain split across lines; "3 x 4-foot boards", "2 coats\n3 days" and short numbers on separate lines still accepted; each in notes, original wording and a tier name; a per-visit quote without a schedule bounced, with one accepted. Update the `quotes` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
