## Goal

T191 built the unreachable-homeowner steps but nothing runs them (D-1814: "the caller (the worker, not wired yet)"), the missed-call text has no send time (D-1819), a number that can't be texted gets no TXT-030 email copy (D-1819), and the 72-hour emailed question is not sent (D-1818) (D-1845). `app/picks/src/unreachable_steps.rs` registers a `picks.unreachable_step` `core.task` handler in the shared worker, enqueued by the release (`app/picks/src/release.rs`, in the release's transaction, deduped per pick and step) at release + 24, + 48, + 72 and + 96 hours, by T271's route after a logged business call, and by T277 on an email-contact day-3 NO. Each run gathers the facts (`calls::api::call_log` through T270's `call_record`, `messaging::api::send_window`, the pick's contact choice from T266, the day-3 answer, the charge) and calls `queue_reminders`, `missed_call_text`, `no_call_at_72_hours` and `decide_at_96_hours` as due; `RefundWaiting` enqueues the step again an hour later. The missed-call text is queued only at its send time (8 a.m. Arizona time unless the job is an emergency) by enqueuing the step for that time, never early; a number that can't be texted gets TXT-030's email copy through `messaging`'s email queue with the postal address and manage link; at + 72 hours on an email-contact job the step sends T278's `emailed_question`. A second handler, `picks.fraud_warning`, enqueued by T259's Stripe dispatch for `radar.early_fraud_warning.created` in the event's transaction, runs `picks::api::fraud_warning` for the warned charge's pick (which calls `payments::api::early_fraud_warning`) and runs again an hour later while T270's `refund_state` answers in flight (FEE-052). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/unreachable_steps.rs` with the clock advanced (no sleeps). Update the `picks` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. A 96-hour run that refunded and closed the job but failed before the business's decision email was queued (`decide_at_96_hours` writes it on a fresh connection after the close, `app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs:365`) is retried by the queue and still calls `decide_at_96_hours` although the job is now Closed (the step's gatherer never skips a closed job for that step); the email's key keeps it once (fourth external audit, related observation, D-2204).

Acceptance: TD-JOB-14 passes against PostgreSQL with the clock advanced; every T191, T270 and T278 case still passes.
