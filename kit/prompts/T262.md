# T262: calls and server: a random stored PIN per line, unique on its number, durable PIN-try counts, and the inbound voice route

## Goal

A line's PIN is its ID modulo 10,000 (`calls::api::job_pin`, D-1684), and line IDs are not secret; the 6-a-day count is the caller's input with no storage (D-1685); and no inbound-voice route serves the TwiML (D-1708). D-1684 and D-1685 named T078 or T080, both DONE without it (external audit A09, D-1755). (1) `migrations/0151_calls_pins_attempts.sql`: a `pin` column on `calls.number_pool` (four digits, drawn from the OS random source, unique among open lines of one pool number by a partial unique index) and `calls.pin_try` (a digest of the calling number, never the number: HMAC-SHA-256 under the ID secret with the domain label `pin-try|v1` over the normalized E.164 number, derived with `core`'s ID-secret derivation as `IdempotencyKey::derive` is, listed under `ID_SECRET` in the PRIV-024 key inventory and re-derived when that secret rotates; pool number; Arizona day; count; queries in `app/calls/src/models.rs`), app role SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE of the count only. (2) `app/calls/src/pins.rs`: allocation in `assign_number`'s transaction (`app/calls/src/service.rs`) retries a collision a bounded number of times, then raises the existing pool alert and refuses the line; `job_pin` and every read of the PIN come from the stored column (`routing.rs`), never the line ID; the try count is one atomic `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE ... RETURNING` per wrong try, so concurrent wrong tries cannot pass 6 a day, and a repeated Twilio callback (same CallSid and digits) is not another try (recorded per CallSid). (3) `app/server/src/inbound_voice.rs`: the signature-checked Twilio voice webhook (SEC-016) for the pool numbers, recorded by CallSid (SEC-017), finding the party, lines, stored counts and STIR/SHAKEN attestation and serving `calls::api` TwiML (prompt, PIN, read-back on A attestation only, dial), merged in the shared `lib.rs`. No line ID reaches a homeowner or business. Update `docs/architecture.md` section 10, the `calls` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-CALL-13 and TD-CALL-14 pass against PostgreSQL; every existing TD-CALL-02 and TD-CALL-11 case still passes with the PIN read from storage.
