# T106: ops: /healthz, health page figures, monthly spot-check

## Goal

The app answers `/healthz` for the uptime monitor, the owner portal shows the OPS-008 health figures, and it offers the monthly spot-check of 10 random job cards (OPS-007 to OPS-009). The `/healthz` probe is `app/server/src/healthz.rs` (the server binary checks the process, the database and the worker heartbeat, which is the age of the newest `core.task` lease or finish, so no new table is needed; `ops` holds no route, D-491); the health page with its figures is a portals page reading each module's `api` (the last-backup figure reads `ops::api`, where `recovery` records each backup and restore result; T109 adds that record and replaces this task's 'not yet recorded' placeholder, D-493). `web` and `worker` exit non-zero when their own liveness check has failed for 3 minutes (a setting) so the host's On Failure policy restarts them (OPS-049), and the health page shows each required secret as present, parsed and environment-matched, never its value, length or hash (OPS-008) (D-492).
