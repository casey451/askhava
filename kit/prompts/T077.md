# T077: calls: PIN routing and the paid-business read-back

Task T077 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: calls-pin-routing.
Branch: `claude/task/T077-calls-pin-routing`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security).

## Goal

A call from an unlinked phone must enter the 4-digit job PIN, at most 3 tries per call and 6 per number per day; a caller with two jobs on one number is routed only by PIN; and the first-name-and-street read-back goes only to a phone linked to the paid business on a call with A-level attestation (CALL-003 to CALL-005, CALL-025). Once the pool gives the masked number, this task also sends the who's-calling text (TXT-027) as an important text through `messaging`, at once on an emergency job and held to 08:00 on a regular one (TD-TXT-29, TD-QH-04); the missed-call text is T191's (D-491). The release (`app/picks/src/release.rs`, T069's, extended here) asks `calls::api` for the job's masked number and then sends the who's-calling text (CALL-001, PICK-022, D-491); on an emergency job no masked line opens before the address is in (TD-CALL-12).
