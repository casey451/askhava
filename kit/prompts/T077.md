# T077: calls: PIN routing and the paid-business read-back

## Goal

A call from an unlinked phone must enter the 4-digit job PIN, at most 3 tries per call and 6 per number per day; a caller with two jobs on one number is routed only by PIN; and the first-name-and-street read-back goes only to a phone linked to the paid business on a call with A-level attestation (CALL-003 to CALL-005, CALL-025).
