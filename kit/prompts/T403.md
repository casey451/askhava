## Goal

Plan change D-3106, review round (QH-004, QH-001; D-2628, D-3055): split from T402. After T356 the outbox sender holds a row outside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. unless its `quiet_exempt_until` lets it go, and an emergency job's texts are exempt only through `messaging::api::enqueue_exempt`. Two producers queue such texts with plain `enqueue`: the who's-calling text TXT-027 (`app/calls/src/whos_calling.rs`, T318's) and the missed-call text TXT-030 (`caller_missed`, `app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs:214`, T355's; `rules::Kind::PickStep`, `app/messaging/src/rules.rs:168`). Each passes the job's emergency choice through `enqueue_exempt`, read where the producer already has it (the job row), so on an emergency job either text goes at any hour and on a regular job it waits for the window; nothing else in either file changes. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/quiet_exempt_call_texts.rs` (TD-QH-08). Every TD-QH, TD-TXT, TD-CALL and TD-FEE case passes unchanged. Update the `calls` and `picks` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
