## Goal

Plan change D-4292: after T643 makes `portals::api::is_cross_site` accept a browser's own same-origin form post (`Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin` with the `Origin: null` that `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` produces), the nine e2e specs, the two e2e helpers and the three Ally specs this task owns (fourteen files) still re-send every `POST` through `page.route` with a forged `Origin: https://<host>` and `sec-fetch-site: same-origin` (the `httpsOrigin` helpers, `ownerOrigin` in `tests/e2e/owner_session.ts:143` to `:149`, and the Confirm re-send in `tests/e2e/keyed.ts:195`; D-1250, D-4162), so they test a request no browser sends. Remove every `httpsOrigin` helper and call, the export in `tests/e2e/j2.ts` and its imports in `tests/e2e/j2_business.spec.ts` and `tests/a11y/j2_keyboard.spec.ts`, and the comments that explain them; `ownerOrigin` and its call in `owner_session.ts` go too, and the Confirm route in `keyed.ts` keeps capturing each `POST`'s `postData` for its callers and then calls `route.continue()` with no header rewrite; each spec then posts with the browser's own headers. Any wait that relied on the re-send's `maxRedirects: 0` follows the browser's own redirect instead; every assertion stays, and none is loosened. After this task no file under `tests/` sets a request's `Origin` or `Sec-Fetch-Site` through `page.route` (TD-SEC-73), which `grep -rn "sec-fetch-site" tests/e2e tests/a11y` shows. The removed lines move later lines up; T614's goal cites the Tab limits at `tests/a11y/j1_keyboard.spec.ts:105` to `:109` and `tests/a11y/j2_keyboard.spec.ts:61` to `:85` by line, and the code it means is unchanged.
