### 2. Consent: what Ask Hava has and why it is enough (lines naming this task's requirements)
**Rules that follow** (already in the spec): no reply is required to continue (CON-004, CON-027); the only opt-in is her own first inbound text; the web chat collects no SMS consent (while texting is on it shows the number and asks her to text it; in web-and-email mode its Verify code checks the number only, CON-005, CON-025, D-5279); consent is to Ask Hava only and is never shared (CON-023) [S10 5.1.4, S12].
