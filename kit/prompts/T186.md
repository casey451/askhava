## Goal

The business's original wording is always stored and shown, an AI summary that adds any claim is rejected, every quote card shows the required quote facts in order in the homeowner's language, and each card shows the business name, license numbers with their record lines and review links (PICK-003, PICK-004, PICK-040, PICK-041). The Google and Yelp review links appear once T165 stores them; this task renders the quote facts and leaves the link slot to T165, which adds the PICK-041 link case to its tests (D-491). The business sees its own quote card exactly as homeowners do (PICK-005), and each card of a business that ticked the after-hours box (BIZ-007, T041's data) shows "Gets emergency requests at night" with the card order unchanged (PICK-044) (D-493).
