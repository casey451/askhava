## Goal

Plan change D-4360 (unwired sweep, orphan 4; CARD-018, CARD-024, CARD-020; D-1021, D-2508). `quotes::api::send_late_cards` and `send_late_cards_in_reach` (`app/quotes/src/service.rs:103`, `:120`) have no caller, so a business activated, unpaused or newly ticking a job type while a matching job is Open or No quotes yet never gets its card. Every status move of a business goes through `businesses::service::move_status` (`app/businesses/src/service.rs:118`), and job-type, town and tick changes through `set_job_types`, `set_towns` and `set_ticks` (`:468`, `:434`, `:399`; T375's Job types page reaches `set_job_types` through `capability::save_job_types`, so T375 needs no change). (1) In `app/businesses/src/service.rs`, in the same transaction: a move into Active from any other status, and a change that adds a job type, a town or a tick, queues one `businesses.became_eligible` task (`business` only, deduplicated per business and minute; no homeowner detail). (2) A new `app/quotes/src/late_cards.rs` handles it, registered in `app/server/src/lib.rs` (shared): for each job in Open or No quotes yet whose card the business has not had, built from the stored card and the job's place as `cards_out` builds them, `send_late_cards_in_reach`, then the card notice through `queue_card_notice` (CARD-020, its 24 hours from now), an emergency job's card as `queue_emergency_card` sends it. Tests (TD-CARD-17) in the new `tests/integration/tests/late_cards.rs`, PostgreSQL, fake data only, the clock set: activation, a self-unpause, a rule unpause and a new job-type tick each send the open job's card once with its notice; a job in another status, a business already carded, and a business outside the job's reach get nothing; a rerun sends nothing twice.

Acceptance: TD-CARD-17 passes against PostgreSQL; each eligibility event sends a matching open job's card and notice once; nothing is sent for other statuses or to a business already carded.
