## Goal

The Confirm view, form and script exist (T224) but no route serves them (D-1480 (a), D-1740 (1)), the global `security_headers` layer replaces every response's CSP with the self-only policy so Stripe.js could never load (D-1480 (c)), the script ignores `handleNextAction`'s result, and the only browser test is a hand-written substitute page (external audit A08, D-1754). (1) Serve `GET` and `POST /b/picks/{pick}/confirm` from `app/portals/src/confirm_pay.rs` (T084's session and principal scoping, T232's terms record), merged in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` with T260's `StripeHttp` as the `IntentStripe`: only a signed-in owner or "Confirm and billing" member of the pick's business reaches it (any other answers the one not-found page); `intake::api::paused_by_homeowner` and the pick's status are checked again on every load and press (CON-029); the fee, currency and job type come from the server's stored facts, never the form: the fee is `payments::api::pick_fee`, the fee T287 fixed at the pick, and every `Press` carries exactly that amount (D-1871); `open_intent` on the first load, `waive_at_confirm` for a $0 pick, `may_confirm` and `confirm_and_pay` per press, `press_reply` as JSON with the client secret only for a bank approval. When `may_confirm` answers `ConfirmError::FeeModeChanged` (the owner switched the fee mode after the pick), nothing is charged, the page says the fee terms changed after this pick so it can't be confirmed, and the pick ends at once with no fee through the same `picks` call as T232's "Can't take it" with its own reason, never counted against the business (PICK-050, OPS-034, D-1871). (2) CSP: `app/portals/src/security.rs` keeps the self-only policy for every page and lets the Confirm route alone add `https://js.stripe.com` to `script-src` and `frame-src` (and Stripe's documented `connect-src`), by a response marker the layer reads rather than by trusting any handler header; a test proves every other page still gets the strict policy (SEC-012). (3) `app/portals/static/confirm_pay.ts`: `handleNextAction`'s error or cancel shows the try-again line and never a success, success shows "Checking with your bank" until the server's answer, and the page then asks the server for the attempt's state rather than trusting the browser. (4) Tests: `app/server/tests/confirm_route.rs` (the served route with the real middleware) and `tests/e2e/confirm_served.spec.ts` against the app and the stand-ins; T224's `tests/e2e/confirm_pay.spec.ts` stays as it is and counts as a component test of the script only, never as journey evidence (D-1754). Update `docs/architecture.md` section 10, the `portals` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`. The Confirm route is served only after T328 registers the payments tasks it relies on (the charge check, the pay timer, the refunds and the reconcile), so no Confirm press can queue a task the worker would dead-letter (fifth external audit AH-001, D-2286). (Sixth audit U-02, D-2302; IC-33.) The $0 handoff: on a $0 decision the route records it with `waive_at_confirm`, then takes `payments::api::no_fee_release(conn, pick)` (bound to the pick's own stored fee row since T337) and hands that `Releasable` to the production release sink (`app/server/src/release_sink.rs`), which settles and releases once; a press repeated after a crash between the waiver and the release finds the waiver recorded and the release row, and releases nothing twice. Tests in `app/server/tests/confirm_route.rs` (TD-FEE-58).

Acceptance: TD-FEE-45 (with its D-1871 cases) and TD-SEC-37 pass (`app/server/tests/confirm_route.rs` against PostgreSQL, `tests/e2e/confirm_served.spec.ts` in Playwright with axe); `tests/e2e/confirm_pay.spec.ts` passes unchanged.
