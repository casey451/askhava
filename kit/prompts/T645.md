## Goal

Plan change D-4292 (spot review of texting and consent, verified on `main` 67827d85): the web chat skips three rules the text path keeps. (a) INT-038 says "a message or web chat", but `conversation::stop_if_under_18` (`app/intake/src/conversation.rs:289`) is called only from `replies.rs`; `chat_message` screens a pre-code message with `screen` (911 and business intent only) and after the code calls `receive_text` directly, so "I'm 15" opens a request, records consent and gets cards. (b) `chat_message` builds its `Inbound` with `opt_out: false` (`app/intake/src/web_chat.rs:263` to `:269`) and nothing on the chat path runs `messaging::api::detect_opt_out`, so "STOP", "cancel" or "stop texting me" typed into a verified chat is taken as an intake answer while texts keep coming (CON-008, CON-010, CON-032's "any reasonable revocation"; D-4292 adds the web chat to CON-035's channels). (c) `open_verified` takes its language from `ChatSettings.language`, which `site` always sets to English, and the first message's words are not kept (PRIV-008), so TXT-002 and TXT-041 never apply to a web-chat request. (1) Opt-out: after the code, before anything else, `chat_message` runs `messaging::api::detect_opt_out` on the words; on a sole opt-out word or a revocation phrase (English or Spanish, "cancel" included) it lists the session's number through `messaging::api::suppress` with a new `RevokeChannel::WebChat` (stored `web_chat`; `migrations/0210_messaging_web_chat_optout.sql` widens the channel check of `migrations/0044_messaging_optout.sql:21` and any later copy of it), answers `ChatAnswer::Shown` with the opt-out template and CON-009's confirmation words in the session's language (no new `ChatAnswer` variant: `site::chat::message`, which this task does not own, matches it exhaustively at `app/site/src/chat.rs:340` to `:347`; `Shown`'s doc in `web_chat.rs` is widened to say it also carries the opt-out confirmation and the INT-038 reply), sends no text, and does not call `receive_text`, so no job or request changes ("cancel" alone never closes a job, JOB-020); "can stop by at 3" and "please cancel my request" are not opt-outs (CON-010).
