A successful charge writes its attempt status and `payments.charge_evidence` (`write_evidence`, `app/payments/src/charge.rs:656`, from `effects`, `app/payments/src/handlers.rs:144`) but no `charge` ledger entry, since `record_fee_at_confirm` (`app/payments/src/service.rs:401`) has no caller. So a later job of the same customer is charged again (`free_wins::is_repeat` reads only ledger rows, FEE-013); the statement and money view show refunds with no charges (FEE-030); the reconcile would raise a High `payments.unknown_charge` alert for every charge; the evidence lacks the charge ID (FEE-042); and `queue_receipt` (`app/payments/src/receipts.rs:56`) has no caller, so no Ask Hava receipt is emailed (FEE-031). Also, for a fee-free job-type pick `waive_at_confirm` returns `NotWaived` and writes nothing (`app/payments/src/free_wins.rs:139`), so `no_fee_release` finds no $0 entry and the pick is never released (FEE-045). Fix: (1) the release proof's fresh fetch (`paid_release`, `app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:193`) requires a `ch_` `latest_charge` on a succeeded intent (missing: refused like any unreadable field, so Stripe's resend retries) and carries it to the success write; (2) in the transaction that writes the evidence (`migrations/0183_payments_charge_id.sql` adds a nullable `stripe_charge_id` to `payments.charge_evidence`, still append-only for the app role), the success path stores the charge ID, appends one `charge` ledger entry at the pick's stored fee (`payments.pick_fee`, equal to the checked intent amount) under a key derived from the pick, not from (job, business), so a second paid pick of the same business on the same job after a refund keeps its own entry; the reconcile's own-intent charge key (`key_and_id`, `app/payments/src/reconcile.rs:381`) uses the same per-pick key, so it converges instead of doubling or hiding a charge; and it calls `queue_receipt` when the evidence row was newly written; a replayed success writes nothing more; (3) `waive_at_confirm` writes the `fee-free-job-type` $0 entry (D-1419) when the pick's stored fee row says the pick is fee-free (FEE-045, fixed at the pick by T340), with no card and no `may_confirm` proof needed (FEE-036, BIZ-001), and answers so that `no_fee_release` clears it. (4) `is_repeat` (`app/payments/src/free_wins.rs:186`) counts a charge on another of the customer's jobs only when that charge was an actual introduction (FEE-013): a charge whose pick was refunded before any release, a late success auto-refunded with nothing released (T332's trigger) or an unreleased fraud-warning refund (`fraud-warning`), is not counted; refunds after a release (unreachable, no-show) still count; (5) `record_fee_at_confirm` (`app/payments/src/service.rs:401`, its key over job and business at `:428`) stays as it is and unused in production: the success path writes the charge entry and `waive_at_confirm` the $0 ones, and four done tests call it (`fee_mode_switch.rs`, `ledger_1000_jobs.rs`, `fee_at_pick.rs`, `fee_fixed_at_pick.rs`), so its doc comment says it is not the production writer and must not be called for a charge, whose key is per pick (D-2623). T261's Confirm route keeps calling `waive_at_confirm` then `no_fee_release` and needs no change. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/charge_ledger_entry.rs` (TD-FEE-64), each failing on today's `main`: a stand-in success webhook leaves exactly one `charge` row with the `ch_` ID and the stored cents, the evidence row holds the same ID, and one `payments.receipt` task is queued; the replay adds nothing; a succeeded intent with no `ch_` ID releases nothing; `business_statement` totals equal the charges; after a released, paid pick a pick of the same business on another job of the same customer is waived as `Repeat`; after a late success auto-refunded with nothing released, or an unreleased pick refunded for a fraud warning, the next job is not waived; a charge, its full refund, a re-pick of the same business on the same job and a second charge leave two `charge` rows, and a reconcile over both appends nothing and raises no `payments.unknown_charge`; a fee-free job-type pick of a business with no saved card gets its `fee-free-job-type` entry from `waive_at_confirm` and `no_fee_release` clears it.
