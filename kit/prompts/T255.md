## Goal

On an emergency job a release that comes before the address stores one `picks.address_wait` row and queues one `picks.address_needed` event (T069, TD-INT-34), but nothing handles the event, and nothing releases once the address arrives: the proof was consumed and `paid_release` needs a new Stripe event (D-1563).
