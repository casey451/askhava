# T035: licensing: handyman limit, gas and permit block, split-job check, certification

## Goal

- **AI-012** The gas keyword list, kept in the catalog and including at least gas, propane, natural gas, pilot, pilot light, "won't light", furnace, gas line, gas dryer, gas stove, gas range, propano and piloto, overrides the AI's reading of whether a job is handyman work (D-157). (`docs/spec/ai.md:16`)
- **LIC-019** A handyman quote for construction-type work whose total (labor plus materials, top of any range) is $1,000 or more is refused with an explanation of Arizona's handyman limit. (`docs/spec/licensing.md:23`)
- **LIC-020** The $1,000 limit does not apply to non-construction services such as detailing or moving. (`docs/spec/licensing.md:24`)
- **LIC-021** Gas work, hardwired smoke alarms and fire sprinklers are never sent to handymen at any price. (`docs/spec/licensing.md:25`)
- **LIC-022** Permit-type jobs (water heater replacement, panels, new circuits, re-roofs and similar) are never sent to handymen. (`docs/spec/licensing.md:26`)
- **LIC-023** A split-job check runs when a handyman's quote is submitted and blocks it when the same business has another handyman job at the same address within 30 days and the two jobs share a job type or together reach $1,000 (D-027, D-081, D-154). (`docs/spec/licensing.md:27`)
- **LIC-024** Every handyman quote card shows "Not a licensed contractor. Only for jobs under $1,000 total with no permit." (`docs/spec/licensing.md:28`)
- **LIC-025** A handyman quote is accepted only with the certification box ticked on the same screen as the price, stating that the quote is the full-job price under $1,000 with no permit and no gas, smoke-alarm or sprinkler work; a quote of $1,000.00 or more is refused (LIC-019); and when Casey records a confirmed breach (a complaint or a check showing permit, gas, smoke-alarm or sprinkler work, or a job total of $1,000 or more), the business is removed: its account ends and it gets no further cards (D-485). (`docs/spec/licensing.md:29`)
- **LIC-036** Handyman quotes are taken through the portal quote form, whose required certification box sits on the same screen as the price and is ticked on every quote, and a handyman's text quote gets a link to that form (D-081, D-186). (`docs/spec/licensing.md:40`)

Reason: follows the statute's "under" wording and the changes file's construction-only scope.
- **D-081** Question: how do handymen "certify each quote" (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_AUTHORITATIVE.md:241`), and what is "overlapping scope" in the split-job check (`sources/01_spec/Changes_Since_v9.3_ … Choice: handyman quotes go through the portal quote form with a required certification box (a text quote from a handyman gets the form link); overlapping scope means the same category or a shared job type at the same property within 30 days (D-027). Reason: a stored, versioned certification per quote; the broader overlap test is legally safer.
