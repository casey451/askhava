## Goal

Fourth external audit F17 (D-2206; PICK-041, BIZ-022, TXT-031, D-336, D-1310, D-1687): no code supplies a business's public name, so the LIST reply sends none (`app/server/src/lib.rs:277` builds `names: Vec::new()`, D-1310) and the who's-calling text cannot be built (D-1687: "no table holds one"). (1) `app/businesses/src/public_name.rs` exports `businesses::api::public_names(conn, &[Uuid])`: for each business, the name on the record it signed up with, read through `licensing::api` at call time (the record's trade name or DBA when it has one, else its business name; a no-license business's city-list entry), screened by `screen_public_name` (D-336); a name that fails the screen, or a business with no record, is left out (the caller shows its own fallback) and raises one Low alert per business with its ID only. If `businesses` keeps no link to the record, stop and report rather than guess. Nothing is stored and no new table is added; a name the business edits later in the portal is not built here (recorded in the decision). (2) Tests in `tests/integration/tests/business_public_name.rs` (TD-BIZ-43): a record with a trade name gives the trade name, one without gives the business name, a no-license business its city-list name, a name holding a phone number or a link is left out with one alert, and an unknown business is left out. Its callers are T317 (LIST) and T318 (who's calling); the quote page's cards (PICK-041) are register row IC-71. Update the `businesses` `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `docs/decisions.md`.
