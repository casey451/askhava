## Goal

Plan change D-3189 (PRIV-008, PRIV-011, CARD-007, CARD-023, INT-041; D-801, D-1521, D-1274, D-2330, D-3118), the plan fix for BLOCKED T251's reported gap (D-2859). The homeowner's scrubbed job description and the card she approved are stored nowhere: `messaging.inbox` keeps only the provider ID and time (D-801), a sent text's body is cleared (`migrations/0042_messaging_outbox.sql:34`), `intake.intake_answer` holds codes only (`migrations/0053_intake_tables.sql:68`) and `quotes.job_card` holds IDs only (`migrations/0067_quotes_job_card.sql:13`), while the approval event carries only the card digest (`app/intake/src/summary_step.rs:415`), the emergency release only the request (`app/intake/src/service.rs:672`) and the business job page needs the card she approved from its caller (`app/quotes/src/job_page.rs:33`); so T251's card sender, T162's card build (step 12 needs the words of step 1) and T301's quote form have nothing to read. PRIV-008 keeps job text, with contact details removed, for 3 years; this task is that store (owner call under Casey's Nov 11 standing rule, D-3189). Raw inbound bodies are still never stored (D-801) and the outbox still clears a sent body. (1) `migrations/0154_quotes_job_text.sql`: `quotes.job_description`, one row per inbound text whose scrubbed words describe a job (request ID, job ID, a message ID typed `uuid`, the words, the recorded time; unique on message ID and job ID together, since a SPLIT opens a second job from the same text, `app/intake/src/routing.rs:423` to `:443`; the message ID is the `messaging.inbox` row ID for a text and the chat message's own ID for web chat, which has no inbox row, `migrations/0053_intake_tables.sql:54` to `:59`; no foreign key to `messaging.inbox`, so a later inbox purge is never blocked), and `quotes.shown_card`, one row per card shown or released for a job (request ID, job ID and the 32-hex card digest, unique together and checked by pattern; the card text, town, place code and photo IDs exactly as `build_card` made them; the category code and the remaining candidate job type codes; an `emergency` mark, true only for a card stored on the INT-029 emergency path; the recorded time; `sent_at`, null until the card sender marks it); no name, phone, email, street address or contact column; app role SELECT, INSERT and DELETE on both, and UPDATE of `shown_card.sent_at` only. The number is the free 0154: this task depends on the done T157 (0153) and sits just before T287 (0155) in `kit/tasks.json`, so its number rises in the runner's serial order (V9). (2) `app/quotes/src/job_text.rs`, through the shared `quotes::api`: `keep_description(conn, (request, job), message, &ScrubbedText, at)` takes only the scrubber's `ScrubbedText` (`app/privacy/src/scrub.rs:320`, made only by `privacy::api`), so raw words cannot be stored (T277's rule, D-3118), and a repeat for the same message and job stores nothing; `description(conn, job)` gives the job's stored words in time order, or `None`; `keep_card(conn, (request, job), digest, &JobCard, (category, candidates, emergency), at)` stores a card only when `digest` is its text's card digest, which `quotes` computes itself with the workspace `sha2` (added to the shared `app/quotes/Cargo.toml`; `quotes` may not call `intake`, `kit/layers.json`): the first 32 hex digits of the SHA-256 of `JobCard::text`, the value `intake::api::card_digest` gives and approval stores, the test asserting the two agree, refuses any other digest without writing, and stores nothing on a repeat; `stored_card(conn, job, digest)` gives that card (rebuilt through a new crate-private constructor in `app/quotes/src/cards.rs`; `build_card` stays the only public way to make one; `JobCard`'s derived `Debug` (`cards.rs:108`) becomes a hand-written one that hides the text, as `CardParts`' does, `:89` to `:102`), its category and candidates, only while the stored text's digest still equals `digest` (checked on every read, so the card sent is the card approved, CARD-007); `unsent_cards(conn, request)` gives, for each job of the request with no card sent, its newest stored card with its `emergency` mark, oldest job first (the emergency path, which has no approval step and sends only marked cards, T251); `mark_card_sent(conn, job, digest, at)` sets `sent_at` once; `sent_card(conn, job)` gives the card that went out, for the business job page and the quote form (CARD-023). Access: the words and card text are read only by the card sender (T251), the intake order that builds and shows the card (T162) and the quote form with the job page's data (T301); every returned type's `Debug` hides the words and the card text, nothing here logs them, and nothing here sends them to an AI call. (3) Retention and deletion: `quotes::api::run_text_purge(pool, task)` deletes a job's rows on `privacy`'s decided PRIV-008 text stage (3 years after close, holds applied) and does nothing on the other stages, safe to run twice; it is called in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` `privacy::api::PURGE_KIND` registration after `intake::api::run_purge`, as T315 does for feedback; "Delete my data" (`confirm_request`, `app/portals/src/data_download.rs:239`) deletes the rows of every request of hers in the transaction of `privacy::api::delete_my_data` (PRIV-011), by request ID, through a new `conversations_of_contact(conn, contact)` in the shared `app/intake/src/api.rs` (her requests through `intake.homeowner.contact_id`, open or closed): job links cannot be used, since `jobs_of_contact` (`app/intake/src/api.rs:140`) returns only each request's first job (`intake.conversation.job_id`, set once, `app/intake/src/models.rs:798`) and closing a request deletes its `job.%` links (`close_request`, `models.rs:711` to `:729`). Before deleting them it reads the jobs those rows name and adds them to the jobs it passes to `delete_my_data`, so a later job's photos go too (every job T162 opens stores its opening words; the photo gap of `jobs_of_contact` is closed here, D-3189). The text stage deletes by job ID, which every row still holds. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/job_text_store.rs` (TD-PRIV-27), fake data only; `job_text.rs` is privacy code and its tests reach at least 95% branch coverage (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:481`); every existing purge, data-download, card and job-page test passes unchanged.
