## Goal

Fourth external audit F41 (D-2209; AI-019, D-988): `adds_fact` (`app/ai/src/output_checks.rs:281`) never counts a capitalized word that starts a line or follows `.`, `!`, `?` or `:`, so "Rheem unit is leaking." passes AI-019's "adds no fact" check when her words never named Rheem; the only case plants the name mid-sentence (`app/ai/src/tests/prop_ai_output_limits.rs:220`). Check every fixed Ask Hava text the AI may echo so none trips it. Tests in `app/ai/src/tests/adds_fact_openers.rs` (TD-AI-26): "Rheem unit is leaking." and "Water heater broke. Rheem says so." are refused when her words never named Rheem and pass when they did; "The water heater is leaking." and "¿Cuándo empezó?" pass; every existing `ai` output-check test passes unchanged. Update the `ai` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
