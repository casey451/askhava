# T336: server, adapters and stand-in (harness): a text stopped by a module error is retried a bounded number of times from Twilio's copy of its body, never stored

Task T336 (harness, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: inbound-text-bounded-retry.
Branch: `claude/task/T336-inbound-text-bounded-retry`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security, harness).

## Goal

Sixth audit U-06 (D-2307; TXT-012, TXT-020, CON-008, CON-018; D-801): a text stopped by a non-database module error (`Halt::Failed`) is answered 200 and, after T325, raises an alert, but nothing processes it again; D-2284's reason (no body to replay) was incomplete: Twilio keeps the body until the app redacts it on day 31 (`app/messaging/src/purge.rs:3`) and the inbox row holds the MessageSid. After T325: (1) `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`: a `Halt::Failed` text also queues one `messaging.inbound_retry` task in the delivery's transaction (payload the inbox row ID only; dedupe per inbox row), retried at most 3 times with backoff (5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours); the last failure leaves T325's alert open and adds nothing. (2) `app/adapters/src/sms.rs`: `SmsBodyRead::fetch_message_body(sid)` (GET Messages/{sid}.json) answers the body, or `Gone` for a redacted or unknown message; `stand_ins/twilio.py` already serves that GET (D-2156) and gains only what the contract test needs. (3) `app/server/src/inbound_retry.rs` (registered in `worker_registry` in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` (`:456`) under the Twilio settings; not `bin/worker.rs`, D-3118): it reads the inbox row, fetches the body, runs it through the same scrubbing reader (`privacy::api::read_inbound_for`) and then the same steps in the same order as the route, skipping the inbox insert; it never stores or logs the body (D-801). Three rules: (a) opt-out and HELP always run (CON-008, CON-018); (b) if a newer inbox row from the same sender has already been processed, the retry does only (a) and closes, so stale content never overrides what she said since, and T325's alert stays as the record; (c) anything the retry sends goes through the normal outbound path under quiet hours (8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the stricter of Arizona and the area code's time, QH-001), never as an immediate reply outside them. Before building, read how a `Halt::Failed` delivery commits the steps that ran before the failure and state it: each step is either keyed once per inbox row or MessageSid, so a repeat changes nothing, or the retry starts after the last committed step; a step that cannot be made safe to repeat stops this task, and it is reported instead. `Gone` (redacted on day 31, or by `Delete my data`) closes the task with no error. Permanent classes are not retried: a delivery refused as `BadWebhook`, a sender on the suppression list, and a step that answered a known refusal (`Halt::Ends`). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/inbound_text_retry.rs` (TD-TXT-35, including a STOP retried after a newer text, a retry after a newer processed text doing only opt-out and HELP, and a retry at 22:00 whose reply waits for 8 a.m.) and `tests/contract/test_twilio_message_read.py`. A harness task because `stand_ins/` is protected. Privacy and security: the overseer runs an independent review of the hand-in. T299 and T129 run after it. Update the `server` and `adapters` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
