## Goal

The first message to any new number is the exact CON-031 consent text (in Spanish when CON-026 says so), after the INT-004 text on a 911-list match; it asks for no reply, carries no fee line, links the Terms and privacy policy, and any later reply is handled as part of the conversation (CON-001 to CON-004, CON-026, CON-027, CON-031). Its migration `intake_tables` creates the intake tables later intake tasks fill (`intake.homeowner`, `intake.property`, `intake.consent_record`, `intake.conversation`, `intake.intake_answer`, `intake.emergency_event`, `intake.recruiting_demand`, architecture section 3; the first three added by D-493) with the consent columns, and `models.rs` holds their row types; T039, T040 and T043 extend `models.rs` (D-492).
