# T084: portals: business one-time sign-in link and 30-day sessions

Task T084 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: portals-business-signin.

## Goal

A business person signs in with a single-use 15-minute link to the address on file and keeps a 30-day session; every portal route is scoped to the signed-in principal under the generated authorization matrix; and the owner realm stays separate (SEC-004 to SEC-006, SEC-008, SEC-019). It also scopes the Confirm-and-pay route (`confirm_pay.rs`, T224's) to the signed-in principal: only the owner or a "Confirm and billing" person of the picked business may complete it (PICK-033, D-492).
