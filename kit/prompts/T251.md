# T251: intake and quotes: the ZIP question, the job location, and the card-sending caller (every window-opened card goes out once, idempotent, quiet hours honoured)

Branch: `claude/task/T251-intake-zip-and-cards-out`

## Goal

Job cards never go out in production today (D-1521): nothing outside `quotes` calls `quotes::api::send_job_cards`, no code builds the `CardJob` and the business `Pool` it takes (both caller-filled, D-1013, D-1014), the `intake.cards_approved` event T208's approval step enqueues has no handler (D-1274, D-1277), nor has the `intake.emergency_released` event of INT-029, and no code asks the homeowner's ZIP or writes `intake.property` (only tests insert rows). Build the missing pieces. (1) The ZIP question (INT-049): `app/intake/src/zip_step.rs` asks the 5-digit ZIP of the home the job is at (new `zip_question` and `zip_again` templates in the shared English and Spanish template files, drafts for the copy panel, WORD-028), reads one 5-digit ZIP from the reply, maps it to its service-area town with `catalog::api::town_for_zip` (T248's market data, INT-048, including reviewed extra ZIPs with no centroid, which give no location), and stores the property with the ZIP and the town (`app/intake/src/models.rs`; `migrations/0165_intake_property_zip.sql` adds `intake.property.zip`, 5 digits, nullable for rows written before, PRIV-028); a ZIP in the Arizona centroid table but in no service-area town goes to `routing`'s out-of-area close (INT-022); any other reply (no ZIP, two ZIPs, or a well-formed ZIP Ask Hava does not know) asks once more (the twin's re-ask, `twin/app.py:521`, is the draft) and then waits for a usable reply without closing the request (INT-019's no-reply wait still applies). Its integration test asserts `intake.property` holds exactly the contact ID, the town and the ZIP, never an address (TD-PRIV-13's schema case). The ZIP goes into no AI call, card, log line or audit value. T162's intake order calls this step at step 4; this task adds the step and its tests, not the order. (2) The card pool (`app/quotes/src/card_pool.rs`, exported through the shared `app/quotes/src/api.rs`): build `Pool` for a job's towns and candidate trades from `businesses::api` (status, towns, ticked job types, the overflow switch), `licensing::api` (each business's classes and other licenses, and CARD-015's latest check for `verified`) and `ops::api::is_paused` (the owner pauses), never naming another module's tables. (3) The card sender (`app/intake/src/cards_out.rs`, its handlers registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`): on `intake.cards_approved` it sends only when T208's `approved_card` holds for the payload's digest, so the card the businesses get is the card she approved (TD-INT-23); on `intake.emergency_released` it sends at once (INT-029). It builds the `CardJob` from the request (the job's own town, the remaining candidate job types and the scrubbed text; neighbouring towns stay the caller's later choice, D-1014), calls `send_job_cards` in one transaction (idempotent: `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so a repeat or a retry records no second card), enqueues each new card's notice through T147's `app/quotes/src/card_notices.rs` (held whole by quiet hours, QH-005; each card's 24 hours start at the actual send, D-042, D-1021), and queues the homeowner's TXT-032 cards-out text through the homeowner-text steps T240 uses (`may_text`, `send_window` for quiet hours and opt-out, the outbox). Late cards (CARD-018, `send_late_cards`) keep their trigger with the tasks that make a business newly eligible. Tests: `app/intake/src/tests/zip_step.rs` (TD-INT-38) and `tests/integration/tests/cards_out.rs` (TD-CARD-14: approval and the end of the wait send once, a changed card is refused, a rerun sends nothing new, the emergency path, paused businesses skipped, night notices held, one TXT-032 text). Every existing test must still pass; if a done task's test breaks, stop and report. Update `app/intake/CLAUDE.md`, `app/quotes/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/architecture.md` section 10. The card pool holds only businesses for which `businesses::api::may_get_cards` is true at send time, beside `receives_cards` (D-1715, D-1760). On the emergency path (`intake.emergency_released`) each card's notice is queued through T150's `quotes::api::queue_emergency_card` (CARD-021: at once for the people with "Emergency emails at any hour" on at an after-hours business, at 8 a.m. for the rest, with the EMERGENCY subject and first line), not `queue_card_notice` (fourth external audit F42, D-2203). The card sender also writes the job's first facts row through T339's `jobs::api::record_job_facts`, in the transaction that sends the cards, on both the approval and the emergency path, so the fee at the pick can read it (seventh audit N7-01, D-2330): the category and exactly the candidate job types the cards went with. On the emergency path the category is always known at release (the trade check, step 5, comes before "How soon?" and the choice, INT-016, INT-030, and the choice is offered only with the category, `app/intake/src/service.rs:604`); the job type may not be, so the candidates are the ones intake still holds, which is every job type of the category when the trade questions have not narrowed it; later answers narrow the row (T162). Tested in TD-CARD-14. Nov 11 code review batch 3, F6 (a) (D-2708; CARD-001, BIZ-049, D-2507, D-2508 (1)): every pool this task builds goes through `quotes::api::capable_pool` before matching, so the largest-job and recurring-work answers skip a card: `cards_out.rs` fills `JobFit` from the job's stored size-band answer (`quotes::api::band_floor_cents`; none when unanswered) and whether any remaining candidate job type is recurring (`businesses::api::is_recurring`), and `card_pool.rs` reads the answers with `businesses::api::capabilities`. TD-CARD-14 gains, on the approval and the emergency path: a business whose largest job is below the band floor gets no card; one that said no to recurring work gets no card for a recurring job; a business with no capability row still does. Each business's licensing `Holder` (classes, agency licenses, pest attestation, handyman and city-list standing) comes from T374's `businesses::api` holding read (D-2712).
