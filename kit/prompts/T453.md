# T453: quotes: the job's quote rules are read from the stored job, catalog and card, so the served quote form can check a quote

## Goal

Plan change D-3675 (T301 plan gap; PICK-053, LIC-019, LIC-025): the quote form must check a quote against the job's rules, but no app code builds `QuoteRules` (`app/quotes/src/intake.rs:245`; it is made only in tests), so `parse_quote` (`:344`) has no production caller. A new `app/quotes/src/quote_rules.rs`, through `quotes::api`, adds `quote_rules(conn, job, business, certified) -> Result<Option<QuoteRules>, QuotesError>`: the job's type is the first candidate of its latest `jobs::api::job_facts` row and its category that row's category (as `app/quotes/src/quote_facts.rs:45` reads it); the category is `catalog::api::category(conn, code)`; `format` is `quotes::api::format_for(&category, job_type)` (PICK-053); `construction_type` is the category's; `license` is `LicenseKind::parse(&category.license_kind)` (as `app/quotes/src/card_pool.rs:142` reads it); `handyman` is the `quotes.job_card` row's `handyman` for that job and business (the card it was sent, `app/quotes/src/models.rs:236`); `certified` is the caller's (the form's tick, LIC-025). `None` when the job has no facts, the category or job type is not in the catalog, or no card went to that business; never a guess. Tests: `tests/integration/tests/quote_rules.rs` against PostgreSQL with fake data: a firm job, a range job type inside a category of another format, a handyman card on a construction-type category (a quote of $1,000 or more then bounces through `parse_quote`), a business with no card (`None`) and an unknown job type (`None`). Update the `quotes` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test quote_rules` passes against PostgreSQL with every listed case; every existing quotes test passes unchanged.
