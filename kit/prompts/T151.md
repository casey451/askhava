# T151: ops and messaging: HELP requests with one owner notification each and the plain-word auto-answers

Task T151 (build, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: help-requests.
Branch: `claude/task/T151-help-requests`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security, compliance).

## Goal

Each HELP request produces one owner notification by alert email within the hourly and daily limits, its body scrubbed of contact details, inbound help emails arrive only through the signed webhook into `privacy`, and plain-word questions after HELP get the fixed auto-answers (CON-034, OPS-036, PRIV-026, PRIV-030). The `ops.help_request` and `ops.help_message` tables of architecture section 3 are created by this task's migration in the `ops` schema and written only through `app/ops/src/help_requests.rs` and `ops::api`; the owner's reply box and its link are T200's (TD-OPS-23); the HELP lines with their due dates are added to the digest's `digest_flags.rs` (T204's, extended here) (D-491).
