# T039: intake: emergency stems on every message, safety text, urgent or regular

Task T039 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: intake-emergency.
Branch: `claude/task/T039-intake-emergency`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance).

## Goal

The emergency keyword list runs by word stem on every inbound message before anything else; a 911-list match gets the INT-004 text within 5 minutes and no card until the homeowner replies safe; then she chooses emergency or regular (with the night count of emergency businesses), the urgency question is always asked, and an emergency job is released for cards (an event `quotes` handles) before its remaining questions (INT-002 to INT-006, INT-024 to INT-026, INT-029, INT-030, INT-033, INT-037). The night count (INT-033) is computed in `emergency_count.rs` from `businesses::api::night_count(trade, town)`, which T041 provides with the after-hours tick only; T150 later narrows that function with the per-person switch and the test-email Yes, without touching intake (D-491). The card timing and the no-quote-yet texts are T045's (TD-INT-33), the cards-out line T047's and the call-back T048's; the after-hours cards are T150's and an early acceptance T069's (TD-INT-31), the masked line T077's (TD-CALL-12) (D-491).
