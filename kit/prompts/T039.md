# T039: intake: emergency stems on every message, safety text, urgent or regular

Task T039 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: intake-emergency.
Branch: `claude/task/T039-intake-emergency`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance).

## Goal

The emergency keyword list runs by word stem on every inbound message before anything else; a 911-list match gets the INT-004 text within 5 minutes and no card until the homeowner replies safe; then she chooses emergency or regular (with the night count of emergency businesses), the urgency question is always asked, and an emergency job's cards go out before its remaining questions (INT-002 to INT-006, INT-024 to INT-026, INT-029, INT-030, INT-033, INT-037).
