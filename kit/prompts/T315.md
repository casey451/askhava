# T315: jobs and portals: feedback words are blanked by the 3-year purge and by "Delete my data", and the data download masks bare domains

## Goal

Fourth external audit F31 and F34 (D-2180; PRIV-009, PRIV-011, PRIV-031, JOB-017; D-1821 (4)): the homeowner's feedback words in `jobs.feedback` (her own unscrubbed text, up to 2,000 characters, shown on askhava.com when ticked) outlive both "Delete my data" and the 3-year text purge, because the app role has no UPDATE or DELETE on the body (`migrations/0139_jobs_feedback.sql:31`) and neither `privacy` nor `intake` may name a `jobs` table; and the "Download my data" masking (`app/portals/src/data_download.rs:313`) misses a bare domain ("acmeplumbing.example") and a phone number split across lines. (1) `migrations/0175_jobs_feedback_purge.sql` adds `body_purged_at` to `jobs.feedback`, lets the app role clear the body once (a column-scoped `UPDATE (body, body_purged_at)` grant; the CHECK allows an empty body only with `body_purged_at` set) and adds a trigger that refuses any other change to the body, so a purged body never comes back. (2) `app/jobs/src/feedback_purge.rs`, through `jobs::api`: `purge_feedback(job)` blanks the body, sets `body_purged_at` and writes one `ops` audit row (IDs only), once; `feedback::shown` never returns purged feedback, so it leaves askhava.com at once. It runs on the text stage in the purge composition (the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`, in the handler that runs `privacy`'s decided text stage, so complaint and dispute holds apply to it as to the job text) and in the PRIV-011 path: `data_download.rs`'s confirmed delete calls it for her own jobs in the transaction of `privacy::api::delete_my_data`. (3) `data_download.rs` also masks a bare domain (two or more dot-separated labels ending in 2 to 24 letters, never a decimal number such as "3.5") and a run of 7 or more digits broken only by line breaks and the separators it already allows. Tests: `tests/integration/tests/feedback_purge.rs` (TD-PRIV-22) and `app/portals/src/tests/data_download_mask.rs` (TD-PRIV-23); every existing T087, T230 and purge test passes unchanged. The overseer runs an independent privacy review of the hand-in (D-2180). Update the `jobs` and `portals` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `docs/decisions.md`.
