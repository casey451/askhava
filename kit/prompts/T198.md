# T198: messaging and portals: daily open-cards email, missed-card account email and the per-job notice log

Task T198 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: messaging-open-cards-notice-log.
Branch: `claude/task/T198-messaging-open-cards-notice-log`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security, compliance).

## Goal

A business with open cards gets the daily open-cards email at its chosen times, a business that misses cards gets the account email, and the portal shows a per-job notice log of send times, provider statuses and first page opens with no homeowner detail (BIZ-044, BIZ-045, CARD-022, OPS-037). The open-cards and missed-card lines are composed in `app/picks/src/open_cards_email.rs`, a `picks` handler that reads `quotes::api`, `picks::api` and the per-person times through `businesses::api`, and enqueues the finished email for `messaging` to send, since `messaging` sits below `quotes` and `picks` (D-491). This task adds `messaging::api`'s notice-log record call; the quote form records its first opening in T199 (`business_actions.rs`) and the Confirm page in T214 (`confirm_pay.rs`), and the health-page share is T106's (D-491).
