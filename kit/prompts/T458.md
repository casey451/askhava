## Goal

Plan change D-3675 (T301 plan gap; CALL-011, CALL-012, BIZ-033, BIZ-038; D-078, D-311, D-3567): the "Link this phone" link's object is the blocked call's pending link (`calls.link_request`, T390), which holds only the caller's keyed digests, never the number; only the main phone's "LINK <PIN>" text can link it today (`calls::api::link_reply`, `app/calls/src/link_reply.rs:62`). (1) `businesses::api::link_by_reply` (`app/businesses/src/reply_link.rs:49`) always inserts the phone as linked and approved by the owner, which is right for the main phone's reply but not for a web action a "Confirm and billing" member may take (CALL-012: a phone a member other than the owner links works only after the owner approves it, BIZ-033). So `app/businesses/src/reply_link.rs` adds `link_pending_phone(conn, by: Acting, now)`: for the owner, as `link_by_reply`; for a "Confirm and billing" member, `linked_by` and `number_set_by` that member, `approved_at` NULL, and the owner's `PHONE_LINKED_KIND` email queued as `businesses::api::link_phone` queues it (`complaints::phone_link_notice`); anyone else is refused. `app/calls/src/link_reply.rs` adds `calls::api::link_by_action(conn, by: Acting, request, now)`: for a live pending link (`now < expires_at`, unused) of `by`'s business it runs, in a savepoint, `link_pending_phone`, `messaging::api::sender_put_pending` with the pending digests, then, for a member's link, `messaging::api::sender_hold` on the new line (so the phone stays out of the sender directory until T396's `approve_phone` releases it), marks the pending link used and deletes its digests; it answers linked (approved or waiting for the owner) or refused with no change (expired, used, another business's, a role that may not link); no number or PIN is read, stored or logged. (2) `app/server/src/action_links.rs`: the Link this phone link's `POST`, with a business session `portals::api::session_may` allows, acts once through `use_action_link` and `link_by_action` with the session's `Acting`; a member's link says the owner must approve the phone before it works; a refusal leaves the link unused and says the phone could not be linked. Tests: `tests/integration/tests/link_by_action.rs` and cases in `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs` (TD-SEC-44). Not in this task, logged in D-3675: no production code mints action links into the CALL-011 email (`portals::api::mint_action_link` has no production caller). Update the `calls` and `businesses` `CLAUDE.md` files, `app/server/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-3877 (Fable's pre-claim check of stage-5 wave 0, Nov 15, 2026): T477's `quotes::api::record_note_returned` is called where the served quote form returns a note: `check_note`'s refusal (`app/server/src/action_links.rs:686` to `:693`) and `parse_quote`'s bounce (`:709` to `:712`), with one case in `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs` posting a note with a phone number through the served `POST` and finding one `quotes.note_returned` audit row with no note text. Budget: 600 lines. `record_note_returned` is called only when the returned `Problems` has a note problem, so a price-only bounce writes no row (`bounced`, `app/server/src/action_links.rs:731` to `:736`). Plan change D-3889 (Fable's pre-claim check, Nov 16, 2026): the owner's `businesses.phone_linked` email this task queues cannot be delivered for a phone with no stored digits (`linked()` fails without the last 4 digits, `app/businesses/src/notice_emails.rs:479` to `:484`, and production `line_last4` answers `None`, `app/server/src/lib.rs:1470` to `:1476`); the owner approves on T397's page; log it in `docs/for-casey-review.md`. `app/server/CLAUDE.md` is at its 8,192-byte cap, so server notes go in `app/server/NOTES.md`.
