## Goal

Radius matching (CARD-024, D-1519): `quotes`' matching is pure and caller-filled (D-1013, D-1014). Add the job's location (`CardJob`: its ZIP centroid from `catalog::api::zip_location`, or none) and each business's reach (`CardBusiness`: its base ZIP centroid and radius from T248's `businesses.service_area`, or none) in `app/quotes/src/models.rs`, and change `rules::card_for`'s town test (`app/quotes/src/rules.rs`) to: serves an offered Arizona town (CARD-002, INT-022), or the job has a location, the business has a reach and `catalog::api::miles_between(base, job)` is at most the radius (inclusive). Every other CARD-001 test (active, verified, licensed for every candidate job type as the license map says for the job's place, a ticked job type, the BIZ-049 skips), "jobs nobody else covers" (CARD-004) and the owner pauses are unchanged, so a business serving the job's town keeps the card and a radius only adds reach; a business whose base ZIP has no location matches by its towns served alone, and only Arizona towns match (INT-022). `coverage` (the count intake tells the homeowner, INT-017, INT-032, D-1022) uses the same test, so the count and the recipients agree, and `send_job_cards` and `send_late_cards` (`app/quotes/src/service.rs`) carry the new fields. T251's card sender (`app/intake/src/cards_out.rs`) and card pool (`app/quotes/src/card_pool.rs`) build `CardJob` and `Pool`; this task fills the job's location there from the ZIP T251 stores on `intake.property` and each business's reach from `businesses.service_area` (D-1521); a job with no stored ZIP matches by towns only. The portal edit (BIZ-010, BIZ-058): the business portal's settings (`app/portals/src/business_actions.rs`, `app/portals/templates/business_actions.html`) let the owner change the base ZIP and radius with sign-up's checks and accessible errors, audited by field name only (draft wording through the copy panel, WORD-028). Tests: `app/quotes/src/tests/radius_matching.rs` (TD-CARD-13's pure cases with fixture centroids, including a Phoenix metro fixture of several towns: a card crossing a town line and one not, overlapping radii, the inclusive boundary, a business in reach without the license for a candidate job type, no location meaning towns only, the coverage count equal to the recipients) and `tests/integration/tests/radius_matching.rs` (`send_job_cards` sending to a business in reach in another town; TD-BIZ-38's portal edit). No geocoding service and no new crate. Update `app/quotes/CLAUDE.md`, `app/portals/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/architecture.md` section 10. T301 serves the email action links (D-2128): keep the signatures `app/server/src/action_links.rs` calls; any page or action this task adds is served by T306, not by a `.route` in `server`.
