## Goal

Radius matching (CARD-024, D-1519): `quotes`' matching is pure and caller-filled (D-1013, D-1014). Add the job's location (its ZIP centroid from `catalog::api::zip_location`, or none) and each business's reach (its base ZIP centroid and radius from T248's `businesses.service_area`, or none) as one new `Reach` value in `app/quotes/src/models.rs`, and give matching an in-reach town test (`app/quotes/src/rules.rs`): D-3118: `CardJob` and `CardBusiness` (`models.rs:71`, `:82`, no `Default`) are built by struct literal in done files this task does not own (`app/server/src/bin/rules_bridge.rs:172`, `:189`; `app/server/tests/release_recovery.rs:208`, `:220`; `app/server/tests/door_matrix.rs:619`, `:643`; `app/quotes/src/tests/prop_matching_props.rs`, `overflow.rs`, `matching_examples.rs`; `tests/integration/tests/capability_setup.rs`, `routing_confidence.rs`, `accepted_quote_email.rs`, `card_late_eligible.rs`, `check_in_texts.rs`), and `rules::coverage`, `coverage` and the send functions are called with today's arguments in `app/quotes/src/tests/overflow.rs:216`, `:265` and `tests/integration/tests/card_late_eligible.rs:134` to `:373`, so neither struct gains a field and no existing signature changes: `Reach` goes to parallel functions (`card_for`, `coverage`, `send_job_cards` and `send_late_cards` each gain an in-reach twin), the existing ones meaning no reach (towns only) by calling their twin with an empty `Reach`. The in-reach test is: serves an offered Arizona town (CARD-002, INT-022), or the job has a location, the business has a reach and `catalog::api::miles_between(base, job)` is at most the radius (inclusive). Every other CARD-001 test (active, verified, licensed for every candidate job type as the license map says for the job's place, a ticked job type, the BIZ-049 skips), "jobs nobody else covers" (CARD-004) and the owner pauses are unchanged, so a business serving the job's town keeps the card and a radius only adds reach; a business whose base ZIP has no location matches by its towns served alone, and only Arizona towns match (INT-022). `coverage` (the count intake tells the homeowner, INT-017, INT-032, D-1022) uses the same test, so the count and the recipients agree, and the in-reach twins of `send_job_cards` and `send_late_cards` (`app/quotes/src/service.rs`) carry the `Reach`. T251's card sender (`app/intake/src/cards_out.rs`) and card pool (`app/quotes/src/card_pool.rs`) build `CardJob` and `Pool`; this task builds the `Reach` there and calls the in-reach twins, filling the job's location from the ZIP T251 stores on `intake.property` and each business's reach from `businesses.service_area` (D-1521); a job with no stored ZIP matches by towns only. The portal edit (BIZ-010, BIZ-058): the business portal's settings (`app/portals/src/business_actions.rs`, `app/portals/templates/business_actions.html`) let the owner change the base ZIP and radius with sign-up's checks and accessible errors, audited by field name only (draft wording through the copy panel, WORD-028).
