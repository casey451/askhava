## Goal

Plan change D-4361 (unwired sweep orphan 2 and its second pass, `main` 77a49d7cc; FEE-023, FEE-024, FEE-026; D-048, D-141). `payments::api::decide_cancel` (`app/payments/src/refund_rules.rs:236`) has no production caller: `cancel` (`app/intake/src/closing.rs:145` to `:155`) only moves the job to Cancelled, so once T663 makes Payment pending and Connected reachable, a business that paid and whose homeowner cancels before any call answered by a person is never refunded. FEE-025 (a charge after the cancel) is not this task's: a success that lands after the job moved on is `Settled::Left` and refunded by `payments::late_success(PickNow::MovedOn)` (`app/server/src/release_sink.rs:118` to `:139`), so `RefundReason::ChargedAfterCancel` stays a rule-level case. (1) `cancel`, when the job moves to Cancelled from Payment pending or Connected, queues one `picks.cancel_refund` task `{job, cancelled_at}` in the same transaction through a new `picks::api::queue_cancel_refund` (dedupe per job; `intake` may call `picks`, `kit/layers.json`); from any other status nothing is queued. (2) The new `app/picks/src/cancel_refund.rs`: `run_cancel_refund` finds the job's pick that is `paid` (a `free` pick has nothing to refund) or still waiting with a charge that succeeded, gathers `CancelFacts` (the charge's success time through a new `payments::api` read, `charged_at(db, pick)`, in the new `app/payments/src/charge_time.rs`; the pick's call log both directions through `calls::api` as `unreachable_refund.rs` reads it; `refunded_before` from `refund_state`) and calls `decide_cancel`; a `Refund` goes through `payments::api::request_refund` with `RefundTrigger::Rule(reason)`, whose per-pick claim makes it exactly once even when the late-success path also refunds the same pick (FEE-026); `NoRefund` records nothing new. The business is told by the refund's existing receipt and notice path; no homeowner contact appears in any payload (PRIV-005). The handler is registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` with the Stripe adapter and ID secret, as `UNREACHABLE_STEP_KIND` is. (3) `app/picks/src/cancel_refund.rs` is held at 95% branch coverage, shown, not floored (D-4788: `tests/test_coverage_floor.py:183` to `:196` accepts only the per-file floors D-3974 names): the hand-in quotes its `cargo llvm-cov` branch figure; T761 adds its floor after this task; `tests/coverage_floor.json` is not edited (`app/payments/` keeps its 95% floor; `charge_time.rs` falls under it). (4) INT-038's under-13 stop (`cancel_job`, `app/intake/src/conversation.rs:361`) cancels the request's jobs the same way and queues the same task from Payment pending or Connected, the refund judged by the same rule with the stop's time as the cancel (the business is never kept paid for a request Laftly ended); the purge in that transaction removes no call-log or payment row the handler reads. A waiting pick on a job she cancels in Payment pending is still not ended by the cancel (D-4349's open item; a later success is refunded as `Left`). Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/cancel_refund.rs` (TD-FEE-71), PostgreSQL and the Stripe stand-in, fake data only. Update `app/picks/NOTES.md`, `app/intake/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-FEE-71 passes against PostgreSQL, its refund cases failing on `main`; `cancel_refund.rs` is at or above 95% branch coverage, its `cargo llvm-cov` figure quoted in the hand-in and `app/payments/` holds its floor; every closing, refund and late-success test passes unchanged.
