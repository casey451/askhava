## Goal

Sign-up shows the job types the license map gives the business's class, pre-ticked, with untick, no-license extras, towns, largest job, recurring and after-hours, as checkboxes or a guided one-question flow (BIZ-049, BIZ-004, CARD-001); the matching then skips a business by its largest-job and recurring-work answers, the CARD-001 skips T044 left to this task (TD-BIZ-29, D-492).
