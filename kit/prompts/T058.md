## What this task rests on

Requirements (each sentence as it stands in `docs/spec/`):

- **FEE-001** The introduction fee is $25 when the fee basis is under $500.00, $75 when it is $500.00 up to and including $1,500.00, and $199 when it is over $1,500.00 (D-009). (`docs/spec/fees.md:5`)
- **FEE-002** No fee is ever a percentage of the job, and there is no percentage cap (D-357). (`docs/spec/fees.md:6`)
- **FEE-003** The fee basis of a range quote is its midpoint. (`docs/spec/fees.md:7`)
- **FEE-004** A diagnostic-visit quote (a visit fee plus an estimate on site) exists only in a category whose quote format is `diagnostic_visit` (PICK-053) and uses the set fee tier stored for its category, which is $25 by default and can never be set above $75 (D-399, D-418, D-441). (`docs/spec/fees.md:8`)
- **FEE-008** Fees are tax-included: no tax is ever added on top of $25, $75 or $199. (`docs/spec/fees.md:12`)
- **FEE-034** Fee calculation is unit-tested from the K2b worked examples. (`docs/spec/fees.md:38`)
- **FEE-043** An hourly quote must state an estimated number of hours or a range of hours on the quote form and is then treated as a price range from the rate times the low estimate to the rate times the high estimate, its fee basis being that range's midpoint (FEE-003) and the handyman limit (LIC-019) judged on its top, while the homeowner sees the rate and the estimate as the business wrote them; an hourly quote with no estimate is treated as a diagnostic-visit quote in a `diagnostic_visit` category and is returned to the business for an estimate in any other (D-399, D-441). (`docs/spec/fees.md:47`)
- **FEE-044** A one-time setup charge in a recurring quote is shown as the business wrote it and is never added to the fee basis, which stays 4 months of the recurring price (REC-002), capped at $75 (FEE-039) (D-399). (`docs/spec/fees.md:48`)
- **FEE-045** A pick of a fee-free job type (the catalog's `fee_free_job_types`, at launch the sale, purchase and refinance home inspections and termite reports of LIC-015) charges no introduction fee: the Confirm page and the card say "No introduction fee for this job.", the ledger records a $0 entry of kind "fee-free job type", no free first win is used, and the catalog check fails if a sale-related inspection is ever charged (D-403). (`docs/spec/fees.md:49`)
