- **FEE-043** An hourly quote must state an estimated number of hours or a range of hours on the quote form and is then treated as a price range from the rate times the low estimate to the rate times the high estimate, its fee basis being that range's midpoint (FEE-003) and the handyman limit (LIC-019) judged on its top, while the homeowner sees the rate and the estimate as the business wrote them; an hourly quote with no estimate is treated as a diagnostic-visit quote in a `diagnostic_visit` category and is returned to the business for an estimate in any other (D-399, D-441). - **FEE-045** A pick of a fee-free job type (the catalog's `fee_free_job_types`, at launch the sale, purchase and refinance home inspections of LIC-015 and pest control's termite report for a sale or loan, D-5216) charges no introduction fee: the Confirm page and the card say "No introduction fee for this job.", the ledger records a $0 entry of kind "fee-free job type", no free first win is used, and the catalog check fails if a sale-related inspection is ever charged (D-403).
