## Goal

At the pick the homeowner may choose a backup quote, offered automatically if the first business lapses or can't take it, and a lapse with no backup sends her the other quotes (PICK-046, PICK-047); the backup selector sits on the quote page's pick review step (`pick_review.html`, `pick_flow.rs`), which this task extends, and the lapse in `confirm_reminders.rs` (T197's, extended here) moves the pick to the backup at once (D-491). The PICK-020 ineligibility re-check at Confirm moves the pick to the backup in `confirm.rs` (T063's, extended here); the other triggers call `picks::api::backup_choice`: 'Can't take it' (T214), leaving Ask Hava (T228), and the payments paths (a fee limit FEE-041 and Stripe unreachable FEE-018, T062; a card not fixed in time FEE-020, T062, D-493), each through `picks`' handler on the payments event (D-492).
