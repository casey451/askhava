## Goal

Plan change D-4748 (Fable's decision (7), its interim part; OPS-036, WORD-029). The help-page form (`app/site/templates/help.html:28` to `:39`, handled by `app/portals/src/help.rs`) has an optional email field but the handler discards it and opens its request with no asker ID (`receive_help_email(db, &site_email(message), now)`, `:384`), so the owner's reply cannot reach its sender until T742 and T740 add the required email field; yet its confirmation (`help_form_page`, `:433` to `:439`) says "Replies usually come within 2 days, or within 24 hours if your request is urgent." This task makes the confirmation promise no reply: the line becomes "The <brand> owner has your message. In an emergency, call 911." (the brand from the brand file as today; the copy panel and the cold read may tighten the words, never to say or imply that a reply will come), on every screen that shows it (`Sent` and `OptedOut`); and the form's optional email field label (`app/site/templates/help.html:33`, "Your email for our reply (optional)"), which the handler discards today (`site_email` keeps no sender), becomes "Your email (optional)" so it promises no reply either; nothing else changes. A unit test in the new `app/portals/src/tests/help_form_reply.rs` (registered in the shared `app/portals/src/tests/mod.rs`) renders each screen and asserts that no line says "Replies", "reply within", "within 2 days" or "within 24 hours", and that the 911 sentence stays. `check_copy.py` and `check_ai_smell.py` pass. Update `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `app/portals/src/tests/help_form_reply.rs` passes; no help-form screen promises a reply; every portals help test passes unchanged.
