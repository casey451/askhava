## Goal

Sixth audit U-02 (D-2302; FEE-012, FEE-013, FEE-045, PICK-022; sets aside only D-1478's premise): `no_fee_release` (`app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:225`) checks only that the pick has no PaymentIntent and that some $0 ledger entry exists for the business and job its caller passes, never that the pick belongs to that pair, and `picks::settle` (`app/picks/src/settle.rs:66`) does not check either; so a $0 waiver recorded for one job of a business could clear a different pick of that business for release with no fee (a fee bypass; the pick still goes to its own business). D-1478's premise, that `payments` holds no record tying a $0 pick to its business and job, is out of date: since T287 `payments.pick_fee` is keyed by pick and carries its business and job (`fix_fee_at_pick`, `app/payments/src/pick_fee.rs:100`). Now `no_fee_release` reads the pick's `payments.pick_fee` row, refuses with `NoFeeDecision` when there is none or when the caller's (business, job) differs from it, and checks the $0 ledger entry against the row's business and job; the signature is unchanged, so its callers (T254's release sink, T255's re-clear) keep compiling, and both inherit the binding. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/no_fee_release_binding.rs` (TD-FEE-57): a $0 waiver for pick A's job does not clear pick B of the same business on another job, with either pair passed; pick B with its own waiver is cleared; a pick with no `pick_fee` row is refused. D-2832 (attempt 1 was red only on two done tests that clear a pick with no stored fee row, or with another pair): T337 also owns `tests/integration/tests/confirm_and_pay.rs` (T189's and T287's done test) for a fixture-only change, and so is a harness task, since only a harness task takes over an earlier task's test (`scripts/validate_tasks.py:597`). Before its `no_fee_release` calls, `td_fee_25_a_paid_pick_is_never_cleared_as_no_fee` stores through `fix_fee_at_pick` a fee-free $0 fee (`fee_at_pick` with `fee_free: true`, tiers mode, as `fixed` builds its $75 one) for `free_pick` (`0x189_0043`) on `BUSINESS` and its job `other` (`0x189_0243`); `td_fee_25_a_recorded_no_fee_decision_clears` stores the same for `PICK` on `BUSINESS` and job `0x189_0242` right after it takes its connection. No existing assertion, call, argument or expected value in that file changes, and no test is removed or weakened; only those fixture lines are added, and the first test's doc comment says that its PaymentIntent case now lives in `no_fee_release_binding.rs` (D-2832). Because that test's `(BUSINESS, JOB)` calls are now refused on the pair before the ledger is read, `no_fee_release_binding.rs` also shows that a pick whose own stored pair has only a charge entry, no $0 one, is refused; and, because the first test's paid pick is now refused on the pair before the PaymentIntent check (`app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:231`) is reached, that a pick with its own stored row, a $0 entry for that same pair and a PaymentIntent is refused, so the PaymentIntent guard keeps a test. D-2896 (attempt 4 stopped, reviewer confirmed, on a third done test that the binding breaks): T337 also owns `app/server/tests/release_recovery.rs` (T254's and T287's done test; T343 and T352, both QUEUED, also own it and both run after T337) for a fixture-only change to `td_pick_50_a_zero_fee_releases_free_and_a_cancelled_pick_nothing`. `quotes::api::page_quotes` numbers a job's quotes by `received_at`, then `business_id`, and the file's fixed clock gives both quotes on `replaced_job` the same `received_at`, so `gone`'s stored `payments.pick_fee` row names the lower of the two random business IDs, while the test assumes it is `first` and passes `(first, replaced_job)`; with the binding, `cleared_free` (`release_recovery.rs:344`) then panics in about half the runs. The fix (the only edits allowed in this file): the lines that create the three businesses and the two owners change so the two quoting businesses are created under other names; the `quoted_job` line for `replaced_job` changes only its two business names (same order); after `gone` is page-picked the test reads `gone`'s row through `payments::api::pick_fee` (`pick_fee` is added to the file's `askhava_payments::api` import, or called by its full path) and binds `first` to that row's `.business.0` (`other` to the remaining one), creating `first_person` as `first`'s owner only then, so `zero_fee`, `cleared_free` and `seen` name the pick's own stored pair; it may add one assertion that the row's `job` equals `replaced_job` (extra checking only). Nothing else in the file changes and no test is removed or weakened: no existing assertion or expected value changes, and every existing assertion and every `zero_fee`, `cleared_free`, `settle_and_release`, `seen`, `had_line` and `count` line stays byte-identical; every other test in the file quotes with one business, so it already passes the pick's own pair.
