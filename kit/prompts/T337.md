## Goal

Sixth audit U-02 (D-2302; FEE-012, FEE-013, FEE-045, PICK-022; sets aside only D-1478's premise): `no_fee_release` (`app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:225`) checks only that the pick has no PaymentIntent and that some $0 ledger entry exists for the business and job its caller passes, never that the pick belongs to that pair, and `picks::settle` (`app/picks/src/settle.rs:66`) does not check either; so a $0 waiver recorded for one job of a business could clear a different pick of that business for release with no fee (a fee bypass; the pick still goes to its own business). D-1478's premise, that `payments` holds no record tying a $0 pick to its business and job, is out of date: since T287 `payments.pick_fee` is keyed by pick and carries its business and job (`fix_fee_at_pick`, `app/payments/src/pick_fee.rs:100`). Now `no_fee_release` reads the pick's `payments.pick_fee` row, refuses with `NoFeeDecision` when there is none or when the caller's (business, job) differs from it, and checks the $0 ledger entry against the row's business and job; the signature is unchanged, so its callers (T254's release sink, T255's re-clear) keep compiling, and both inherit the binding. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/no_fee_release_binding.rs` (TD-FEE-57): a $0 waiver for pick A's job does not clear pick B of the same business on another job, with either pair passed; pick B with its own waiver is cleared; a pick with no `pick_fee` row is refused. D-2832 (attempt 1 was red only on two done tests that clear a pick with no stored fee row, or with another pair): T337 also owns `tests/integration/tests/confirm_and_pay.rs` (T189's and T287's done test) for a fixture-only change, and so is a harness task, since only a harness task takes over an earlier task's test (`scripts/validate_tasks.py:597`). Before its `no_fee_release` calls, `td_fee_25_a_paid_pick_is_never_cleared_as_no_fee` stores through `fix_fee_at_pick` a fee-free $0 fee (`fee_at_pick` with `fee_free: true`, tiers mode, as `fixed` builds its $75 one) for `free_pick` (`0x189_0043`) on `BUSINESS` and its job `other` (`0x189_0243`); `td_fee_25_a_recorded_no_fee_decision_clears` stores the same for `PICK` on `BUSINESS` and job `0x189_0242` right after it takes its connection. No existing assertion, call, argument or expected value in that file changes, and no test is removed or weakened; only those fixture lines are added, and the first test's doc comment says that its PaymentIntent case now lives in `no_fee_release_binding.rs` (D-2832). Because that test's `(BUSINESS, JOB)` calls are now refused on the pair before the ledger is read, `no_fee_release_binding.rs` also shows that a pick whose own stored pair has only a charge entry, no $0 one, is refused; and, because the first test's paid pick is now refused on the pair before the PaymentIntent check (`app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:231`) is reached, that a pick with its own stored row, a $0 entry for that same pair and a PaymentIntent is refused, so the PaymentIntent guard keeps a test. Money: 95% branch floor; the overseer runs an independent money review of the hand-in. Update the `payments` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
