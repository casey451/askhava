## Goal

Plan change D-4765 (Fable's privacy audit of `main` 4ed3a4ff, finding P2; PRIV-011, PRIV-006, PRIV-030). "Delete my data" (`portals::delete_her_data`, `app/portals/src/data_download.rs:349`, which calls `privacy::api::delete_my_data(db, contact, &jobs)` with every job of hers) purges her contact and photos (`delete_my_data`, `app/privacy/src/purge.rs`) but leaves her kept words in `privacy.message_body` (`migrations/0171_privacy_message_body.sql`) for up to the 30-day cut: problem and help words are kept under a job ID or, for a help request with no job, under the help request's own ID (`open_help`, `app/intake/src/handlers.rs`). This task: (1) `delete_my_data`, in its one transaction, also deletes the `privacy.message_body` rows of each job it is given (a new delete by job in `app/privacy/src/message_body.rs`, beside `run_words_purge`'s) and of each help request she asked (`Asker::Homeowner(contact)` in `app/intake/src/handlers.rs`), whose IDs it reads through a new `ops::api::help_requests_of_homeowner(conn, contact)` in the new `app/ops/src/help_request_keys.rs` (re-exported by one line in the shared `ops::api`; `privacy` may call `ops`, `kit/layers.json`); its signature stays, so `data_download.rs` and every caller are unchanged and no `portals` file is needed. Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/delete_message_bodies.rs`, PostgreSQL, fake data only: after "Delete my data", no `privacy.message_body` row of her jobs or her help requests remains, another homeowner's rows remain, and the confirmation sentence is unchanged; every purge and data-download test passes unchanged. Privacy: Opus worker (Fable once the runner's `models` map names it, D-4761), Opus privacy and security review. Update the `privacy` and `ops` notes and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-5387 (outside review job 13 pre-flight): this task adds a second reader of `ops.help_request`, so the shared `app/ops/NOTES.md` says so; the comment at `app/ops/src/help_requests.rs:4` ("the only writer and reader") is not this task's file (T742 owns it), so T126 corrects it. Plan change D-5399 (privacy review job 14, finding P-orphan, verified in the tree; PRIV-011, PRIV-006): the `ORPHAN_SWEEP_KIND` handler (`app/privacy/src/purge.rs:342` to `:350`) runs `sweep_orphans` and only then `schedule_orphan_sweep`, so a sweep that fails all 5 of its attempts (`app/core/src/tasks.rs:45` to `:47`, `:166`) never queues the next one and the daily chain dies for good.
