The portal saves each member's open-cards times (`save_open_cards_times`, `app/server/src/business_portal.rs:407`) and `picks` builds the email (`open_cards_email` and `chosen_minutes`, `app/picks/src/open_cards_email.rs:179`, `:59`; `messaging::api::digest_goes`, `app/messaging/src/open_cards_digest.rs:57`), but no task kind, schedule or sender exists, so nobody ever gets it. Each email is stored with `messaging::api::queue_email` as `EmailClass::Bulk` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:140` to `:146`, `:390`), never through the business-notice path, which stores every email as non-bulk (`app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs:409`), so above 90% of the day's email limit it waits for the reset (OPS-038).
