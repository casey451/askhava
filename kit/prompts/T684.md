## Goal

Plan change D-4361 (unwired sweep orphan 7 and its second pass, `main` 77a49d7cc; CARD-022, BIZ-037, BIZ-038; D-1993). The portal saves each member's open-cards times (`save_open_cards_times`, `app/server/src/business_portal.rs:407`) and `picks` builds the email (`open_cards_email` and `chosen_minutes`, `app/picks/src/open_cards_email.rs:179`, `:59`; `messaging::api::digest_goes`, `app/messaging/src/open_cards_digest.rs:57`), but no task kind, schedule or sender exists, so nobody ever gets it. The new `app/picks/src/open_cards_run.rs`: an hourly `picks.open_cards_email` run (registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`, scheduled in the shared `app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`, the worker clock's now) that, for each member who gets job cards (BIZ-037) whose chosen slot (8 a.m., noon or 5 p.m. Arizona time, 5 p.m. by default) is due, lists the cards the business has not quoted with at least 1 hour (a setting) of the window left, sends only when `digest_goes` says so, once per member and slot (dedupe key), with the pick-waiting subject and the pick first while a pick waits for that member's business, as one email; each card shows only trade, town, end time and its Quote link (BIZ-038, no homeowner detail). `queue_email` renders only the closed `messaging::Notice` enum (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:151`), so this task adds a `Notice::OpenCards` variant there with its English and Spanish words (after T470, the queued owners of `email.rs`). Each email is stored with `messaging::api::queue_email` as `EmailClass::Bulk` (`app/messaging/src/email.rs:140` to `:146`, `:390`), never through the business-notice path, which stores every email as non-bulk (`app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs:409`), so above 90% of the day's email limit it waits for the reset (OPS-038). Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/open_cards_email_run.rs` (TD-CARD-18), PostgreSQL, fake data only. Update `app/picks/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-4822 (Opus scout): `app/server/src/lib.rs` `worker_registry` (`:1054`) and the start-up scheduling are at or near clippy's `too_many_lines` limit (about 50 of 50 and 46 of 50 lines), so fold this task's new seed into the existing `schedule_checks` helper rather than adding lines to either function. Plan change D-5017: `picks` may not query `quotes.*` and `card_sent_at` is crate-private, so this task adds `app/quotes/src/open_cards.rs` with `open_cards(db, business, now)`, exported by one `mod` line in the shared `lib.rs` and one `pub use` in the shared `api.rs`: for each card the business holds and has not quoted, on a job still taking quotes, the job, trade, town and window end (send time plus `QUOTE_WINDOW`), no homeowner detail. The signed Quote and Confirm links come through a small link trait declared in `open_cards_run.rs` and implemented in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`, as `PortalNoticeParts` is. The recipient is the member's mailbox, as `business_notices.rs` addresses one. `Notice::OpenCards` carries its own English and Spanish words; with no stored member language the email goes in English, logged in `docs/for-casey-review.md`.

Acceptance: TD-CARD-18 passes against PostgreSQL; every open-cards and business portal test passes unchanged.
