This task adds the trigger and the read; T583 adds the sender. In the new `app/quotes/src/count_update.rs`: `COUNT_UPDATE_KIND` (`quotes.count_update`) and a queue call, run in the same transaction as a quote that becomes shown (in `submit_quote` when not AI-read, and in `confirm_quote_at` when applied) on a job that was already Quotes ready (the first quote's text stays `jobs::api::first_quote`'s), one `core.task` per newly shown quote (dedupe key: job and business), payload the job ID only.
