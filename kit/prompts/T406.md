## Goal

Plan change D-3134 (PICK-040, PICK-051; D-233, D-283, D-1049). Every quote card prints PICK-040's six facts (`fact_lines`, `app/quotes/src/quote_card.rs:426` to `:435`) from `CardSource.facts` (`:280` to `:290`, required), but only the earliest visit is stored (`quotes.quote.earliest_visit` and `visit_part`, written by `upsert_quote`, `app/quotes/src/models.rs:301`; `stored_card` reads format, original wording, earliest visit and edited time only, `:470` to `:487`); no table holds the trip fee, parts, permit, may-change or Spanish answers, and `portals::api::parse_facts` (`app/portals/src/business_actions.rs:601`) has no production caller, so T393 could not build a card and the form's "Same as last time" pre-fill has nothing to read. (1) `migrations/0168_quotes_quote_facts.sql`: `quotes.quote_facts`, one row per quote (job ID and business ID, primary key, referencing `quotes.quote`), the five answers as checked codes and a boolean, the job's type code and the time recorded; app role SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE of those columns only, nothing deleted (a quote edit replaces its facts, PICK-051). The number is the free 0168: this task depends on the done T272 (0167) and sits just after it in `kit/tasks.json`, so its number rises in the runner's serial order (V9). (2) `app/quotes/src/quote_facts.rs`, through `quotes::api`: `submit_quote_with_facts(db, (job, business), quote, facts, now)` opens one transaction, runs `submit_quote` inside it (its own `begin` becomes a savepoint) and, only when it answers `Saved`, upserts the facts with the job's type read through `jobs::api`, so a quote and its facts commit together and a refused quote stores neither; `stored_facts(conn, job, business)` gives the `QuoteFacts` of a quote with both its facts row and its earliest visit, otherwise `None`; `last_facts(conn, business, job_type)` gives the facts of the business's most recently recorded quote on a job of that type, for `portals::api::prefill` (which drops the visit). `submit_quote`, `QuoteForm`, `Quote` and `CardSource` keep their shapes, so no done test changes. T301 (the served quote form) and T393 (the served cards) depend on this task and call these. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/quote_facts_store.rs` (TD-PICK-63), fake data only. Update the `quotes` `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/architecture.md` (plan change D-3134 already names `quotes.quote_facts` in section 3) and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-PICK-63 passes against PostgreSQL; every existing quote, quote-card, quote-form and pick test passes unchanged; no done file changes.
