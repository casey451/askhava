## Goal

Plan gap 2 of T255 (D-3431): after T255, `intake::api::take_name_address` writes the homeowner's first name and address onto her own vault contact and `ask_name_address` is asked again by T255's `picks.address_needed` handler, but no production code routes a homeowner's answer to `take_name_address`, and the regular intake order never asks the INT-027 question (only tests call either; step 11 of the intake order, `docs/spec/intake.md:71`). Wire the live path. Production today calls neither `conversation::after_questions` (`app/intake/src/conversation.rs:80`) nor `zip_step::answer_zip` (`app/intake/src/zip_step.rs:149`), so the area check and steps 9 to 11 are not reached by a live text either; this task wires only step 11 and its answer, and says in its PR which earlier step it found unwired. (1) Ask: where the intake order reaches step 11 (for a regular or urgent job after the area check, as the last question; for a job chosen as an emergency right after the cards go out, INT-029), the ask goes in `app/intake/src/order.rs` (T162's, done) just before step 12, which starts at `show_built_card` (`app/intake/src/order.rs:268`): `ask_name_address` is called once per request, with the who-sees-it line (INT-027, D-238, D-437). (2) Route: `server`'s inbound-text route (`app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`) and `intake`'s dispatcher (`app/intake/src/replies.rs`, `receive_reply`) send a reply in a request whose open question is the name-and-address one to `take_name_address` with the raw text as a `SecretString` (never the scrubbed words, never the AI, never a log line or an error message, D-146) and screen it with `Asked::NameAndAddress`, so a street name never matches the 911 list (INT-002, D-165); `Taken::Again` asks again once; `Taken::Stored` goes on to the next step (`take_name_address` itself already queued T255's `address_arrived` event for a job with a waiting pick; this task never calls `address_arrived`, so the event is queued once); `Taken::Ended` sends nothing. How the open question is known: `ask_name_address` also stores one `intake.intake_answer` row with question `name_address_asked` (no words, the ask's time) in the transaction that queues the question; the open question is the name-and-address one when the request's latest `name_address_asked` row is newer than its latest `address_contact` answer; no new table, no read of `messaging` tables; named in the `intake` `CLAUDE.md`. An opt-out, HELP, CLOSE or LIST text keeps its own path first. Fake data only. End-to-end tests in `app/server/tests/name_address_answer.rs` (TD-INT-42): a regular job is asked the question once after the area check and its answer lands on the homeowner's own contact (the contact the release reads), no second contact row is made, the scrubbed words and every log line hold neither the name nor the address; an answer naming a street like a 911-list word opens no 911 text; an answer with no street number is asked again once; an emergency job whose release waited gets the address released once through T255's path. Update the `intake` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/architecture.md` section 8 (found by grep, never loaded whole). Uncertain at planning time (D-3431): whether `inbound_text.rs` already holds the raw body where `receive_reply` is called; if either needs a file this task does not own, report a plan gap rather than reach outside `owns`.
