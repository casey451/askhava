## Goal

Plan gap 2 of T255 (D-3431): after T255, `intake::api::take_name_address` writes the homeowner's first name and address onto her own vault contact and `ask_name_address` is asked again by T255's `picks.address_needed` handler, but no production code routes a homeowner's answer to `take_name_address`, and the regular intake order never asks the INT-027 question (only tests call either; step 11 of the intake order, `docs/spec/intake.md:71`). Wire the live path. (1) Ask: where the intake order reaches step 11 (for a regular or urgent job after the area check, as the last question; for a job chosen as an emergency right after the cards go out, INT-029), `conversation.rs` (T040's, extended by T162 and T255) calls `ask_name_address` once per request, with the who-sees-it line (INT-027, D-238, D-437). (2) Route: `server`'s inbound-text route (`app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`) and `intake`'s dispatcher (`app/intake/src/replies.rs`, `receive_reply`) send a reply in a request whose open question is the name-and-address one to `take_name_address` with the raw text as a `SecretString` (never the scrubbed words, never the AI, never a log line or an error message, D-146) and screen it with `Asked::NameAndAddress`, so a street name never matches the 911 list (INT-002, D-165); `Taken::Again` asks again once; `Taken::Stored` goes on to the next step and, for a job with a waiting pick, T255's `address_arrived`; `Taken::Ended` sends nothing. How the open question is known is the worker's to settle from `intake`'s own rows (the request's last asked question and whether an `address_contact` answer exists), named in the PR and the `intake` `CLAUDE.md`; it adds no table unless the worker reports a plan gap. An opt-out, HELP, CLOSE or LIST text keeps its own path first. Fake data only. End-to-end tests in `app/server/tests/name_address_answer.rs` (TD-INT-42): a regular job is asked the question once after the area check and its answer lands on the homeowner's own contact (the contact the release reads), no second contact row is made, the scrubbed words and every log line hold neither the name nor the address; an answer naming a street like a 911-list word opens no 911 text; an answer with no street number is asked again once; an emergency job whose release waited gets the address released once through T255's path. Update the `intake` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/architecture.md` section 8 (found by grep, never loaded whole). Uncertain at planning time (D-3431): which existing step function in `conversation.rs` or `order.rs` reaches step 11, and whether `inbound_text.rs` already holds the raw body where `receive_reply` is called; if either needs a file this task does not own, report a plan gap rather than reach outside `owns`.
