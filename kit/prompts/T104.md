# T104: Wording scan: brand, banned words and the not-a-contractor phrases in every template, page and document

Task T104 (build, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: wording-scan.
Branch: `claude/task/T104-wording-scan`

## Goal

A docs test scans every template, page, email, text and drafted document and fails on a wrong brand or entity name, "vetted", "best" or rankings, "commission", "lead", "concierge", judging refusals, any not-a-contractor phrase from `scripts/trace_spec.py`, uncited cost comparisons, and policy text that says numbers are shared or sold (TERMS-010, TERMS-012, WORD-001, WORD-003, WORD-004, WORD-010 to WORD-012, WORD-015, WORD-019, WORD-020, WORD-022).

Smoke test: start `bash scripts/smoke.sh >/tmp/s.log 2>&1; echo exit=$? >>/tmp/s.log` with the Bash tool's `run_in_background`, then poll `timeout 200 bash -c 'until grep -q ^exit= /tmp/s.log; do sleep 10; done'; tail -5 /tmp/s.log`, at most 4 minutes a call or the cache expires (D-1316). No `exit=` by about 15 minutes: stop it, report, do not hand in; stop it if you leave early. If `main` is red, stop and report: the only task then is "fix main".
