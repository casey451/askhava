## Goal

Casey's November 3 decisions (D-1515, D-1518): Ask Hava is national-ready, so market values are data, and each business types how far it will travel. Build the data layer; matching by radius and the portal edit are T249's. (1) Markets (INT-048): `docs/catalog/markets.yaml` (new, reviewed, owned here) with one market, `lake-havasu-city`: its name, state `AZ`, IANA time zone `America/Phoenix`, the identity source of its no-license categories (the city business-license list of LIC-011) and its towns (the town codes of the service-area setting, INT-022), each town with its ZIP codes taken from the US Census ZCTA-to-place relationship file (never typed from memory), plus reviewed extra ZIPs with no centroid (such as a PO-box-only ZIP), which give the town but no location, a ZIP under two towns being refused so the reviewer assigns it to one; the service-area towns INT-022 checks are read from this file (no separate towns setting exists), and a test asserts every market town passes `routing::check_towns`; `catalog::api::town_for_zip(zip)` gives the town or none (T251's ZIP step reads it, D-1521). `app/catalog/src/markets.rs` compiles it in and checks it as `loader.rs` checks `categories.yaml`, refusing a town in two markets, a duplicate ID, an unknown time zone, a market with no towns and a state other than AZ without a decision ID; the catalog sync writes `catalog.market` (ID, name, state, time zone, identity sources, `launched_at`, empty: nothing here sets it, a launch being a later owner action) and `catalog.market_town` from `migrations/0162_catalog_markets.sql`, with no foreign key to or from another schema's tables. A Phoenix metro market (several towns, `America/Phoenix`) is a fixture inside the tests, for the tests and the war games, never in the reviewed file and never launched; which market launches first is open, so no code names either market. (2) Job locations: `app/catalog/data/zcta_centroids_az.csv`, the Arizona rows of the US Census Bureau Gazetteer ZCTA file (public domain; the ZCTA and its internal-point latitude and longitude), at least every ZCTA of the Lake Havasu City market's towns and of the Phoenix metro fixture's towns, with the source URL, vintage and download date in its header, loaded into `catalog.zcta_centroid` (same migration). Copy the rows from the Census file; never type or estimate a coordinate; if the file can't be reached, stop and report BLOCKED. `catalog::api::zip_location(zip)` gives the centroid or none, and a pure `catalog::api::miles_between(a, b)` the great-circle distance; no geocoding service and no new crate. (3) The service radius (BIZ-058): `migrations/0163_businesses_service_area.sql` adds `businesses.service_area` (business ID, base ZIP as 5 digits, radius as whole miles from 1 to 100, both required; SELECT, INSERT and UPDATE granted to `askhava_app` explicitly, since T250 narrows the default to SELECT and INSERT); `app/businesses/src/service_area.rs` sets and reads it (any well-formed 5-digit base ZIP is accepted; one with no centroid, such as an out-of-state base, is stored with no location, its radius then unused and its towns served alone deciding its cards, BIZ-058) with an audit row naming the fields only; sign-up (`app/portals/src/business_signup.rs`, `app/portals/templates/business_signup.html`) asks it beside towns served (BIZ-049): a base ZIP field and a radius field ("How many miles will you travel for a job?", draft wording for the copy panel, WORD-028, with a Spanish draft), with the accessible error pattern of A11Y-010 to A11Y-013 and every value kept, and sign-up cannot finish without both; the BIZ-030 summary reads them back. New code takes market values from this data, never a Lake Havasu City or Arizona literal (`docs/code-docs.md` section 4). Tests: `app/catalog/src/tests/markets.rs` (TD-INT-37) and `tests/integration/tests/service_area.rs` (TD-BIZ-37). Update `app/catalog/CLAUDE.md`, `app/businesses/CLAUDE.md`, `app/portals/CLAUDE.md` and `docs/architecture.md` section 10 (section 3 already lists the tables).
