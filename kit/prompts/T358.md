## Goal

Nov 10 code review, messaging F6 (D-2630; CON-018, QH-002, D-100): the two area-code tables disagree. `CANADIAN_AREA_CODES` (`app/messaging/src/keywords.rs:25`) lists 382, 683, 753 and 942, which `app/messaging/area_codes.json` (the quiet-hours zone table) lacks, so a homeowner on one of those numbers gets the HELP reply and nothing else (`NotTextable`); and 257 (British Columbia) is in the zone table but not in the Canadian list, so its HELP reply misses the roaming line. Fix: the four Ontario codes join `area_codes.json` under their zone (each checked against the North American numbering plan's published assignment, cited in the decision), and the Canadian set is derived from the zone table's Canadian entries (one source; a code's country is read from its zone, the table's Canadian zones such as `America/Toronto` and `America/Vancouver`; no tag or other key is added to the file, since `AreaCodes::parse` (`app/messaging/src/rules.rs:70`, not owned) reads only `{zone: [codes]}` and refuses a key that is not a known time zone (`:74`), D-3094), so the two cannot drift. Tests in `app/messaging/src/tests/area_codes.rs` (TD-QH-06), each failing on today's `main`: every code of the Canadian set has a zone in the table; 257 gets the roaming line; 382, 683, 753 and 942 are textable inside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in both Arizona and Toronto time; a US code never gets the roaming line. Every TD-QH and TD-CON case passes unchanged. Update the `messaging` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
