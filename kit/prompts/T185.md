- **CARD-010** Before a homeowner's description goes on a job card, the final gate (CARD-008) removes every phrase on the carrier-restricted content list (the CTIA SHAFT categories: sex, hate, alcohol, firearms and tobacco, plus cannabis and other drugs), and each removal is logged with the list entry that matched, never the removed text (D-485); the list is kept in `catalog` and read through `catalog::api` by `quotes` for this gate and by `ai` for the AI-021 screen (D-492). - **CARD-019** Every job card says "Sent to <N> businesses.", where N is the number of businesses whose card notice the email provider (or, for a text, the texting provider) had accepted when the card is sent, and the business portal's job page shows the current number sent, leaving out a business whose every card notice hard-bounced, and the number of quotes so far; no cap is added (D-188, D-397). (`docs/spec/job-cards.md:23`)
- **FEE-045** A pick of a fee-free job type (the catalog's `fee_free_job_types`, at launch the sale, purchase and refinance home inspections and termite reports of LIC-015) charges no introduction fee: the Confirm page and the card say "No introduction fee for this job.", the ledger records a $0 entry of kind "fee-free job type", no free first win is used, and the catalog check fails if a sale-related inspection is ever charged (D-403).
