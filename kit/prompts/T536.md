Every turn re-reads all earlier tool output, so a large result is paid for again on each later turn (D-3305). Never cut a failure line to save tokens.

Do not re-read a file, or a region of one, you already read unless it changed since: use your earlier output.
2. Build, test and lint output goes to a log, and you print only what decides: `cargo test -p <crate> >/tmp/t.log 2>&1; echo exit=$?; grep -nE 'FAILED|panicked|^error|test result' /tmp/t.log | head -n 60; tail -n 15 /tmp/t.log`.
