(3) The page renders each card's lines (`ShownCard::lines`), review and record links as plain links with no stars, counts or excerpts, the top sentence once and "doesn't rank businesses; check it yourself" once per card (today's template line goes once the card carries it); it still shows no homeowner detail and no business phone or address (PICK-010). Update the `portals` and `licensing` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/decisions.md`, and name in the hand-in the commit and tests that close IC-71.
