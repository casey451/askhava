# T218: Wording and template scans from the brains: sales phrases, non-transactional invitations and text segments

Task T218 (build, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: brain-phrase-scans.

## Goal

A docs test fails any business page, template or document that uses a lead-selling phrase ("ready to hire", "serious homeowners", "qualified", "pre-qualified", "high-intent", a promise of jobs) or a win rate not computed from the ledger; a template test fails any notice holding a recruiting, referral, discount, survey, new-feature or social-media invitation from the list in docs/ai_smell.yaml; and a docs test over the template files reports every template's encoding and segment count and fails when the English consent text needs UCS-2 or more than 2 segments, or when any template grows past the segment count recorded beside it (TXT-044) (WORD-030, TXT-046, TXT-044). The same phrase scan also fails, in any letter case, any text in `app/*/templates/`, `app/messaging/templates/*.json`, `docs/copy/` or the public `docs/legal/` drafts that says or implies joining early locks in prices ("price lock" or "price-lock", "lock in your rate", "founding rate", "grandfathered pricing", "your rate never goes up"); internal records such as `docs/decisions.md` are not scanned (WORD-032, D-1494). Turning Spanish conversations on without the campaign note and sample recorded is refused in code (TXT-043's code half; the campaign document is T113's, TD-TXT-26) (D-493).
