# T218: Wording and template scans from the brains: sales phrases, non-transactional invitations and text segments

Model: `claude-sonnet-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance).

## Goal

A docs test fails any business page, template or document that uses a lead-selling phrase ("ready to hire", "serious homeowners", "qualified", "pre-qualified", "high-intent", a promise of jobs) or a win rate not computed from the ledger; a template test fails any notice holding a recruiting, referral, discount, survey, new-feature or social-media invitation from the list in docs/ai_smell.yaml; and a docs test over the template files reports every template's encoding and segment count and fails when the consent text, HELP reply or opt-out confirmation needs UCS-2 or more than 2 segments (WORD-030, TXT-046, TXT-044).
