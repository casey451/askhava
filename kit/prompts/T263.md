# T263: payments: the monthly fee limit is a hard cap; capacity is reserved per business and month before Stripe, and setting a limit is atomic

Task T263 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: fee-limit-hard-cap.

Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: money).

## Goal

D-1427 accepted that two presses on two picks can both pass the FEE-041 check and take the month over the limit; Casey's overseer decided the limit is a hard cap (D-1758, external audit A11). (1) In `app/payments/src/charge.rs`, the limit check moves into `begin_attempt`'s transaction: it locks the business's `payments.fee_limit` row (`FOR UPDATE`; no row, no limit, no lock), sums this Arizona month's succeeded attempts plus every undecided (`sent`, `processing`) attempt of the business's other picks, each counted in the month its press started, and inserts the new attempt only when that sum plus this fee is at or under the limit; the inserted undecided attempt is the reservation. The transaction commits before Stripe is called; no lock or transaction is held across Stripe. A failed, declined or canceled attempt frees its capacity when it is decided; a success keeps it; a delayed success counts in the month its press started. Over the limit keeps D-1427's outcome (nothing charged, Stripe not called, the pick ends, the reply with the portal link). (2) `set_fee_limit` becomes one statement (`INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (business_id) DO UPDATE`, or `DELETE` for `None`), so an interrupted change never leaves the business without its limit. D-1427's pause rules stand. Update the `payments` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-FEE-46 passes against PostgreSQL across separate connections; every existing FEE-041 case still passes; the `app/payments/` 95% floor holds.
