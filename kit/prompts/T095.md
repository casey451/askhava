## Goal

When Ask Hava can't text the homeowner, the pick code goes by email to an address she gave in her own conversation with the PICK-013 limits, and a "<number> YES" from an opted-out phone picks without a text back once the Terms line was sent (PICK-029, PICK-030). It also adds the TXT-018 channel choice to the quote page (`quote_page.rs`, `quote_page.html`, T052's, extended here): "Get updates by email instead" and "by text and email", the one-tap confirm email and the bounce back to text with a quote-page note; the choice is stored in T094's `messaging` table through `messaging::api` (TD-TXT-23, D-491). The homeowner's page (`quote_page.html`) also shows the approved card (INT-041, T208's) and holds every quote, its license lines, the card, the WORD-002 statement and the free line (TXT-040); the review links are T165's (D-493).
