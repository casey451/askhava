# T204: ops: phone pushes by alert email, the weekly digest, owner flags, the away setting and security-event alerts

Task T204 (build, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: ops-pushes-digest-security-alerts.

## Goal

Only the listed alerts push to Casey's phone, as alert emails, with the 9 a.m. and 3 p.m. call-back pushes; the weekly digest and the owner-portal flags carry counts and numbers only; the away setting changes what businesses (on the portal home's review and dispute lines, `business_portal.rs` and `business_home.html`) and HELP replies (`keywords.rs`) are told, all extended here; security events are logged and a vault read count over three times its baseline alerts (OPS-026, OPS-027, OPS-029 to OPS-035, SEC-047, SEC-048). `ops` decides and records alerts; the sending is `messaging`'s handler (`alert_emails.rs`) and the digest's cross-module counts are read by `portals` (`digest_flags.rs`) through each module's `api` (architecture section 3, D-491). The away date, the digest's last-opened time, the done marks and the once-a-day push record are `ops` tables this task's migration adds; the digest also rolls up the not-covered requests by trade (OPS-041) and the BIZ-054 leaving answers (TD-OPS-09); the health-page figures are T106's and the failed-restore and HELP digest cases T109's and T151's (TD-OPS-22) (D-491). A daily worker check raises a Low alert above 70% of the database volume and a High alert, listed in the next OPS-032 daytime push, above 85%, neither starting safe mode (OPS-052); OPS-033's restart relies on OPS-049's self-exit, not the host's health check (D-492). The portal home also lets the business edit its Google and Yelp links through `businesses::api` (T165's storage; BIZ-052's portal half, D-492).

### 6. Health endpoint, restarts and the health page (OPS-007, OPS-008, OPS-033) (lines naming this task's requirements)
