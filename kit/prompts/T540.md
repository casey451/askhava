(1) `app/portals/src/owner_release.rs` and `templates/owner_release.html`: the owner enters a job number and presses "Re-send the release". The new `app/picks/src/release_resend.rs` (exported through the shared `app/picks/src/api.rs`), `resend_release(conn, job, press, now)`, in one transaction reads the job's `picks.release` row by `job_id` (the newest `released_at` when a job has more than one, and only that newest row is ever re-sent; its pick and business; none is `NotReleased` and queues nothing), the job number and the business's `NoticePurpose::PicksAndRelease` addresses (`businesses::api::notice_addresses`, as `release.rs:618` reads them), and queues one `core.task` of kind `messaging.business_notice` (`picks::api::BUSINESS_NOTICE_KIND`, `app/picks/src/money_notices.rs:35`) with `release_notice(business, pick, to, job)` (`release.rs:199`) as its payload and dedupe key `messaging.business_notice.release_resend.<pick>.<press>`, so one press queues one notice and a repeated submit of the same press none; it writes one audit row of IDs only. No release row, charge or fee decision is created; a pick with no release is refused on the page; the homeowner's phone is never shown (PICK-023). (2) Served in T310's `app/server/src/owner_portal.rs` (owned here after T310, before T407) behind the owner session with `owner_power(OwnerPower::Release, ...)` (SEC-006; SEC-043 asks no fresh factor for a release, and `owner_power` asks none, `app/portals/src/owner_auth.rs:614`; D-3892), `refuse_cross_site` and the CSRF check before the session check, `no_store`, one section 13 row per method and path (Wired `yes`); Every owner route constant is a fixed path under `/owner/` with no path parameter; record IDs (alert, category, sign-up, press) travel in the form body (`app/server/tests/door_matrix.rs:302` to `:322` fills only `{pick}`, `{complaint}`, `{kind}`, `{object}`, `{token}`). Tests: `tests/integration/tests/owner_release.rs` (TD-PICK-41's integration half: a released pick's press queues one `messaging.business_notice` task with that dedupe key and creates no release row, and the same press again queues none; a pick with no `picks.release` row (paid or not, in safe mode or paused by CANCEL) is refused and queues nothing; fake data only) and `tests/e2e/owner_release.spec.ts` (the browser half, signed in through T303's `tests/e2e/owner_session.ts`).
