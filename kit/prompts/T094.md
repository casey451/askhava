# T094: messaging: 3 retries over 30 minutes, then email and portal, then call-back alert

Task T094 (build, phase 1, stage 3). Slug: messaging-retries-email.

## Goal

Important texts are retried 3 times over 30 minutes, then sent by email and shown in the portal; a homeowner whose texts keep failing with no email on file becomes a call-back alert for Casey; and above 90% of the email provider's daily limit, only digests and open-cards emails wait for the reset (TXT-014, TXT-015, OPS-038). This is the email pipeline every later sender uses, so it runs first in stage 3: `messaging.email_outbox` sends each email once, every email template extends `app/messaging/templates/email_layout.html`, which carries the SEC-028 footer with the LLC postal address and "Manage email notices", and a module below or beside `messaging` (payments, picks) never sends mail itself: it enqueues a `core.task` event that a `messaging` handler turns into the email (D-491). This task's migration also holds the homeowner's update-channel choice (text, email or both, TXT-018), read and written only through `messaging::api`; the quote-page control is T095's (D-491).
