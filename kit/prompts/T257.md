# T257: privacy and server: the scrubber remembers names across a homeowner's texts and knows her vault name

Task T257 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: conversation-aware-scrub.

## Goal

The served SMS route scrubs each text with `vault_name: None` and a fresh `NameMemory` (`read` in `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`), so a name she stated in an earlier text, and her name in the vault, are not removed from a later one (AI-003, AI-020; external audit A02, D-1752). Build the privacy-owned, contact-aware read. (1) `migrations/0130_privacy_stated_names.sql`: `privacy.stated_name` (contact ID with a foreign key to the vault contact `ON DELETE CASCADE`, the name sealed like a vault field with its key version, created time; unique per contact and blind-indexed value so a repeat is one row); the app role gets SELECT, INSERT and DELETE only. (2) `app/privacy/src/scrub_context.rs`: `privacy::api::read_inbound_for(conn, vault, contact, raw, words)` opens the contact's vault name under the existing `(Messaging, Inbound)` grant and her stated names, scrubs with both (`Known::vault_name` and `NameMemory::mark_known` per stored name), stores any name the scrub newly marks as stated, and returns only the `Inbound` (flags and `ScrubbedText`): no plaintext leaves `privacy`. Because no plaintext leaves, this read may run inside the delivery's transaction (D-1752 narrows D-752's in-transaction refusal to functions that return plaintext); its audit rows commit or roll back with the delivery and a resent delivery is read and audited again. SEC-047's hourly rate check stays on for this read: it is counted under the `(Messaging, Inbound)` grant's own action, once per text, against its 14-day baseline with D-750's floor of one read an hour, so the alarm tracks inbound text volume (a flood of texts is a signal Casey should see); no exclusion and no new threshold, tested with a steady hour (no alert) and a burst over three times the baseline (one alert). (3) The route resolves the sender (blind index only) before reading, then calls `read_inbound_for`; a business sender's text is scrubbed with an empty context as now. (4) Purge and rotation: the PRIV-007 purge and "Delete my data" (`app/privacy/src/purge.rs`, T092's) delete her stated names with her name; `rotate_batch` in `app/privacy/src/vault.rs` re-seals them under the new key. No raw name, message history or digest of a name goes to any intake row, log, task payload or alert. Update `docs/architecture.md` section 8, the `privacy` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-AI-19 passes through the real inbound route against PostgreSQL and the stand-in AI's recording transport; the `app/privacy/` 95% coverage floor holds; `tests/security/tests/vault_readers.rs` passes unchanged (no new reader or grant).
