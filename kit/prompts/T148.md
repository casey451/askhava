# T148: portals: secure email action links for every former text keyword, signed Hold links and the email sender rules

Task T148 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: business-actions-pick-call.
Branch: `claude/task/T148-business-actions-pick-call`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: money, privacy, security, compliance).

## Goal

Every action a business once did by text keyword has a signed, single-purpose email link to a portal page that shows what it will do first; an email reply never acts; Hold works without a session; and the action pages and emails meet the A11Y-023 and A11Y-024 rows (BIZ-028, BIZ-032 to BIZ-034, BIZ-038, CALL-011, SEC-025, A11Y-023, A11Y-024).
