## Goal

Plan change D-4051 (Casey's outside code audit, item B3, Nov 16, 2026): texting CLOSE does nothing. `answer_close` (`app/intake/src/closing.rs:60`) has no production caller (its doc, `:12`, says it waits for the D-999 dispatcher), and `reply_in` (`app/intake/src/replies.rs:104`) only uses `closes()` (`:150`, which reads `close_reading(words, &[])`, so a lone number answering "Close which?" is not even seen) to skip the name-and-address and last-question steps, then hands the text to `receive_text`, which closes nothing. This task wires `answer_close` into `reply_in`: in an open request, and only when the text is not an opt-out or HELP (`answered_elsewhere`) and its 911 screen is clear (`emergency::check(words, false)` is `Clear`, as `clear` at `replies.rs:186` reads it), a CLOSE or CERRAR text with a number or ALL, a cancel-intent message, and, while a "Close which?" is asked (`close_which` answer `asked`), a lone open request number or ALL go to `answer_close` inside the reply's transaction (its `db.begin()` is then a savepoint), and its texts are the reply's. (D-4064, the Opus pre-claim check) A text that is not clear never goes to `answer_close`: `shows_intent` matches any one clause (`app/intake/src/rules.rs:682` to `:693`), so "never mind, I smell gas" reads as intent, and `receive_text` (`app/intake/src/service.rs:144`) holds the only 911 screen (INT-002); such a text goes to `receive_text` as today, so its 911 text is queued and no job is closed. Only `Closing::Closed` and `Closing::Which` end the reply; on `Closing::Explained` (a mere mention of cancelling, or a CLOSE number that is not open) the JOB-023 line is added to the reply's texts and the text still goes on to `receive_text` and the last-question step as today, so a mention inside an intake answer is still taken; anything `answer_close` answers `Nothing` goes on as today. "cancel" alone is an opt-out answered by `messaging` and never closes a job (JOB-020, D-005). The result is returned through `Reply` (an existing variant or a new one); `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs` is not changed (its `opened` uses `matches!`, so a new variant needs no arm there), and `answer_close` is called as it is: `closing.rs` changes only in its module doc, whose last sentence (`:12`, it waits for the D-999 dispatcher) becomes false (T402, the later owner of `closing.rs`, already depends on this task). Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/close_by_text.rs`, against PostgreSQL through `intake::api::receive_raw_reply`, fake data only: "CLOSE <n>" closes that open job (Cancelled, with its audit row) and texts `job_closed`; "CLOSE ALL" with two open jobs closes both and texts `jobs_closed`; "please cancel my request" with one open job closes it and says so; with two open jobs it asks "Close which?" and closes nothing, and a following lone open number closes that one; "cancel" alone closes nothing; "how do I cancel" gets the JOB-023 line and closes nothing; a mention inside a longer text gets the JOB-023 line and is still handled by `receive_text` as without it; "never mind, I smell gas" with one open job queues the 911 text and closes no job; a CLOSE text never reaches the name-and-address step. `tests/integration/tests/inbound_text_retry.rs:597` (TD-TXT-35, "never mind, all fixed" with no open request) must still pass; run it. Edit the D-999 note on `answer_close` in place at `app/intake/NOTES.md:33` ("no callers until the D-999 dispatcher"; the `intake` `CLAUDE.md` has no such note) and update `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `tests/integration/tests/close_by_text.rs` passes against PostgreSQL with every listed case, and the CLOSE, "Close which?" answer and cancel-intent cases fail on `main` before the change (no job is closed there), and the "never mind, I smell gas" case queues the 911 text and closes no job; every existing `intake` test and TD-JOB-05 case passes unchanged.
