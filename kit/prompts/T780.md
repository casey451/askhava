## Goal

Plan change D-5081 (external code review of Nov 20, 2026, finding s F37 and F38; OPS-039). (1) `ledger_totals` in `app/payments/src/scorecard_inputs.rs` counts refunds, disputes and charges each by their own record time inside the window, and `money_figs` in `app/portals/src/scorecard.rs` divides one by the other, so a week with two refunds of earlier charges and one new charge reads "200.0% (2 of 1)" and a week with refunds and no charge reads "No data yet". The rates are now a cohort: of the charges recorded in the window, how many have a refund and how many a dispute recorded by now, so no rate passes 100%; a window with no charge shows "No data yet" for the rate and, beside it, the plain count of refunds and disputes recorded in the window, so they are never hidden. Say in the decision entry and on the page's note that a recent window's rate can still rise. (2) `ScorecardLines::from_pairs` in `app/portals/src/scorecard_lines.rs` checks only that each key is present, so a swapped GO and STOP pair turns an amber or red figure green. It now refuses, with a new error naming the key, a line whose GO is worse than its STOP for that line's direction (equal values pass); start-up refuses as it does for a missing key, and the shipped defaults pass. Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/scorecard_rates.rs`, PostgreSQL, fake data only: a refund in week 2 of a week-1 charge counts against week 1 and not week 2; no figure is over 100%; a week with refunds and no charge shows the count and no rate; unit cases in-crate for each direction of a swapped line, an equal pair and the shipped defaults. `tests/integration/tests/scorecard.rs` passes unchanged; if one of its cases pins the old division, report it, do not change it. `scorecard_inputs.rs` is money code under the `payments` 95% branch floor. Build notes go to the end of each crate's `NOTES.md`; update `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `tests/integration/tests/scorecard_rates.rs` and the new in-crate cases pass; no scorecard figure can pass 100%; a swapped line refuses start-up and the shipped defaults start; `tests/integration/tests/scorecard.rs` passes unchanged; `app/payments/` stays at or above its branch floor.
