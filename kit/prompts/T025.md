## Goal

No AI call runs in live until the zero-retention agreement is signed or the owner's "AI on scrubbed text" switch is on after the provider's terms are recorded; with it off intake runs in keyword-only mode, structured readings are written off-database, and the weekly summary takes counts only (AI-008, AI-013, AI-014). The privacy-policy version the AI-008 gate checks for the AI-018 wording is a row of this task's migration (version, date and whether it holds the AI-018 sentence), entered by the owner; the policy text itself is T112's (D-491). The switch can't first turn on until the provider-terms record also holds the flagged-content retention period and a Console monthly spend limit of no more than 3 times the expected cost (AI-008, D-492).
