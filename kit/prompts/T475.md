## Goal

Plan change D-3753: no module has a reader for an OPS-008 health figure and `portals` may not query another module's schema (`tests/integration/tests/architecture_migrations.rs:271`, `schema_refs`, covers code), so T476's health page needs one read-only function per figure in the module that owns the data, each exported through that module's shared `api.rs`, each taking a window (`from`, `to`) and returning counts or durations only, never a phone, email, address, name or message body (PRIV-005). (1) `app/messaging/src/health_figures.rs`: text delivery rate and failed texts per audience (homeowner, business) from `messaging.outbox` and `messaging.delivery_event`; business email rates (delivered, bounced, complained) per audience from `messaging.email_outbox` and `messaging.email_event`; today's and this month's sent email counts for the sending-limit use (OPS-004; the limits are settings the serving route passes to T476's page, not read here; a row with a status this task does not know, such as T470's later `dropped`, counts as not sent); and the OPS-037 weekly medians from `messaging.notice_log` (sent to first opening, to quote, to Confirm) and the share of cards quoted within 6 hours (a setting), as T198 records them (`app/messaging/src/email_timing.rs`). (2) `app/quotes/src/health_figures.rs`: this month's job cards and the share that ended without a quote (BIZ-044; a withdrawn quote counts as none, D-3155). (3) `app/payments/src/health_figures.rs`: payment errors in the window (failed or undecided charge attempts and refund failures, `payments.charge_attempt` and `payments.refund`), counts only. (4) `app/ai/src/health_figures.rs`: AI calls and AI errors in the window from `ai.call_stat`. Medians and shares are computed in SQL in one query each (no per-row loop), an empty window gives `None`, never a division by zero. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/health_figures.rs` against PostgreSQL with fake data only: each figure from planted rows, including TD-OPS-12's 20- and 60-minute medians, an empty window, rows outside the window left out, and no figure carrying a contact field. Update each crate's `NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-3844 (the overseer's pre-claim scout, Nov 15, 2026): `app/quotes/src/health_figures.rs` also adds `spot_check_cards(conn, from, to, n)` for OPS-009's monthly spot-check on T476's health page: `n` (10) distinct random job cards of the window, with only the fields the owner's card view shows, through `quotes::api`, tested for 10 distinct cards of the month and fewer when fewer exist. Plan change D-3877 (Fable's pre-claim check of stage-5 wave 0, Nov 15, 2026): The owner-card view `spot_check_cards` returns, per card, the card ID, job ID, trade and `sent_at` (`quotes.job_card` holds `id, job_id, business_id, handyman, sent_at`, `migrations/0067_quotes_matching.sql:13`; the trade from `quotes.shown_card.category`). Payment errors count charge attempts in `declined`, `needs_action` or `unreachable` as failed and in `sent` or `processing` past the window's end as undecided (statuses `sent, processing, succeeded, declined, needs_action, unreachable`, `migrations/0096_payments_charge.sql:28`).

Acceptance: `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test health_figures` passes against PostgreSQL: every OPS-008 figure the page shows has a reader in its own module's `api`, each tested from planted fake rows, empty windows give `None`, and no reader returns a contact field (D-3753).
