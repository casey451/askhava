## Goal

The quote form's route (`business_actions.rs`) records its first opening through T198's `messaging::api` call (OPS-037, D-491). No Verify voice call ever reaches a business phone and identity codes go by text, email or letter; the homeowner's voice code call happens only on her tap and opens "This is Ask Hava LLC"; businesses get the "Ask Hava homeowner calls" contact card; and opening any email link only shows its page, a POST with a CSRF token acting, with a "This inbox is shared" switch requiring a fresh code; the switch and the current contact-card list are on the portal home (`business_home.html`) and the contact-card offer on the sign-up done screen (`business_signup.html`), both extended here; the welcome email's offer of the card is a link to the portal's card download, added to the welcome email's template keys here (TD-BIZ-22) (D-491) (BIZ-002, BIZ-026, BIZ-039, BIZ-046, SEC-004, SEC-008, SEC-026, SEC-027, PICK-013, TXT-038, CALL-014, CALL-015).
