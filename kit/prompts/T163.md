## Goal

AI-written questions, job cards and project files pass code checks on every output (no price, cause, diagnosis, business name or contact detail, no added claim) and fall back to the fixed templates or the homeowner's own words; AI output is content-screened and capped in cost (AI-019, AI-021). The cause-word check also covers the Spanish words AI-019 lists (D-492). The WORD-022 phrase list the checks use is `app/ai/data/word_022_phrases.json`, owned here and kept equal to `scripts/trace_spec.py`'s list by `tests/docs/test_word_022_phrases.py`; business names are passed in by the caller, since `ai` cannot call `businesses` (D-493).
