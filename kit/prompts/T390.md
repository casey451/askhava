# T390: calls and server: a blocked staff call queues the LINK notice, and the main phone's "LINK <PIN>" reply links that phone, failing closed

## Goal

Register row IC-74 (D-3079; CALL-011, CALL-003, CALL-012, CALL-025; D-1696, D-2247, D-078, D-311, D-1755): nothing in production calls `request_link` (`app/calls/src/lifecycle.rs:279`, exported by `calls::api`), and no code handles the business's "LINK <PIN>" reply (the inbound text route sends a business phone's text only to `phone_stopped` or the auto-reply, `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs:246`), so the LINK notice of CALL-011 is never sent and a reply to it would do nothing. After T326 (the notice names the line's stored PIN) and T357 (the last stage-4 owner of `inbound_text.rs`). Which call is "blocked": an unlinked phone made to enter the PIN (D-078: "the blocked caller hears the PIN prompt"). Its business is known only once the PIN it entered connects a line, so only then is a notice queued; a call that connects no line queues nothing, because a business picked any other way could be told the last 4 digits of another business's caller. (1) `app/calls/src/link_reply.rs` (new), through `calls::api`: `link_after_dial(db, vault, call_sid, caller, now)`, which the voice route (`answer` in `app/server/src/inbound_voice.rs`) calls after a turn answered with the dial: when the caller's party is `Party::Unlinked` (`routing::party_of`) and the CallSid's stored call start (`models::call_start`) names a line, it calls `request_link` for that line and, in the same transaction, when a notice was queued, stores one pending link for the line in `calls.link_request`: the line, its business, the caller's keyed digests under the vault's `sender-dir` index (`Vault::blind_index`, as `messaging.sender_directory` stores a linked phone), the request time and an expiry 24 hours later; never the number, its last 4 digits or the PIN (PRIV-002, D-1696). A homeowner, a linked business phone, a wrong-PIN hang-up and a repeat on the same line (`request_link`'s once-per-line rule) store nothing. A failure here is one log line naming no number, line or PIN and never changes the TwiML: the dial still answers. (2) `link_reply(conn, vault, (business, from), pin, now)`, which the inbound text route calls in the delivery's transaction for a text from a linked business phone (`Relation::Business`) that is exactly `LINK` and 4 digits (any case, surrounding spaces; any other text keeps today's path). It links only when the replying phone is the business's main phone (the owner's, CALL-012; any other phone of the business is refused) and a live pending link on an open line of that same business has a stored PIN (`pins::stored_pin`) equal to the 4 digits. Then, in one transaction: a new `businesses.staff_phone`, linked as by the owner so it works at once (BIZ-033), through `businesses::api` (`app/businesses/src/reply_link.rs`, new; one `businesses.phone_linked` audit row naming IDs only); the pending digests put in the sender directory for that phone through `messaging::api` (`app/messaging/src/sender_pending.rs`, new; a digest another phone already holds refuses the link); and the pending link used up, its digests deleted. Everything else fails closed with no change and the same outcome: a wrong PIN, the PIN of another business's line (the lookup is by the replying business), an expired or used pending link (so a replayed LINK), a closed line, no pending link. At most 3 refused LINK replies per replying phone per Arizona day (the per-call PIN count of CALL-025), counted by the phone's keyed digest, never its number; past that, every LINK from it that day is refused without the PIN being compared, so the reply is no PIN oracle. Expired and used rows are deleted when met. A LINK text gets no auto-reply and no text answer (no new customer copy; a confirmation text is listed for Casey, D-3079). (3) `migrations/0185_calls_link_request.sql` (new): the pending links and the refused-reply counts, with no number column; the app role gets only the grants the two functions use. The email's "Link this phone" page (`ActionKind::LinkPhone`, T148's) can call the same link step later; wiring its button is not this task's. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/link_reply.rs` (TD-CALL-23) through the served voice and inbound text routes with signed fake Twilio requests and fake numbers only. Update the `calls`, `businesses`, `messaging` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-CALL-23 passes against PostgreSQL through the served routes; each of its cases fails on today's `main`; every TD-CALL-14, TD-CALL-17, TD-CALL-18, TD-BIZ-39 and other existing voice and inbound text case passes unchanged; no table, log line, task payload or audit row holds a blocked number, its last 4 digits outside the notice, or a PIN.
