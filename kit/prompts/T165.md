## Goal

Sign-up (`business_signup.html`, `business_signup.rs`, extended here) saves the business's Google and Yelp links (fetched only through the SSRF guard) and license numbers for its quote cards (BIZ-052, SEC-041); it fills the review-link slot T186 left on every quote card (`quote_card.rs`, PICK-041); leaving Ask Hava is T228's (D-491). Editing the links later in the portal is T204's, the first portal-home task after this one (BIZ-052's portal half, D-492). Plan change D-2943 (BIZ-022, PICK-041, D-336; the business name on the business record, `sources/01_spec/Havasu_Home_Quotes_Technical_Blueprint_v9.3.md:90`): sign-up also asks the business's name (required) and its trade name or DBA (optional), the names it goes by, each up to 200 characters and screened by `businesses::api::screen_public_name`, a failing name shown again with the reason (BIZ-022) and nothing stored; `migrations/0160_businesses_links.sql` adds `businesses.business_name` (one row per business: `business_id` referencing `businesses.business`, `business_name` not blank, `trade_name` null or not blank, both at most 200 characters; no owner name, phone, email or address), with its own explicit `GRANT SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE` to `askhava_app` since a save replaces the row and migration 0110's default for `businesses` gives only `SELECT, INSERT` (TD-SEC-34 reads the grant), and `links.rs` exports `businesses::api::save_business_name(conn, business, name, trade_name)`, which screens again, writes or replaces the row and audits by field name only; T302's sign-up `POST` calls it, and T316 reads the row (`public_names`). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/business_links.rs` (TD-BIZ-32's name case): a good name and trade name are stored, a second save replaces them, a name holding a phone number, a link or a channel direction is refused with the reason and stores nothing. It also corrects the stale module doc at `app/businesses/src/holding.rs:8` ("No business table holds a license number or a name (PRIV-001)"): the business's own names are now stored in `businesses.business_name` (PRIV-001 covers homeowner details only); doc lines only, no code change. Plan change D-2954 (LIC-011, D-255): when a no-license business enters its city license number at sign-up, `businesses.business_name` also keeps it (`city_license_number`, null or at most 64 characters, not blank) and `save_business_name` takes it, so T385's `signup_record` reads it by business ID and never from a request; TD-BIZ-32's name case stores and replaces it.
