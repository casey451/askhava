# T236: AI-call budgets per conversation and per job, and no AI call before the area check

Task T236 (build, phase 2, stage 6). Slug: ai-call-budgets.

## Goal

Credit-drain defense for the war games (D-1154): AI-021's global daily cap stays, and below it AI-028's per-step budgets apply: no AI call before the consent text and the area check; 2 readings per inbound message, 1 reword per bank question (at most 8), 1 follow-up, card and project file 1 each plus 1 per EDIT (at most 3), 4 concept prompts, 2 screening calls a photo for at most 3 photos; backstops of 40 calls per conversation per Arizona day and 60 per job, with the card call always reserved so no budget lands on it; a verified web-chat session shares its phone's budget and an unverified one makes no call; quote reads 3 per business per job, outside the job's budget; only the first 1,600 characters of a scrubbed message are sent, while the AI-off card keeps her full scrubbed words. Past a budget the step runs on the AI-off path (INT-021) with nothing said and an `ai.call_stat` row with outcome `budget`; `models::calls_today` (`app/ai/src/models.rs`) excludes `budget` rows as it excludes `daily_cap` rows, so refused calls never use up the AI-021 cap. `IntakeCall` carries the conversation, job, step and (for quote reads) business it is for; the caller in `intake` passes them; the budgets are settings in `AiConfig`; the migration adds the `budget` outcome and the conversation, job, step and business columns to `ai.call_stat`.
