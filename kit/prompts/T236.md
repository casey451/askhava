## Goal

Credit-drain defense for the war games (D-1154): AI-021's global daily cap stays, and below it no AI call is made before the consent text went and the ZIP and area check passed, only the first 1,600 characters of the scrubbed message are sent, and each conversation (one phone, or one verified web-chat session) and each job has its own AI-call budget, past which the step runs on the AI-off path with nothing said to the person and an `ai.call_stat` row with outcome `budget` (AI-028). `IntakeCall` carries the conversation and job it is for; the caller in `intake` passes them; the budgets and the length cap are settings in `AiConfig`; the migration adds the `budget` outcome and the conversation and job columns to `ai.call_stat`.
