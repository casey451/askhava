## Goal

Plan change D-3676 (split from T302; BIZ-042, BIZ-040, SEC-004, SEC-034, SEC-042; D-462, D-2128, D-3641): the inbox check (BIZ-042) comes after the email is verified, and a correct email code activates the business (T461), after which every sign-up step refuses (D-3641); so it is served behind the business session. `app/server/src/business_signup.rs` adds a route constant `INBOX_CHECK_ROUTE` = `/b/signup/inbox`, a path under `/b/signup` that is not one of D-3641's nine open step paths and so keeps the Business door (`tests/security/door_cases.txt` pins `Business /b/signup/other` the same way; no door file changes), served behind `portals::api::business_principal`: its `GET` shows `portals::api::email_check_page` for the signed-in person (`businesses::api::email_check` with that person's `Acting`), the mail-app choice coming back as `?app=` (the page's own `GET` form, `app/portals/templates/email_check.html:10`); its `POST`s (the forms have no `action`, so they post to the same path) send the sample card (`businesses::api::send_sample_card`) or record the answer (`record_inbox_answer`); its "Skip for now" links to `/b`. There is no sample pick step here (D-462: `portals::api::sample_pick_on_open` offers it at the first real job card). Every response is `no_store`; a cross-site or wrong-CSRF `POST` is refused, changes nothing and is a security event; a signed-out visitor gets the Business door's refusal. Its rows in `docs/architecture.md` section 13 are marked `yes`, and its `GET`'s `PAGE_STATES` entry goes in the shared `tests/a11y/page_states.ts` with a seeded business session (T452's cookie-carrying seeds; no vault is read). Update the `server` `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
