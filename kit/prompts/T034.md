# T034: licensing: agency-licensed categories and boundary job types

## Goal

`licensing::rules` routes agency-licensed categories only to their licensees (Department of Agriculture pest licensees by attestation, alarm, camera, inspection and septic boards) and no-license categories only to businesses matching the city list, and splits the boundary job types (dryer vents, holiday lights, appliances, pools) between no-license, handyman and licensed work (LIC-011 to LIC-016, LIC-028 to LIC-031, LIC-047).
