## Goal

The owner refunds a charge through a review step that issues one Stripe refund and one ledger row, a refund on a disputed charge being blocked; the owner portal alone has pause, refund and release powers; and the audit log viewer shows every action with its actor (FEE-026, SEC-006). T304 serves these pages (D-2128): page functions and route constants only, no `.route` in `server`; the browser half of J3's refund and audit-log steps runs in T304's spec, and `tests/e2e/owner_refund.spec.ts` checks what an unserved page allows. The review step of a disputed charge also offers "I submitted this dispute's evidence in Stripe myself", which after a fresh second factor calls `payments::api::record_pick_owner_submission` (T722, D-4506) once for that charge's own dispute (D-229, FEE-050; fourth external audit F44, D-2204). Plan change D-3744: if `owner_refund.html` or `owner_audit_log.html` draws a native checkbox or radio, that template links `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/static/portals/forms.css">` (T466's shared form stylesheet) first in its `main` block, as `app/portals/templates/feedback.html:10` does (D-3700, A11Y-007, A11Y-009); T466 is a dependency. Plan change D-3883 (Fable's pre-claim checks, Nov 16, 2026): The refund calls `payments::api::request_refund` (`app/payments/src/refunds.rs:107`) with `RefundTrigger::Owner`, which T529 adds. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/owner_refund.rs`, fake data only: a replayed press gives one refund and one ledger row; a disputed charge is blocked; the evidence press calls `record_pick_owner_submission` once. Budget 450 lines (400 before D-4506). The press names P, the pick the review step was drawn for (the route's `{pick}` or the form's pick field, D-3890), and the `dp_` ID and, after a fresh second factor, calls `payments::api::record_pick_owner_submission(conn, P, dp, at)` once, never `record_owner_submission`. `Recorded` writes one owner audit row whose subject is P and whose change names the `dp_` ID, in the same transaction (SEC-006, D-074). `AlreadyIn` writes no second audit row and shows that the evidence is already in. `NotThisPick` (a `dp_` of another pick, or none) is refused with no audit row, no ledger row and no change to that dispute, and the page shows a plain error (D-229, FEE-050). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/owner_refund.rs` (TD-FEE-74; T722's half is TD-FEE-73): with disputes on picks P and Q, P's review step lists P's `dp_` and not Q's; a press on P naming Q's `dp_` is refused and leaves Q's `submitted_at`, `submitted_by` and `auto_submit` unchanged and writes no audit row or countering fee (T722's TD-FEE-73 proves Q is still auto-submitted); the press on P's own dispute records once with one audit row under P, and a replay writes no second audit row.
