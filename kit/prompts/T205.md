## Goal

The owner refunds a charge through a review step that issues one Stripe refund and one ledger row, a refund on a disputed charge being blocked; the owner portal alone has pause, refund and release powers; and the audit log viewer shows every action with its actor (FEE-026, SEC-006).

- **SEC-006** The owner portal is the only portal with pause, refund and release powers, a release there only completes or re-sends one for a pick already confirmed and paid or with a recorded $0 fee decision (a free first win, a repeat customer under FEE-013 or a fee-free job type under FEE-045, D-477), and every owner action is audit-logged (D-074). (`docs/spec/security.md:10`)

- **TD-FEE-14** (kind I; layers In, P; covers FEE-026): Any refund trigger replayed or duplicated → one refund; a failed refund retries hourly with an alert; a disputed charge's refund is blocked and flagged
- **TD-SEC-01** (kind I; layers S; covers SEC-004, SEC-005, SEC-006, SEC-008, SEC-019, SEC-034): Every route that shows contact details or photos answers with `Cache-Control: no-store` and no third-party script (D-454); authorization matrix generated from the route table: every route × anonymous, homeowner A, homeowner B, business X, business Y, owner → expected allow or deny; homeowner A's token never reads B's job; only the owner has pause, refund and release; sign-in rules per portal (link single use and 15 minutes, new-device code, 30-day sessions, owner 12 hours)

Decisions (`docs/decisions.md`: the question's opening and the choice of each):

- **D-074** Question: the brief gives the owner portal "release" power (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:316`) but no contact detail may leave the vault except on a confirmed, paid pick (`BUILD_BRIEF.md:151`). Choice: the owner's release only completes or re-sends a release for a pick already confirmed and paid, or a recorded free win; it can never release otherwise. Reason: keeps the vault invariant; both lines are the brief's.
