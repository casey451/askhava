# T191: payments: the unreachable-homeowner refund at 96 hours and its texts

Task T191 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: payments-unreachable-refund.
Branch: `claude/task/T191-payments-unreachable-refund`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: money, privacy).

## Goal

When the business could not reach the homeowner, the refund decision waits 96 hours after the release, the homeowner gets the TXT-026 and TXT-030 texts in the calling hours, and the job closes as JOB-027 says (FEE-022, JOB-027, TXT-026, TXT-030). `picks` owns the decision and the job close (calling `payments::api` for the refund and `jobs::api` for the close); `payments` keeps only the refund rule (D-491). It also decides a dispute's evidence auto-submit from the recorded release and call attempt (FEE-050) and, on an early fraud warning for an unreleased charge, ends the pick as fraud-refunded and returns the job to Quotes ready (FEE-052), calling `payments::api` (TD-FEE-39); it sends the missed-call text (TXT-030, TD-TXT-30); and after a release it moves a call-contact job with no call in 72 hours back to Quotes ready and keeps an email-contact job Connected until the day-3 reply, NO returning it with a no-show (TD-JOB-07, moved from TD-JOB-03) (D-491).
