## Goal

Register row IC-74, reply side (D-3079; CALL-011, CALL-003, CALL-012, CALL-025; D-1696, D-2247, D-311, D-1755): T390 queues the LINK notice and stores one pending link (`calls.link_request`, the blocked caller's keyed `sender-dir` digests only, with an expiry time) after a blocked call connects a line, but no code handles the business's "LINK <PIN>" reply (the inbound text route sends a business phone's text only to `phone_stopped` or the auto-reply, `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs:302`), so a reply to the notice does nothing. After T390 (the pending links, the refused-reply counts and their grants, all in its `migrations/0185_calls_link_request.sql`) and T357 (the last earlier stage-4 owner of `inbound_text.rs`). (1) `app/calls/src/link_reply.rs` (new), through `calls::api`: `link_reply(conn, vault, (business, from), pin, now)`, which the inbound text route calls in the delivery's transaction for a text from a linked business phone (`Relation::Business`) that is exactly `LINK` and 4 digits (any case, surrounding spaces; any other text keeps today's path). It links only when the replying phone is the business's main phone (the owner's, CALL-012; any other phone of the business is refused) and a live pending link on an open line of that same business has a stored PIN (`pins::stored_pin`) equal to the 4 digits. A pending link is live only while its expiry time is still in the future (`now < expiry`), so one exactly 24 hours old is refused. Then, in one transaction: a new `businesses.staff_phone`, linked as by the owner so it works at once (BIZ-033), through `businesses::api` (`app/businesses/src/reply_link.rs`, new; one `businesses.phone_linked` audit row naming IDs only); the pending digests put in the sender directory for that phone through `messaging::api` (`app/messaging/src/sender_pending.rs`, new; a digest another phone already holds refuses the link); and the pending link used up, its digests deleted. Everything else fails closed with no change and the same outcome: a wrong PIN, the PIN of another business's line (the lookup is by the replying business), a pending link no longer live or already used (so a replayed LINK), a closed line, no pending link. At most 3 refused LINK replies per replying phone per Arizona day (the per-call PIN count of CALL-025), counted by the phone's keyed digest, never its number; past that, every LINK from it that day is refused without the PIN being compared, so the reply is no PIN oracle. Pending links no longer live and used ones are deleted when met. A LINK text gets no auto-reply and no text answer (no new customer copy; a confirmation text is listed for Casey, D-3079). The email's "Link this phone" page (`ActionKind::LinkPhone`, T148's) can call the same link step later; wiring its button is not this task's. (2) `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`: the LINK branch above.
