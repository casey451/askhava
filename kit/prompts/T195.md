## Goal

The business portal's billing page shows charges, the free first win, refunds, credits, payment methods and CSV statements, all built from the ledger (BIZ-015). Statements are CSV only in V1 (BIZ-015 as reworded by D-491). The portal also shows each complaint (kind and date, no homeowner detail) with its reply box open for 5 days, read and written through `businesses::api` (T083's), on `business_complaints.html` routed from `business_portal.rs` (BIZ-029, TD-BIZ-09, moved from T083, D-492). Changing a payment method or the fee limit needs a Verify code from the last 5 minutes (SEC-038, TD-SEC-17, D-492).
