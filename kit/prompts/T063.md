# T063: picks: Confirm within 4 business hours, license at pick and at Confirm

Task T063 (build, phase 1, stage 3). Slug: picks-confirm-window.
Branch: `claude/task/T063-picks-confirm-window`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: licensing, money, privacy).

## Goal

After a pick the business has 4 business hours (a setting) from the pick notice to confirm; its license and account are re-checked at the pick with the one live lookup and again at Confirm; a recurring pick shows how its fee was set; a late answer is told the pick expired; and the notice goes to the owner and every "Confirm and billing" member (PICK-019, PICK-020, PICK-033 to PICK-035, PICK-037). The pick notice email goes through T094's email pipeline as a `core.task` event a `messaging` handler sends; the reminder emails are T197's (D-491). The pick notice's "Hold for 2 more hours" link is T148's (BIZ-038), its "Can't take it" link T214's (PICK-050), and the page scoping that lets only the owner and "Confirm and billing" people complete a Confirm is T084's (TD-PICK-15) (D-493).
