Plan change D-3331: the look is V6 (daylight and evergreen), so the fonts are Gelasio and DM Sans (no Cormorant Garamond) and the test checks the flat section 2 tokens on `canvas`, `surface`, `band` and `night`, input borders and focus rings included, with no texture sample.
