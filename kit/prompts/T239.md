The task then completes `docs/phase2/war-games.md` as the report: the cast, the runs, every finding with its fix (commit) or open status, the money reconciliation, the credit-drain defense as proven (each drain persona's AI calls stayed within AI-021 and AI-028, measured), and the final run. Acceptance: `docs/phase2/war-games.md` records round 3: start and end times, the simulated business days and their wall-clock spans, completed jobs and how many were paid in Stripe test mode, conversations by persona class (every class of `wargames/personas.py` at least 3 times, abusers at least 40 conversations), every finding with its IDs and status, and the AI calls made per abusive conversation against the AI-028 budgets (from the `ai.call_stat` counts on the owner pages); at least 5 simulated business days and 100 completed jobs (40 paid) in this round, and at least 15 days and 300 jobs (120 paid) over the three rounds; the round finds no new finding of any severity, or the report says plainly that it did and which; the report has the cast, runs, findings, fixes and final-run sections; `python3 -m wargames reconcile` is green over every job of the campaign so far.
