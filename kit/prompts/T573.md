## Goal

Plan change D-4051 (Casey's outside code audit, item B2, Nov 16, 2026): business texts are queued and never sent. The business auto reply (`auto_reply`, `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs:1037`) stores the outbox row's `recipient` as the business ID, because `messaging::api::sender_business` (`app/messaging/src/service.rs:421`) answers only the business, while `businesses::api::may_text(business, phone)` and the notice worker (`app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs` `store_texts`) use the phone's line ID (D-2042 (1)); and `models::due` (`app/messaging/src/models.rs:372`) takes only `recipient_kind = 'homeowner'` rows, so the outbox sender (`app/messaging/src/outbox_sender.rs`, its doc `:18`) never sends a business row (D-3147 (1)). D-2042 said the gap must close before an outbox sender is registered, and one now is. This task closes it, before T526 turns business texting on from the stored switch (T526 depends on it): (1) `sender_business` (or a new `messaging::api` call beside it, exported by one line in the shared `api.rs`) also returns the linked phone's line ID, and `auto_reply` stores that line ID as the recipient; (2) `models::due` also takes queued business rows, and the sender gets a business row's number at send time through the phone reader of D-4913 (6), checks `may_text` for that business and phone at send time through a gate (a refused or unlinked phone expires the row with one `messaging.text_expired` audit row, as D-3149 does for a homeowner row that can never be sent), sends it on the business line (`mode::send_on_line`, `Audience::Business`; with the business switch off or no business number the row waits, TXT-022's rule for the homeowner line). `OutboxSender` (`app/messaging/src/outbox_sender.rs:67` to `:98`) has neither a gate nor a business number today (it carries only the homeowner `number`, and `:152` calls `load_lines(.., (number, None))`): this task adds a builder on `OutboxSender` that takes a gate implementing `messaging::api::SendChecks` (`app/messaging/src/business_notices.rs:79`, whose `may_text` is the check; a narrower trait for `may_text` alone is allowed) and an optional business `SendingNumber`, which `load_lines` then gets as its business number; `new` and `register_send_due`'s callers keep their signatures, so `tests/integration/tests/outbox.rs` passes unchanged, and without the builder no business row is sent (it waits); the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` composition passes `NoticeChecks` (`:1374`) as the gate, and production's business number stays `None` until T526 passes the configured one. This task's tests build the sender with a fake business number under the same quiet-hour, suppression and retry rules as a homeowner row; a row whose recipient is still a business ID (queued before this change) is expired, never guessed. It is a harness task because it changes `tests/integration/tests/inbound_first_contact.rs` (T256's and T317's), whose TD-BIZ-39 case pins the business ID as the recipient (`:294` to `:302`, D-2042 says so): that assertion, and only it, changes to the line ID.
