## Goal

TXT-027's who's-calling text has no owner (D-1687 took it out of T077; TD-TXT-29 and TD-QH-04 stay open), and `picks::release` says so (`app/picks/src/release.rs:31`) (fourth external audit F17, D-2181). Build it from D-1687's design. (1) Templates `whos_calling` and `whos_calling_email` in English and Spanish in the shared template files (drafts through the copy panel, WORD-028), with TXT-027's words, the picked business's public name from T316's `businesses::api::public_names` (a business with none gets the generic wording the copy panel approves) and the masked number; each listed once in `quotes`' `UPDATE_TEXTS` or `NOT_UPDATE_TEXTS` (`app/quotes/src/update_texts.rs`) and in `messaging`'s `IMPORTANT_TEMPLATES` (`app/messaging/src/retries.rs`), so TD-TXT-25's scan covers them and the text is retried as an important text. (2) `app/calls/src/whos_calling.rs`: `send_whos_calling` sends at once on an emergency job (QH-004), else in `messaging::api::send_window`'s window, else queues a `calls.whos_calling` task at the window's start, run by a handler registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`; an email-contact pick (PICK-018) gets the email words instead. (3) The release (`release.rs`, after T281) calls it in its transaction once the line is open, once per pick. (4) Fourth audit F23: `picks::service::find` (`app/picks/src/service.rs:223`) falls back to the first view (another job's number) when the job is not among the views and panics on an empty list; it returns `Option` and each caller treats a missing view as an error. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/whos_calling.rs` (TD-TXT-29, TD-QH-04) and `app/picks/src/tests/find_view.rs` (TD-PICK-53). Update the `calls`, `picks` and `quotes` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
