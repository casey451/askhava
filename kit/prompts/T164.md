# T164: quotes: one quote format per category, screened notes, and edit or withdraw before a pick

Task T164 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: quotes-uniform-notes.

## Goal

Every quote uses its category's `quote_format`; a quote's 300-character note goes back with the reason when it holds a contact detail or an off-platform phrase; and before any pick a business may edit (re-checked, shown as updated) or withdraw its quote (PICK-051 to PICK-053); the edit and withdraw controls and the format choice go on the business job page and quote form, rendered by the portals route `business_actions.rs` with `business_actions.html`, both extended here; `quotes::job_page` is data only (D-491, D-492). On the homeowner side this task only stores the change (D-2912): an edit through the existing upsert (`edited_at`, `quotes::api::submit_quote` through `models::upsert_quote`, re-checked) and a withdrawal by setting `withdrawn_at` from `edit_withdraw.rs`, never deleting the row; both run in one transaction that first takes the job's row lock (a `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the job row through a new public function of this task's in `jobs`' shared `api.rs`; a pick's status move updates that row, `app/jobs/src/service.rs:13`), then re-reads the status and `models::edit_facts` under it and refuses (`EditClosed`) once the job has left the statuses that take edits or any quote on it is marked unavailable, the edit calling `submit_quote` inside that same transaction and the withdrawal a guarded `UPDATE ... WHERE withdrawn_at IS NULL`, so neither can commit after a pick's move; a test shows a withdrawal and an edit after a pick's move refused, so the quote keeps its first `received_at` and its place; showing them to the homeowner ("Updated <time>" and "Withdrawn by the business" on the quote page, in the LIST reply and on the quote card) is T384's, which runs right after this task; `quote_card.rs` stays owned here only for this task's own format lines (TD-PICK-34's edit re-check, the withdrawal stored and no edit or withdrawal after a pick are tested here, D-492). T301 serves the email action links (D-2128): keep the signatures `app/server/src/action_links.rs` calls; any page or action this task adds is served by T306, not by a `.route` in `server`. Seventh audit N7-04 (D-2333; REC-010): the quote form asks a per-visit price's visit schedule (weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly, the frequencies `payments::rules::Period` converts) and requires it; any other schedule (for example twice a week) is offered as "Other: enter the monthly amount", which makes the quote a monthly price, as D-1407 has the form convert it, and `migrations/0157_quotes_formats.sql` stores it with the quote; T342 parses and bounces, T341 prices it at the pick. Tested in `tests/integration/tests/quote_formats.rs` (TD-PICK-57).
