# T164: quotes: one quote format per category, screened notes, and edit or withdraw before a pick

Task T164 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: quotes-uniform-notes.
Branch: `claude/task/T164-quotes-uniform-notes`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance, privacy, money, security).

## Goal

Every quote uses its category's `quote_format`; a quote's 300-character note goes back with the reason when it holds a contact detail or an off-platform phrase; and before any pick a business may edit (re-checked, shown as updated) or withdraw its quote (PICK-051 to PICK-053); the edit and withdraw controls and the format choice go on the business job page and quote form, rendered by the portals route `business_actions.rs` with `business_actions.html`, both extended here; `quotes::job_page` is data only (D-491, D-492). On the homeowner side an edited quote shows "Updated <time>" and a withdrawn one is replaced with "Withdrawn by the business" on the quote page (`quote_page.html`) and in the LIST reply and quote card text (`quote_card.rs`), both extended here (TD-PICK-34, D-492).
