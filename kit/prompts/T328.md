## Goal

Fifth external audit AH-001 (D-2286; FEE-018, FEE-041, QH-004): `payments` queues `payments.pick_ended` (`app/payments/src/handlers.rs:47`, queued at :381) when it ends a pick, but no handler exists, so the event is dead-lettered and the homeowner is never offered the next quote. Plan change D-3468 split T328 in two: this task builds the `picks` handler and registers it; T433 registers the seven `payments` kinds, seeds the reconcile and wires the late-success refund. (1) `app/picks/src/pick_ended.rs` handles `payments.pick_ended` (`{pick, business, job, reason}`) as FEE-018, FEE-020 and FEE-041 say: the pick is released and the homeowner is offered the next quote (the PICK-046 "couldn't take it this time" text and quote list, under quiet hours unless the job is an emergency, QH-004), once per pick. It is reached through `picks::api` (a `run_pick_ended` beside `run_card_fix_notice`), so nothing in it is dead code. (2) The shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` registers it in `register_handlers` under `askhava_payments::api::PICK_ENDED_KIND`, the way it registers `payments.card_needs_fix` to `askhava_picks::api::run_card_fix_notice`. (3) Tests in `tests/integration/tests/pick_ended.rs` (TD-PICK-54; log the D-2853 exception for this file in `docs/decisions.md`) run the production worker registry (`askhava_server::worker_registry` from dev stand-in settings, as `tests/integration/tests/emergency_notices.rs` does) against PostgreSQL: a `payments.pick_ended` event ends the pick's wait and offers the homeowner the next quote with the PICK-046 text, held to 8 a.m. at night for a regular job and at once for an emergency; a repeat of the event sends nothing twice. The `payments.pick_ended` entry of `tests/task_kinds_deferred.json` names T328 and is only printed as stale once this merges; that file is T327's test and is not edited here. Money code: the overseer runs an independent money review of the hand-in. Update the `picks` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`. (4) Plan change D-3579 (plan gap reported): `picks` holds no homeowner contact (`picks.pick`, `migrations/0079_picks_code.sql:18`) and may not call `intake` (`kit/layers.json`), so PICK-046's text goes the way `picks`' JOB-027 close text does (`app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs:414`): when the ended pick has no backup (`Backup::NoBackup`), `pick_ended.rs` queues one `jobs::api::TEXT_KIND` task with template `pick_lapsed`, the job's version and `values` `{job, pick, business}` (IDs only, never a contact), at `picks::api::lapse_plan`'s `send_at`, its dedupe key naming the pick. This task also owns `app/intake/src/job_texts.rs`: `pick_lapsed` joins `JOB_TEXT_TEMPLATES` with the `/q/` link; its body is `picks::api::lapse_text` (English and Spanish, with the Terms line) from the business's public name (`businesses::api::public_names`), the TXT-031 lines whose numbers `lapse_plan` keeps in `others` (the lapsed business's own never, PICK-035) and the issued quote-page link; its outbox subject is the job and the pick, so it goes once per pick; consent (CON-021), opt-out (CON-028) and the area-code check apply as to every job text; outside the window a regular job's text is queued again for the next open instant as today, and an emergency job's (the emergency choice read from the job at send time, never from the payload) goes at once through `messaging::api::enqueue_exempt` with `Exemption::EmergencyJob` (QH-004).
