# T061: payments: free first win for the first 90 days, entity keys, repeat customers

Task T061 (build, phase 1, stage 3). Slug: payments-free-wins.

## Goal

A business that joins within 90 days of launch or of its trade opening gets one free first win per entity key, checked when the win is used and recorded as a $0 ledger entry with the waived amount, and repeat work from a customer it was already introduced to is always $0 without using that win (FEE-010 to FEE-013).
