# T253: privacy, picks and portals: the signed-in business reads a released pick's details again

## Goal

Today a business sees a released pick's details only in the one answer `picks::api::release` gives its caller, and nothing stores them (D-1565): if the vault read fails after the claim commits, the response is lost or the page reloads, nothing shows them again (D-1564). Build the business portal's own read, through `picks` (architecture section 8: the business portal asks `picks.api`, never `privacy`), with no stored copy (PRIV-002) (D-1739). (1) Bind the release: `migrations/0129_picks_release_binding.sql` adds to `picks.release` the homeowner contact ID the proof named (`contact_id`, an ID only) and her PICK-018 choice of email contact (`email_chosen`); `release` writes both in its claim (`app/picks/src/models.rs`, `app/picks/src/release.rs`), on the emergency path too. (2) The proof: `privacy` adds `BusinessSessionProof` (private fields: the person and the business), made only by a new `SessionMint::business`, which T084's business session extractor in `app/portals/src/business_auth.rs` calls for a verified session, as the homeowner and owner realms do (SEC-046); business code still names no vault read. (3) The grant and the read: `Purpose::ReleasedView` and the grant `(Reader::Picks, Purpose::ReleasedView, [Name, Address, Email], "vault_read.picks.released_view")` in `GRANTS` (`app/privacy/src/models.rs`; no phone), and `privacy::api::read_released` (`app/privacy/src/released_reader.rs`) taking `&BusinessSessionProof`, the pick and the bound contact, audited per field with the person as actor and the pick as subject (PRIV-003), refused on a connection inside a transaction; add `("read_released", &["picks"])` to `READERS` in `tests/security/tests/vault_readers.rs` (T022's and T246's test, taken over here). (4) The rule, `picks::api::released_view(pool, vault, &BusinessSessionProof, pick)` (`app/picks/src/released_view.rs`): the proof's business is the pick's business; the person is now its owner or a "Confirm and billing" member, read through `businesses::api` (PICK-024 and BIZ-043 send the release only to them); the pick has a `picks.release` row and status `paid` or `free`; then it reads the bound contact's name and address, and the email only when `email_chosen`, and returns the first word of the name as `ReleaseView` does. Every refusal is one not-found answer that reads nothing and names nothing about the pick. Viewing is not an introduction: it writes no release row, notice, charge, fee or masked line, and may repeat while the pick stays paid or free and the vault holds the fields; after the PRIV-007 purge or "Delete my data" (T092) it answers that the details were removed. T085's `job_release.rs` renders it. Tests: `app/picks/src/tests/released_view.rs` (the pure refusal rule over every status, role and business pairing) and `app/server/tests/released_view.rs` (TD-PICK-48 against the database; `server` is the one test home allowed the session mint, `tests/integration/tests/architecture.rs:30`). Update `docs/architecture.md` sections 8 and 10, `app/privacy/CLAUDE.md` and `app/picks/CLAUDE.md`.
