## Goal

Nov 10 code review batch 2, jobs F2 (D-2640; JOB-003, JOB-006, OPS-004, PRIV-007): a job timer that runs out of attempts leaves its job stuck for good, with no alert and its contact never purged. `fail` (`app/core/src/tasks.rs:262`) dead-letters after `DEFAULT_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (5, about 7.5 minutes of backoff) and `reap_expired` (`:318`) on a last-attempt lease expiry; `run_timer` (`app/jobs/src/service.rs:236`) turns every error into a retry; the dedupe key `jobs.timeout.<job>.<version>.<timer>` (`:226`) stays on the dead row, so the timer can never be queued again; a job left in Quotes ready never closes, and `sweep_closed` never purges it; `jobs.homeowner_text` and `jobs.check_in` dead letters drop silently; T327 alerts only on `NO_HANDLER` dead letters. Fix: (1) after T327, its dead-letter alert (`app/server/src/no_handler_alert.rs`) covers every dead-lettered task: one High alert per kind and day with the kind and count, no payload; (2) `app/jobs/src/timer_backstop.rs`: a daily `jobs.timer_backstop` task (registered in the shared worker, queued once a day) finds each non-final job whose current timer task is dead-lettered or missing and revives it (clears `dead_at`, resets attempts, `run_at` now, in one statement on that row, or re-queues under a revival counter in the key), at most a bounded batch per run, with one `ops` audit row of IDs per revival; a timer that dies 3 times raises a High alert and is left for Casey. The per-kind dead-letter count (1) and the one-row revive (2) are new public functions in the shared `app/core/src/api.rs`, next to T327's `dead_without_handler` (`:46`), because `jobs` and `server` may name no other module's schema, `core.` included, in SQL (`tests/integration/tests/architecture_migrations.rs:44`, `:250`); `app/core/src/tasks.rs` is not needed and stays unchanged (D-3094). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/timer_backstop.rs` (TD-OPS-38), clock advanced: `run_timer` failing 5 times on a Quotes ready job dead-letters it and raises one alert; the backstop revives it; with the fault gone the job reaches Closed and its purge stages are queued; a final job's dead timer is not revived; TD-OPS-36 and TD-OPS-37 pass unchanged. ### 2. Principles (lines naming this task's requirements)
**P9. The queue table is hot: index it narrowly, keep transactions short, vacuum it harder.** PostgreSQL: skipping locked rows "provides an inconsistent view of the data, so this is not suitable for general purpose work, but can be used to avoid lock contention with multiple consumers accessing a queue-like table" (select.sgml text, commit https://forger.sitiv.fr/open-dsi/postgres/commit/df630b0dd5ea2de52972d456f5978a012436115e.diff). Dead tuples pile up when a long transaction holds back vacuum, and lock queries slow down scanning them (Brandur 2015); "A single transaction that takes 2 minutes to complete pins the horizon for the full 2 minutes" (PlanetScale). Autovacuum's defaults are threshold 50 and scale factor 0.2, overridable "for individual tables by changing table storage parameters" (autovacuum docs). ### 2. Principles that bind this codebase (lines naming this task's requirements)
**R11.
