## Goal

The homeowner portal signs in with phone and texted code (the Verify send caps are T089's, PICK-055, D-493), lets her review and change her properties, language and texts setting through realm-checked vault reads, and never collects SSNs, driver's licenses or tax ID images (PRIV-012, PRIV-021, SEC-046); encrypted backups are T109's and the 95% floor T056's (D-491). The portal offers "Download my data" (her requests, quotes, messages and consent records still held), and a delete or copy request is acted on only after a Verify code to the number on file, answered within 30 days (PRIV-031, D-492); the same rule for a request by email or the help form is T200's (D-493).
