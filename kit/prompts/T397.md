(4) Tests in `app/server/tests/phone_approval_route.rs` (TD-SEC-65) against the assembled router, fake numbers only. The page's `PAGE_STATES` entries (with and without a waiting phone) go in the shared `tests/a11y/page_states.ts` (D-1239, D-1788) and its three rows in `docs/architecture.md` section 13 are marked `yes`; its routes pass T348's doors unchanged (`tests/security/tests/auth_matrix.rs`, `app/server/tests/door_matrix.rs`), neither of which this task edits, and a route the probe cannot reach is reported, not worked around.
