## Goal

Register row IC-95, the owner's page (D-3081; BIZ-033, BIZ-038, SEC-038, SEC-004, SEC-009, SEC-034, SEC-042, SEC-048; D-311, D-480, D-1848, D-2440, D-2636). The owner has no page that lists a phone waiting for approval. (1) `app/portals/src/phone_approval.rs` and `app/portals/templates/phone_approval.html` (new): the owner's waiting-phones page; for each waiting phone, the sign-in email address of the member who set its number (a member has no stored name; the address is read from the vault in `server` by the member's `mailbox_id` with T412's `privacy::api::read_business_mailbox` under `BusinessPurpose::PhoneApproval` on a bare connection, D-1848, D-3262), the phone's last 4 digits (`number_end`) and the time its number was set (Arizona time, never a countdown), with an Approve and a Don't approve button, each its own form with the CSRF token and the phone's `number_set_at` as shown; a `POST` whose `number_set_at` no longer matches (a member changed the number since the page was shown) changes nothing and shows the page again with the new number's digits and a line saying the number changed; an empty state; works without JavaScript; route constants `PHONES_ROUTE` `/b/phones`, `APPROVE_PHONE_ROUTE` `/b/phones/approve` and `REJECT_PHONE_ROUTE` `/b/phones/reject` (the line in the form body, so T348's probe needs no path parameter). (2) `app/server/src/phone_approval.rs` (new) serves `GET /b/phones` and both `POST`s behind `portals::api::business_principal`: a signed-out request gets the 4xx answer with the `/b/sign-in` link; a member who is not the owner, another business's line and an unknown line get the one not-found answer and change nothing; every response is `no_store`; each `POST` passes `refuse_cross_site` and the CSRF check (a refusal is a security event), then `require_step_up` with `SensitiveChange::ApprovePhone` before the change's transaction (no fresh code: to the step-up page and back, nothing changed), then calls `approve_phone` or `reject_phone`; merged in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` and composed in `main.rs`. (3) Tests in `app/server/tests/phone_approval_route.rs` (TD-SEC-65) against the assembled router, fake data only. The page's `PAGE_STATES` entries (with and without a waiting phone) go in the shared `tests/a11y/page_states.ts` (D-1239, D-1788) and its three rows in `docs/architecture.md` section 13 are marked `yes`; its routes pass T348's doors unchanged (`tests/security/tests/auth_matrix.rs`, `app/server/tests/door_matrix.rs`), neither of which this task edits, and a route the probe cannot reach is reported, not worked around.
