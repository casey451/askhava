# T189: payments: Stripe Elements on the Confirm page, the card authorization, and the ReleaseProof from a verified event

Task T189 (build, phase 1, stage 3). Slug: payments-confirm-release-proof.

## Goal

Cards are collected only by Stripe Elements on the Confirm page (the page and the webhook route are T224's) and charged on-session when the business presses "Confirm and pay", with the authorization text and version stored; and `payments` alone builds a `ReleaseProof`, only from a signature-checked Stripe event whose livemode, account, amount and pick match, never from a redirect (FEE-014, FEE-015, FEE-036, PICK-021, PICK-022, PICK-033, JOB-014, SEC-030, SEC-039). Each successful charge writes its one FEE-042 evidence record (IDs and timestamps only, append-only, in this task's migration) at charge time, and the PaymentIntent carries the FEE-049 statement descriptor and the "Ask Hava introduction service, job <n>" description (TD-FEE-38, D-491). This task also writes payments' minter (a `VerifiedStripeEvent` for `payment_intent.succeeded`, or a no-fee decision, into a `ReleaseProof`) and the `register_handlers()` hookup in `app/server/src/lib.rs` that takes the mint once (D-488).

### 2. Principles that bind this codebase (lines naming this task's requirements)
**R2. Fields are private; constructors are the only way in.** Every struct with an invariant has private fields and a `parse`/`new` that checks it.
