(4) The mapping goes in a small private helper in `conversation.rs` taking `Result<Queued, IntakeError>` to `Result<Option<Queued>, IntakeError>` (`IntakeError::NoConsent` to `Ok(None)`, every other error stays `Err`), unit-tested in `app/intake/src/tests/conversation.rs`: `NoConsent` gives `None`, `Database` and `Outbox` stay `Err`, and a `Queued` passes through; before T857 no guard refuses this text, so these unit tests keep the branch covered (the `conversation.rs` coverage floor), and the end-to-end refusal is proven by T857's refused-text test (`docs/handoff/drafts/T857.md` point 11: `Some((Under18, None))` at 16 with the request closed, `Some((Under13, None))` at 12 with the data purged). Read docs/handoff/drafts/T872.md's 'Worker: read this first' header first; the goal and acceptance win over the draft.
