# T133: Resend and Lob test mode behind the adapters

Task T133 (harness, phase 2, stage 6). Slug: sandbox-resend-lob.

## Goal

The Resend and Lob test-mode clients sit behind their adapters with signed webhooks (Svix for Resend) and the signed inbound-email route into `privacy`, and the identity chain's email and letter steps and the email fallback for failed texts pass against them (BIZ-001 to BIZ-003, BIZ-016, TXT-014, TXT-015, SEC-039, PRIV-026). The fake Resend gains the suppression-list endpoints (list, and delete for one address), and the BIZ-056 re-verification calls the adapter's suppression removal (T176's) before writing its audit row; BIZ-056 moved here from T149 because the fake endpoint must exist first and T149 runs before this task (TD-BIZ-36, D-492). The call is added to `app/businesses/src/email_verification.rs` (T196's, extended here) and tested in `tests/integration/tests/email_reverify.rs` (D-493).
