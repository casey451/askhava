## Goal

A pest business is identified by the city list plus its Department license attestation, and each not-covered request sends one owner-inbox email with trade, town, request number and the month's count and no homeowner detail, (LIC-048, OPS-041); the weekly digest's roll-up is T204's. `intake` enqueues the not-covered event from `routing.rs` (extended here); a `businesses` handler (`coverage_notice.rs`) composes the coverage email and sends it through `messaging::api`, whose template sender is `coverage_email.rs`, as architecture section 12 says; the "What we cover now" page and the no-pest-advertising scans are TD-WEB-04's and TD-WEB-03's (D-491).
