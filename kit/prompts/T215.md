## Goal

A pest business is identified by the city list plus its Department license attestation, and each not-covered request sends one owner-inbox email with trade, town, request number and the month's count and no homeowner detail, (LIC-048, OPS-041); the weekly digest's roll-up is T204's. `intake` enqueues the not-covered event from `routing.rs` (extended here); a `businesses` handler (`coverage_notice.rs`) composes the coverage email and sends it through `messaging::api`, whose template sender is `coverage_email.rs`, as architecture section 12 says; the "What we cover now" page and the no-pest-advertising scans are TD-WEB-04's and TD-WEB-03's (D-491). Scope (plan change D-3055): TD-BIZ-32 and TD-LIC-22 are shared rows, and this task proves only the cases its files reach. In `tests/integration/tests/pest_identity.rs`: TD-BIZ-32's pest cases (a pest business activates on a city-list match plus the attestation, gets cards with no daily state check, and termite and weed-control jobs only with that attested category, through T374's `businesses::api::holding` and `licensing::api::eligibility`); the leaving, pause-reason, saved-card, session, link and business-name cases are T165's (DONE) and T228's; and TD-LIC-22's attestation cases (pest cards only to an attesting business, termite and pre-emergent only with the matching category) and its switch-off case in `routing.rs` (with pest control switched off, a pest request closes early with the fee-free pointer to the Department's license search). The homeowner's wrong-license report and its pause need a licensing writer and are T389's; the sign-up attestation checkbox is T193's (DONE). TD-OPS-18 and TD-INT-07 in `tests/integration/tests/coverage_email.rs`.
