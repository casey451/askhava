Nov 10 code review, messaging F3 and CON-016, the email half (D-2629; CON-016, CON-030, CON-009, D-226). (1) CON-030: while texting is off or suspended an opt-out is confirmed by email if one is on file, never by a later text; no code sends that email. `app/messaging/src/optout_email.rs` adds `messaging::api::queue_optout_confirmation_email(db, vault, contact, language, event)`: it reads her email through the vault (never logged), and when one is on file queues one email under the opt-out event as its subject through T094's email outbox (`send_email`), with the CON-009 wording as an email (English and Spanish), once per event. The route that calls both is T357's. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/optout_emails.rs` (TD-CON-34): with an email on file one confirmation email per event and none on a repeat; none without an email; a listed homeowner's message gives one email, a second the same day gives none, the next day one; without an email one audit row per message and no email; no body, phone or email address in any audit row or log line.
