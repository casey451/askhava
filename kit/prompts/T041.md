## Goal

`businesses` stores each business's status (pending, active, paused with a reason, waitlist, needs review, removed), towns, job-type checklist within its license, the "jobs nobody else covers" switch, the Spanish tick and the business-reported insurance answer (BIZ-004 to BIZ-006, BIZ-008, BIZ-009). It also stores BIZ-007's after-hours tick as a column and offers `businesses::api::night_count(trade, town)`, which counts the active, unpaused businesses in that trade and town with the tick; T150 later narrows that count with the per-person emergency-email switch and the test-email Yes (D-491).
