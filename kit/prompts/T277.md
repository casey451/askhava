# T277: intake and privacy: a check-in reply reaches its job, and a PROBLEM's scrubbed words are stored before the text is acknowledged

Task T277 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: check-in-replies.

## Goal

D-1810 says the owner digest reads a PROBLEM reply's scrubbed words from `messaging.inbox`, but that table holds only the Twilio SID and time (D-1801); `jobs::followups::Inbound` carries the inbox ID and the safety flag, and nothing calls `answer_check_in` (D-1811 (3)), so a PROBLEM's words would be acknowledged and lost (second external audit B04, D-1840). (1) `migrations/0171_privacy_message_body.sql` builds `privacy.message_body`, the table `docs/architecture.md` section 3 already names (D-801): one row per inbound text kept for a reason (here a check-in PROBLEM), the `messaging.inbox` row ID unique, the job ID, the scrubbed words only (never raw text, never a contact field), the safety flag and the created time; app role SELECT, INSERT and DELETE. (2) `app/privacy/src/message_body.rs`: `keep_problem_words(conn, inbox, job, &ScrubbedText, safety)` (it takes only a `ScrubbedText`, which only the scrubber makes, so raw words cannot be stored) and `problem_words(conn, &OwnerSessionProof, inbox)`, the owner-only read that the weekly digest (T204) and the owner portal use; no business or homeowner read exists; rows are deleted 30 days after they are made by a daily step registered in the shared worker (TXT-020) and with the job's PRIV-008 text stage. (3) `app/intake/src/check_in_replies.rs`, called from `receive_reply` (`app/intake/src/replies.rs`) inside its transaction for a reply on a Connected job with a check-in due: `jobs::api::parse_answer`, `businesses::api::has_safety_word`, then for a PROBLEM `keep_problem_words` before `answer_check_in`, all in the delivery's transaction, so the route answers Twilio only after the words are stored; a day-3 NO under email contact enqueues T281's `picks.unreachable_step` for the pick, due now and deduped per pick and answer, in the same transaction (`intake` may not call `calls`, whose call log the no-show needs, `kit/layers.json`), and T281's step records the no-show (FEE-035). The alert's subject stays the inbox row (D-1810), and the digest finds the words through it. `jobs`' `Inbound` and T097's tests are unchanged. D-3118: `keep_problem_words` takes a `ScrubbedText`, which only `privacy::api::scrub` makes (`app/privacy/src/scrub.rs:320`, `:383`), while `receive_reply` gets the words as `&str` (`TextIn`, `app/intake/src/service.rs:51`), already scrubbed by the inbound route with her vault name and stated names (`app/server/src/inbound_text.rs:342`); the check-in path makes its `ScrubbedText` by scrubbing those words again with `privacy::api::scrub` (the common words, no vault name, a fresh `NameMemory`), which can only remove more, so the stored words are never less scrubbed than what `intake` was handed; no route, `TextIn` or `receive_reply` signature changes. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/check_in_replies.rs` through the real inbound route with fictional texts. Update the `intake` and `privacy` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-JOB-12 passes against PostgreSQL through the served inbound route; every TD-JOB-04, TD-JOB-08 and TD-JOB-09 case and every T241 dispatcher case still passes; the `app/privacy/` 95% floor holds.
