## Goal

Sixth audit U-05 (D-2303; FEE-026, FEE-028, FEE-053): the app only ever asks Stripe for a full refund (`app/adapters/src/payments.rs:326`), but a refund Stripe reports `succeeded` is recorded at the charged amount whatever Stripe says it refunded: `Refund` carries `amount_cents` and `currency` (`app/payments/src/refund_stripe.rs:53`), `Refund::checked` (:67) compares neither, and `succeeded` and the ledger `append` in `app/payments/src/refunds.rs` (:376, :474) write `charged.amount`. Now, in `refunds.rs` (with a shared check in `refund_stripe.rs` that `reconcile` also uses, T334): a succeeded refund in `usd` whose reported amount is present and differs from the charged amount records the ledger refund at the amount Stripe reported (the ledger matches the money), raises one High `payments.refund_amount_mismatch` alert for that pick (only while none is open for it), and sends no "refunded in full" notice; a refund whose currency is present and not `usd` is treated as unconfirmed: it is recorded at the charged amount, never at the foreign figure, with one High `payments.refund_amount_unconfirmed` alert, and no "refunded in full" notice; a refund whose amount is missing is re-read once with `fetch_refund`, and if it is still missing it is recorded at the charged amount (the app only asks for full refunds) with the same unconfirmed alert, and its notice goes as today; a refund with an amount but no currency is handled as a missing amount (re-read once, then recorded at the charged amount with the unconfirmed alert, its notice going), never booked at the reported figure. The refund state machine and its keys are unchanged, so a duplicate event or retry records nothing twice. A harness task because T258's `tests/integration/tests/refund_lifecycle.rs` fakes must report the amount and currency a real full refund carries; their expected values do not change. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/refund_amounts.rs` (TD-FEE-59): full; $25 of a $75 charge; amount missing then present on the re-read; amount missing on both; currency `eur`; the same event twice. Money: the overseer runs an independent money review of the hand-in. Two places outside these files carry the check, as D-3018 records. (a) The webhook path: `refund_event` in `app/payments/src/routes.rs` (T259's) re-reads a `succeeded` refund with `fetch_refund` but hands `refund_succeeded` only the `re_…`, so a webhook-driven success would be booked at the charge unchecked; `refund_succeeded` now takes the re-read `Refund`, which counts as D-2303's re-read (a missing amount or currency is booked at the charge with the unconfirmed alert and nothing is fetched again). Its callers pass the `Refund` they hold: `refund_event`, and `run_refund_check` (`app/payments/src/refunds.rs:661`, the `Refund` it fetched); the owned `refund_lifecycle.rs` calls (:303, :305) change only to pass a `Refund`, and no expected value changes. In `routes.rs` only `refund_event` changes (with its `use` line and the `//!` and `///` lines that describe it); nothing else in that file changes. (b) The "refunded in full" notice is `picks`' `unreachable_refunded` text, queued by `decide_at_96_hours` (`app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs`) once `payments::api::refund_state` reads `Succeeded`. A new read `refund_in_full(pool, stripe, pick)` in `refunds.rs` (exported through `api.rs`) answers `false` only on positive evidence that the pick's succeeded refund is not the whole charge in `usd`: the ledger holds its refund at an amount other than the charge, or a re-read of its `re_…` with `fetch_refund` reports a currency other than `usd` or a `usd` amount other than the charge; it answers `true` when the re-read reports no amount or no currency, or fails with `AdapterError::NotSent` (what the provided `read_refund` answers, `app/adapters/src/payments.rs:189`, and so the test fakes), so a missing amount's notice goes as D-2303 says; any other read error is `Err(MoneyEventError::Stripe)`, never `true`, because a foreign-currency refund must not be announced and neither the ledger nor an alert tells it from a missing amount. In `unreachable_refund.rs` only `decide_at_96_hours` changes (a private helper and its doc lines may join it): it calls `refund_in_full` before `close_unreachable`, so an error returns before anything closes and the next run retries (the existing finish-on-next-run path), and it queues `unreachable_refunded` only when `refund_in_full` answers `true`; the job, the call line and the homeowner's text close and go as today, and on `false` no business text is queued (Casey has the alert); `refund_state`, `PickRefundState`, `refund_waiting` and `fraud_warning` are unchanged. Every existing test of these paths (`unreachable_refund.rs`, `unreachable_refund_states.rs`, `check_in_texts.rs`, `stripe_webhook_dispatch.rs`) passes unchanged. `refund_amounts.rs` also shows: `refund_succeeded` given a $25-of-$75 `usd` refund books $25 with the mismatch alert, as the request path does; `refund_in_full` answers each TD-FEE-59 case; and through `picks::api::decide_at_96_hours`, a $25-of-$75 refund and an `eur` refund close the job and queue no `unreachable_refunded`. Update the `payments` and `picks` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
