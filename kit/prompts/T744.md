## Goal

Plan change D-4748 (Fable's decision (4); FEE-021, PICK-038; D-4361, D-4349, D-1576). After T681, `jobs::service::move_to` queues one `jobs.final_status` task `{job, status}` when a job enters a final status and its handler in `app/picks/src/aftermath.rs` sends `no_longer_active` (`:319`); a pick still awaiting Confirm or in Payment pending on that job is left as it was, so its Confirm window, reminders and open PaymentIntent outlive the job and a late payment is caught only by the Left-and-refund path (FEE-021). This task, after T681: (1) the new `app/picks/src/final_status_end.rs` `end_for_final(tx, job, status, at)`, called by that handler in the same transaction before `no_longer_active`, sends every waiting, not-ended pick on the job (`awaiting_confirm` and Payment pending alike) through T663's `picks::api::end_pending` with a new reason `job_final` (added in `app/picks/src/connect.rs`), which ends the PaymentIntent through `payments`' own pick end so it is cancelled at Stripe and a charge that still succeeds is refunded (FEE-021, D-2958); an `awaiting_confirm` pick can have a PICK-048 auto-confirm charge in flight (`charge_off_session`, `app/picks/src/auto_confirm.rs:280`), and with no PaymentIntent the cancel is a no-op; its queued Confirm reminders and window end then act on nothing, as `cant_take` leaves them. `picks.pick.end_reason` is CHECK-constrained (`pick_end_reason_check`, last replaced by `migrations/0189_picks_fee_terms_changed.sql:7` to `:11`), so the new `migrations/0214_picks_job_final_end.sql` (after T745's 0213 and before T729's 0215 in the runner's serial order, which is why this task also waits for T745) replaces it the same way (NOT VALID, then VALIDATE) with every value the check allows on `main` at claim (re-read the newest migration that replaces it) plus `job_final`; re-check the number at claim against the migration-order rule; a pick already `paid`, `free` or ended is untouched; the job is not moved again (it is already final) and no backup is offered (the job is over). (1b) The handler first, before ending any pick or sending any notice, calls T573's `messaging::api::expire_job_rows(conn, job, closed_at)` in the same transaction, with `closed_at` the job's final-status time carried in the task's payload as `at` (D-5664; never the handler's own `at`, and never read from `jobs.status_history`, which `picks` may not query), so a text queued or held for the closed job before the close is expired, never sent, and a replayed delivery cannot expire the notices the first delivery queued (QH-007; Fable's texting audit, D-4764). (2) A second delivery of the same task finds nothing to end and sends nothing twice (the shared dedupe key, D-1576). The Left-and-refund path is unchanged and stays the backstop. Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/final_status_pick_end.rs`, PostgreSQL and the Stripe stand-in, fake data only: a job the homeowner closes with a pick awaiting Confirm ends the pick and no reminder or Confirm-end task acts after; a job cancelled in Payment pending cancels its PaymentIntent once and a late `payment_intent.succeeded` is refunded; a job closed while a PICK-048 auto-confirm charge is in flight on its `awaiting_confirm` pick, its PaymentIntent already stored (case A, D-5664), ends the pick, cancels that intent and refunds a charge that still succeeds; a paid pick is untouched; a replayed task changes nothing, and a `job_no_longer_active` row the first delivery queued and quiet hours held survives the replay. T681's `tests/integration/tests/aftermath_notices.rs` passes unchanged. Money: Opus worker, Opus money, privacy and security review, two review rounds. Update the `picks` notes, `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-5664 (outside pre-flight, each finding checked on `main` 0c7342d7; FEE-021, QH-007, PICK-048): (a) The close time travels in the task. The final-status payload is only `{job, status}` (`queue_final_status`, `app/jobs/src/final_event.rs:34`), `run_final_status` reads only `job` (`app/picks/src/aftermath.rs:371` to `:380`), and `picks` may not query `jobs.status_history` (`docs/architecture.md:305`). So this task also owns `app/jobs/src/final_event.rs` (its only other owner, T681, is DONE): the payload gains `"at"`, the same `at` that `apply` writes to `jobs.status_history` and passes to `queue_final_status` (`app/jobs/src/service.rs:172`, `:178`), as RFC 3339 text (`at.to_string()`, as `app/quotes/src/handlers.rs:57` does); the dedupe key is unchanged. The handler passes the parsed `payload["at"]` to `messaging::api::expire_job_rows` as `closed_at` (`app/messaging/src/outbox_sender.rs:506`). A payload with no `at` (queued before this change) or an unreadable one expires nothing and runs the rest of the handler as written (ends the picks, sends the notices), never falling back to the handler's own clock, which on a replay would expire the notices the first delivery queued; a test covers it. `tests/integration/tests/aftermath_notices.rs` reads only `job` and `status` and passes unchanged. (b) Two cases. Case A, the pick's PaymentIntent is stored in `payments.pick_intent`: `end_pending` ends it through `payments`' own pick end, so it is cancelled at Stripe and a success, stored before or recorded after the end, is refunded once; every pick test above is case A. Case B, no PaymentIntent stored yet, is out of scope here and is T689's (D-5664): with no intent row `payments`' pick end ends nothing (`end_pick`, `app/payments/src/handlers.rs:352`), and the off-session charge then creates and stores the intent itself (`app/payments/src/charge.rs:166` to `:174`) after an ended-pick check that reads only `pick_intent` (`pick_record`, `charge.rs:433` to `:452`), with `PickNow::Payable` hard-coded (`app/payments/src/off_session.rs:209`); the fix belongs in `app/picks/src/auto_confirm.rs`, which T689 (QUEUED) owns and which has no production caller until T689 wires it.

Acceptance: `tests/integration/tests/final_status_pick_end.rs` passes against PostgreSQL and the Stripe stand-in; `tests/integration/tests/aftermath_notices.rs` and every pick-ending test pass unchanged; no PaymentIntent stored in `payments.pick_intent` for a pick of a final job stays open (case A; case B, no intent stored at the close, is T689's, D-5664).
