Before relying on it, check that `quoted_job` in `business_portal_route.rs` issues a job number. Edit D-3664's gap line in `app/server/NOTES.md` in place (number real, calls post-launch); add the call log to the Post-launch list in `docs/for-casey-review.md`; update `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-5585 (job 34 pre-flight, checked on main fbf6b9f5d): `own_pick_job` (`app/server/src/business_portal.rs:988`) admits any member (`Need::Member`), while the page's own role check ("Confirm and billing" or the owner) runs later inside `picks::api::released_view` (`app/picks/src/released_view.rs:91`, through `job_release_page`), so before reading the job number the handler checks `who.may(business, Need::ConfirmAndBilling)` on the pick's business (from the same `pick_fee` read) and answers the one not-found answer otherwise; no job or number is read for a cards-only member or another business, and the release page is rendered once. `quoted_job`'s `create_job` (`jobs::api::create`) already issues a job number (`app/jobs/src/service.rs:58`).
