## Goal

`scripts/check_copy.py` measures each section of the six public legal drafts (homeowner terms, business terms, privacy policy, SMS terms, business email notice terms and accessibility statement) and fails a homeowner-document section above Flesch-Kincaid grade 9 or a business-document section above grade 10, sentences a requirement fixes word for word excepted; the over-grade sections are rewritten in shorter, plainer sentences without changing any clause's meaning or any phrase `scripts/check_legal_docs.py` lists, so both scripts pass; `tests/test_check_copy.py` plants an over-grade section for each audience and an excepted fixed sentence (WORD-031). The security program, the incident plan and the README are internal and not measured (D-492).
