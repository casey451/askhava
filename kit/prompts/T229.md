Meaning rule, closed: a rewritten sentence that changes who acts, what is allowed, required or promised (may / must / will / never / only), an amount, a time, a condition or an exception is a finding; word choice alone is a note. Passing the closed scans is the wording bar; an off-list phrasing that no file in scope contains is a note under 'Scan list notes (not findings)', not a finding (D-4038).
