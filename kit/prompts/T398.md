## Goal

Register row IC-95, the gate behind the hold (D-3081; BIZ-033, CALL-012; D-1695, D-3079). T396 keeps a waiting phone's digests out of the sender directory; this task makes both routes check the phone's standing as well, so a digest left in the directory by any path still cannot act as the business, and gives the pure `calls::lifecycle::phone_works` and `gate` (`app/calls/src/lifecycle.rs:241`, `:248`, today called only by tests) their production caller. (3) Tests in `tests/integration/tests/phone_approval_routes.rs` (TD-CALL-25), signed fake Twilio voice and text requests through the served routes and `calls::api::answer_call`, fake numbers only. Update the `calls` and `server` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
