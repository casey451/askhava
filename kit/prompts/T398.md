(2) `app/server/src/inbound_text.rs`: a text whose sender the directory names is the business's (`Relation::Business`: `phone_stopped`, the auto-reply, T391's LINK reply) only when `phone_standing` is `Some(true)`; otherwise it takes the path of an unknown number, so a waiting phone's STOP, LINK or other text never acts for the business. (3) `app/businesses/src/channel_preference.rs`: `notice_recipients` (`:142`, the one list every business notice text reads its phones from, `quotes::card_notices` and `quotes::emergency_card_notice` among them) lists only phones with `approved_at` set, so a waiting phone gets no job card or other notice text and a member whose only phones are waiting gets the email instead (its `email` flag follows the filtered list). (4) `app/businesses/src/complaints.rs`: `phone_link_notice` (`:502`) keys its task by phone and `number_set_at` (`businesses.phone_linked.<line>.<number_set_at in microseconds>`, was the phone alone, `:521`) and adds `number_set_at` to the payload, and `businesses::api::change_phone` (the shared `api.rs`) wraps `team::change_phone` as `api::link_phone` wraps the link: a number change by a member who is not the owner queues a fresh owner email in the same transaction, so the owner is told of every number they are asked to approve. (5) Tests: `tests/integration/tests/phone_approval_routes.rs` (TD-CALL-25), signed fake Twilio voice and text requests through the served routes and `calls::api::answer_call`, and `tests/integration/tests/phone_approval_notices.rs` (TD-BIZ-53) through `businesses::api` and `quotes`' card notice step, fake numbers only.
