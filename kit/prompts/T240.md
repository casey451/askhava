# T240: intake: the homeowner's job texts when a request moves on by itself

Task T240 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: homeowner-job-texts.
Branch: `claude/task/T240-homeowner-job-texts`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance, privacy).

## Goal

`intake` registers and runs the `jobs.homeowner_text` handler (D-871, D-1030 (A)) in `app/intake/src/job_texts.rs`, so the texts that `jobs` and `quotes` already queue reach the homeowner: the 6 p.m. move of an Open job to No quotes yet (`no_quotes_yet`, JOB-010), the 5-day close with no quotes (`closed_no_quotes`, JOB-011), the 3-day close with no pick (`closed_no_pick`, JOB-012) and every quote window ended on a Quotes ready job (`all_quotes_in`, TXT-042). For each event it finds the request and homeowner from the job ID (the `job.<trade>.<property>` intake answer whose choice is the job ID, D-993; its own queries in this file), skips a job whose status moved on since the event's version, a contact without a consent record (CON-021) and an opted-out contact, whose job carries on by email and the quote page instead (CON-028), renders the template in her language with the request number and, for TXT-042, `quoted`, `count` and her quote-page link issued at send time by `jobs::api::quote_token::issue` (T014's, the URL T052's page serves; never in a payload or a log, SEC-037, TXT-040), and queues one outbox row under the event's dedupe key. Quiet hours use both zones: `messaging::api::send_window` (new, `app/messaging/src/send_window.rs`) reads the recipient's phone from the vault inside `messaging` and returns the next instant inside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in both Arizona and the area-code zone by `rules::decide` (QH-001, QH-006), so intake never sees the number (the vault grants `messaging` and `calls`, never `intake`, D-1196); a text outside it is queued again for that instant, and a held close text is still sent while any other held text of a closed job is dropped (QH-007). Registers the handler in `app/server/src/lib.rs` (shared). The 24-hour reminder and the INT-019 close keep T048's Arizona-only `texting_window`; switching them to `send_window` is listed for the plan, not done here (D-1215).
