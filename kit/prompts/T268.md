## Goal

T082 built the business consent record and its gates, but D-1715 left their callers unowned: the sign-up form never shows `consent_box` or posts to `record_consent`, activation never calls `request_welcome`, and no handler sends the `businesses.welcome` or `businesses.phone_stopped` email, so a business can be activated with no written E-SIGN consent recorded and never gets the CON-020 welcome (D-1788). (1) `app/portals/templates/business_signup.html` and `app/portals/src/business_signup.rs`: the form shows `businesses::api::consent_box` for the chosen channels, unticked (CON-019, PICK-049's no-pre-ticked rule), and posts it to `record_consent` in the sign-up's transaction; a sign-up without the tick is not submitted and says why. (2) `app/businesses/src/identity.rs` (after T196): activation (`settle`, `approve`) calls `request_welcome` in the same transaction. (3) `app/messaging/src/business_account_emails.rs`: the `businesses.welcome` handler sends the CON-020 welcome as an email whose body is `businesses::api::welcome_lines` (`app/businesses/src/consent.rs:341`, T082's, with T199's card link) in the shared email layout (`app/messaging/templates/email_layout.html`), never a text, so it goes out while the business-texting switch is off (TXT-035), with the contact-card file and calls `welcome_sent` after the provider accepts; the `businesses.phone_stopped` handler sends `stopped_email_line` with the last 4 digits read from the vault to the owner's account address; both registered in the shared `app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`, deduped per business and kind. Update the `businesses`, `messaging` and `portals` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-CON-29 passes against PostgreSQL and the stand-ins; every existing sign-up, consent and identity case still passes; `Ally` is green on the sign-up page with the box.
