## Goal

The real-texts gate (T130, gate.conditional true) clears on either 'yes' or 'no': the answer is recorded on the gate and passed to its dependent task, so 'no' means T131 uses Twilio's test credentials and magic numbers only and lists what it could not check, and the gate's message says 'no' is a fine answer. Today only 'yes' clears it, so a 'no' would hold T131 and everything after it forever (found by the paper rehearsal, D-472). Other gates keep their exact phrases.

Acceptance: A conditional gate clears on a short, unnegated "yes" or "no", the answer is recorded on the gate and passed to its dependent task, every other gate still needs its exact phrase, and `python3 -m pytest tests/test_runner_logic.py` passes with the new cases.
