## Goal

Plan change D-4349 (CALL-001, CALL-009, JOB-015, JOB-003; D-807, D-1688, D-2017, D-2930, D-4338). Split from T663 for size. (1) Reconnect line: `retry_line` (`app/picks/src/settle.rs:175`, the `picks.line_retry` task a failed release line queues, D-1688) returns early whenever `calls::api::job_had_line` finds any line the job ever had (`settle.rs:198`, `app/calls/src/api.rs:82` to `:93`), so once T663 lets a job reach Connected, a job connected, returned under JOB-015 (no call in 72 hours), re-picked and connected again never gets its failed second line retried, though CALL-001 gives each connected job a line. It now skips only when this pick's own line exists: a line of the release row's job, business and contact assigned at or after the release's `released_at` (a new `calls::api` read in `app/calls/src/pick_line.rs`, exported through the shared `calls::api`; `calls.number_pool` has no pick column; `job_line`, `app/calls/src/api.rs:104`, already keys on job, business and homeowner). Keying on job and business alone would reopen a line closed for the same business's earlier connection (CALL-009). T662's final-status check and D-2017's paid-or-free check stay. The one-open-line-per-job index (`migrations/0114_calls_pool.sql:32`) means the earlier line must be closed first (T662's close or the no-show path, `app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs:407`); while it is open the retry fails and the worker retries it, then dead-letters it with the release's High alert. (2) Connected's start: the release's claim (`check_ins_at_release`, `app/picks/src/release.rs:517`) restarts the Connected wait with `jobs::api::restart_timer` (D-807 names JOB-015's later of release and visit as a restart event) from the instant its check-ins count from (the later of the release and the earliest visit), so a release that waited for the address (an emergency job) or a later visit date does not shorten Connected's 21 days (`app/jobs/src/status.rs:130`); a restart never shortens a wait, and a job no longer Connected is left alone. Tests in the new `tests/integration/tests/reconnect_line.rs` (TD-CALL-28), PostgreSQL, fake data and numbers only. `release_recovery.rs` (`:642` to `:669`, `:738` to `:770`), `whos_calling.rs`, `line_close_paths.rs`, `address_rerelease.rs`, `check_in_texts.rs`, `followups.rs` and T663's `connect_moves.rs` pass unchanged; `app/picks/src/settle.rs` stays at or above its 95% branch floor and `release.rs` at or above 81% (`tests/coverage_floor.json`). After T663 (both own `settle.rs`). Cite by name, the line numbers above are stale: `retry_line` and its `job_had_line` call in `app/picks/src/settle.rs`, `job_had_line` and `job_line` in `app/calls/src/api.rs`, `check_ins_at_release` in `app/picks/src/release.rs`; `release_recovery.rs` is `app/server/tests/release_recovery.rs` (tests `td_pick_50_a_failed_line_opens_on_the_retry_and_never_reopens`, `td_pick_49_a_failed_vault_read_recovers_and_shows_once`).
