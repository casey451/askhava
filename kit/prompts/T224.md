## Goal

The Confirm review step is a portals page (`confirm_pay.html`, `confirm_pay.rs`) that shows the job number, fee, tier, basis, refund line, terms version and one "Confirm and pay $<fee>" button, loads Stripe.js only on this page (TD-SEC-04) and collects the card through Stripe Elements (`confirm_pay.ts`, compiled by `tsc`), with the unticked save box and its authorization text; the pick's one PaymentIntent is created on the first page load through `payments::api` (FEE-047), a 3-D Secure step completes in the page, a decline shows at once and the next press uses the next attempt key; and `app/payments/src/routes.rs` is the signature-checked Stripe webhook route that hands verified events to T189's minter, so nothing is released from a redirect (PICK-021, TD-FEE-25). The charge rules are T062's and the `ReleaseProof` minter T189's; this task builds the page and the route that call them (D-491). At Confirm the page passes the pick's business to `payments::api` so the free first win (T061) is checked on its entity keys; a fee-free job type shows "No introduction fee for this job." (FEE-045); and a job paused by the homeowner (CON-029) refuses Confirm with no charge or release (TD-FEE-37, D-491). The e2e signs a business person in by seeding a session through the test-control router, since business sign-in is T084's; T084 later adds principal scoping to this route (`confirm_pay.rs`) so only the owner or a "Confirm and billing" person can complete it (PICK-033, D-492). The route also enforces SEC-039's signature check and SEC-049's address rule (TD-SEC-31); the Confirm-page fee-free, save-box, authorization-version and fee-limit cases are TD-FEE-40's (D-492).
