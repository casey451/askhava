# T197: picks: Confirm by email alone with the 4-business-hour window, reminders, the end-time text and lapse re-pick

## Goal

A pick is confirmed from the pick email alone within 4 business hours, with a reminder email every business hour carrying the Hold link; the homeowner is told the end time; and a lapsed pick sends her the other quotes to re-pick with "<number> YES" (BIZ-039, PICK-019, PICK-033, PICK-037, PICK-043 to PICK-046); it also adds the PICK-019 all-bounced grace to `confirm.rs`, and when a phone other than the owner's confirms a pick the owner gets the BIZ-033 email (D-491).
