## Goal

Plan change D-3753 (split from T106): the owner portal's health page shows every OPS-008 figure and offers the OPS-009 monthly spot-check (its cadence the setting `ASKHAVA_SPOT_CHECK_EVERY_DAYS`, default 30, D-4280) of 10 random job cards. `app/portals/src/health_page.rs` renders `templates/health.html` from T475's readers (`messaging::api`, `quotes::api`, `payments::api`, `ai::api`) only, computing no figure itself: text and email rates per audience, sending-limit use (T475's counts against the daily and monthly email limits, settings the serving route passes in), the OPS-037 medians and the 6-hour share, the share of this month's cards that ended without a quote, payment errors, AI errors, uptime, last backup, and each required secret as present, parsed and environment-matched beside the SEC-036 identifiers, never its value, length or hash (D-492), and no business-call rate (D-331, D-344). Uptime is the web process's time since start and the worker state `/healthz` reports (T106), and the secret report is T304's `SecretReport`: both come in as page inputs from the serving route, since `portals` cannot read `Settings` or the process; no uptime history is stored, so UptimeRobot's own 30-day figure is linked, not copied. The last-backup figure shows 'not yet recorded' until T109 replaces it with `ops::api::latest_audit` (D-493). The spot-check picks 10 random cards through `quotes::api` and shows only what the owner's card view shows. T304 serves the page (D-2128): the page function and route constant only, no `.route` in `server`. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/health_page.rs`: the page from planted data shows every OPS-008 figure (TD-OPS-02's page half, TD-OPS-12's medians, TD-OPS-10's health-page figures), the spot-check gives 10 distinct cards of the month, and a sentinel secret's value, length and hash appear nowhere in the page. Review checklist (D-3753): the secrets readout of `docs/brains/deploy-operations.md` (item 6, SEC-002, SEC-036, OPS-008) holds, each secret shown only as present, parsed and environment-matched. Update the `portals` `CLAUDE.md` or `NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`. Plan change D-3844 (the overseer's pre-claim scout, Nov 15, 2026): `SecretReport` does not exist before T304 and `portals` may not depend on `server` (`kit/layers.json`), so this task defines the page's own plain input in `health_page.rs`, one line per required secret: its name and three flags (present, parsed, environment-matched), never a value, length or hash; T304 converts its `SecretReport` into that list. The spot-check's 10 random cards come from T475's `quotes::api::spot_check_cards`. Plan change D-3882: the input also carries the four SEC-036 account identifiers (Twilio Account SID, Stripe account ID, Resend sending domain, bucket name; not secrets), passed in by the route. Plan change D-4280 (the Opus pre-claim scouts of origin/main 25b746d1, Nov 18, 2026, each finding re-checked on `main`): "monthly (a setting)" named no key.
