## Goal

A message opens a job only when it is more than two words or holds an emergency keyword and is not a cancel-like message; one active job exists per homeowner, property and trade; a follow-up within the INT-010 window joins the open job (the after-2-hours same-trade rule is T042's, D-493); answers count only for open jobs and match exactly; the name and address come last; an ALSO reply's second job is opened through this task's job-opening path (the two-trade detection and reply are T042's, D-493); and an under-18 sender gets the INT-038 reply (INT-007 to INT-012, INT-027, INT-032, INT-038); INT-032's SPLIT offer needs the license match and is T184's (D-491).

## What this task rests on

Requirements (each sentence as in `docs/spec/`):

- **INT-007** A message of one or two words never opens a new job (for example "ME", a bare ZIP code or "ok sure"), except a message with an emergency keyword, which opens a job whatever its length (D-138). (`docs/spec/intake.md:11`)
- **INT-008** A cancel-like message never opens a new job. (`docs/spec/intake.md:12`)
- **INT-009** At most one active job exists per homeowner, property and trade. (`docs/spec/intake.md:13`)
- **INT-010** A second message from the same phone within 2 hours of the open job's last homeowner message (a setting, default 2 hours) joins that job even if it is read as a different trade; after that window, a message read as the same trade joins the open job only if at least half of its words, leaving out common words such as "the", "my" and "is" (a setting, default 50%), also appear in the job's scrubbed description, and otherwise it opens a new job (D-485). (`docs/spec/intake.md:14`)
- **INT-011** An answer to an intake question counts only for a job that is still open, and is matched exactly (so "ok" inside "broke" is not an answer). (`docs/spec/intake.md:15`)
- **INT-012** Word answers to intake questions are accepted (for example "the AC one", "Yes!", a first name, or "my email is ...") (D-103). (`docs/spec/intake.md:16`)
- **INT-027** The homeowner's first name and street address are asked as the last intake question, after consent and the area check (for a job the homeowner chose as an emergency, right after the job cards go out, INT-029), the question says "Only the business you pick gets this, after you pick it. We never ask about immigration status or ID.", and that answer is stored only in the vault and never sent to the AI (D-146, D-164, D-238). (`docs/spec/intake.md:31`)
- **INT-032** A first message that names two trades starts a job for the first and replies "Laftly: That's 2 jobs. We'll start with <first>. Reply ALSO to start <second> too.", and a reply of ALSO (or TAMBIÉN) starts the second job; and when no business's license covers every candidate job type of one request (CARD-001), the homeowner gets "Laftly: This needs 2 kinds of business. Reply SPLIT to send it as 2 requests, one per trade." (DIVIDIR in Spanish), and no card goes to a business that covers only part of the job (D-246, D-393). (`docs/spec/intake.md:36`)
- **INT-038** A message or web chat in which the sender gives their own age as under 18 (for example "I'm 15", "I am 16 years old", "tengo 15 años") or says they are in middle school, junior high or high school gets one reply, "Laftly: Sorry, you must be 18 or older to use Laftly. Please ask a parent or adult to text us." (Spanish "Laftly: Lo sentimos, debe tener 18 años o más para usar Laftly. Pídale a su madre, a su padre o a otro adulto que nos escriba.", D-5222), its request closes without any card, and when the stated age is under 13 the request and its data are deleted except the consent or opt-out record; a message about someone else's age ("my 15-year-old broke the door") is a normal request; an emergency keyword is still handled as INT-002 says first (D-385, D-485). (`docs/spec/intake.md:42`)
