## Goal

The business portal shows how the homeowner chose to be contacted, the "Your numbers" panel, the released job's call log, and asks for a fresh Verify code before any sensitive change (BIZ-013, BIZ-025, BIZ-035, SEC-038). The released job's portal view (`job_release.rs`, `job_release.html`) prints the homeowner's first name once with the PICK-024 tip, never her number, and an email-contact release's line (TD-PICK-39, D-491). Each person chooses their open-cards email times in a section of the portal home (`business_portal.rs`, `business_home.html`; CARD-022's setting, D-491, D-492), stored per person in `businesses.open_cards_time` through `app/businesses/src/open_cards_times.rs`; T198's composer reads them through `businesses::api` (D-491). The step-up checks are tested in `tests/security/tests/step_up.rs` and the per-person times table in `app/businesses/src/tests/open_cards_times.rs` (D-492).
