## Goal

Casey decided on November 8 that the brand may change from "Ask Hava" (likely to "Laftly") before launch, so the brand becomes data instead of hundreds of literals; the value stays "Ask Hava" until he decides, and the rewritten copy for a new name comes after the build (D-1932). This task adds the one source and makes the build's own checks read it; T292 to T294 move the app's copy onto it and T295 proves a switch (D-1933). (1) `app/core/data/brand.json`, compiled in with `include_str!`: `display_name` "Ask Hava", `business_name` "Ask Hava for Business", `short_name` "Ask Hava" (every text begins `<short_name>:`, WORD-008), `legal_name` "Ask Hava LLC", `spoken_legal_name` "Ask Hava L L C" (said by the voice line), `domain` "askhava.com", `email_from_name` "Ask Hava", `email_from_address` "jobs@notify.askhava.com", `statement_prefix` "ASKHAVA" (the Stripe account's descriptor prefix, FEE-049), `forbidden_alone` ["Hava"] (WORD-021), and `es` holding the Spanish `display_name`, `business_name`, `short_name` and `legal_name` (today the same words). ## Output discipline

Every turn re-reads earlier tool output; large results cost tokens on every later turn (D-3305). Never omit failure lines to save tokens.

1. For a file over 200 lines (`wc -l`), never `cat` or `Read` it whole. Use `grep -n` with at most `-C` 3, then `sed -n 'A,Bp'` or Read with offset/limit: at most 120 lines, or 250 if one read replaces two. Don't re-read unchanged files or regions; use earlier output.
2. Log build, test and lint output; print only decisive lines. Example: `cargo test -p <crate> >/tmp/t.log 2>&1; echo exit=$?; grep -nE 'FAILED|panicked|^error|test result' /tmp/t.log | head -n 60; tail -n 15 /tmp/t.log`.
