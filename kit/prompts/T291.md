## Goal

Casey decided on November 8 that the brand may change from "Ask Hava" (likely to "Laftly") before launch, so the brand becomes data instead of hundreds of literals; the value stays "Ask Hava" until he decides, and the rewritten copy for a new name comes after the build (D-1932). This task adds the one source and makes the build's own checks read it; T292 to T294 move the app's copy onto it and T295 proves a switch (D-1933). (1) `app/core/data/brand.json`, compiled in with `include_str!`: `display_name` "Ask Hava", `business_name` "Ask Hava for Business", `short_name` "Ask Hava" (every text begins `<short_name>:`, WORD-008), `legal_name` "Ask Hava LLC", `spoken_legal_name` "Ask Hava L L C" (said by the voice line), `domain` "askhava.com", `email_from_name` "Ask Hava", `email_from_address` "jobs@notify.askhava.com", `statement_prefix` "ASKHAVA" (the Stripe account's descriptor prefix, FEE-049), `forbidden_alone` ["Hava"] (WORD-021), and `es` holding the Spanish `display_name`, `business_name`, `short_name` and `legal_name` (today the same words). The server's and worker's start-up (`app/server/src/main.rs`, `app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`, shared paths) checks and installs the embedded brand before anything renders and stops naming the field. (3) `scripts/brand.py`: `load()` and `fill(text, spanish=False)` over the same file, tokens and checks. `scripts/check_legal_docs.py` builds every phrase that names the brand from it (for example `f"{display_name} is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work."`) and its WORD-021 rule from `forbidden_alone` and `display_name`; `scripts/check_catalog.py`'s safety-prompt rule requires `<short_name>:` after filling; `scripts/pii_scan.py` allows the brand's `domain` where it allowed the literal; `tests/docs/test_template_scan.py` fills each template's tokens before its checks, requires `<short_name>:` and allows links only on `domain` (the ROC link `<domain>/help/roc-complaint`).
