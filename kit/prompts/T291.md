## Goal

Casey decided on November 8 that the brand may change from "Ask Hava" (likely to "Laftly") before launch, so the brand becomes data instead of hundreds of literals; the value stays "Ask Hava" until he decides, and the rewritten copy for a new name comes after the build (D-1932). This task adds the one source and makes the build's own checks read it; T292 to T294 move the app's copy onto it and T295 proves a switch (D-1933). (1) `app/core/data/brand.json`, compiled in with `include_str!`: `display_name` "Ask Hava", `business_name` "Ask Hava for Business", `short_name` "Ask Hava" (every text begins `<short_name>:`, WORD-008), `legal_name` "Ask Hava LLC", `spoken_legal_name` "Ask Hava L L C" (said by the voice line), `domain` "askhava.com", `email_from_name` "Ask Hava", `email_from_address` "jobs@notify.askhava.com", `statement_prefix` "ASKHAVA" (the Stripe account's descriptor prefix, FEE-049), `forbidden_alone` ["Hava"] (WORD-021), and `es` holding the Spanish `display_name`, `business_name`, `short_name` and `legal_name` (today the same words). (2) `app/core/src/brand.rs`, re-exported through `core::api`: `Brand` (typed, `deny_unknown_fields`); `Brand::embedded()` parses and checks that file (every field non-empty; `short_name` at most 20 characters with no `:`; `legal_name` contains `display_name`; `domain` a lower-case host name; `email_from_address` at `domain` or a subdomain of it; `statement_prefix` 5 to 10 upper-case letters or digits with at least one letter, and short enough that `<statement_prefix>* JOB ` plus a 7-digit job number fits the card statement's 22 characters, FEE-049), naming the bad field in its error; `install(Brand)` sets the process's brand once (a second call is an error), for the binaries' start-up and for tests; `brand()` returns `&'static Brand`, the installed one or else the embedded one, with no panic path the workspace lints refuse; `Brand::fill(text, spanish)` replaces the tokens `{brand}`, `{brand_business}`, `{brand_short}`, `{brand_legal}` and `{domain}` (Spanish names from `es` when `spanish`) and leaves every other `{name}` placeholder alone, and an unknown `{brand...}` token is an error. The server's and worker's start-up (`app/server/src/main.rs`, `app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`, shared paths) checks and installs the embedded brand before anything renders and stops naming the field. (3) `scripts/brand.py`: `load()` and `fill(text, spanish=False)` over the same file, tokens and checks. `scripts/check_legal_docs.py` builds every phrase that names the brand from it (for example `f"{display_name} is not a contractor and does not perform, bid on or supervise work."`) and its WORD-021 rule from `forbidden_alone` and `display_name`; `scripts/check_catalog.py`'s safety-prompt rule requires `<short_name>:` after filling; `scripts/pii_scan.py` allows the brand's `domain` where it allowed the literal; `tests/docs/test_template_scan.py` fills each template's tokens before its checks, requires `<short_name>:` and allows links only on `domain` (the ROC link `<domain>/help/roc-complaint`). Under the brand as it is, each check accepts and refuses exactly what it does today: `tests/test_check_legal_docs.py`, `tests/test_check_catalog.py`, `tests/test_pii_scan.py` and the template scan's own planted cases pass unchanged and none of them is edited. Exempt by name in the test, each with its reason: `payments`' `AUTHORIZATION_TEXT` (a legal record stored under `AUTHORIZATION_VERSION`) and `app/ai/data/word_022_phrases.json` (it mirrors `scripts/trace_spec.py`'s spec patterns, D-493); T295 tests that both name the brand.
