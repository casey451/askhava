# T188: payments: the 4-month rule for recurring quotes and its $75 cap

## Goal

A recurring quote in a recurring category is priced as 4 months of service (weekly ×52÷12, every two weeks ×26÷12, yearly ÷12) with the $75 recurring cap, one-time jobs in recurring categories at face value, and a period refused in a non-recurring category (REC-001 to REC-006, REC-010, REC-011, FEE-039, FEE-040).
