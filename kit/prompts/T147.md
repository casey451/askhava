# T147: messaging and businesses: business notices by chosen channel and the business-texting switch

Task T147 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: business-notices-email.
Branch: `claude/task/T147-business-notices-email`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security, compliance).

## Goal

Every business notice goes by the business's chosen channels, email by default and text only while the business-texting switch is on, never carrying homeowner details by text; job cards go to the owner and every "Cards and quotes" member; texts that fail fall back to email and the portal; and business quiet times hold texts, not emails (TXT-014, TXT-035 to TXT-037, BIZ-019, BIZ-036, BIZ-037, CARD-020, QH-005); every recipient address comes from `businesses::api::notice_addresses` (T149 adds the backup addresses there, D-491).
