# T147: messaging and businesses: business notices by chosen channel and the business-texting switch

Task T147 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: business-notices-email.

## Goal

Every business notice goes by the business's chosen channels, email by default and text only while the business-texting switch is on, never carrying homeowner details by text; job cards go to the owner and every "Cards and quotes" member; texts that fail fall back to email and the portal; and quiet hours hold each business notice as a whole, email and any text together (TXT-014, TXT-035 to TXT-037, BIZ-019, BIZ-036, BIZ-037, CARD-020, QH-005); `messaging` never calls `businesses`: the enqueuing module (`quotes` for cards, in `app/quotes/src/card_notices.rs` owned here; `picks` for pick and release notices; `businesses` for account notices; `picks`' `money_notices.rs` for the payments notices, T062 and T068) resolves the recipients and channels through `businesses::api::notice_addresses` and puts them in the event payload as opaque address IDs, and `messaging` sends to what it is given (T220 created that lookup; this task extends it with each person's channel choice, and T149 extends it with the backup addresses, D-491, D-492). The card notices' provider acceptance feeds `quotes`' sent count through `card_notices.rs`, so the cards-out text's N and the job page count leave out a business whose every notice hard-bounces (TD-TXT-22, D-491). Card emails carry no image bytes, only a link to the signed-in job page (PRIV-027's card-email clause, TD-PRIV-12) (D-493). Every business text send checks `businesses::api::may_text` for the receiving member's phone at send time, before `send_checked`, so a revoked or unticked text box gets email instead (D-1715, D-1760). The `release_notice` and `card_fix_notice` emails' `{link}` is filled per recipient with a signed sign-in link: `core`'s `link_token(secret, LinkKind::Action, person, LinkSalt::from_bytes(job number as 8 big-endian bytes ‖ expiry as 8 big-endian Unix seconds))`, the person ID, job number and expiry (7 days on) in the URL beside the token (the format T269 checks at sign-in; no `core` change), never an unsigned or shared one, and the email still holds no homeowner detail (PICK-024, D-1565 (2), D-1761).
