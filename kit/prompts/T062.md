# T062: payments: Confirm-and-pay charge with a key per attempt, Stripe outage and the decline window

## Goal

A charge runs only from the business's Confirm press, with a new idempotency key per attempt and the FEE-017 check first; when Stripe is unreachable the pick is released after 4 hours and the next quote offered; a decline opens the card-fix window; and charges are paused while safe mode or the charging switch says so (FEE-016 to FEE-020, FEE-037, FEE-038, FEE-041).

## What this task rests on

Requirements (each sentence as it stands in `docs/spec/`):

- **FEE-016** Each charge attempt starts only from the business pressing "Confirm and pay", or, for a quote the business set to auto-confirm (PICK-048), from the homeowner's pick of it (D-477), and uses its own idempotency key (for example pick-1042-try1, then try2 for a press after a decline), a repeated press, reload or duplicate callback of the same attempt reusing its key, so an attempt can never charge twice, a failed attempt never blocks a later one, and the server never retries a charge on its own (D-365). (`docs/spec/fees.md:20`)
- **FEE-017** Before every charge attempt, including retries, the app checks that the job is still waiting for payment and the pick is still current. (`docs/spec/fees.md:21`)
- **FEE-018** If Stripe has been unreachable for 4 hours, the pick is released and the homeowner is offered the next quote. (`docs/spec/fees.md:22`)
- **FEE-019** If no Stripe notice arrives 30 minutes after a charge, the app asks Stripe directly, and asks again before giving up on a card that needed approval. (`docs/spec/fees.md:23`)
- **FEE-020** A card that fails or needs bank approval is shown on the Confirm page at once (bank approval by 3-D Secure in the page), the business gets a notice by its chosen channels (BIZ-019) with the Confirm link, and it can try again or use another card on that page for 1 business hour (60 clock minutes at any hour on a job chosen as an emergency, PICK-044), after which the homeowner is offered the next quote (D-270, D-308, D-365). (`docs/spec/fees.md:24`)
- **FEE-037** A charge attempt that timed out is not retried until Stripe, asked directly, confirms it failed, and the nightly reconcile refunds any second kept charge for the same pick (D-153). (`docs/spec/fees.md:41`)
- **FEE-038** Payment timers (the 1-business-hour card fix and the 4-hour Stripe release) stop while charges are paused by safe mode or the owner's charging switch, and resume with the time they had left (D-135). (`docs/spec/fees.md:42`)
- **FEE-041** A business can set an optional monthly fee limit in its portal and on the card-authorization screen, and a Confirm that would take the month's charges over it is not charged, gets a reply saying the limit is reached with the portal link, and ends that pick at once, the homeowner being told the business couldn't take it so they can choose another quote (D-204, D-264). (`docs/spec/fees.md:45`)
- **FEE-047** Each pick owns at most one Stripe PaymentIntent: it is created once with the idempotency key pick-<id>-intent and every Confirm press, card change or press after a decline confirms that same PaymentIntent with the attempt's own key (FEE-016), so two open tabs, a reload or a retry can never produce a second intent or a second charge (D-490). (`docs/spec/fees.md:51`)
