## Goal

War games part 2 (D-1154): `wargames/money.py` runs full jobs to completion on the Railway test site with Stripe in test mode, so introduction fees by job size ($25 / $75 / $199, each tier at and either side of its edges), free first wins, repeat-customer and fee-free $0 rows, declined and 3DS test cards, a double confirm, a payment that succeeds after the job moved on, refunds on a cancel before the business talked to the homeowner and on a failed pick, and a dispute all appear in the owner portal's money view and the businesses' statements. `wargames/reconcile.py` proves, to the cent, that the ledger (the owner portal's ledger export) equals the Stripe test-mode records (charges, refunds, disputes and processing fees read with the test key) and that the owner portal's rundown and each business statement CSV equal the ledger; any difference is an anomaly with its job and Stripe IDs. Fake data only (BUILD_BRIEF.md:66, PRIV-019): every persona's name, phone (555-0100 to 555-0199), email (example.com) and street comes from the fixture generator, and no real number, key or person is ever used; signing secrets and test keys are read the way T129's sandbox mode reads its credentials (from the build environment's secrets, never printed, logged or committed); an entry point whose secret this session cannot reach runs on the local lane only and the run log says so; `docs/phase2/money-war-game.md` records the jobs, the totals by entry type and the reconciliation result. On the test site the AI-008 switch is on during the money run and the rounds, so real Claude calls are made on fake data from the test project's Console key (T137's), and the campaign's AI spend is capped at $40 in all (a test-project daily AI-call cap, AI-021, of 600 calls, and the Console spend limit Casey set at the account checkpoint, whichever is lower); outbound texts use Twilio's test credentials and magic numbers only, so no real phone receives anything (BUILD_BRIEF.md:190). The money runs cover the pentest matrix's MONEY-01 to MONEY-05 and HOOK-02 to HOOK-04 cases (`docs/reviews/war-games-inputs/2026-11-09-pentest-matrix.md`) or state the skip (D-2350).
