## Goal

Plan change D-4051 (Casey's outside code audit, item B27, Nov 16, 2026): INT-038 says that under 13 the request and its data are deleted, except the consent or opt-out record, but `stop_if_under_18` (`app/intake/src/conversation.rs:276` to `:330`) leaves the vault contact holding the name and address she gave, because `privacy` had no delete call (D-996). `privacy::api::purge_contact` (`app/privacy/src/purge.rs:376`, PRIV-007, D-1839) now exists: it clears the contact's name, email, address and on-site contact, its email index and every stated name, keeps the sealed phone and its homeowner-phone index (the consent and opt-out records need them), audits once, and runs as a savepoint inside the caller's transaction. This task calls it on `AgeStop::Under13`, in the stop's own transaction, right after `models::purge_request`, for the sender's contact (`msg.contact`), so a failure keeps nothing; the 13-to-17 stop keeps the contact as today (INT-038 deletes data only under 13). The doc comment's D-996 sentence is replaced. (D-4064, privacy and consent, the Opus pre-claim check) `stop_if_under_18` has no production caller on `main` (only its definition, the `app/intake/src/api.rs:43` re-export and the unit tests), so "I'm 11" by text closes and purges nothing; this task wires it into `reply_in` (`app/intake/src/replies.rs:104`) in an open request, for a text not answered elsewhere (opt-out or HELP), after the 911 screen as its doc requires (emergency first): a clear text (`clear`, `replies.rs:186`) is stopped before T570's close step and the name-and-address step; a text that is not clear goes through `receive_text` first, so its 911 text is queued first, and is stopped after it; while the name-and-address question is open, `reply_in` returns early through `answer_name_address` (`replies.rs:131` to `:137`), which screens the text itself, so a text that is not clear on that path is stopped after it too, before the reply returns; a first text that opens the request ("I'm 11, my sink leaks") is stopped after `receive_text` on the request it opened (`Handled::conversation`).
