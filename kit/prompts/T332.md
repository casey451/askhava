## Goal

Fifth audit review fix round (D-2290; FEE-021, FEE-018, FEE-020, FEE-026; D-1547). Two preconditions of T328's `payments.late_success_refund` registration. (1) D-1547's tracked follow-up: `payments.refund.trigger`'s check (`migrations/0102_payments_refunds_disputes.sql:14`) has no late-success code, so `LATE_TRIGGER` stores a late-success refund as `charged-after-cancel` (`app/payments/src/late.rs:32`). `migrations/0177_payments_late_success_trigger.sql` replaces that check with one that also allows `late-success` (rows already stored keep their code); `app/payments/src/models.rs` (after T295) gains `RefundTrigger::LateSuccess` with code `late-success`, read back by the same parser; `late.rs`'s `LATE_TRIGGER` becomes it. `tests/integration/tests/late_success.rs` (T064's) changes only in the expected trigger, `charged-after-cancel` to `late-success`, as D-2290 records, and in one assertion of `td_fee_12_own_end_counts_once_the_check_records_the_success`, as D-2970 records: once the check round itself queues the late success (part (2) below), the report after it answers `Ok(LateSuccess::AlreadyQueued)`, not `Ok(LateSuccess::Queued)`; that test's `(1, 1)` alert and task count after the report stays; every other input, count, assertion and comment stays byte-identical, except that, as D-3007 records, that test's `///` comment may drop its clause "so `paid_release` stores no success", which part (2) makes untrue. (2) A success on a pick that `payments` itself ended (`payments.pick_intent.ended_at` set by the FEE-018 four-hour end or the FEE-020 card-fix window) never reaches `late_success` today: `paid_release` stops at `ReleaseError::PickEnded` before the success is stored (`app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:304`), and the `CHECK_KIND` round that stores it later calls nothing (`app/payments/src/handlers.rs:371`; D-1547's known gap). Now: `paid_release`, on a verified success for an ended pick, stores the success and its evidence as the check round would and calls `late_success` in the same transaction, then answers `PickEnded` (nothing is released, no `ReleaseProof` is built); `run_charge_check`, when its round records a success on an ended pick, calls `late_success` in that round's transaction; a declined attempt ended by the card-fix window that later succeeds reaches the first path through the webhook. `late_success` already queues once per pick and alerts only on a new queue, so both paths together give exactly one refund and one `payments.late_success` alert. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/late_success_ended.rs` (TD-FEE-56). T189's `tests/integration/tests/confirm_and_pay.rs` test `td_fee_25_an_ended_pick_is_never_cleared` asserts the old "stores nothing", so, as D-3007 records, only these lines of it change: its `///` comment's "and stores nothing" becomes a statement that the verified success and its evidence are stored and one late-success refund is queued (it may add FEE-021, FEE-042 and D-3007 to its IDs); `assert!(evidence(&pool).await.is_empty());` becomes `assert_eq!(evidence(&pool).await.len(), 1);`; `assert_eq!(status, "processing");` becomes `assert_eq!(status, "succeeded");`; new assertions after them (with at most one small counting helper added beside `evidence`) check exactly one `core.task` row of kind `payments.late_success_refund` and exactly one `ops.alert` row of kind `payments.late_success` (`LATE_KIND` and `LATE_SUCCESS_ALERT` may join the file's existing `askhava_payments::api` import); the `Err(ReleaseError::PickEnded)` assertion, the press, the end and every other line of the file stay byte-identical.
