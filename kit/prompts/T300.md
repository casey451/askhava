## Goal

No task serves the business portal (D-2116, D-2117): `server::build_router` merges no `/b` page, and `portals` exports the portal home, the released job's page, billing with its CSV statement and notice settings only as page functions with route constants (D-1728, D-1848, D-1896). (1) `app/server/src/business_portal.rs` builds the routes `GET /b` (`business_home_page`), `POST /b/open-cards` (`save_open_cards_times`), `GET /b/picks/{pick}` (`job_release_page`, which reads `picks::api::released_view` on every load with the session proof from `server::session_minter()`, D-1739), `GET /b/billing` (`billing_page`) and `GET /b/billing/statement.csv` (`statement_csv`), and `GET` and `POST /b/notices` (`notice_settings_page`, `change_channel`; the addresses read from the vault in `server`, D-1848), each page's facts built through each module's `api`; it merges them in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs` and composes them in `main.rs`. Every route reads the person with `portals::api::business_principal`: no business person (signed out, a homeowner or owner session, a removed member) gets a 4xx answer with a link to `/b/sign-in` and no homeowner detail, so T085's signed-out checks in `tests/e2e/business_portal.spec.ts` stay true; a refusal (another business's pick, a role the page needs missing, an unknown ID) is the one not-found answer; every response is `no_store` (SEC-034); both `POST`s pass `refuse_cross_site` and the CSRF check (SEC-009, SEC-042), and a refusal is a security event (SEC-048). (2) Each served `GET` page's `PAGE_STATES` entry goes in the shared `tests/a11y/page_states.ts` (D-1239, D-1788), and its rows in `docs/architecture.md` section 13 are marked `yes` once T284 has added the table. (3) Tests: `app/server/tests/business_portal_route.rs` against the assembled router (TD-SEC-43 and the router half of TD-BIZ-42) and `tests/e2e/business_portal_served.spec.ts` (the browser half of TD-BIZ-42; T085's spec is not changed, D-2117). In that spec the fake owner signs in through the served sign-in pages with no read of any email: the spec seeds, through `psql` on `DATABASE_URL` as `tests/e2e/j1_reduced.spec.ts` seeds its quote token, the active business, its owner with a linked fake phone (555-01xx) that takes texts, and a `portals.login_link` row whose `token_sha256` is the SHA-256 of a token the spec chose, then opens `/b/sign-in/{token}`; the new-device code is the one the stand-in Verify issues for that phone, computed in the spec from its rule in `stand_ins/twilio.py` (`start_verification`: the sum of the E.164 number's character codes modulo 1,000,000, six digits); no test-control route, stand-in change or email read is needed (`/__test/messages` lists texts only and the Resend stand-in has no read route). The complaints page, the "Why was I charged?" page, the emergency-email switch and business sign-up are not this task's. Update the `server` and `portals` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
