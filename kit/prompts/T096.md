## Goal

The homeowner-texting switch (web-and-email mode) and a suspended number move homeowner updates to email and the quote page, no second number is ever used, people with no email go on Casey's call list, emergencies still raise critical alerts and safety messages, and every step can be finished without a text (TXT-021 to TXT-024, TXT-034); the answered-call acknowledgement and the owner bridge are T080's (OPS-028, D-491). On a suspended number the safety voice call goes through the `core.task` event T080's `calls` handler places (TD-TXT-12, TD-TXT-16, D-491).
