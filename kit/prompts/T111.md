# T111: Draft homeowner and business terms with every required clause

Task T111 (docs, phase 1, stage 5). Slug: terms-homeowner-business.
Branch: `claude/task/T111-terms-homeowner-business`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance, money).

## Goal

`docs/legal/` holds plain-English drafts of the homeowner and business terms with every required clause (18 or older, Arizona law, no arbitration, not an emergency service, the not-a-contractor statements, warranty disclaimer, non-discrimination, card authorization and handyman certification, refunds as coded, the introduction-fee wording), plus the business terms summary in English and its Spanish draft (`docs/legal/business-terms-summary-es.md`, TD-TERMS-03), and a docs test finds each clause (TERMS-001 to TERMS-004, TERMS-006 to TERMS-009, TERMS-011, TERMS-013 to TERMS-016, TERMS-018, TERMS-022, TERMS-024, TERMS-026 to TERMS-028, FEE-002, FEE-006, BIZ-030). The homeowner terms describe both ways of picking (the quote page and a "<number> YES" text pick, TERMS-001); both terms end with the TERMS-035 General section (severability, entire agreement, no waiver, transfer, notices, the 14-day business change notice and the homeowner rule that a change applies only to later requests), and the business terms add the 30-day write-first step and the TERMS-036 account-ending clause and no-promise sentence (D-492).
