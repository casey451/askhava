## Goal

J1 to J4 run against the Railway test site with every vendor in its sandbox and fake data only, and each journey's result, with any failure filed as a task, is recorded in `docs/phase2/e2e-test-site.md`. J4's outage steps stay on the stand-ins; J1 to J3 run on the test site; the task records results and files gaps in `docs/for-casey-review.md`, it fixes nothing itself (D-491). (D-493)
