# T032: jobs: every waiting status's timeout on the business clock

Task T032 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: jobs-timers.
Branch: `claude/task/T032-jobs-timers`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: none).

## Goal

Every waiting status has its timer on the business clock or the emergency clock: Open to No quotes yet at 6 p.m. the next day, No quotes yet and Quotes ready closing after 5 and 3 days with their texts, Awaiting confirm and Payment pending returning to Quotes ready, Connected moving on DONE or closing after the check-ins, and `jobs::api::all_windows_ended`, the entry point `quotes` calls once every card's quote window has ended, which sends the all-quotes-in text and closes a job with no choosable quote left (the trigger is T046's, D-491) (JOB-007 to JOB-015, JOB-025, JOB-026, TXT-042).
