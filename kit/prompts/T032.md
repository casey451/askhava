# T032: jobs: every waiting status's timeout on the business clock

Task T032 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: jobs-timers.

## Goal

Every waiting status has its timer on the business clock or the emergency clock: Open to No quotes yet at 6 p.m. the next day, No quotes yet and Quotes ready closing after 5 and 3 days with their texts, Awaiting confirm and Payment pending returning to Quotes ready, Connected moving on DONE or closing after the check-ins, and `jobs::api::all_windows_ended`, the entry point `quotes` calls once every card's quote window has ended, which sends the all-quotes-in text and closes a job with no choosable quote left (the trigger is T046's, D-491) (JOB-007 to JOB-015, JOB-025, JOB-026, TXT-042).

Hand-in is refused while the gate is red (D-1300).
- The commands under "Run your tests" pass, and every test entry named above has, in a file you own, a test carrying its marker for the cases that fall to your requirements (an entry shared with another task is completed by that task's cases).
- `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test` are clean for the Rust you changed; `tsc --strict` and eslint for any TypeScript; `ruff check` for any Python tooling (where the task has code).
- `python3 scripts/check_code_docs.py` reports 0 problems, and every requirement listed above is named in a file you own.
- Customer copy: `scripts/check_ai_smell.py` and `scripts/check_copy.py` report 0 problems, and the cold read and the copy panel find nothing open in any changed customer copy.
- Only the files you own and the shared paths changed.

## End routine

If the smoke fails only in files and tests your task did not touch (a red `main` or a broken environment, D-498), do not fix it: stop, do not hand in, and open your report with `MAIN RED: <failing test or check>` and its output. Fast loop after any fix (D-3530): `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` (it compiles every dependent crate), `cargo test -p <crate>` per crate the fix touched, `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test <file>` per file `grep -l 'askhava_<crate>' tests/integration/tests/*.rs` lists (more than 3 files: the full smoke instead), and each failing or named test; then the full smoke once more before you commit and hand in.
