# T150: picks and quotes: EMERGENCY card and pick emails with the emergency-email switch and its test email

Task T150 (build, phase 1, stage 4). Slug: business-calls-link-hardening.
Branch: `claude/task/T150-business-calls-link-hardening`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort high. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: privacy, security, compliance, accessibility).

## Goal

Emergency job cards and picks go by email with the EMERGENCY subject and first line, at night only to people with "Emergency emails at any hour" on, whose switch needs a passed test emergency email renewed every 90 days; a business without that stops counting for after-hours coverage (CARD-021, BIZ-007, BIZ-042, INT-033); the INT-033 night count of emergency businesses counts only businesses with a person whose emergency emails are on with a current Yes (TD-BIZ-24, moved from TD-INT-15, D-491); the after-hours cards going out as soon as the ZIP is in are this task's (TD-INT-31, D-492). The night count's text is T039's; this task narrows `businesses::api::night_count` (T041's) to businesses with such a person, and the after-hours tick column is T041's (D-491). The switch page with its "Send a test emergency email" button and the Yes/No answer POST is the portals route `emergency_switch_page.rs` rendering `emergency_test_email.html` (D-492).
