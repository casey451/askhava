## Goal

Nov 10 code review, messaging F1 (D-2621; CON-010, CON-013, CON-035, AI-020, D-146): the inbound route classifies opt-outs on the scrubbed text (`app/server/src/inbound_text.rs:233`, `messaging::rules::detect_opt_out`, `app/messaging/src/rules.rs:503`), and `mark_stated` (`app/privacy/src/scrub.rs:657`) removes up to two words after "this is", "I'm", "I am", "it's" or "soy" (the first not a common word), so the second word is often the first word of the opt-out phrase: "This is Bob stop texting me" scrubs to "This is [removed] texting me" and is answered as an intake reply, never listed (the same for "I am Bob unsubscribe me", "It's Maria remove me", "Soy Ana dejen de escribirme"). `read_inbound_for` (`app/privacy/src/scrub_context.rs:58`) then remembers "stop" as one of her names, and `mark_known` (`scrub.rs:704`) deletes it from every later text, so "Stop texting me please" is missed too. No field is added to a struct done tests build by name (`InboundWords`, `Flags`). Tests in `tests/integration/tests/optout_after_name.rs` (TD-CON-32), each failing on today's `main`: for every phrase in `messaging::api::OPT_OUT_PHRASES`, prefixed with "This is Bob ", "I'm Bob ", "I am Bob ", "It's Maria " and "Soy Ana ", `privacy::api::scrub` with an empty context then `detect_opt_out` still finds it; "This is Bob stop texting me" then "Stop texting me please" from the same number through `read_inbound_for`: the second is found as an opt-out (on today's `main` the first stores "stop" as a name and the second scrubs to "[removed] texting me please"; "Bob here" states no name, "here" being no trigger, `TRIGGERS`, `scrub.rs:197`, so it already passes and is not the case, D-3094); the coverage check over messaging's lists; "This is Bob, sink leaking" still removes "Bob" (the AI-020 name cut is unchanged).
