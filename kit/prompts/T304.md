## Goal

T205 builds the owner refund review step and the audit log viewer and T106 and T109 the health page, as portals pages no route serves (D-2128); J3 (T118) refunds a charge and reads the audit log. (1) `app/server/src/owner_tools.rs` builds those pages and the refund `POST` behind T303's owner session (T304 uses T303's owner-session check): the refund needs a fresh second factor (SEC-043), issues one Stripe refund and one ledger row, and is blocked on a disputed charge (FEE-026, SEC-006); no other realm reaches them (a 4xx with a link to `/owner/sign-in`); every response `no_store`; every `POST` passes `refuse_cross_site` and the CSRF check, a refusal a security event; merged in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`. It also serves T205's "I submitted the evidence myself" `POST` (fresh second factor, CSRF, `no_store`), which calls `payments::api::record_owner_submission` once (fourth external audit F44, D-2179).

Acceptance: TD-SEC-47 passes against PostgreSQL through the assembled router and in Playwright against the stand-ins, and `Ally` passes with the new page states; every T205, T106 and T109 case still passes.
