## Goal

Fourth external audit F44 (D-2179; FEE-027, FEE-050): `payments::mark_auto_submit` (`app/payments/src/disputes.rs:169`) commits the `auto_submit` mark and only then queues the submit task, and `record_owner_submission` (`:197`) commits `submitted_at` and only then appends the $15 countering fee, each on whatever connection its caller passes; a failure between the two leaves the mark or `submitted_at` set, every retry answers `false`, and the submit task or the fee is lost for good. Nothing calls `picks::dispute_auto_submit` (`app/picks/src/unreachable_refund.rs:467`) outside tests, so no dispute is ever auto-submitted. (1) In `disputes.rs` both functions open one transaction on the connection they are given (`Acquire::begin`, a savepoint inside a caller's transaction) for their two writes, so the mark and its task, and `submitted_at` and its fee, commit together or not at all; `record_dispute` queues one `picks.dispute_opened` `core.task` (dedupe key per dispute) in the transaction that records a new dispute. (2) `app/picks/src/dispute_steps.rs` holds the `picks.dispute_opened` handler, registered in the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`: it reads whether the disputed charge's pick was released, its call attempts (`calls::api::call_log` through T270's `call_record`) and whether the payment was 3DS-authenticated, and calls `dispute_auto_submit` in one transaction; a failed run is retried by the queue and a repeat marks nothing twice. FEE-050 is judged again at the due date less `SUBMIT_BEFORE` (2 days): `record_dispute` also queues a second `picks.dispute_opened` run for that time (its own dedupe key), so a call attempt or release recorded after the dispute opened still marks it before the submit task runs; an already-marked or submitted dispute is unchanged. (3) Tests in `tests/integration/tests/dispute_submit.rs` (TD-FEE-51): a failure injected between the mark and its task, and between `submitted_at` and its fee, leaves neither, and the retry marks, queues or records once; a released, called dispute is marked through the worker and its evidence submitted 2 days before the due date; a dispute whose first call attempt is logged after it opened is marked by the run at due less 2 days; an unreleased one waits for Casey; Casey's own submission (T205's page, T304's route) records the fee once and stops the auto-submit. `payments` is money code: its 95% branch floor holds; the overseer runs an independent money review of the hand-in (D-2179). Update the `payments` and `picks` `CLAUDE.md` files and `docs/decisions.md`.
