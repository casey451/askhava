# T181: intake: consent and terms records, and no card before consent

Task T181 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: intake-consent-records.
Branch: `claude/task/T181-intake-consent-records`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance, privacy).

## Goal

The first inbound text (or the web-chat start) is stored as the consent record with its ID, time, channel and consent-text version; terms versions and acceptance times are stored for homeowners and businesses; only people who wrote first are texted; and no job card goes out before consent is recorded (CON-005, CON-007, CON-021, TERMS-001, TERMS-016). Each homeowner terms acceptance also stores the document, the SHA-256 of the exact published text, the language, the channel (quote page or text pick), the assent line shown and, for a text pick, the inbound message ID, in this task's migration (TERMS-033, D-492).

### 6. Electronic assent and the consent record (lines naming this task's requirements)
