## Goal

The first inbound text (or the web-chat start) is stored as the consent record with its ID, time, channel and consent-text version; terms versions and acceptance times are stored for homeowners and businesses; only people who wrote first are texted; and no job card goes out before consent is recorded (CON-005, CON-007, CON-021, TERMS-001, TERMS-016). ### 6. Electronic assent and the consent record (lines naming this task's requirements)
