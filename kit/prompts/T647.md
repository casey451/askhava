# T647: payments: rebuild_charge reports Booked inside a caller's transaction and takes its locks in record's order

## Goal

Plan change D-4292 (spot review of money, verified on `main` 67827d85; both latent, as today's only caller passes a bare connection). (a) `rebuild_charge` (`app/payments/src/charge_rebuild.rs:63` to `:82`) opens `conn.begin()` and then decides `Booked` by `xmin = pg_current_xact_id()::xid` on the evidence row; inside a caller's transaction that `begin` is a savepoint, the row's `xmin` is the subtransaction's ID while `pg_current_xact_id()` returns the top-level one, so a charge this call just booked is reported `AlreadyBooked` (TD-OPS-04: `Rebuilt` must say whether this call booked). Decide it without `xmin`: `held_try` has already seen no evidence row under the `pick_intent` row lock, and every writer of `payments.charge_evidence` (`charge::book_success` through `record`'s effects after `hold_pick`, `reclear`) takes that row's lock first, so the row existing after `book_success` means this call wrote it; check each writer, list them in the doc, and report a plan gap if one does not take the lock. (b) Lock order: `rebuild_charge` locks `pick_intent` `FOR UPDATE` and then inserts evidence whose foreign key to `charge_attempt (pick_id, try_no)` takes a key-share lock on the attempt row, while `record` (`app/payments/src/handlers.rs:126` to `:131`, then `hold_pick`) locks the attempt row `FOR UPDATE` and then `pick_intent` `FOR SHARE`; run together on one pick (a restored 30-minute check or a Stripe resend during a replay) they deadlock (40P01). Make `rebuild_charge` take the newest attempt row `FOR UPDATE` before `pick_intent`, the order `record` uses, and re-check under the `pick_intent` lock that it is still the newest (`max(try_no)`; a newer attempt made meanwhile answers as `held_try` does today), after checking the order of every other path that locks both (`confirm_and_pay`, `end_pick`, `end_intent_unless_charging`, `late_success`) and naming it in the doc. Correct the function doc at `app/payments/src/charge_rebuild.rs:41` to `:42`, which says the webhook does not take the pick's lock: `record` takes it `FOR SHARE` through `hold_pick`. `payments::api` and `Rebuilt` keep their shape. (3) Tests, fake data only, in `tests/integration/tests/rebuild_charge_locks.rs` on PostgreSQL: every case of TD-OPS-45; the payments branch coverage floor holds (95%). T110 depends on this task. Update `app/payments/NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
