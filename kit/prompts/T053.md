## Goal

The `Ally` suite runs axe-core on every route and state at 320, 768 and 1280 px from a page list generated from the route table, and checks one `h1`, landmarks, skip link and `lang`, 24 px targets, contrast, reflow at 320 px and 400% zoom, the text-spacing override, reduced motion, help in one place and the status and alert roles, any failure turning `Ally` red (A11Y-001 to A11Y-004, A11Y-006 to A11Y-009, A11Y-017, A11Y-018).

Acceptance: A page with one planted axe violation, a second `h1`, a 20 px target or sideways scroll at 320 px each turns `Ally` red (shown in the task report), and every current route passes.
