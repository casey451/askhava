# T755: messaging: a sole opt-out word counts with any spacing ("STOP ALL"), and decomposed accents fold

## Goal

Plan change D-4764 (Fable's texting audit of `main` a9e2cb79, findings T1 (major) and T4; CON-008, CON-010, CON-035; D-020, D-477). `detect_opt_out` (`app/messaging/src/rules.rs:503`) compares the folded text with each sole opt-out word, but `fold` (`:574`) keeps an inner space, so "STOP ALL", "stop all" or "Stop  all" (CON-008's stopall said as two words) and "opt  out" spaced oddly are not opt-outs; and `fold` maps only precomposed accents, so a phrase typed with decomposed accents (a base letter followed by a combining mark, U+0300 to U+036F, as some phones send it: "qu\u{0069}\u{0301}tenme de la lista") misses the Spanish CON-010 phrases. This task, in `app/messaging/src/rules.rs` only: (1) a text is a sole opt-out word when the folded text with every space removed equals a listed word (`PROVIDER_OPT_OUT_WORDS`, `APP_OPT_OUT_WORDS`) with its spaces removed, English and Spanish alike; the strict rule stays: only a text that is nothing but an opt-out word or phrase is one, so "stop please" and "please stop" are still not opt-outs (a spec question left for later, recorded in D-4764), and no ordinary word pair may collapse into a listed word (the test lists the word list's pairs and asserts none is a common two-word phrase); (2) `fold` drops combining marks (U+0300 to U+036F) after lower-casing, so a decomposed accent folds as its precomposed letter does; the phrase match (CON-010) and the word match both use it. Tests: new cases in `app/messaging/src/tests/optout_words.rs` (its existing cases unchanged): "STOP ALL", "stop all", "Stop  all", "opt out", "OPT-OUT", "PARAR" and "Baja." opt out; "stop please", "please stop", "can stop by at 3" do not; each Spanish CON-010 phrase typed with decomposed accents is detected; `app/messaging/src/tests/prop_optout_props.rs` passes unchanged. Every opt-out integration test passes unchanged. Consent: Opus worker (Fable once the runner's `models` map names it, D-4761), Opus security and compliance review. Update the `messaging` notes and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `app/messaging/src/tests/optout_words.rs` passes with its new cases and its existing cases unchanged; `prop_optout_props.rs` and every opt-out integration test pass unchanged; "stop please" is still not an opt-out.
