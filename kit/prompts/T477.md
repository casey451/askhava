## Goal

Plan change D-3753: the pilot scorecard (OPS-039, OPS-047) reads about twenty inputs no `api` provides, and `portals` may not query another schema (`tests/integration/tests/architecture_migrations.rs:271`). This task adds read-only functions, per trade and in total over a window (`from`, `to`), counts, sums in cents and durations only: (1) `app/payments/src/scorecard_inputs.rs`: paid introductions and their average fee, the free share counted only on picks after each business's first win (D-458), refunds and bank disputes, refund requests with their reasons, businesses paying again within 60 days, and net per paid introduction (the fee less the FEE-046 set-aside at its setting, Stripe fees and refunds); (2) `app/picks/src/scorecard_inputs.rs`: picks per request with quotes, picks confirmed rather than lapsed, picks among requests with 2 or more quotes, the share of picks that were diagnostic-visit quotes; (3) `app/quotes/src/scorecard_inputs.rs`: requests with 1 or more, 2 or more and 3 or more quotes within 24 hours, the median and 75th-percentile time to the first quote, quotes per win and per pick per business, active businesses per trade (quoted in the last 30 days, D-468), the median of the optional "minutes this quote took" field (PICK-040, OPS-047), and notes returned to businesses (PICK-052). Each is exported through its crate's shared `api.rs`, one SQL query each, an empty window gives `None`. Tests in `tests/integration/tests/scorecard_inputs_money.rs` against PostgreSQL with fake ledger and pick data: each input from planted rows, the free share leaving out a first win, rows outside the window left out. Update each crate's `NOTES.md` and `docs/decisions.md`.
