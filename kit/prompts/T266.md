## Goal

At the page pick the homeowner chooses a masked call or an email she shares (PICK-018), but `app/portals/src/pick_flow.rs` checks the choice and the email and passes them on unstored (`pick_flow.rs:38`, D-1264), so the release can only ever be a masked call (`email_chosen` false, D-1740 (2)) and PICK-042's accepted-quote email has no address (D-1761). (1) `migrations/0165_picks_contact_choice.sql`: `picks.contact_choice` (pick ID, `call` or `email`, decided time; one row per pick, app role SELECT and INSERT). (2) `app/privacy/src/vault.rs`: `privacy::api::store_shared_email(conn, vault, contact, email)` seals the email into the contact's vault `email` field (field-bound encryption, a new version, one audit row naming the actor and purpose `shared_at_pick`; no plaintext returned), so the email is stored only in the vault (PRIV-001, PRIV-002). (3) `app/picks/src/contact_choice.rs`: `picks::api::record_contact_choice` (in the page pick's transaction, after `page_pick`) and `picks::api::contact_choice(conn, pick)` for the release's caller. (4) `pick_flow.rs` stores both in the transaction that records the pick; an error keeps her choices on the page as now; a text pick (no page) stays a masked call. (5) `app/server/src/release_sink.rs` (T254's sink, extended by T255) passes `picks::api::contact_choice` as `email_chosen` instead of D-1740 (2)'s masked-call default, on the first release and on T255's re-release (D-1761). No email reaches a log, task payload or business page. Update the `picks`, `privacy` and `portals` `CLAUDE.md` files, `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: TD-PICK-52 passes against PostgreSQL: the page-pick cases in `tests/integration/tests/pick_contact_choice.rs` and the release cases in `app/server/tests/release_contact_choice.rs` (only `server` may mint, D-1563 (4)); every existing pick-flow, TD-PICK-49 and TD-INT-39 case still passes; the `app/privacy/` 95% floor holds.

### 2. Principles (lines naming this task's requirements)
**P12. Blind-index lookups are equality-only and leak equality.** The phone lookup is HMAC-SHA-256 under `VAULT_INDEX_KEY` (Arch 8). A blind index "leaks that rows have the same value", open to frequency analysis on low-entropy fields (blind_index README); a btree on the hash column is right, a range or `LIKE` query is meaningless. Never index plaintext, never put a plaintext phone in a `WHERE` [inferred]. Serves PRIV-002, PRIV-004.
**P13. Disk encryption at the host is not the vault.** Railway staff: "All customer data within Railway projects is encrypted at rest" (community answer, https://station.railway.com/questions/are-databases-encrypted-at-rest-0e719d6c); Render's security page claims AES-256 at rest (quoted in https://community.render.com/t/encryption-at-rest/3609). Neither is first-party documentation of backups: **verify with host** in its security page or DPA. Host encryption does not protect against a leaked database credential or a dump; field encryption under `VAULT_KEY` does (PRIV-002) [inferred]. Serves PRIV-002, OPS-042.
