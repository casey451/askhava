# T029: messaging: sole-word opt-out in English and Spanish, one confirmation, no text after

Task T029 (build, phase 1, stage 2). Slug: messaging-optout.
Branch: `claude/task/T029-messaging-optout`
Model: `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium. Reviewer: `claude-opus-5-5` (areas: compliance, privacy).

## Goal

A text that is only an opt-out word (English or Spanish, any case) or contains a listed revocation phrase opts the number out of every text at once with exactly one confirmation within 5 minutes; nothing else goes to it until START, 21610 failures are never retried, every revocation channel feeds one suppression list, and the provider's Advanced Opt-Out matches the app's words (CON-008 to CON-011, CON-013 to CON-017, CON-030, CON-032, CON-035). The provider's Advanced Opt-Out settings are T167's committed file `stand_ins/provider_optout.json` (read here, not owned): START and UNSTOP the only re-subscribe words, exactly CON-030's twelve opt-out words (stop, stopall, unsubscribe, cancel, end, revoke, optout, quit, parar, alto, cancelar and baja), and the CON-009 wording and its Spanish counterpart as the provider's replies; a test checks the app's own opt-out and re-subscribe word lists equal the file's (D-493); the provider sends the Spanish confirmation for the Spanish words and the app sends none (CON-030); the fake Twilio's matching behavior is T167's (D-492).
