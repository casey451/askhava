Plan change D-5420 (job 18 pre-flight): both steps sit after `Fetch main` (`ci.yml:138`) and before `Plan lock and change rules`; no new step name starts with any prefix `tests/test_ci_workflow.py:156` lists (it expects exactly one `Secret scan` step), no step but `Fetch main` contains `main:refs/remotes/origin/main` (`:176` to `:187`), no new `curl` release download (`tests/test_prehandin.py:410` expects exactly 5), and no `claude/train` text or `if:` on a ref name (`test_ci_workflow.py:205` to `:214`).
