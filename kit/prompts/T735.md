(1) New `app/portals/src/business_towns_hours.rs` and `app/portals/templates/business_towns_hours.html`: the towns served (the sign-up list and checks, saved through the same `set_towns` path `save_capability` uses, under the business lock) and an optional own window ("Send me job notices only between", start and end inside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Arizona time, or "Any time in the app's hours"), labels and hints before inputs, A11Y-030 errors with values kept; owner or "Confirm and billing" only. Section 13 rows (added `T735` by D-4727) marked `yes`; page states in the shared `tests/a11y/page_states.ts`.
