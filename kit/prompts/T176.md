## Goal

The payments, AI, email, letters, license-source and storage clients exist behind their traits with idempotency keys; the AI client uses only the deterministic stand-in during the build; and every server-side fetch of a URL a person or vendor supplied follows no redirect off the allowed hosts and refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses (AI-009, SEC-041). 2. Log build, test and lint output; print only decisive lines. Example: `cargo test -p <crate> >/tmp/t.log 2>&1; echo exit=$?; grep -nE 'FAILED|panicked|^error|test result' /tmp/t.log | head -n 60; tail -n 15 /tmp/t.log`.
