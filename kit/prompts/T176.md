## Goal

The payments, AI, email, letters, license-source and storage clients exist behind their traits with idempotency keys; the AI client uses only the deterministic stand-in during the build; and every server-side fetch of a URL a person or vendor supplied follows no redirect off the allowed hosts and refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses (AI-009, SEC-041). The email client also offers removal of one address from the provider's suppression list (BIZ-056), which the re-verification calls; its stand-in endpoint is T133's (D-492).
