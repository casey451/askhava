## Goal

Nothing calls `picks::api::release` in production (D-1563): `server` builds no `payments` `ReleaseSink` and does not merge `stripe_webhook_routes` (D-1480), and nothing moves a pick to `paid` or `free`, so every release would be `MovedOn` (D-1564); a masked line that fails to open is never tried again (D-1688). Compose it (D-1739). (1) `app/picks/src/settle.rs`: `picks::api::settle(tx, &Releasable)` takes `payments`' cleared `Releasable` (private fields, no `Clone`, `app/payments/src/release_proof.rs:147`), never a bare basis, so no crate can mark a pick paid or free without `payments`' clearance; it moves an `awaiting_confirm` pick to `paid` (a paid charge) or `free` (a $0 decision) and leaves any other status alone, saying whether it moved. (2) `app/server/src/release_sink.rs`: the `ReleaseSink`. For a `Releasable` it settles the pick and calls `release` whenever the pick is now `paid` or `free` for the proof's basis, whether this call moved it or an earlier one did (a restart between the settle's commit and the claim then releases on the retry, exactly once); finds the job's homeowner contact through `intake::api` (the one T246's page pick uses), mints with `release_minter()` and calls `release` with the vault and the masked pool; `email_chosen` is the stored PICK-018 choice when one exists and otherwise false, a masked call (D-1740); `Already`, `MovedOn` and `WaitingForAddress` are success to the route, a database error its 500 so Stripe sends the event again. The same sink takes `payments::api::no_fee_release`'s `Releasable` for a $0 decision. `server::build_router` (the shared `app/server/src/lib.rs`) merges `stripe_webhook_routes` with the Stripe client and webhook secret from settings, served with connect info (SEC-040); a setting it needs that does not exist yet goes in the shared `app/core/src/settings.rs` (D-493). (3) The masked-line retry (D-1688): `line_failed` in `release.rs` also queues one `picks.line_retry` core task with the pick; its handler, `picks::api::retry_line` in `settle.rs`, registered in the shared `app/server/src/bin/worker.rs`, opens the line from the release row's bound contact (T253) only if the job never had one, so a line closed after 30 days is never reopened; the worker's retries and dead letter apply and the High alert stays. (4) Tests in `app/server/tests/release_recovery.rs` (TD-PICK-49, TD-PICK-50), through the sink with real proofs, since only `server` may mint (`tests/integration/tests/architecture.rs:30`): `no_fee_release` with a seeded $0 decision, the webhook route with a signed fake event and a fake Stripe client, a vault that cannot read and then can, and a new pool, vault and sink for the restart case. No test-only route and no public proof constructor: production authorization stays as it is. Update `docs/architecture.md` section 10 and `app/server/CLAUDE.md`.
