## Goal

Performance track (D-3292), part 2: `perf/` (Python, standard library only, D-453), a seeded fake-data benchmark of the app's main routes, and its first baseline. Fake data only (BUILD_BRIEF.md:66, PRIV-019): names from a fixed fake list, phones 555-0100 to 555-0199, emails at `example.com`, streets such as "1 Test Way"; `scripts/pii_scan.py` scans `perf/` and `docs/audit/`. (1) `perf/seed.py` seeds, against the CI Postgres (`DATABASE_URL`, after `server migrate`), a deterministic Phoenix-metro-size background in one transaction: about 1,500 businesses across the open trades and the catalog's towns, 20,000 past jobs, 50,000 quotes and their picks (the exact counts are constants the report prints). Rows follow the migrations as they are on `main`; a table it cannot seed faithfully is skipped and named in the report, never guessed. The objects each measured route needs (a job with quotes and its quote-page link, a signed-in business, a pick awaiting Confirm) are made through the app's real entry points the way `tests/e2e/fixtures.ts` does, links read through the test-control routes. (2) `perf/bench.py` boots the stand-in server and the test-control build of `server` and `worker` the way `tests/e2e/webserver.ts` does (random per-run secrets, kept in memory, never printed), seeds, then for each route (home, ZIP lookup, intake acknowledgement, matching (the results step, AI off), quote page and its polling refresh request, pick and Confirm (the paying request only against the stand-ins, once per pick, with its idempotency key), business dashboard) runs a warm-up and then 1, 10 and 50 concurrent users for a fixed request count, recording per request the wall time, status and response bytes; at 1 user it reads T416's `GET /__test/perf/db` before and after each request for queries per request and pool acquire wait. Per route and concurrency it reports p50, p95 and p99 (nearest rank), mean queries per request, p95 and maximum acquire wait, median payload bytes and the error rate (5xx and timeouts, expected 4xx excluded), each a number or "not exposed" with the reason, judged against D-3292's goals (ZIP lookup p95 under 100 ms, matching p95 under 250 ms, error rate under 0.1%) as met or missed, never as a failure; the full run also runs T416's `perf_zip_scale` test and records its national-size p95. `--quick` (1 and 10 users, fewer requests, under 3 minutes, never `perf_zip_scale`) is for `Ci`'s report (T418). `--out` names the JSON file; nothing is written under the repository except by `--baseline`. (3) `tests/perf/test_bench.py` runs offline (no app, no database): the nearest-rank p50, p95 and p99 on planted timings (1 to 100 ms give 50, 95 and 99), the bucket-difference p95 on planted snapshots, the error-rate rule (a 404 is not an error, a 500 and a timeout are), the goal verdicts at and just over each goal, and a record holding a planted non-fake phone, email or street is refused before it is written (TD-OPS-42). (4) The first baseline, measured in the task's session against the CI-shaped Postgres: `docs/audit/perf-baseline.json` (one entry per route and concurrency, plus the machine, Postgres version, data counts and git SHA) and `docs/audit/perf-baseline.md`, a short report: the method (lab numbers on a build machine, not production and not field data), the table, the goal verdicts, and for each of PERF-001 to PERF-005 confirmed, rejected or not yet measurable with the numbers behind it; no fix is made or claimed (fixes are later tasks, each with before and after numbers, D-3292). Update `docs/decisions.md`.

Acceptance: `python3 -m pytest tests/perf/test_bench.py` passes offline; `python3 -m perf.bench --quick --out <tmp>` exits 0 against the CI Postgres with every route and figure present (a number or "not exposed" with its reason); `docs/audit/perf-baseline.json` and `docs/audit/perf-baseline.md` come from a full run in the session; `scripts/pii_scan.py` passes on `perf/`, `tests/perf/` and both reports.

Every turn re-reads all earlier tool output, so a large result is paid for again on each later turn (D-3305). Never cut a failure line to save tokens.

1. Never `cat` or `Read` all of a file over 200 lines (`wc -l`): `grep -n` (context `-C` 3 at most), then `sed -n 'A,Bp'` or Read with offset/limit, at most 120 lines per read, or up to 250 when one read then covers what would take two. 2. Build, test and lint output goes to a log, and you print only what decides: `cargo test -p <crate> >/tmp/t.log 2>&1; echo exit=$?; grep -nE 'FAILED|panicked|^error|test result' /tmp/t.log | head -n 60; tail -n 15 /tmp/t.log`.
