## Goal

Register row IC-95, the hold (D-3081; BIZ-033, CALL-012; D-311, D-288, D-963, D-1695, D-3079). BIZ-033 and CALL-012 say a phone linked by a team member other than the owner works only after the owner approves it, but nothing holds it back: `team::link_phone` (`app/businesses/src/team.rs:483`) and `team::change_phone` (`:547`) put the number's digests in `messaging.sender_directory` at once, so `messaging::api::sender_business` (the inbound text route's lookup) and `calls::routing::party_of` treat the phone as the business; `businesses.staff_phone` (`migrations/0056_businesses_team_members.sql:32`) has no approval column; and two DONE tests, `td_biz_34_a_staff_phone_follows_into_the_directory` in `tests/integration/tests/sender_directory_sync.rs` and the member-link case in `tests/integration/tests/team_members.rs`, assert the opposite of BIZ-033. (1) `migrations/0186_businesses_phone_approval.sql` (new, headed `-- businesses (T396)`): `businesses.staff_phone.approved_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()` (a row inserted without naming it is approved, so the DONE tests that insert a staff phone row directly, `tests/integration/tests/business_notices.rs:135` and `business_consent.rs:62`, keep today's answer; every link by a member who is not the owner writes NULL explicitly, as the migration's comment says, and TD-BIZ-52 asserts it, D-3263), `number_set_at timestamptz` and `number_set_by uuid` (when and by which member the current number was set; every link and number change writes both, so an approval can name the exact number it approves), and `number_end char(4)` (the number's last 4 digits, staff data the owner needs to know which phone to approve, as the LINK notice already names them, D-078; never the whole number); every row that exists when it runs gets `approved_at = linked_at`, `number_set_at = linked_at` and `number_set_by = linked_by` (those phones were linked under the earlier rule, and no row exists outside a test database before Gate C); and `migrations/0187_messaging_sender_held.sql` (new, headed `-- messaging (T396)`, a file of its own because a migration may name only its own module's schema, `tests/integration/tests/architecture_migrations.rs`, D-3263): `messaging.sender_held` (line ID, index version, digest, business; unique on index version and digest; no number) with the app role's SELECT, INSERT and DELETE and nothing more. Nothing logs a digest. (3) `app/businesses/src/team.rs`: every link and number change stores `number_end`, `number_set_at` (`now`: a new `change_phone_at(..., now)` takes it, and `change_phone` keeps its signature and calls it with `Timestamp::now()`, so callers this task does not own, such as `app/server/tests/business_contacts.rs:147` (T415, DONE), compile unchanged; plan change D-3618) and `number_set_by`.
