## Goal

BIZ-033 and CALL-012 say a phone linked by a team member other than the owner works only after the owner approves it, but nothing holds it back: `team::link_phone` (`app/businesses/src/team.rs:439`) puts every new phone's digests in `messaging.sender_directory` at once, so `messaging::api::sender_business` (the inbound text route's lookup) and `calls::routing::party_of` (`app/calls/src/routing.rs:419`) treat it as the business; the pure `calls::lifecycle::phone_works` and `gate` (`app/calls/src/lifecycle.rs:241`, `:248`) have no production caller; `businesses.staff_phone` (`migrations/0056_businesses_team_members.sql:32`) has no approval column; and two DONE tests, `td_biz_34_a_staff_phone_follows_into_the_directory` in `tests/integration/tests/sender_directory_sync.rs` and the member-link case in `tests/integration/tests/team_members.rs`, assert the opposite of BIZ-033 for a member-linked phone. After T391 (which adds owner-linked phones from a LINK reply), T365 (the last earlier owner of `routing.rs`) and T322 (of `team.rs`). (1) `migrations/0186_businesses_phone_approval.sql` (new): `businesses.staff_phone.approved_at timestamptz` (NULL until the owner approves; every row that exists when it runs gets its own `linked_at`, as those phones were linked under the earlier rule and no row exists outside a test database before Gate C), and `messaging.sender_held` (line ID, index version, digest, business; unique on index version and digest; no number and no last 4 digits) with the app role's SELECT, INSERT and DELETE and nothing more. (2) `app/messaging/src/sender_held.rs` (new), through `messaging::api`: `sender_hold(db, line)` moves that line's directory digests into `sender_held` (a digest another waiting phone already holds is `SenderTaken`); `sender_release(db, line)` moves them back (a digest another phone now holds in the directory is `SenderTaken` and nothing moves); `sender_drop_held(db, line)`; and `sender_line(db, vault, phone)`, the directory's business and line for a number by the same `sender-dir` digests `sender_business` uses. Nothing logs a digest. (3) `app/businesses/src/team.rs`: `link_phone` by a member who is not the owner (`team_member.is_owner` false, even one holding both roles) stores `approved_at` NULL and, after `SenderLink::link`, calls `sender_hold` in the same transaction (`SenderTaken` is `PhoneTaken`), so the phone is in no directory lookup until approved; the owner's links (the main phone always) work at once, as today. `change_phone` on a phone still waiting holds the new number again; `unlink_phone` and `remove_member` also call `sender_drop_held`. (4) `app/businesses/src/phone_approval.rs` (new), through `businesses::api`: `approve_phone(conn, by, line, now)` and `reject_phone(conn, by, line, now)`, owner only (any other member, a "Confirm and billing" member holding both roles included, is `NotAllowed`), each only on a waiting phone of the owner's own business (another business's line, an unknown line and a rejected one are `UnknownPhone`; an owner-linked or already approved phone is a new `TeamError::NotWaiting`), in one transaction with one audit row of IDs only (`businesses.phone_approved`, `businesses.phone_rejected`). Approve sets `approved_at` and calls `sender_release` (`SenderTaken` is `PhoneTaken`: nothing changes and the phone stays waiting); reject deletes the staff phone row and its held digests, so it can never work, and a later link of the same number is a new waiting phone. Also `phone_standing(conn, line)` (linked by the owner; approved) for `calls`, and `waiting_phones(conn, business)` (line and linked time, no contact detail) for T397's page. The SEC-038 step-up is the route's (T397). (5) `app/calls/src/routing.rs`: `party_of` resolves a directory hit through `sender_line`, reads `businesses::api::phone_standing` and applies `lifecycle::gate`, so `phone_works` is the production check behind the hold: a phone with no staff phone row or not yet working is `Party::Unlinked` and hears the PIN prompt, even if its digests were in the directory.
