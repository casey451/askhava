# askhava-portals: portals and the shared web security

## Purpose
The layer-8 crate for the homeowner, business and owner portals (`docs/architecture.md` section 3).

## Never break
- No cookie without the `__Host-` attributes, and no cookie survives a login: `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`. A CSRF token works only with its own cookie; a cross-site post is refused even with a good token; a browser's own post (`Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`, `Origin: null`) passes, and `Origin: null` without `Sec-Fetch-Site` is left to the token check, so every caller of `refuse_cross_site`/`is_cross_site` must check the token (D-4292): `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`.
- Every response carries the SEC-012 headers, HSTS has no `preload`: `tests/security/tests/headers.rs`. More: `(never 2)` in NOTES.md.
- Security events carry no phone, name or path, 50 of one kind in an hour raise one alert, and a steady 50 an hour raises one per hour, not one per event: `cookies_csrf.rs`. More: `(never 3)` in NOTES.md.
- Quote numbers never move; Pick and "or reply <n> YES" from #1; no sort or filter control: `src/tests/prop_arrival_order.rs`, `tests/integration/tests/quote_page.rs`. The page, packages and pick forms number by the kept shown number (`number_shown`), the forms carry the business it was shown for, and a form naming another business stores, notifies and charges nothing: `tests/integration/tests/pick_bound_forms.rs` (T441). More: `(never 4)` in NOTES.md.
- Only the Terms line beside the final Pick, link underlined in the link colour, no $100 or liability words, Go back to the card, no end time on the review step (the result may show it, PICK-045): `src/tests/pick_flow.rs`, `tests/e2e/pick_flow.spec.ts`.
- The code step: labels before hints before inputs, one paste-friendly one-time-code field, a linked focused error summary, values kept, no timer, masked call or email only: `tests/a11y/pick_forms.spec.ts`, `pick_flow::code_tests`.
- A sign-in link works once and for 15 minutes, a GET uses nothing, the session is 30 days, a removed member is signed out at once, only owner or "Confirm and billing" Confirms, and an owner session is never a business principal: `tests/security/tests/business_signin.rs`.
- A notice link opens sign-in for its own person only, a changed job, person or expiry or an expired link opens plain sign-in, another signed-in person gets plain sign-in, a bad or repeated `?job=` or an unknown query field changes nothing, no `with_notice_links` is plain sign-in, nothing but the one-time link and code signs in, Guarded by `tests/integration/tests/notice_signin_link.rs`. More: `(never 8)` in NOTES.md.
- The feedback share box is unticked on first view, has JOB-017's label, and no page offers a reward or says shown feedback is all of it: `feedback::tests`, `tests/e2e/feedback.spec.ts`.
- A guarded change without a code from the last 5 minutes is refused and logged, at 4 minutes it passes, at 5:01 it is refused: `tests/security/tests/step_up.rs`.
- Billing and CSV equal the ledger's business lines with no PDF; a billing change without a code from the last 5 minutes is refused before `payments` is called; Guarded by `business_portal::tests`. More: `(never 11)` in NOTES.md.
- The release page prints the first name once, ends the details with the tip, never holds a phone: `job_release::tests`; signed out, nothing: `tests/e2e/business_portal.spec.ts`. No page of the homeowner portal asks for an SSN, a driver's license or a tax ID image; only the right code signs her in or acts on a delete; More: `(never 12)` in NOTES.md. Served (T300): no business person gets a 401 linking to sign-in, any refusal the one 404, `no-store`: `app/server/tests/business_portal_route.rs`.
- `refuse_cross_site` is never layered over webhooks (their callers send no `Origin`).
- Owner sign-in needs both factors; 29:59 idle live, 30:01 gone; 12 hours max; an owner session idle over 30 minutes gets no `current_session` (so no owner proof); 5:00 fresh, 5:01 refused; 14-character and breached passwords refused; logout and "Sign out everywhere" end sessions; recovery codes need two factors; Guarded by `tests/security/tests/owner_auth.rs`. More: `(never 14)` in NOTES.md.
- An action link opens only its own action and object, works once, ends at its window and dies with its member: `tests/security/tests/action_links.rs`. More: `(never 15)` in NOTES.md.
- The action links are served by `server::action_links` (T301): `action_page` and `quote_form_page_priced` keep their signatures for it, and `business_actions.html` links `/static/portals/forms.css`: `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs`.
- SEC-004, SEC-027, D-3675: `confirm_link_session` signs in only the person a used Confirm link was emailed to, in the transaction that used it, and the server calls it only after that person's right Verify code: `app/server/tests/action_links_route.rs`.
- `send_sign_in_link` sends only to a current member and never past `LINKS_PER_LIFE` in `LINK_LIFE`: `tests/integration/tests/signup_mail_paths.rs` (T468).
- `decide_at_confirm` gives no free first win to a business with no sign-up row or with no launch setting, and `release_no_fee` hands the sink only `no_fee_release`'s pick for its own business and job, after the waiver committed: `tests/integration/tests/confirm_waiver.rs` (T494).
- The Confirm route charges or waives only the stored `pick_fee`, refuses a paused or moved-on pick, says "Nothing was charged" only while no attempt can succeed, and without `ConfirmSettings` charges nothing: `app/server/tests/confirm_route.rs`.
- The quote page's close button (JOB-019) posts to `CLOSE_ROUTE`, never the page's own path; only a same-site `POST` with its CSRF token closes, a final job or second press changes nothing, and no text goes: `tests/integration/tests/quote_page_close.rs`.
- BIZ-049, D-3676: the sign-in done screen adds its capability-step link only for an owner whose business has no saved capability row; every other sign-in (an owner with a saved step, a member who is not the owner) keeps the done screen as it was: `app/server/tests/business_signup_route.rs`.
- SEC-014, SEC-020, D-2634: every pick code send (final Pick, "Send a new code") passes `admit_code_send` and every shaped "Finish my pick" try passes `admit_code_try` for the client IP before any Verify call; a refusal shows the daily-cap line and calls nothing: `tests/integration/tests/pick_code_limits.rs`.
