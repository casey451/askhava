# askhava-portals: portals and the shared web security

## Purpose
The layer-8 crate for the homeowner, business and owner portals (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T051 built the shared web security every browser-facing page uses: sessions and their `__Host-` cookies, CSRF tokens, the cross-site refusal, the SEC-012 headers, `no-store` for contact and photo pages, the realm proofs for vault reads and the SEC-048 security events.

## Never break
- No cookie without the `__Host-` attributes, and no cookie survives a login: `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`. A CSRF token works only with its own cookie; a cross-site post is refused even with a good token: `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`.
- Every response carries the SEC-012 headers, HSTS has no `preload`: `tests/security/tests/headers.rs`. No third-party script, style, image, frame or font host, and the CSP names none: `tests/security/tests/no_third_party.rs`.
- Security events carry no phone, name or path, 50 of one kind in an hour raise one alert, and a steady 50 an hour raises one per hour, not one per event: `cookies_csrf.rs`. More: `(never 3)` in NOTES.md.
- Quote numbers follow arrival and never move; Pick and "or reply <n> YES" from #1; no sort or filter control: `src/tests/prop_arrival_order.rs`, `tests/integration/tests/quote_page.rs`. A withdrawn quote keeps its place with no wording or Pick button; an edit shows "Updated <time>": `tests/integration/tests/quote_shown_changes.rs`. More: `(never 4)` in NOTES.md.
- Only the Terms line beside the final Pick, link underlined in the link colour, no $100 or liability words, Go back to the card, no end time on the review step (the result may show it, PICK-045): `src/tests/pick_flow.rs`, `tests/e2e/pick_flow.spec.ts`.
- The code step: labels before hints before inputs, one paste-friendly one-time-code field, a linked focused error summary, values kept, no timer, masked call or email only: `tests/a11y/pick_forms.spec.ts`, `pick_flow::code_tests`.
- A sign-in link works once and for 15 minutes, a GET uses nothing, the session is 30 days, a removed member is signed out at once, only owner or "Confirm and billing" Confirms, and an owner session is never a business principal: `tests/security/tests/business_signin.rs`.
- A notice link opens sign-in for its own person only, a changed job, person or expiry or an expired link opens plain sign-in, another signed-in person gets plain sign-in, a bad or repeated `?job=` or an unknown query field changes nothing, no `with_notice_links` is plain sign-in, nothing but the one-time link and code signs in, Guarded by `tests/integration/tests/notice_signin_link.rs`. More: `(never 8)` in NOTES.md.
- The feedback share box is unticked on first view, has JOB-017's label, and no page offers a reward or says shown feedback is all of it: `feedback::tests`, `tests/e2e/feedback.spec.ts`.
- A guarded change without a code from the last 5 minutes is refused and logged, at 4 minutes it passes, at 5:01 it is refused: `tests/security/tests/step_up.rs`.
- Billing and CSV equal the ledger's business lines with no PDF; a billing change without a code from the last 5 minutes is refused before `payments` is called; a complaint shows no job or homeowner detail and its box closes at exactly 5 days: `business_portal::tests`; signed out, nothing: `tests/e2e/business_billing.spec.ts`.
- The release page prints the first name once, ends the details with the tip, never holds a phone: `job_release::tests`; signed out, nothing: `tests/e2e/business_portal.spec.ts`. No page of the homeowner portal asks for an SSN, a driver's license or a tax ID image; only the right code signs her in or acts on a delete; More: `(never 12)` in NOTES.md.
- `refuse_cross_site` is never layered over webhooks (their callers send no `Origin`).
- Owner sign-in needs both factors; 29:59 idle live, 30:01 gone; 12 hours max; an owner session idle over 30 minutes gets no `current_session` (so no owner proof); 5:00 fresh, 5:01 refused; 14-character and breached passwords refused; logout and "Sign out everywhere" end sessions; recovery codes need two factors; the bootstrap runs once and the app role cannot insert an owner; Guarded by `tests/security/tests/owner_auth.rs`. More: `(never 14)` in NOTES.md.
- An action link opens only its own action and object, works once, ends at its window and dies with its member: `tests/security/tests/action_links.rs`. No quote fact is optional, the earliest visit is never pre-filled, and every quote type shows its fee before submit: `tests/integration/tests/business_actions.rs`. More: `(never 15)` in NOTES.md.
