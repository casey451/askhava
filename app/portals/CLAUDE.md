# askhava-portals: portals and the shared web security

## Purpose

The layer-8 crate for the homeowner, business and owner portals (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T051 built the shared web security every browser-facing page uses: sessions and their `__Host-` cookies, CSRF tokens, the cross-site refusal, the SEC-012 headers, `no-store` for contact and photo pages, the realm proofs for vault reads and the SEC-048 security events. T052 added the homeowner's quote page (D-1233 to D-1236); T055 the pick review step and the pick result page (D-1248 to D-1252); T224 the business Confirm review step's page and press answers (D-1479), whose served route waits on D-1480. T246 the page pick end to end (D-1516, D-1602 to D-1606). T084 business sign-in by emailed link and the business principal (D-1724 to D-1726). T194 the new-device code (D-1773 to D-1777). T230 the private feedback page after Done (D-1820 to D-1823). T085 the business portal home, the SEC-038 step-up gate and the released job's page (D-1896 to D-1900). T195 the billing page, the CSV statement, the guarded billing changes and the complaints page (D-1912 to D-1916). T086 owner sign-in (D-1917 to D-1921). T087 the homeowner portal and "Download my data" (D-1934 to D-1939). Not here yet: the Confirm-link code (T148), the served owner and homeowner routes, homeowner address and language changes (D-1935), action links (T148), the PICK-045 text and end time after a page pick (D-1606), the emergency switch on the review step (D-1249), the quote page's close button, STOP's still-open line, card facts and names, Spanish (D-1236).

## Rules it enforces

- SEC-009, D-099: every cookie is `__Host-` with `Secure`, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Path=/`, no `Domain` (`session::host_cookie`); a form carries `csrf_token` and is read through `CsrfForm`.
- SEC-042, D-480: `start_session` deletes the browser's earlier session and drops the pre-session cookie, so no pre-login cookie survives a login; `refuse_cross_site` refuses a POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE whose `Sec-Fetch-Site` is not `same-origin`, whose `Origin` is not `https://` on the `Host` (plain `http://` is refused), or that has neither; forms without a session are bound to `__Host-askhava-pre` (`ensure_pre_session`).
- SEC-011: autoescaping on in every template; `scripts/check_rust_rules.py` bans the escapes; `security::tests` renders `<script>` through `base.html` as text.
- SEC-012, SEC-054, D-492: `security_headers` (layered outermost in `server::build_router`) sends the CSP, one-year HSTS with `includeSubDomains` and no `preload`, the Permissions-Policy, `nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` on every response, replacing any a handler set; a page-specific CSP (Stripe on Confirm, a nonce) needs that layer changed first (D-946).
- SEC-013: the CSP allows the app's own origin only; templates and static files load no other host (Stripe.js on the Confirm page is the one exception, FEE-014, which that page's task adds to its own CSP).
- SEC-034: `no_store` on every page with homeowner contact details or job photos.
- SEC-046: `Session::homeowner_proof` and `owner_proof` mint a `privacy` proof only for a session of that realm; business sessions get none. An owner session's `portals.session.expires_at` is kept at its 30-minute idle end (never past 12 hours) by `owner_sign_in` and `owner_principal`, so `current_session` refuses an idle one too (D-1917).
- SEC-048, D-943, D-945: `record_security_event` logs one `security event` line (kind, route template, request ID, actor reference) and stores a row; a refusal's actor reference is the signed-in session's row ID, never a phone or name. The event that makes its kind's count over the last hour at least `EVENTS_PER_HOUR_ALERT` (50) raises one High `ops` alert `portals.<kind>_burst` unless an event of that kind in the last hour already did (`raised_alert`), so a steady attack raises at most one per kind per hour. An advisory lock per kind serializes the count.
- PICK-008, D-084, D-1233: `number_quotes` numbers `quotes::api::page_quotes` by `received_at` then business ID; an unavailable quote keeps its number, shows "no longer available" and has no Pick button.

SEC-014, SEC-020, SEC-040, PICK-055, D-1837, D-1950: the `limits` checks count requests per IP, per business or number and for everyone, never links or code tries (T272's caps in `business_auth.rs` stay); keys are IPs from `ClientIpPolicy::client_ip` or record IDs, never a phone or email; the first refusal of a scope per window raises one High alert.

## Never break

- No cookie without the `__Host-` attributes, and no cookie survives a login: `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`.
- A CSRF token works only with its own cookie; a cross-site post is refused even with a good token: `tests/security/tests/cookies_csrf.rs`.
- Every response carries the SEC-012 headers, HSTS has no `preload`: `tests/security/tests/headers.rs`.
- No third-party script, style, image, frame or font host, and the CSP names none: `tests/security/tests/no_third_party.rs`.
- Security events carry no phone, name or path, 50 of one kind in an hour raise one alert, and a steady 50 an hour raises one per hour, not one per event: `cookies_csrf.rs`.
- Quote numbers follow arrival and never move; Pick and "or reply <n> YES" from #1; no sort or filter control: `src/tests/prop_arrival_order.rs`, `tests/integration/tests/quote_page.rs`.
- Token 30-day edge, 404 for re-issued, revoked and malformed tokens, noindex, no contact, cross-site POST refused: `tests/security/tests/quote_token.rs`.
- The quote page's fixed lines, no rating or summary element, empty status region, still-open line: `tests/integration/tests/quote_page.rs`.
- Only the Terms line beside the final Pick, link underlined in the link colour, no $100 or liability words, Go back to the card, no end time on the review step (the result may show it, PICK-045): `src/tests/pick_flow.rs`, `tests/e2e/pick_flow.spec.ts`.
- The code step: labels before hints before inputs, one paste-friendly one-time-code field, a linked focused error summary, values kept, no timer, masked call or email only: `tests/a11y/pick_forms.spec.ts`, `pick_flow::code_tests`.
- A sign-in link works once and for 15 minutes, a GET uses nothing, the session is 30 days, a removed member is signed out at once, only owner or "Confirm and billing" Confirms, and an owner session is never a business principal: `tests/security/tests/business_signin.rs`.
- A new device signs in only with a correct code sent to that person's own phone or address, never by a call, and a used link's token or another member's device never skips the code: `tests/integration/tests/new_device_code.rs`.
- The feedback share box is unticked on first view, has JOB-017's label, and no page offers a reward or says shown feedback is all of it: `feedback::tests`, `tests/e2e/feedback.spec.ts`.
- A guarded change without a code from the last 5 minutes is refused and logged, at 4 minutes it passes, at 5:01 it is refused: `tests/security/tests/step_up.rs`.

- The release page prints the first name once, ends the details with the tip, never holds a phone: `job_release::tests`; signed out, nothing: `tests/e2e/business_portal.spec.ts`. No page of the homeowner portal asks for an SSN, a driver's license or a tax ID image; only the right code signs her in or acts on a delete; the download holds only her items: `homeowner_portal::tests`, `data_download::tests`; signed out, nothing: `tests/e2e/homeowner_portal.spec.ts`.

## How to change

- Add a portal page: a template in `templates/` that `{% extends "base.html" %}` and fills `title` and `main`; its router gets `from_fn_with_state(security, refuse_cross_site)`; every POST handler takes `CsrfForm<YourForm>` (with `#[serde(default)] csrf`) and the page puts `csrf_token(&jar)` in a hidden `csrf` field; pages with contact details or photos add `from_fn(no_store)`.
- Scope a business route: call `business_principal(db, &jar, now)` first and refuse unless `principal.may(business, need)` (Confirm: `may_confirm`); never take a business ID from the browser as proof.
- Serve business sign-in: implement `SignInMail` and `CodeAddress` in `server`, build the state with `SignIn::new(..).with_device_codes(DeviceCodes::new(verify, code_address, key))` (always: without it the link alone signs in, D-1777) (the address directory and a sign-in email in `messaging`) then in `server` write `.route(SIGNIN_ROUTE, get(signin_form).post(signin_request::<M>))` and `.route(SIGNIN_LINK_ROUTE, get(signin_open::<M>).post(signin_post::<M>))` and pass them to `SignIn::new(..).into_router(routes)`; the same change adds both pages' rows to `PAGE_STATES` in `tests/a11y/fixtures.ts` (A11Y-002, D-1239, D-1728).
- Log a sign-in failure, a denial or a webhook signature failure: `record_security_event` with the route template and an actor ID, never the phone or the path.
- Change the CSP or a header: `security.rs` constants, then `headers.rs` and `no_third_party.rs` (`cargo test -p askhava-security --test headers --test no_third_party`).
- Change a quote-page line: `templates/quote_page.html` (the fixed lines change only with the spec), then `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test quote_page` and `python3 scripts/check_copy.py`; status words are the `data-one`/`data-many` attributes, never in `quote_page.ts`.
- Change the pick flow: `src/pick_flow.rs` and `templates/pick_review.html` (PICK-017's two sentences, the Terms line and PICK-054's lines are spec words), then `cargo test -p askhava-portals tests::pick_flow` and `npx playwright test --config tests/e2e/playwright.config.ts tests/e2e/pick_flow.spec.ts`; the code step replaces `final_pick`'s "Nothing was picked" render and calls `result_page` with the Confirm window's end time (T187, T063).
- Add to a quote card: a field on `quotes::api::PageQuote` (`app/quotes/src/api.rs`) or the card read, then the template; check `tsc --noEmit` and `eslint` for `static/quote_page.ts`.
- Add a table or column: a new migration (`-- portals (T###)` first line), regenerate `.sqlx/` (online build with `SQLX_OFFLINE_DIR=.sqlx`).
- Serve the homeowner portal (after T089's Verify caps): in `server`, `GET`/`POST HOMEOWNER_SIGNIN_ROUTE` (`send_sign_in_code`, then `finish_sign_in`), `GET HOMEOWNER_HOME_ROUTE` (`current_session`, `homeowner_proof`, `OnFile::read`, `homeowner_page`), `POST TEXTS_OFF_ROUTE`, `GET DOWNLOAD_ROUTE` and `POST DATA_REQUEST_ROUTE`, all with `refuse_cross_site`, `CsrfForm` and `no_store`; add the `PAGE_STATES` rows and sign in from `tests/e2e/homeowner_portal.spec.ts`. Sign a person in: after the realm's checks pass, `start_session(db, jar, (realm, actor_id), lifetime, now)` with that realm's lifetime (SEC-005, SEC-008); return the jar with the response.
- Serve owner sign-in: in `server`, a `GET`/`POST` on `OWNER_SIGNIN_ROUTE` with `refuse_cross_site`, `CsrfForm` and `no_store`; the GET renders `owner_signin_page(csrf, passkey_registered(..), false)` and stores nothing; `POST OWNER_PASSKEY_ROUTE` (CSRF, `no_store`) answers `{"challenge", "options"}` from `passkey_sign_in_options`; the sign-in POST calls `owner_sign_in` and on `Refused` re-renders with `failed`; serve `static/passkey.js`; add the `PAGE_STATES` row. Every pause, refund and release handler calls `owner_power` first; every `OwnerAction` handler calls `require_owner_step_up`.
