# askhava-quotes: who receives each job card, the cards and the quotes

## Purpose
The layer-5 crate that decides who receives each job card, and later holds the cards and the quotes (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T044 built the matching: a card goes to every active, verified business serving the job's Arizona offer towns whose licenses cover every candidate job type and which ticked one, with no cap (D-1013 to D-1015).

## Never break
- PRIV-008, PRIV-006, CARD-007, D-3189: the store takes only a `ScrubbedText`, never logs or sends its words or card text to an AI call, stores a card only under its own text's digest and returns one only while the digest still matches; Guarded by `tests/integration/tests/job_text_store.rs`. More: `(never 1)` in NOTES.md.
- A class name is read only by the license map: ROC cover comes from `ClassMap::covers`, never from code: `src/tests/matching_examples.rs::td_lic_02_matching_examples_m01_to_m16`.
- Every candidate must be covered, not just one: `src/tests/matching_examples.rs::td_lic_02_every_candidate_must_be_covered_and_the_town_served`. A card goes exactly when qualified, neighbouring towns by the same rule, no cap: `src/tests/prop_matching_props.rs`.
- One card row per job and business: `src/tests/matching_examples.rs::td_lic_02_fifty_qualified_businesses_get_fifty_cards`.
- No status is compared outside `businesses`' `status.rs` or `jobs`' `status.rs`; ask `receives_cards` and `takes_late_cards` (SEC-031).
- No contact survives a card, the CARD-009 line replaces fewer than 5 words, and the gate runs last: `src/tests/prop_card_no_contact.rs`. No restricted phrase survives the gate and the log holds only the entry; the Spanish line only for Spanish speakers; the sent and fee-free lines: `src/tests/card_lines.rs`.
- Overflow only when no checklist business gets the card; pauses send nothing: `src/tests/overflow.rs`.
- A late card goes only in Open or No quotes yet, with its own send time: `tests/integration/tests/card_late_eligible.rs`. A quote is taken only inside its card's 24 hours and before Confirm; an unconfirmed quote is never shown; the all-quotes-in text is queued once, after the last window: `tests/integration/tests/quote_window.rs`.
- Contact in a quote bounces, and a bounce line repeats nothing the business wrote: `src/tests/quote_bounce.rs`. Contact split across lines, tabs or no-break spaces bounces, while "2 coats\n3 days", "2\n3\n10" and a list of sizes or prices with no phone-shaped stretch pass: `tests/integration/tests/quote_whole_field_gate.rs`.
- The business's preview is the homeowner's card, character for character, and the night line moves nothing: `tests/integration/tests/quote_card.rs`, `src/tests/quote_card.rs::td_pick_44_after_hours_line_only_when_ticked`. No summary while the setting is off, none that adds a word or flips or moves a claim: `src/tests/quote_card.rs::td_pick_29_summary_off_by_default_and_never_adds_a_claim`.
- A setup charge never changes whether a quote passes: `src/tests/quote_parse.rs::td_fee_27_setup_never_counts`.
- No photo reaches the screen before zero retention or without her confirmation; any flag keeps it off: `tests/integration/tests/card_photos.rs`.
- A job page opens only for that job's card holder, shows no contact she typed and keeps her other words: `tests/integration/tests/job_page.rs`.
- Every quote-update template passes the TXT-040 scan; a 21-word sentence fails: `src/tests/update_texts.rs`. No customer-visible line here writes the brand (T293, D-1932, D-2393): the card words, the contact bounce, `notify`'s joined-text opening (`<short_name>: ` of the reader's language) and the card email's job-page link read `core::api::brand()`; More: `(never 14)` in NOTES.md.

A per-visit price stores its schedule and "Other" a monthly price: `tests/integration/tests/quote_formats.rs`. More: `(never 15)` in NOTES.md.
- A withdrawn quote keeps its number and is never pickable or shown with its words; LIST numbers are page numbers; an edit shows "Updated <time>" on page and card; all withdrawn is no LIST text: `tests/integration/tests/quote_shown_changes.rs`. Updates held to 2:59:59, sent at 3:00:00: `src/tests/prop_quote_texts.rs`, `quote_texts.rs::td_txt_06_first_quote_then_updates_at_most_every_three_hours`. More: `(never 16)` in NOTES.md.
