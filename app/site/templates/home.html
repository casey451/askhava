How it works

Start in the chat on this site. You don't need to know which trade to ask for.

    Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.

How we keep it fair

Your number stays with us. Businesses see the job, not you. Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick.

Nobody pays to be first. Quotes show in the order they came in. {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} doesn't rank or rate businesses, and no business can pay for a better spot.

Asking costs you nothing. {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} is a quote-request service; the business you choose pays {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} a set introduction fee. For businesses

Quote for free. Pay only if a homeowner picks you.

When a homeowner sends a job you do, we email you the details and you decide whether to quote. Every card says how many businesses got it. You quote once. No bidding rounds, no paid placement.

If the homeowner picks you, you pay a fixed introduction fee by job size, never a share of the job.

${{ fees.small }} for a quote under $500. ${{ fees.medium }} for $500 to $1,500. ${{ fees.large }} for over $1,500.

Launch offer: your first win is free, on any job size, when you join in the first 90 days or within 90 days after your trade opens.
