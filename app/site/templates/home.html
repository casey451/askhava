How it works

  1. Tell us what you need. Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.
  2. Havasu businesses quote. Your request goes to Havasu businesses that take this kind of job. Each one has 24 hours to quote. Every quote lists the same details: the price, what's included and when they can come. Not every business answers.
  3. You pick one. Check their license and reviews, then choose. You hire the business directly. If nothing fits, you don't have to pick anyone.

How we keep it fair

Your number stays with us. Businesses see the job, not you. Your number is never shared. Your first name and address go only to the business you pick.

Nobody pays to be first. Quotes show in the order they came in. {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} doesn't rank or rate businesses, and no business can pay for a better spot.
