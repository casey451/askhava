Help for businesses

Safety when you work alone

If you go into customers' homes alone, these habits can keep you safer.

  • Before you go, tell someone where the job is and when you expect to finish.
  • Keep your phone charged and on you.
  • If something feels wrong, you can leave. You don't have to finish the job.
  • If anyone is in danger, call 911.
