How it works

Tell us about a job you need done. Havasu businesses that do that work can quote, and you choose.

1. Tell us what you need

Answer a few short questions and add a photo if you have one. Unless it's an emergency, you see the request before it goes out. Your name and number are never on it.

2. Havasu businesses quote

Your request goes to Havasu businesses that take this kind of job. Each one has 24 hours to quote. Every quote lists the same details: the price, what's included and when they can come. Not every business answers.

3. You pick one

Check their license and reviews, then choose. You hire the business directly. If nothing fits, you don't have to pick anyone.

What it costs

The business you choose pays {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} a set introduction fee (${{ fees.small }}, ${{ fees.medium }} or ${{ fees.large }}, by quote size). You pay {{ askhava_core::api::brand().display_name() }} nothing.

If none of the quotes suit you, you can close the request.

Start a request

Start in the chat on this site. It works any time, with or without texting.
