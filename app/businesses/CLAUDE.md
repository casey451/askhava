# askhava-businesses: business accounts and the records matching reads

## Purpose
The layer-4 crate that holds each business account (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T041 built the account status and its moves, the towns, the job-type checklist within the license, the overflow switch, the Spanish tick, the business-reported insurance answer and the after-hours tick with the night count (D-112).

## Never break
- Every identity code goes to a record contact the caller read from `licensing`, never a typed one, and never by voice: `RecordContacts` has no typed field and `send_code` uses only `CodeChannel::Sms` and `Email` (`tests/integration/tests/identity_chain.rs::td_biz_01_code_and_match_activate_without_approval`). More: `(never 1)` in NOTES.md.
- No Stripe call inside the sign-up transaction (R11); a failed customer create returns `customer_ready: false` for a retry of `ensure_customer`, never a second business.
- `businesses.terms_acceptance` is append-only for `askhava_app`: `td_terms_08_business_terms_acceptance_is_stored`. TERMS-033 (T232): a Confirm press stores one `confirm_page` row per pick with the assent line the page shows, in the caller's transaction; More: `(never 3)` in NOTES.md.
- A status moves only through `status::next`: `src/tests/account_status.rs::td_biz_02_each_status_reachable_only_by_its_rules`. Casey's approval alone never activates (BIZ-003): `src/tests/account_status.rs::td_biz_02_owner_approval_alone_never_activates`. Insurance No never pauses: `src/tests/account_status.rs::td_biz_02_ticks_insurance_and_pause_are_stored`.
- A paused or unticked business never counts at night: `src/tests/account_status.rs::night_count_counts_active_after_hours_businesses`.
- Own quiet times never widen the window, in code and in the table: `src/tests/own_quiet_times.rs::td_qh_03_own_quiet_times_only_narrow_the_window`, `td_qh_03_the_table_refuses_a_wider_window`.
- A business never resumes itself from a pause it did not choose, also after a rule paused it on top of its own pause: `src/tests/own_quiet_times.rs::td_biz_02_business_pauses_and_resumes_itself`, `td_biz_02_a_rule_pause_replaces_a_self_pause`. More: `(never 7)` in NOTES.md.
- A business row is never deleted by the app: `src/tests/account_status.rs::the_app_role_cannot_delete_a_business`. BIZ-054 (T228): the reason is stored once per business (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), optional, the box only beside "Something else"; More: `(never 8)` in NOTES.md.
- LIC-043 holds only as far as the caller's `allowed` set is right: `checklist` and `set_job_types` refuse anything outside it but cannot check the set against the license (D-954); every caller must build it from the license map plus the no-license job types.
- The owner always holds both roles and is never removed: `src/tests/team.rs::td_biz_34_the_owner_always_holds_both_roles`. Removing a member unlinks its phones and queues one end-sessions event: `src/tests/team.rs::td_biz_34_removal_unlinks_both_phones`, `tests/integration/tests/team_members.rs`. More: `(never 10)` in NOTES.md.
- A phone a member other than the owner links or changes is in no sender-directory lookup until the owner approves the number they were shown, and never once declined (BIZ-033, D-3081): `tests/integration/tests/phone_approval.rs` (TD-BIZ-52).
- Known gaps (D-963): a member who loses "Confirm and billing" keeps the phones it linked; audit rows name the business, not the acting member; no task sends BIZ-023's owner email yet.
- A team member row is never deleted by the app: `src/tests/team.rs::the_app_role_cannot_delete_a_team_member`.
- No contact value in any table, audit row or error (PRIV-001, PRIV-028); contacts live in `privacy`. No customer-visible line here writes the brand (T293, D-1932, D-2392, D-2393): the consent, welcome, STOP, recruiting, waitlist, fee-box, complaint, phone-link, About us and returned-address lines read `core::api::brand()` (a text opens `<short_name>:`, Spanish names from `es`; Guarded by `tests/docs/test_brand_literals.py`. More: `(never 13)` in NOTES.md.
- The consent box is never pre-ticked and no call consent can be stored: `tests/integration/tests/business_consent.rs::td_con_08_box_starts_unticked_and_names_each_channel`, `td_con_08_consent_is_stored_with_version_and_time`.
- A business STOP never pauses the account: `td_con_08_business_stop_stops_texts_only`. A changed notice address gets nothing until verified and the old address is told: `tests/security/tests/notice_addresses.rs`. Activation waits for the sign-up email: `tests/integration/tests/email_verification.rs::td_biz_13_the_signup_email_is_verified_before_activation`. More: `(never 15)` in NOTES.md.
- Known gap (D-1846): a sign-up with no `mailbox_check` row is not held; the sign-up route must call `start_signup_check` first.
- Backups and the release: `tests/security/tests/backup_address.rs` (TD-BIZ-19, TD-SEC-17); a backup is refused at confirmation when its mailbox is by then a current member's main address, a later `add_member` cannot make a verified backup a main address, and each mailbox is listed once: `tests/security/tests/backup_address_confirm.rs` (TD-BIZ-44); More: `(never 17)` in NOTES.md.
- Known gaps (D-1715, D-3186): the card sender checks `may_get_cards` (T251's `quotes::card_pool`), business text sends do not yet check `may_text`, and the composition registers the welcome and STOP handlers with no mailer (no Resend client, no readable business address or phone digits), so those tasks retry and no activated business gets cards until a task passes one. Guarded by `tests/integration/tests/business_consent_wiring.rs`. More: `(never 18)` in NOTES.md.
