# askhava-businesses: business accounts and the records matching reads

## Purpose

The layer-4 crate that holds each business account (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T041 built the account status and its moves, the towns, the job-type checklist within the license, the overflow switch, the Spanish tick, the business-reported insurance answer and the after-hours tick with the night count (D-112). T220 added team members with their roles, the main and staff phones kept in `messaging`'s sender directory, and `notice_addresses`. T182 added the self-pause and the business's own quiet times. T081 added sign-up, the identity chain with automatic activation, the About us screen and the business terms acceptance. T082 added the E-SIGN consent records, the welcome request and gate, the business STOP email request, the auto-reply rule and the recruiting-email rules. T083 added complaints (the BIZ-011 reply, the 5-day reply box, the 3-in-90 pause, the safety alert and the digest listing) and the owner's email request when another member links a phone (BIZ-033). T196 added verified notice addresses, the address-change step-up and the identity episode (D-1846 to D-1850). T085 added each person's open-cards email times and the shared step-up check (D-1899, D-1900). T149 added the sign-up inbox check, the sample pick step and each person's backup notice address (D-1987 to D-1990). T150 added the "Emergency emails at any hour" switch, its test emergency email and the after-hours tick's conditions with the covered night count (D-2008, D-2010). T232 added the Confirm page's business-terms record (D-3070).
