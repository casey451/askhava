# askhava-intake: requests, conversations and homeowner consent

## Purpose

The layer-7 crate that turns a homeowner's inbound text into a request (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T038 built the first reply: the consent text first on every path, after the 911 text on a 911-list match, never asking for a reply and never sent twice for one request, and the `intake` tables. T181 added the consent record, the card and text gates and the homeowner terms acceptance at a pick. T152 added the one INT-004 911 text's words, quiet-hours kind and 5-minute check. T039 added the 911-list stem matcher on every message, the SAFE hold, the INT-037 question, "How soon?", the INT-033 night count, the emergency-or-regular choice and the emergency release. T040 added what opens a job, duplicates, the join window, exact answers, the name-and-address question and the under-18 reply. T042 added the keyword second opinion, the trade question rules, the INT-010 after-window word share and INT-032's two-trade detection (pure; not yet wired into the conversation, D-999). T183 added "Which fits best?" across licenses and its reply (`Candidates::fit_step`, D-1003). T048 added the no-reply reminder and close, the LIST reply and the call-back clock (`handlers.rs`, D-1195 to D-1198). T240 added the `jobs.homeowner_text` sender (`job_texts.rs`, D-1301 to D-1303). T049 added closing at the homeowner's word: CLOSE, the close button, cancel intent and "Close which?" (`closing.rs`, D-1200 to D-1203). T050 added an opt-out's live jobs: no status change, the CANCEL pause and the CON-028 event (`service::on_opt_out`, D-1228, D-1229). T054 added `answer_text_pick` (the "<number> YES" entry into `picks`) and the CANCEL pause's withdrawal of a waiting pick (D-1245, D-1246). T156 added the question set one question at a time, routing confidence and the golden run (D-1269 to D-1273; pure, not yet wired into the conversation). T208 added the INT-041 card approval step: the card shown by text or link, EDIT or SEND, the 30-minute auto-send and the approved-card check (`summary_step.rs`, D-1274 to D-1279; no caller until the D-999 dispatcher and the card sender). Not here yet: the conversation wiring of the question order, the `two_jobs` text and the conversation wiring of T042's rules (no owning task yet, a plan gap for the runner, D-999), the conversation wiring of routing and the coverage line (T043, T184 built, below), web chat (T103).

## Rules it enforces

- CON-001, D-008, D-131: `rules::plan`: a contact with no open request gets `Send::Safety` first on a 911-list match, then `Send::Consent`; an opt-out or a HELP, INFO or AYUDA text (already answered by `messaging`) gets nothing and opens nothing.
- CON-002, CON-003, CON-004, CON-031: the `consent` template is the exact CON-031 text: every CON-002 line, both links, no fee line, no "Reply OK" or "Reply YES".
- CON-026, TXT-039, D-065: `consent_language`: Spanish only when `wanted_language` says Spanish (`reads_spanish`: two words from its list or ñ, ¿, ¡; or the AI reading) and Spanish conversations are on.
- CON-027: `plan` on an open request gives `Then::IntakeReply` (agreement words record nothing) or `Then::DiscloseThenAsk` when `asks_who`; `service::disclosure` renders the CON-027 line for T040 to put before the next question. The rendered line already starts "Ask Hava: "; T040 joins it to the question without a second "Ask Hava:" prefix (D-907). Consent goes once per request: one open conversation per homeowner (`ON CONFLICT ... DO NOTHING`), the outbox key over template, contact and conversation ID (TXT-011).
- CALL-022, D-384: `consent_template(true)` picks `consent_safety_call` only while the safety voice call is on.
- INT-004, D-436: one fixed 911 text, `emergency_911`, in English or Spanish (`safety_text`; its Spanish template is `spanish_stays_on`, so a Spanish match gets Spanish even while Spanish conversations are off); it is never held by quiet hours (`safety_outgoing`, QH-004) and must go within `SAFETY_WITHIN` of the message (`sent_in_time`); it holds no number but 911 and no cause, symptom, health, heat, water, shutoff, utility or appliance advice. No safety-contacts file exists (D-350 withdrawn by D-436); never add a local or utility number to any text or page.
- INT-002, INT-003, INT-026, D-165, D-436, D-980: `handle` (behind `receive_text`/`receive_answer`) runs `emergency::check` first on every message and replaces the caller's `Inbound::emergency` flag with its result; street-address patterns (at most 3 capitalized name words and a house number before a suffix, never a joining or hazard word, D-982) are removed first and the name-and-address answer (`Asked::NameAndAddress`) is never checked. A match queues the 911 text (before the consent text on a new request, in the message's language) and writes an `intake.emergency_event` row with `text_queued_at` and the `intake.emergency_matched` audit row.
- INT-005, INT-024, INT-025, D-982: `safe_hold` is true while any 911 text of the request waits for SAFE; a match while it waits sends no second 911 text (one per hold) and keeps the hold; a safe reply (safe, seguro, segura, we're safe/ok/fine, plain OK or Yes; never a question or a negated reply: "not safe", "I don't feel safe", "is it safe to go back in?") sets `safe_at` and the request continues; nothing is sent after SAFE. Card tasks call `safe_hold` as well as `card_allowed`.
- INT-037, D-361: a smoke or CO alarm said only to beep gets `beep_question` (after the consent text on a new request) and a `beep_question` event; the next reply is stored once as `emergency_beep` (`alarm` or `chirp`); `alarm` sends the 911 text and holds the cards. A chirp, or a beep with a battery word, is a normal request.
- INT-030, INT-006, INT-033: `answer_how_soon` stores `how_soon` (`today`, `few_days`, `no_rush`, `by_day`); `by_day` always asks `needed_by_day`; `today`, or any other answer after a 911 match answered SAFE, offers the choice (while a 911 text waits for SAFE nothing is offered, `Answered::choice_after_safe` is set and the conversation calls `offer_emergency_choice` after SAFE; negated answers such as "not urgent" never read as Today, D-982) through `emergency_count::offer` (by day `emergency_choice` alone; 8 p.m. to 8 a.m. Arizona the count text first; a count of 0 sends only `night_count_none` and stores `emergency_choice = regular`).
- INT-029, D-164: `answer_choice` stores `emergency_choice`; `release_emergency` enqueues one `intake.emergency_released` task (dedupe key `intake.emergency_released.<request>`) only with consent, no SAFE hold, the emergency choice and an `intake.property` row (the ZIP and area check); the conversation calls it again once the ZIP is in. `quotes` handles the event.
- JOB-005: `on_text` writes the `intake.consent_sent` audit row in the transaction that opens the request and queues its texts.
- CON-005, D-933: `receive_text` writes one `intake.consent_record` (channel `sms`, inbox ID, arrival time, version `<template>:<language>:<12 hex of the SHA256 of the rendered text>`) only when the text queued the consent text; replies, opt-outs and HELP record nothing. `on_text` alone writes no record (T038's `consent_records_are_append_only_for_the_app_role` counts on that), so other crates call `receive_text` only.
- CON-007, CON-021: `card_allowed(conversation)` is true only with a consent record; `may_text(contact)` only for a homeowner with one (tenants and on-site contacts never have one). Card and send tasks call them first. `card_allowed` gates text-delivered cards only; the CON-007 web-chat request with no verified mobile number is T103's to allow. `may_text` covers homeowner request texts only and ignores opt-outs; HELP and opt-out confirmations and the suppression list are the send path's.
- TERMS-001, TERMS-016, TERMS-033, D-934: only a pick calls `record_terms_acceptance`; intake hashes the published text itself; the same pick twice returns `false` and stores nothing more; an unknown request is `IntakeError::UnknownRequest`.
- INT-007, INT-008, D-138, D-994: `opens_job` is false for one or two words and for any cancel-like message; an emergency keyword opens at any length.
- INT-009, INT-010, D-993: one active job per homeowner, property and trade; a follow-up strictly inside the window joins the newest job even as another trade.
- INT-011, INT-012: answers count only for an open request and match whole words ("ok" is not in "broke"); free-answer questions take any words and store only the code `text`.
- AI-011, R-37, D-998: `keyword_reading` takes the longest phrase first, so "water heater" is plumbing and "pool heater"/"spa heater" pool repair, never the furnace rule's "heater".
- AI-011, INT-013, D-104: `matching::second_opinion` asks only when the readings differ or the keywords name trades without the readings' one; the question offers every distinct trade, readings first, up to three.
- INT-012, INT-015, D-103: `TradeQuestion::read` matches whole words only ("repair" never matches heating/AC); generic label words ("repair", "replacement") name no option; "Not sure" counts only when no option is named ("no sé" at most three words); `step` re-asks once with the same options, then applies `fallback` (the keyword trade if exactly one matched, else the first reading); "Not sure" applies it at once (D-998).
- CARD-001, D-102, D-105: `matching::candidates` keeps the chosen trade's keyword job types (never `<trade>_other` when one was named) and every license-boundary keyword job type; `which_fits` lists the trade's job types or the cross-trade candidates.
- INT-014, CARD-001, D-1003: candidates across trades fall under different licenses (no license-map class covers two keyword trades), so `which_fits` is set; the trade-only licensed phrases "water heater", "pool heater", "spa heater" add the catalog's licensed-only job type ("water heater replacement", "gas pool heater"; `KeywordReading::licensed_only`, never listed by `job_types_of`) whatever other job type of the trade is named; with no job type of the answered trade named, the list is that trade's job types (or `<trade>_other`) then every other trade's candidate, never fewer than two options; `Candidates::fit_step` takes a lone number or exactly one option's words (a number and words naming a different option are unusable), re-asks once with the same list, then keeps every candidate; a choice leaves that job type alone under its own trade.
- INT-010, INT-032, D-485, D-493: `rules::follow_up_joins` (from 2:00:00 a same-trade message joins only when `shares_words` holds); `matching::two_trades`, `starts_second` (ALSO or TAMBIÉN alone).
- INT-027, D-146, D-238, D-995: the name-and-address question follows the area check, and its raw answer goes to the vault only, never to the AI.
- INT-038, D-385, D-994: an under-18 sender gets one `under_18` reply and the request closes; under 13 the data is deleted except the consent record.
- INT-039, CARD-007, AI-019, D-1269: `BankRun` asks in set order, applies `ask_if`, asks only `emergency` questions on an emergency job (after the cards, `starts_now`), never more than 8 prompts, "Not sure"/"No sé" last on every bank list; AI off: templates word for word, skip only by `keyword_answer`; AI on: rewording and the one `kb_trade` follow-up only through `ai::api::accept_question`, options always the bank's, skip only at a reading at or above `skip_threshold` (unset, so none, D-492). A smell answer of unstated kind asks the exact smell question once; 1 or 2 is `Heard::Safety911` (the caller sends INT-004 and holds). INT-040, AI-017, D-1270, D-1272: `decide` goes on only on agreement at or above the high setting, else the INT-013 question; `after_fit` marks a kept-all job `NOT_CERTAIN`; `quotes::api::rules::match_job` with all candidates reaches only covering-all businesses; `unsure_reach` gives `NoneCovers`. No class is read here.
- OPS-040, D-493, D-1271: `run_golden` stores one `intake.golden_result` per `catalog_version`; `golden_results` is T210's read.
- INT-016, INT-017, INT-022, LIC-017, LIC-018, D-1008: `route` checks the area first (outside the towns list: Closed early, no demand row), then moves outside Arizona, then home watch rent or leasing words, then a trade that is not open; only the last writes a recruiting-demand row; `apply` is safe to run twice.
- INT-017, INT-032, D-1019, D-1020: `tell_coverage` queues the count line (N from `quotes::api::coverage`, the singular for 1) or the SPLIT offer, and on `Coverage::NotCovered` moves only the told job to Not covered (plus a demand row) while another job of the request is live, else runs `apply` with `Why::NobodyCovers` (D-1020); `split_request` opens the second trade's job on SPLIT or DIVIDIR alone and does not check that a SPLIT offer was made: the conversation wiring must call it only for the reply to a `Coverage::Split` offer (D-1020). A job already final gets no count line or SPLIT offer.
- INT-019, D-1195, D-807: every new job gets a reminder task at `texting_window(timer_from + 24h)` and a close at `timer_from + 72h`; `hear` restarts each Intake job's wait and queues a new pair; a step runs only while the job is in Intake with the same `timer_from` (the close also when Closed early was entered exactly at the 72-hour mark, i.e. `jobs`' own timer won). The close closes the request once all its jobs are final. Texts go only with a consent record, once per job; a night close sends its text at 8 a.m. through a `tell` step (D-1196: Arizona only; the area-code zone is the send path's). `no_reply_step` takes the run time: before the step's due time it does nothing, and a text step run outside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Arizona re-queues itself for 8 a.m. (QH-008, D-1199); dedupe keys carry the run time. Job texts: JOB-010 to JOB-012, JOB-025, TXT-042, TXT-040, QH-001, QH-006, QH-007, SEC-037, CON-021, CON-028, D-1301 to D-1303: `job_text_step` skips a job whose version moved on (`jobs::api::text_due`; a close text's version is final, so it still goes), a job no request opened, a contact with no consent record (`may_text`), an opted-out or untextable number (`messaging::api::send_window`, which reads the phone; intake never does); outside 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in Arizona or her area-code zone it queues the same event again at the window's start (dedupe key with that instant); inside, one transaction re-checks the version, issues the quote-page token for `all_quotes_in` only (`https://askhava.com/q/<token>`), renders in the request's language and enqueues one outbox row (subject: the job); a row already queued rolls the new token back. No link in a payload or a log.
- TXT-031, D-1197: `answer_list` answers LIST/LISTA (with an optional request number) for the request's jobs with a shown quote; one job: `quotes::api::list_texts` pages; several: `which_job_text`; none: nothing. Names come from the caller.
- INT-021, D-1198: intake makes no AI call (since T156 it depends on `askhava-ai` only for the pure `accept_question` check, D-1273), so every step runs at once on the keyword rules and fixed questions; no template is a "we have your message" text. INT-023: `call_back_due` is 24 hours when `counts_urgent` (Today, emergency job, or a 911-list keyword before "How soon?" is answered), else `days` (default 4). `queue_call_back` raises one `ops` alert per request (`intake.call_back_urgent`, an OPS-032 push item, or `intake.call_back`) with a `call_back` answer row, under the homeowner lock (D-1199); T094 and T103 call it.

The dispatcher call and ending a pause are not built (D-1229). On a pause `on_opt_out` also calls `picks::api::withdraw_paused` in its transaction (the waiting pick cancelled, its business's notice enqueued, no status change, D-1245).
- PICK-015, PICK-016, PICK-031, TERMS-016, TERMS-033, D-1246: `answer_text_pick` (homeowner lock, one transaction) passes the open request's live jobs, each with its pause, to `picks::api::text_pick`; on a pick it stores one terms acceptance (channel `text_pick`, the inbound text, the assent line `picks::api::terms_line`, the caller's `PublishedTerms`). An opt-out, HELP, another phone or a job that moved gives `None`. No caller until the D-999 dispatcher.

- INT-041, CARD-007, D-1274 to D-1276, D-1278, D-1279: `show_card` skips an `emergency_choice = emergency` job (`Shown::Emergency`) and a request with no consent record; otherwise it texts `card_shown` when the whole text with the card fits 2 segments, else `card_link`, stores `shown.<digest>.<show>` and queues one watching `intake.card_timer`, under the homeowner lock in one transaction; every call is a new show with its own text (the outbox subject names the show) and timer, so the same card shown again after EDIT is texted again and an earlier show's timer never approves it; an approved card is never shown again. A watching timer reads the text's delivery status: unsent, it looks again in `RECHECK_AFTER`; sent, it queues the end of the wait at now plus the wait; failed or undelivered, it stops (D-1278). `answer_card`: the whole message SEND, OK, YES or ENVIAR approves the card last shown; EDIT or CAMBIAR stores `edit` (that card's timer then does nothing) and texts `card_edit`; an opt-out or HELP does nothing. Approval (SEND or the waiting timer at or after its due time while its show is still the latest) stores `sent.<digest>`, enqueues one `intake.cards_approved` event per job and one audit row: `intake.card_approved` by the homeowner on SEND, `intake.card_auto_sent` by the system from the timer. The card sender sends only a card for which `approved_card` holds.

## How to change
- Add a cancel-intent phrase: `INTENT_CLAUSES` (whole clause) or `NEED_PHRASES`/`NEED_OBJECTS` in `rules.rs` (folded), a case in `tests/close_cancel.rs`, and a not-intent case showing the phrase inside a longer request does not close (D-1203).
- Add a keyword or a trade to the second opinion: a row in `RULES` in `matching.rs` (catalog category ID and job type name, folded whole-word phrases, boundary true for a licensed job type), a label in `trade_label` if needed, then run both T042 test commands; `td_int_03_keyword_job_types_are_catalog_job_types` checks the job type against `docs/catalog/categories.yaml`.
- Add a 911-list word: INT-002 first (a spec change with a decision), then the list in `emergency.rs` (folded words; stems match word starts, phrases whole words), plus a case in `MATCHES` or `CLEAR` in `tests/emergency.rs`.
- Edit the consent text: CON-031 first, then `consent` (and `consent_safety_call`) in both `en.json` and `es.json` with their `segments`; run both test commands below and `cargo test -p askhava-messaging tests::template_registry`.
- Add a Spanish marker word or a who-is-this phrase: `SPANISH_WORDS` or `WHO_PHRASES` in `rules.rs` (folded: lower case, no accents or apostrophes), plus a case in `tests/consent.rs`.
- Edit the 911 text: INT-004 first (a spec change with a decision), then `emergency_911` in both `en.json` and `es.json` with `segments`, then `INT_004_EN`/`INT_004_ES` in `tests/safety_text.rs`; run both safety test commands.
- Set the skip threshold: only from a recorded AI-027 run in `docs/phase2/real-ai.md`, passed as `BankSettings::skip_threshold`; never a guessed value.
- Add a table or column: a new migration (`-- intake (T###)` first line), its row type in `models.rs`, regenerate `.sqlx/` (online build with `SQLX_OFFLINE_DIR=.sqlx`).
