# askhava-picks: the homeowner's pick and what follows it

## Purpose

The layer-6 crate that takes the homeowner's pick and carries it to the business's Confirm, the charge and the release (`docs/architecture.md` section 3). T054 built the first part: the page pick's 6-digit Twilio Verify code (10 minutes, 5 tries, a 15-minute cooldown, the PICK-055 send caps) and the "<number> YES" text pick, with the Terms line first, the which-request question, a second YES replacing a waiting pick, the status reply and the CANCEL pause's withdrawal (D-1243 to D-1247). T063 added the Confirm window, the license checks at the pick and at Confirm, the recurring fee line, the YES and CONFIRM reply and the pick notice (D-1500 to D-1504). T246 added the page pick (D-1516, D-1602 to D-1606). Not here yet: the PICK-045 homeowner text after a page pick (D-1606), the callers of `check_at_pick`, `check_at_confirm` and `notice_sent` (D-1502, D-1503), expiring a pick when `jobs`' backstop fires with no later send, `all_windows_ended` after an expiry and the PICK-046 notice (D-1503), payments hand-off and the release's callers. T069 added the release itself (D-1562).

## Rules it enforces

- PICK-013, D-101, D-015: `code_state`: open strictly before 10 minutes after the send; the fifth try used starts a 15-minute cooldown from that try, during which `check_code` never calls Verify and `send_code` refuses (`SendRefused::CoolingDown`); after it a new code is needed.
- PICK-013: `check_code` counts the try in a locked transaction before calling Verify, so concurrent tries never pass 5; a passed code is used once.
- PICK-014: `parse_code` takes six ASCII digits once spaces and dashes are dropped; a malformed entry counts no try (the `autocomplete="one-time-code"` field is T187's page).
- PICK-013, D-366: a voice code only for `CodeVia::CallTapped`, the homeowner's tap; never by itself.
- PICK-055, D-480, D-1244: per Arizona calendar day, 5 codes per number (counted by vault contact ID), 3 calls per number, 10 per job; no call from 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. Arizona time unless `own_session`; at or above the caller's global threshold nothing goes and one `picks.verify_daily_cap` High alert is raised per day. Verify is called after the commit, with an `IdempotencyKey` over the send's row ID.
- PICK-015, D-290: `read_text_pick` reads "<n> YES/SÍ/SI" with any words after, or "<request> <n> YES".
- PICK-031, PICK-032, PICK-036, D-068, D-147, D-1246: `plan_text_pick`: a job takes a pick in Quotes ready or Awaiting confirm; a job paused by the homeowner is not counted; more than one not paused asks which, listing their numbers in `{job}` with the first as the `{number}` example; none gives the status reply (confirmed line with CLOSE for Payment pending or Connected, waiting line for a job collecting quotes, the none line otherwise); another quote while one waits replaces it (old pick `cancelled`, `replaced`, a `picks.pick_withdrawn` event for its business; if the business confirmed it meanwhile, `PicksError::MovedOn` and nothing is picked); from Quotes ready the job moves to Awaiting confirm; each new pick enqueues `picks.pick_made` at once.
- PICK-012, PICK-032, PICK-045, CON-029, D-1516, D-1605: `page_pick(tx, (job, paused), quote, actor, at)` takes a pick only while `JobStatus::takes_pick` (Quotes ready, Awaiting confirm), not paused, for a quote on the page and available; the waiting quote again is `Same` (nothing stored); otherwise `place` ends the waiting pick `replaced` with its withdrawal event, moves Quotes ready to Awaiting confirm (Awaiting confirm stays, so the Confirm window does not restart) and stores the pick with channel `page` and no inbox; no fee, free win or release. A waiting pick confirmed meanwhile is `PicksError::MovedOn` (roll back).
- PICK-013, PRIV-003, D-1602: `send_page_code` and `check_page_code` read the homeowner's phone with `privacy::api::read_for_pick_code` on a connection outside any transaction (a malformed code reads nothing) and call `send_code`/`check_code`; the phone is never returned, logged or put in an error (a failed read is `PicksError::Database`).
- PICK-016, D-492: a pick only for a job with a `picks.terms_line` row; otherwise `text_pick_terms` goes and is recorded (template `<id>:<lang>:<12 hex of the SHA256 of the words>`); a text pick is stored with channel `text` and its inbound text; `intake::answer_text_pick` stores the terms acceptance.
- CON-029, D-1245: `withdraw_paused` (called by `intake::on_opt_out` in its transaction on CANCEL or CANCELAR) cancels each waiting pick with `paused` and enqueues the business's notice; job status and timers are untouched; `intake` passes each job's pause, so a paused job's YES is `Plan::Paused` with no pick and no text.
- FEE-020, BIZ-019, BIZ-037, D-492: `run_card_fix_notice` sends the card-fix notice only to `businesses::api::notice_addresses(Money)` (the owner and every "Confirm and billing" member), as opaque address IDs in one `messaging.business_notice` task with the job number and the window's current deadline, deduped per pick and attempt; nothing is sent when `payments::api::card_fix_window` says the pick no longer needs a fix (paid or ended); a bank approval uses `card_approval_notice`; `payments` never names a recipient.
- FEE-031, FEE-053, D-1557: `run_receipt_notice` queues one `messaging.charge_receipt` task per pick to `notice_addresses(Money)` with the job number, fee and "Ask Hava introduction service, job <n>", only for a charge `payments::api::receipt_for` finds for that business; a business with no money recipient gets one High `RECEIPT_NO_RECIPIENT_ALERT` (`picks.receipt_no_recipient`) about the pick and no email (D-1561).
- PICK-019, D-345, D-1500, D-1503: the window is `ConfirmSettings::window_minutes` business minutes (the `ASKHAVA_PICK_CONFIRM_WINDOW_MINUTES` setting) from the notice's real send (`notice_sent`, whose returned deadline is the notice's "Accept by"); `jobs`' backstop is restarted to end one business minute later, once per send; a send after `jobs` returned the job expires the pick and returns `MovedOn`; `run_confirm_ends` expires the pick and returns the job to Quotes ready only while the job's `timer_from` equals its task's `from`; an emergency job keeps `jobs`' 60 clock minutes.
- PICK-020, D-056: one live lookup at the pick (`check_at_pick`); a failed lookup uses a stored Active check under 24 hours old, else `Waits`; `check_at_confirm` never looks up and refuses an ended window, a non-Active account, a reached end or bond date, or a latest check not Active and fresh.
- PICK-033, PICK-035, D-365: `business_reply` never confirms or charges; YES and CONFIRM from a linked phone get the fee and link, after the window the expired reply, any other phone nothing.
- PICK-034, D-196, D-1503: `fee_line` shows the 4-month basis, or the cap when the cap set the fee, in English or Spanish; a zero fee has no line.
- PICK-043, D-252, D-284: `hold` adds `hold_minutes` business minutes to the window's end once, only for the owner or a "Confirm and billing" member, never on an emergency job, a second time, a clock delayed by Away or after the end.
- BIZ-034, D-285: `away_until`/`away_for` allow after now and at most `away_max_minutes` business minutes ahead (AWAY 0 refused at any hour, D-1526), owner or "Confirm and billing" only; `window_start` delays only a non-emergency pick.
- PICK-044, D-270, D-346: `emergency_plan` gives 60 clock minutes from the send at any hour, a reminder at 30; without the after-hours tick a night pick's notice waits for 8 a.m., and a day pick whose window runs past 8 p.m. warns and holds the reminder to 8 a.m., or drops it (`reminder_at: None`) when 8 a.m. is at or after `confirm_by`; `EmergencyPlan::open_at` is open from the send, strictly before `confirm_by`; `emergency_fix_by` is 60 clock minutes.
- PICK-037, D-491: `run_pick_notice` sends one notice per pick to `notice_addresses(PicksAndRelease)` only.
- PICK-022 to PICK-024, PRIV-005, PRIV-020, BIZ-014, INT-029, D-1562: `release` runs only on a `ReleaseProof` (a verified paid charge or a $0 fee decision, minted by `payments`), once per pick (`picks.release` primary key); an ended pick, or one whose status is not `paid` (paid proof) or `free` ($0 proof), releases nothing (D-1564); a regular job claims its release row and notice before opening the vault, an emergency job reads first; it opens name and address, the email only when the homeowner chose email contact, never the phone; an emergency job with no address releases nothing, stores its one `picks.address_wait` row (keyed by pick) and raises `picks.address_needed` once per pick; the notice to `notice_addresses(PicksAndRelease)` carries the job number and templates only, no homeowner detail and no call window, and an empty recipient list raises one High `picks.release_no_recipient` alert instead. The `ReleaseView` goes to the caller once and is stored nowhere; the portal's own read, the signed `{link}` and the contact-to-pick check are not built (D-1565).

- PICK-026, PICK-038, D-150, D-193, D-263, D-1572 to D-1574: `lost_notices` runs only for a `paid` or `free` pick and queues one `messaging.business_notice` per other confirmed, unwithdrawn quoter (unavailable ones included; one whose price does not read back still counts) to `notice_addresses(Cards)`, deduped per pick and business, with the job number and quote count only (never an amount or a name), the first sentence only when the picked quote or the receiver's own does not read back (D-1576); `compare` (one-time prices with each other, a recurring figure with the same period, a visit fee with a visit fee, within 10% both edges in is about the same) and `loss_template` (first sentence only under 3 quotes, "Several" when the card went to 4 or fewer); `no_longer_active` only at a final job status (withdrawn quoters included) and `refused_after_confirm` share one key per job and business and both skip a business whose pick is `paid` or `free` now; a no-show or fraud-refunded pick is not connected (D-1576); `live_pick_reply` (PICK-027) answers only the business whose pick waits.

- PICK-029, D-050, D-1613: a code goes by email only when texting is not open and only to a `ConversationEmail`; it uses Verify's email channel with the same 10 minutes, 5 tries, cooldown and caps, its row stored with channel `sms` (non-voice).
- PICK-030, CON-013, D-1614: `opted_out_yes` queues no text; it picks (through `page_pick`, channel `page`) only when `plan_text_pick` gives a pick, so never before the Terms line was sent (`TermsFirst`).

## Never break

- Refused at exactly 10:00, cooldown after the fifth wrong try, paste reads: `src/tests/pick_code_rules.rs`, `tests/security/tests/pick_code.rs::td_pick_06_*`.
- Voice only on the tap and never at night from another session; every cap holds; one alert a day; no number stored: `pick_code.rs::pick_055_*`.
- No pick before the Terms line; one acceptance per text pick: `tests/integration/tests/text_pick.rs::td_pick_40_*`.
- Another phone gets nothing; two jobs ask which: `text_pick.rs::td_pick_07_*`.
- A second YES replaces and tells the first business, but never after the business confirmed; confirmed and status replies: `text_pick.rs::td_pick_14_*`.
- CANCEL withdraws the waiting pick, keeps the status, blocks new picks: `text_pick.rs::td_con_12_*`.
- A page re-pick replaces the waiting one with its notice and no charge; the waiting quote again stores nothing; a failed acceptance leaves no pick: `tests/integration/tests/page_pick.rs`. Only `picks` reads the phone for the code and no digit leaks: `tests/security/tests/page_pick_privacy.rs`, `vault_readers.rs`.

- The window, its stale-timer fence, a repeated send restarting nothing, a held notice expiring a dead pick and the license checks: `tests/integration/tests/confirm_window.rs::td_pick_09_*`; the notice's recipients: `td_pick_15_*`; pure cases and the spec templates: `src/tests/confirm.rs`.

- Each other quoter told once, never an amount or a name; the 90% and 110% edges; no picked quote gives the first sentence only; "This job is no longer active." once, withdrawn quoters included, never to the connected business: `tests/integration/tests/pick_aftermath.rs`.

- Details only on a proof, once, never the phone, no detail in the notice: `src/tests/prop_release_only_paid.rs`, `tests/security/tests/no_phone_released.rs`. Never add `Field::Phone` to a release field list or a phone field to `ReleaseView`.

- An emailed code keeps the limits, a quote-page address never gets one, an opted-out YES queues no text and picks only after the Terms line: `tests/integration/tests/email_pick_code.rs`.

## How to change

- A cap or a timer: the spec line first (PICK-013, PICK-055), then the constant in `rules.rs` and both test files.
- A reply's words: the `text_pick_*` keys in both `en.json` and `es.json` with `segments`; any text offering a text pick must keep the share sentence and end with the Terms line; run `cargo test -p askhava-messaging tests::template_registry` and the text-pick test.
- A new pick status: a `PickStatus` variant and its code (already allowed by the table's check), arms in `status.rs` only. `Paid` and `Free` are the only statuses `release_status` releases from (D-1564).
- The Confirm window's length: set `ASKHAVA_PICK_CONFIRM_WINDOW_MINUTES` (or pass another `ConfirmSettings` in a test); the backstop follows (D-1500, D-1503).
- A query: `sqlx migrate run`, then `cargo sqlx prepare --workspace -- --all-targets --all-features`.
