# askhava-payments: Stripe and the money ledger

## Purpose
The layer-3 crate through which all money moves (FEE-032, `docs/architecture.md` section 3). T225 built its first part: the append-only ledger `payments.ledger_entry` and its one writer, `ledger_append` (D-1383, D-1384). T058 added the introduction fee by job size of a chosen one-time quote, `fee_for` (D-1399, D-1400).

## Never break
- No `UPDATE`, `DELETE` or `TRUNCATE` on `payments.ledger_entry` for `askhava_app`; a wrong entry is corrected by a new entry (D-092): `src/tests/ledger_append.rs` `the_app_role_cannot_change_a_row`.
- `EntryKind::code` matches the migration's `kind` check exactly: `every_entry_type_appends`.
- Amounts are unsigned whole cents; direction comes from the kind (D-1383). Do not store negative amounts.
- `LedgerError` carries no database message (D-698, D-1384); do not add a `sqlx::Error` source.
- `ledger_append` runs on the caller's connection, so the entry commits or rolls back with the caller's state change (rust brain R11); never call Stripe inside that transaction.
- The fee is always $25, $75 or $199 and never falls as the basis rises; a fee-free job type pays $0: `src/tests/prop_fee_props.rs`.
- Every one-time worked example matches to the cent and the recurring and refused rows are pinned: `src/tests/worked_examples.rs`; every recurring row (and W32) matches through `period_fee_for`, and W20 and W33 need the cap: `src/tests/recurring.rs`.
- Nothing in `models.rs` or `service.rs` creates a PaymentIntent or charge; saving a card charges nothing (FEE-036): `tests/integration/tests/setup_intent.rs` (its fake Stripe panics on any charge).
- `payments.card_authorization` is append-only for the app role, and `payments` code never names a homeowner (D-1409): `setup_intent.rs` `td_fee_10_authorizations_are_append_only`, `no_homeowner_charge.rs`.
- The fee charged at Confirm is the one fixed at the pick; a later quote edit, AI flag or PROBLEM reply changes no ledger row: `tests/integration/tests/fee_at_pick.rs`.
- No Confirm, and no fee entry, without the authorization version the fee terms need: `tests/integration/tests/fee_mode_switch.rs`. Never charge an amount other than the stored `payments.pick_fee` of the press's own business and job, never call Stripe before `fee_matches`, never grant the app role UPDATE or DELETE on `pick_fee`, and never let `record_fee_at_confirm` read the terms in force (D-1871): `tests/integration/tests/fee_fixed_at_pick.rs`.
- One free first win per entity key, never a plaintext key value, and a repeat never uses the win: `tests/integration/tests/free_wins.rs`, `repeat_customer.rs`. `waive_at_confirm` runs before the Stripe charge, in the Confirm transaction; `NotWaived` is the only answer that leads to a charge.
- One PaymentIntent per pick, one key per attempt, no second try before Stripe says the first failed, the timers and the limit: `tests/integration/tests/charge_attempts.rs`. Two presses on two picks never both pass the limit: always lock the pick's `pick_intent` row before the business's `fee_limit` row (one order, no deadlock), keep the limit check and the attempt insert in one transaction, and never hold it across Stripe: `tests/integration/tests/fee_limit_race.rs` (D-2081). Never call Stripe inside a transaction in `charge.rs` or `handlers.rs`; never add a server-side retry of a confirm (FEE-016).
- Release only through `paid_release`, `no_fee_release` or `reclear_release` and then `Minter::mint`; `Releasable`'s one crate-private constructor is `from_stored_success`, which reads only the named pick's own stored, unended, unrefunded, undisputed success; `reclear_release` is called only by `server`'s `intake.address_in` handler after `picks::api::awaiting_address` found the wait row; Guarded by `tests/integration/tests/confirm_and_pay.rs`. More: `(never 14)` in NOTES.md.
- `payments.charge_evidence` is append-only for the app role: `confirm_and_pay.rs` `td_fee_38_evidence_is_append_only`.
- Refund rules are pure and take the call log from `picks`; `payments` never reads job, pick or call tables (D-491). A pick with nothing charged gives `NothingCharged`, a pick already refunded is never refunded again, and every refund of a pick uses `refund_label` (FEE-026): `src/tests/refund_rules.rs`. FEE-026's hourly retry and its block on a disputed charge are not modelled in `refund_rules.rs`; More: `(never 16)` in NOTES.md.
- FEE-027, FEE-050, D-2204: `mark_auto_submit` (mark and its submit task) and `record_owner_submission` (`submitted_at` and its countering fee) write both rows in one transaction on their connection (a savepoint inside a caller's), and `record_dispute` queues its `picks.dispute_opened` checks in the dispute's transaction: `tests/integration/tests/dispute_submit.rs` (TD-FEE-51).
- FEE-045, D-2331: `resolve_fee_facts` makes any fee-free candidate fee-free and is one-time only when every candidate is; an unknown code, a candidate outside the category or a tier not $25 or $75 is `None`, never a guess: `tests/integration/tests/fee_facts_at_pick.rs` (TD-FEE-62).
- A success books one `charge` entry per pick (key `charge.<pick>`, intent amount, `ch_` ID) with its evidence and receipt in one transaction, never without a `ch_` ID: `tests/integration/tests/charge_ledger_entry.rs` (TD-FEE-64).
