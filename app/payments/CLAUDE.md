# askhava-payments: Stripe and the money ledger

## Purpose

The layer-3 crate through which all money moves (FEE-032, `docs/architecture.md` section 3). T225 built its first part: the append-only ledger `payments.ledger_entry` and its one writer, `ledger_append` (D-1383, D-1384). T058 added the introduction fee by job size of a chosen one-time quote, `fee_for` (D-1399, D-1400). T188 added quotes with a period, `period_fee_for` (the 4-month rule and the recurring cap), and the early joiner's price lock (D-1404 to D-1407). T059 added Stripe customers made at sign-up with no card and saved cards with the FEE-015 authorization, cards only (D-1409, D-1410). T060 added the fee fixed at the pick, its ledger entry at Confirm and the fee-mode switch with its new authorization (D-1414, D-1415, D-1417, D-1418). T061 added free first wins, one per entity key inside the 90-day window, and $0 repeat customers (D-1419, D-1420). T062 added the Confirm-and-pay charge: one PaymentIntent per pick, one attempt per press with its own key, the 4-hour Stripe release, the card-fix window, the charging pause and the monthly fee limit (D-1424 to D-1428). T189 added the FEE-042 evidence record of each charge and the release minter (D-1474 to D-1478). T224 added the signature-checked Stripe webhook route (D-1479). Not here yet: the SetupIntent and PaymentIntent adapter calls and the descriptor on the create form (T132), the route's wiring in `server` (D-1480), the route's event-ID dedupe (`payments.webhook_event`, SEC-017, D-1482), the ledger write after a verified success, refunds, disputes and evidence, the webhook and the nightly reconcile (T067).

## Rules it enforces

- FEE-028, D-092: `payments.ledger_entry` is append-only for the app role (grants in the migration); `LedgerEntry::new` and the table's checks agree: $0 kinds (free first win, repeat customer FEE-013, fee-free job type FEE-045) are exactly 0 with no Stripe ID, every other kind is above 0; payout has no business or job; every kind but payout and credit has a job.
- FEE-028, FEE-050, FEE-051: the countering-fee, countering-fee-return and processing-fee kinds exist for the dispute and reconcile tasks.
- FEE-032: `ledger_append` is the only code that writes the ledger; other crates reach it through `api`.
- JOB-006, SEC-018: the caller's derived idempotency key (no personal data) makes a repeated task append once; a reused key with different content is `LedgerError::KeyReused`.
- FEE-001, FEE-002, FEE-008, D-009: `Tier::for_basis`: under $500.00 is $25, $500.00 to $1,500.00 inclusive is $75, over is $199; `Fee::cents` is exactly the tier, no tax, no percentage.
- FEE-003, FEE-043, D-033: `fee_for` prices a range, and an hourly quote with hours (rate times low to rate times high), at the midpoint rounded down to the cent.
- FEE-004, PICK-053, D-418: a diagnostic-visit quote, and an hourly quote with no hours, pays the category's `VisitTier` ($25 or $75 only) in a `diagnostic_visit` job; elsewhere `NotDiagnosticVisit` or `NeedsHours`.
- FEE-045, D-403: a fee-free job type gives `Fee::FeeFree`, $0, for every quote.
- FEE-044: no input has a setup amount, so a setup charge never reaches a basis (D-1400, D-1404).
- REC-002, REC-004, REC-005, REC-006, D-010: `period_fee_for` takes 4 months (`RECURRING_MONTHS`, never another number) of the monthly amount (`Period::per_month`; only the five periods, any other visit schedule comes in converted to `Monthly`, D-1407), a range at its exact midpoint, rounded down to the cent once at the end.
- REC-001, REC-010, D-037, D-159: `Service::of(recurring, one_time_job_type)`; a recurring job type with no period is `NeedsPeriod`, a period outside a recurring category `PeriodNotRecurring`, a period on an hourly or diagnostic-visit quote `NotRecurringPrice`; a one-time job with no period is `fee_for`'s face value.
- FEE-039, D-196, D-265: a recurring job type's tier is never above `RecurringCap` ($75 default, always a tier); a one-time job type is never capped, whatever period is typed.
- FEE-040, D-465: `price_lock_until` is none for a join 90 days or more after launch, else 6 calendar months (Arizona) from the latest of join, first trade on and activation; `locked_cents` charges at most the join-date amount strictly before that end.
- FEE-009: a Stripe customer is keyed by `BusinessId`; no `payments` table, type or function names a homeowner (`tests/security/tests/no_homeowner_charge.rs`).
- FEE-036, D-089: `ensure_customer` makes the customer at sign-up with no card, key derived from the business ID only (SEC-018), no transaction across the Stripe call.
- FEE-014, D-073: `SavedCard::new` refuses every method type but `card` (`CardsOnly`) and the table checks `method_type = 'card'`; only the `pm_…` ID, brand and last four are stored.
- FEE-015: `save_card` stores `AUTHORIZATION_TEXT` with `AUTHORIZATION_VERSION` and the acceptance time in the same transaction as the card; it charges nothing.
- FEE-005, D-009, D-1415: `fee_at_pick` takes the chosen quote, its period, the job and the fee mode only; no AI flag or other quote is an input, and only it makes a `PickedFee`.
- FEE-006, JOB-016, D-071: `record_fee_at_confirm` writes exactly `PickedFee::cents` at Confirm (a `charge` only with a `ch_…` ID, or `fee-free-job-type` at $0 with none, else `ChargeId`), key from job and business IDs; nothing in `payments` takes a final bill or a check-in reply.
- FEE-007, D-072, D-1414: `switch_fee_mode` logs an `ops` audit row (owner actor, `tiers`/`flat`) and returns `FeeTerms` with the next authorization version; `may_confirm` refuses until the business accepted that version (`accept_authorization`) and runs before the Stripe charge, returning the `Authorized` proof `record_fee_at_confirm` takes (no second read after the charge; a proof for another business or mode is `Mismatch`, D-1417); flat mode keeps the FEE-039 recurring cap; flat mode is `flat_tier`: the owner's edit, else the category's set tier; fee-free stays $0.
- FEE-010, D-152, D-195: `in_free_win_window`: the sign-up form submitted strictly before launch + 90 days or a category's switch-on + 90 days (a business that joined earlier counts); exactly 90 days is out; activation is not an input.
- FEE-011, D-151: entity keys are stored only as an HMAC digest under the ID secret of the normalized value (trimmed, lower-case, single spaces); a used win claims every key of the business (and `record_entity_keys` claims a key recorded after the win), and `waive_at_confirm` refuses a win when the business has one or any key is claimed or shared with a business that has one, at use (D-1422). A concurrent claim, deadlock or serialization failure rolls the savepoint back as `FreeWinError::Race` (the caller reruns the Confirm); claims insert in `(kind, digest)` order.
- FEE-012: a free first win is a $0 `free-first-win` ledger entry plus `payments.free_win.waived_cents` (the ledger keeps $0 kinds at exactly 0).
- FEE-053, D-1422: `waive_at_confirm` run again for a business and job already in the ledger returns that answer (`Appended::Already`, or `NotWaived` for a charge) and writes nothing.
- FEE-013, D-069: `waive_at_confirm` checks repeat next: an earlier `charge`, `free-first-win`, `repeat-customer` or `fee-free-job-type` entry of the same business on another job in `same_customer_jobs` gives a $0 `repeat-customer` entry and never touches the win; the caller passes no jobs after the 180-day purge (PRIV-007). A fee-free pick is `NotWaived` (its $0 entry is `record_fee_at_confirm`'s).
- FEE-016, FEE-047, D-1424, D-1425: `confirm_and_pay` is the only charge and runs only for a `Press`; every attempt confirms the pick's one PaymentIntent (`open_intent`, key from `intent_label`) with the key from `attempt_label(pick, n)`; a repeated press answers from its own attempt and never sends again; the attempt is inserted under the pick's row lock only while no attempt is undecided or paid, so two presses at once make one attempt; a press from another business is `MovedOn`; nothing retries a charge.
- FEE-017: before every attempt, the caller's `PickNow` and the pick's own record (ended, or already paid) are checked; a moved-on press charges nothing.
- FEE-037, D-153: an undecided latest attempt (`Sent` after a timeout, `Processing`) is settled by `fetch_payment_intent` first; a new try goes only after Stripe says it failed, never while Stripe is unreachable; a `sent` attempt younger than `IN_FLIGHT` is never read as failed; `may_become`: a success from Stripe always replaces a decline. A 4xx refusal other than 402 and 429 reads the intent again (`after_refusal`); a 429 is `unreachable`; a 5xx stays `sent`.
- FEE-018, FEE-019, FEE-020, D-270, D-1426: an unanswered call starts the 4-hour release; a decline or bank approval opens the card-fix window (1 business hour, 60 clock minutes on an emergency job) and queues `CARD_FIX_KIND`; every attempt queues `CHECK_KIND` 30 minutes after it starts and a check that finds it still undecided queues the next round; at a timer's end Stripe is asked again: paid stays, the release ends the pick only while Stripe is unreachable, the card-fix window only on Stripe's word that the card still needs fixing, anything else moves the timer `RECHECK_AFTER` on; the end raises `PICK_ENDED_KIND` once.
- FEE-038, OPS-024, D-135, D-1427: `charges_paused` is the `ops` `charging` or `all` switch; a press while paused is `Paused`; `sync_pause` stops each running timer with its time left and resumes it with that time; `run_pause_watch` (`PAUSE_KIND`, registered in `server`) runs it every minute while a timer is open.
- FEE-041, D-204, D-1427: a press that would take this Arizona month's succeeded charges over `payments.fee_limit` is `OverLimit`: it is checked after an undecided earlier try is settled and before the PaymentIntent is made, so Stripe is not called, and the pick ends at once (`announce_end` when no intent exists yet); exactly at the limit is charged.
- FEE-048, D-490: `SetupIntentRequest::form` saves with `usage=off_session`, cards only; saved cards are stored with `usage = 'off_session'`.
- FEE-042, D-1474: a succeeded attempt writes its pick's one `charge_evidence` row in the transaction that stores it (`write_evidence` in `handlers::effects`), IDs and timestamps only, with the business's latest FEE-015 acceptance at or before the press (D-1478); an insert that writes nothing for a pick with no record is `ChargeError::Database`.
- PICK-022, SEC-030, SEC-039, SEC-049, D-1475: `paid_release` clears a pick only for a `VerifiedStripeEvent` (from an `adapters`-verified Stripe webhook) of type `payment_intent.succeeded`, livemode equal to `StripeAccount::live`, no other account named, for the pick's stored PaymentIntent, whose re-fetch says succeeded, `Some(live)`, the stored fee in `usd` and this pick; it stores the success first; a pick whose `ended_at` is set, before the fetch or after the store, is `PickEnded` (D-1478). `no_fee_release` clears only on a $0 ledger decision and never a pick with a PaymentIntent; the caller binds the pick to its business and job (D-1478). Only `Minter::mint` turns a `Releasable` into a `ReleaseProof`; a redirect has no event, so it clears nothing.
- SEC-039, SEC-049, PICK-022, D-1479: `stripe_webhook_routes` checks the source address (SEC-040 client IP; Stripe's committed list in sandbox and live, the stand-in's host in stand-in mode) and then the signature on the raw body before anything reads the event; either refusal is 403 with `refuse`'s one log line. Only a `payment_intent.succeeded` goes on, to `paid_release` for the pick its metadata names; only a `Releasable` reaches the `ReleaseSink`. `Stripe` and `Database` failures answer 500 (Stripe resends); every other outcome 200.
- FEE-049, D-1476: `IntentText::for_job` refuses a descriptor over 22 characters.
- FEE-022, FEE-023 to FEE-025, FEE-035, CALL-007, D-141, D-143 (T065): `decide_unreachable` refunds at 96 hours after the release only with 2 business attempts not answered by a person, at least 3 hours apart (`ATTEMPT_GAP`), within 72 hours and 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Arizona (any hour on an emergency), no call answered by a person either way, no DONE/PROBLEM, no email-contact YES, and the missed-call notice sent or attempted; otherwise `NoRefund` names the first failed `Unmet` (D-336). `decide_cancel` refunds a charge taken after the cancel always, else only if no call was answered by a person before the cancel. `no_show` records a no-show (never refunded) for call contact with no business call in 72 hours, for email contact only on a day-3 NO.

## Never break

- No `UPDATE`, `DELETE` or `TRUNCATE` on `payments.ledger_entry` for `askhava_app`; a wrong entry is corrected by a new entry (D-092): `src/tests/ledger_append.rs` `the_app_role_cannot_change_a_row`.
- `EntryKind::code` matches the migration's `kind` check exactly: `every_entry_type_appends`.
- Amounts are unsigned whole cents; direction comes from the kind (D-1383). Do not store negative amounts.
- `LedgerError` carries no database message (D-698, D-1384); do not add a `sqlx::Error` source.
- `ledger_append` runs on the caller's connection, so the entry commits or rolls back with the caller's state change (rust brain R11); never call Stripe inside that transaction.
- The fee is always $25, $75 or $199 and never falls as the basis rises; a fee-free job type pays $0: `src/tests/prop_fee_props.rs`.
- Every one-time worked example matches to the cent and the recurring and refused rows are pinned: `src/tests/worked_examples.rs`; every recurring row (and W32) matches through `period_fee_for`, and W20 and W33 need the cap: `src/tests/recurring.rs`.
- Nothing in `models.rs` or `service.rs` creates a PaymentIntent or charge; saving a card charges nothing (FEE-036): `tests/integration/tests/setup_intent.rs` (its fake Stripe panics on any charge).
- `payments.card_authorization` is append-only for the app role, and `payments` code never names a homeowner (D-1409): `setup_intent.rs` `td_fee_10_authorizations_are_append_only`, `no_homeowner_charge.rs`.

- The fee charged at Confirm is the one fixed at the pick; a later quote edit, AI flag or PROBLEM reply changes no ledger row: `tests/integration/tests/fee_at_pick.rs`.
- No Confirm, and no fee entry, without the authorization version the fee terms need: `tests/integration/tests/fee_mode_switch.rs`.
- One free first win per entity key, never a plaintext key value, and a repeat never uses the win: `tests/integration/tests/free_wins.rs`, `repeat_customer.rs`. `waive_at_confirm` runs before the Stripe charge, in the Confirm transaction; `NotWaived` is the only answer that leads to a charge.

- One PaymentIntent per pick, one key per attempt, no second try before Stripe says the first failed, the timers and the limit: `tests/integration/tests/charge_attempts.rs`. Never call Stripe inside a transaction in `charge.rs` or `handlers.rs`; never add a server-side retry of a confirm (FEE-016).
- Release only through `paid_release`/`no_fee_release` then `Minter::mint`; never mint elsewhere, never give `Releasable` a public constructor or `Clone`: `tests/integration/tests/confirm_and_pay.rs`, `release_proof::tests`.
- `payments.charge_evidence` is append-only for the app role: `confirm_and_pay.rs` `td_fee_38_evidence_is_append_only`.
- Refund rules are pure and take the call log from `picks`; `payments` never reads job, pick or call tables (D-491). A pick with nothing charged gives `NothingCharged`, a pick already refunded is never refunded again, and every refund of a pick uses `refund_label` (FEE-026): `src/tests/refund_rules.rs`. FEE-026's hourly retry and its block on a disputed charge are not modelled in `refund_rules.rs`; they are T066's (`refunds.rs`, `disputes.rs`, D-1531).

## How to change

- Add an entry kind: a spec change to FEE-028 in `docs/spec/fees.md` first; then a new forward migration replacing the `kind` check (and the amount/party checks if needed), a variant in `EntryKind` with its `code` and `ALL` entry, and run `cargo test -p askhava-payments tests::ledger_append`.
- Write a ledger row from a new task: build `NewEntry`, call `LedgerEntry::new`, then `api::ledger_append` inside your transaction with a key derived from IDs only.
- After any query change: apply migrations, regenerate `.sqlx/` and run `cargo sqlx prepare --workspace --check -- --all-targets --all-features`.
- Change a fee tier or edge: `docs/spec/fees.md` FEE-001 and `docs/catalog/worked_examples.yaml` first, then `MEDIUM_FROM_CENTS`, `MEDIUM_UP_TO_CENTS` and `Tier::cents` in `src/rules.rs`; run `cargo test -p askhava-payments -- tests::fee_tiers tests::worked_examples tests::prop_fee_props`.
- Add a worked example: add the row to the YAML; a recurring or refused row also goes in the pinned list in `src/tests/worked_examples.rs`, and the checked count changes there (and, for a recurring row, in `src/tests/recurring.rs`).
- Change what flat mode charges: FEE-007 in `docs/spec/fees.md` and D-072 first, then `flat_tier` and `fee_at_pick` in `src/rules.rs`; run `cargo test -p askhava-payments tests::fee_at_pick` and `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test fee_mode_switch`.
- Change the free-win window or keys: FEE-010/FEE-011 in `docs/spec/fees.md` first, then `FREE_WIN_WINDOW_DAYS` or `EntityKind` (with a migration replacing the `kind` check); never change `EntityKey::new`'s normalizing or `ENTITY_KEY_ACTION`, or stored digests stop matching.
- Change a payment timer (FEE-018, FEE-020) or the check delay (FEE-019): the spec line first, then `CARD_FIX_SECONDS`, `STRIPE_DOWN_SECONDS` or `CHECK_AFTER` in `src/handlers.rs`; run `cargo test -p askhava-payments tests::charge` and `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test charge_attempts`.
- Add an attempt status: a variant in `src/status.rs` with its arms and a code matching the `ops` audit check `[a-z0-9_.]`, a new migration replacing the `charge_attempt` status check, and a case in `src/tests/charge.rs`.
- Wire the webhook route (D-1480): in `server::build_router` merge `stripe_webhook_routes` with a `StripeWebhook` built from settings and a `ReleaseSink` that mints with `release_minter()`, and serve with `into_make_service_with_connect_info::<SocketAddr>()`; run `cargo test -p askhava-payments routes`.
- Wire the release: the webhook route (T224) builds `VerifiedStripeEvent::from_webhook`, calls `paid_release` with the configured `StripeAccount`, then `server::release_minter().mint(cleared, contact)`; a $0 pick calls `no_fee_release`. Run `cargo test -p askhava-integration --test confirm_and_pay` and `cargo test -p askhava-payments release_proof`.
